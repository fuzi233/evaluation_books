Many had attempted to find the mine, but few had succeeded, and even fewer had returned to tell the tale. Nuggets of gold unlike any known grade in the Northland had been discovered within its walls, and the stories of these finds had been passed down through the years, fueling the dreams of prospectors and adventurers alike. John Thornton, ever the pragmatist, approached the challenge with a mix of curiosity and caution. He knew that the road ahead would be fraught with danger, but he was undaunted by the uncertainty.
