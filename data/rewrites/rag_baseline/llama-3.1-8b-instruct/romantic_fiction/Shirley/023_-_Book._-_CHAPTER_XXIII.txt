As the long, languid hours ticked by, Caroline found herself lost in the monotony of her day, her uncle away at Whinbury. The garden, once a vibrant tapestry of colors and scents, now felt desolate, its beauty lost on her as she sat in the alcove, her needle moving mechanically in her hand. Her fingers worked tirelessly, but her mind wandered, a restless creature trapped in a prison of solitude. The sun beat down relentlessly, casting no shadows to alleviate the emptiness within her.

It was Fanny who broke the stillness, her voice carrying across the lawn as she called out, "Miss Caroline!" The sound was like a spark, igniting a flame of hope within Caroline's chest. A low, husky voice responded, "Fanny!" and the maid hastened to the alcove, a note clutched in her hand. She handed it to Caroline, who received it with fingers that seemed to tremble with anticipation.

The note was from Hortense, her dear friend, and the words danced across the page like a promise of deliverance. "I'm back from Wormwood Wells," the message read, "and I'm alone today. I'd love for you to join me for tea – it would do you a world of good, dear Caroline. Robert's at Whinbury market, but I'm sure you'll find a way to get here." The postscript was a gentle nudge, urging Caroline to put on her bonnet and run down to the Hollow immediately.

The words on the page were like a balm to Caroline's parched soul. She laid aside the holland child's slip she was trimming with braid, the dull, brown fabric seeming suddenly dull and lifeless compared to the vibrant colors of her imagination. With a sense of purpose, she rose from her seat, her heart racing with excitement. She hastily covered her curls with her straw bonnet, the familiar ritual bringing a sense of comfort and normalcy to her day. The black silk scarf, a simple yet elegant addition to her ensemble, seemed to shimmer in the sunlight, its dark hue setting off the creamy complexion of her skin.

As she made her way down the green lane, the scent of hedge flowers wafted up to her, a heady perfume that transported her to a world beyond the confines of her sorrow.
