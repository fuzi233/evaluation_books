As the expedition navigated the treacherous terrain of the Cordillera, the team found themselves beset by the harsh realities of their quest. A fortnight of grueling labor had passed, and still, they had yet to uncover any tangible evidence of the riches they so desperately sought. Bidding farewell to the lush expanse of the eastern plains, they had traversed the rugged landscape, catching glimpses of the distant city of Santa Fe perched atop its majestic mountain plateau, teeming with verdant gardens. Though the metropolis loomed large, its grandeur proved an insurmountable obstacle, and they had wisely chosen to bypass it in favor of a more discreet approach.

Their Indian guide, ever vigilant and resourceful, had uncovered a vital piece of information: a gold-train, bound for the Magdalena, was making its way down from Santa Fe. Seizing upon this opportunity, the team had established a strategic vantage point alongside the meandering road, a humble path carved into the side of a steep hill, its precipitous drop-off shrouded in a perpetual veil of mist. Here, amidst a forest of towering oaks, they found themselves transported to a world both familiar and strange, as the tree-ferns that carpeted the forest floor seemed to evoke memories of a bygone era, one that existed beyond the confines of their own tumultuous reality.

As they settled into their makeshift encampment, the air was alive with the whispers of the tropics, a cacophony of sounds that defied easy explanation. Their attempts to locate the elusive sulphur and nitre had proven futile, leaving them reliant on the humble resources of their swords and arrows. Yet, as they sat amidst the vibrant blooms, chewing coca in lieu of sustenance, they couldn't shake the enigmatic presence that trailed behind them – a low-frequency rumble that had become an eerie constant since their departure from the Meta's banks. Though the sound was an enigma, it was eclipsed by the prospect of intercepting the gold-train, an objective that promised to unlock the secrets of this unforgiving yet alluring realm.
