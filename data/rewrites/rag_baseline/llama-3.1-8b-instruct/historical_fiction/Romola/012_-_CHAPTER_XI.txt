Tito navigated the cobblestone Via de' Bardi with the measured steps of a man weighing his options. The evening sun cast a warm orange glow over the terracotta rooftops of Florence, yet Tito's thoughts remained cool and detached, much like the chilled glass of Chianti that awaited him in the taverns along the Arno. He pondered the enigmatic figure of Fra Luca, whose words had stirred within him a sense of foreboding and uncertainty.

As he walked, Tito's gaze fell upon the façade of the Santa Felicita church, its stone carvings weathered to a soft patina. He recalled the friar's words, the gentle cadence of his voice, and the weight of his conviction. Tito's heart, however, remained resolute in its determination to conceal the truth, to shield himself from the consequences of a revelation that could alter the course of his life.

He pushed open the door to his family's modest abode, where Romola and her father sat amidst the familiar comforts of parchment and marble. The scent of baking bread wafted from the kitchen, mingling with the faint aroma of herbs and spices that clung to the walls. Tito's entrance was met with a gentle smile from his sister, her eyes sparkling with a warmth that he had grown accustomed to, but could not reciprocate with equal fervor.

As the evening wore on, Tito struggled to reconcile his conflicting emotions, his mind racing with the implications of Fra Luca's words. He knew that he had reached a critical juncture, a crossroads where the path of truth and deception diverged. His thoughts were a maelstrom of doubt and calculation, as he weighed the pros and cons of disclosure against the comfort of his present life.

The stars began to twinkle above, casting a celestial glow over the city, and Tito's resolve hardened. He would put off the decision, delaying the inevitable until the next day, when he would seek out Fra Luca at the convent of San Marco. There, he hoped to glean more information, to understand the friar's motives and the true nature of his claims. And perhaps, just perhaps, he would find a way to navigate the treacherous waters of his conscience, to find a course that would allow him to maintain his secrets, and yet still chart a future of promise and possibility.
