A Battle Royal

I settled onto the plush cushion, letting the soothing aroma of jasmine tea waft through my senses as I pondered the enigmatic tale the princess had shared with me earlier. Her words had been laced with a subtle nuance, one that hinted at a deeper complexity to her journey than initially met the eye. As I turned the events over in my mind, the image of the leopardess bounding across the forest floor and the princess collapsing beside the stream resurfaced, casting a sense of trepidation over me. It dawned on me that I had inadvertently protected the very being she sought to destroy – the lifeblood of the Little Ones.

My thoughts were interrupted by a languid drowsiness, as if I were floating on the tranquil surface of a moonlit lake. Time seemed to bend and warp, losing all meaning in the gentle haze. I drifted, untethered, until a sudden jolt of pain pierced my heart like a razor-sharp blade. It was a searing agony, unlike anything I had ever experienced before. My hand trembled, unable to reach for the ache, as if an unseen force held it in check.

The pain receded, leaving behind a numbing paralysis that suffused my entire body. Something malevolent lurked within me, its presence suffocating and repulsive. My will struggled to assert itself, but it was no match for the oppressive weight bearing down upon me. I lay still, trapped in a living nightmare.

As I gradually regained control, a soft pressure on my face made me aware of the princess standing beside me, her eyes fixed on some distant point beyond the room. Her features were serene, her full lips curled into a satisfied smile. A crimson streak marred her skin, and she absently wiped it away with the hem of her handkerchief.

Her gaze snapped towards mine, and with a swift, deliberate motion, she struck me across the eyes with the cloth. The sensation was akin to a knife slicing through my vision, leaving me momentarily blind.

A low, heavy thud echoed through the room, followed by the soft rustle of a long tail disappearing through the partially open doorway. I sprang from the bed, racing down the stairs into the grand alabaster hall. The moon cast an ethereal glow, transforming the space into a pale, lunar landscape. The princess was nowhere to be seen, and I knew I had to find her – her presence was the only shield against her insidious influence.
