As I reflect on the past decade, I am reminded of the words of King David, 'We spend our years as a tale that is told.' Ten years have passed since our arrival on the island, and the chronicle of our experiences has unfolded like a rich tapestry. Time has slipped away, imperceptibly, yet the memories remain etched vividly in my mind.

The shade of melancholy cast by retrospection is tempered by the warmth of gratitude for the prosperity and contentment that has defined our family's life on this distant shore. Our sons, too, have grown into capable young men, their characters strengthened by the early lessons they learned from us. My heart swells with pride as I contemplate their futures, wherever duty may lead them.

As I ponder the legacy we've created here, I am struck by the notion that a peaceful, industrious, and pious life, marked by unity and cheerfulness, is the most effective foundation for forming strong, pure, and noble characters. Those who emerge from such a home are destined for greatness, for they possess the resilience, compassion, and wisdom that will serve them well in the challenges they will face.

Having documented our experiences in New Switzerland, I shall refrain from recounting the minutiae of our excursions and expeditions, as well as the innovations and contraptions that, though ingenious, may not be of particular interest to readers. Instead, let me highlight a few key aspects of our situation at the time, which will provide insight into the harmonious balance we've achieved with our surroundings.

Rockburg and Falconhurst continue to serve as our winter and summer headquarters, each having undergone transformations that have enhanced their functionality and aesthetic appeal. The gardens surrounding Rockburg have flourished, with fountains, trellises, and verdant plantations creating a serene ambiance that was previously lacking due to the harsh climate and scarcity of vegetation. Our carefully tended groves of native and European flora now provide a haven for an array of wildlife, including the majestic black swans, snow-white geese, and resplendent ducks that inhabit the tranquil waters of the lake.

Beyond the lake, the marshy area has been transformed into a thriving ecosystem, teeming with waterfowl, including the brilliant Sultan, marsh-fowl, and crimson flamingos, which coexist in harmony with their human caretakers.
