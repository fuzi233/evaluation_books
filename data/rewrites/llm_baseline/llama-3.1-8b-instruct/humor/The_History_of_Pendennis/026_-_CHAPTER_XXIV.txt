The whispered secrets that lurked within the walls of Clavering House were a testament to the notion that even the most seemingly idyllic façades conceal hidden miseries. The gentle, melodious voice and charming demeanor of Blanche Amory, the family's innocent muse, belied a presence that was as much a source of discord as it was a balm to those around her. Like a single pebble lodged in a shoe, she had become an insidious obstacle, capable of derailing the progress of those who encountered her and filling their lives with endless frustration and discontent.

"I've often thought," the Baronet mused, puffing on his cigar as he leaned against the billiard table, "that it would be a blessing if your wife were no longer with us, Strong."

Strong raised an eyebrow, his expression amused. "Indeed? And why is that, my friend?"

"Because, were she gone, you might consider marrying Missy. She's a beautiful young woman, and her dowry would provide a welcome injection of funds into your household," the Baronet replied, his tone dripping with condescension.

Strong chuckled, his eyes glinting with mirth. "Ah, but I think I'll have to decline. I've seen many a pretty face in my time, but none quite so... trying as yours, Missy."

The Baronet's countenance darkened. "I must confess, I find her increasingly infuriating. Her affectations and sentimental prattling are enough to drive one to distraction. I've grown to detest her, Strong, I assure you."

Strong's laughter was a low, throaty sound. "I wouldn't take her at any price, old chap. In fact, I think I'd rather not have her at all."

The Baronet's gaze turned cold. "I'm not surprised. She has a knack for making everyone miserable, and I daresay she's been at it again. Last evening, she sent the governess fleeing from the dinner table in tears. I overheard her terrorizing her brother in his room, spinning tales of the ghost said to haunt these halls. And at luncheon, she managed to unsettle my wife, though my wife is a kind soul – a fool, perhaps, but kind nonetheless."

Strong's interest was piqued. "What did she do to your wife this time?"
