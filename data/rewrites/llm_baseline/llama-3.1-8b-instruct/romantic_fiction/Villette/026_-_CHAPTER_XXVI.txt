Her measured approach to self-interest was a quality I admired, as she navigated the complexities of running a prestigious boarding school with tact and foresight. While she never openly praised me or my acquaintances, a single instance stood out in my mind – a warm, understated remark she made one sunny afternoon in the garden. As I sought her permission to take a break, she smiled graciously and said, "Oui, oui, ma bonne amie, je vous donne la permission de cœur et de gré.
