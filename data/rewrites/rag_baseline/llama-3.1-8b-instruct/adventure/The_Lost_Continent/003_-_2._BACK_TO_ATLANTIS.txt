The darkness receded slowly, like a tide pulling back from the shore. I lay on a narrow bed, the worn wood creaking beneath me. The air was heavy with the scent of damp earth and forgotten memories. My mind, a canvas of worn and faded hues, slowly regained its shape. The echoes of the past whispered through my thoughts, a melody of regret and longing. A memory surfaced – the sea, a vast expanse of grey water, stretching towards the horizon. I recalled the weight of responsibility, the burden of governing Yucatan. The thought of returning to Atlantis, to the Empress and her court, felt like a physical weight settling upon me.

Tatho stood beside me, his eyes filled with a mixture of concern and curiosity. He spoke in a low tone, his words measured and deliberate. "Deucalion, I've brought a ship, one that will take you back to Atlantis." His voice was a gentle breeze on a summer's day, but beneath it lay a current of unease. "It won't be fast, the voyage will be long, but it will be safe."

I struggled to sit up, the movement sending a wave of pain through my body. The room, once a blur, now took shape – a small, dimly lit chamber, the walls adorned with intricate carvings that told stories of a long-forgotten past. I focused on Tatho's face, his features a map of concern and doubt. "Why not my own navy?" I asked, the words tasting bitter on my lips.

Tatho's expression turned enigmatic. "You were Yucatan, Deucalion, or so you were. Twenty years have passed since you left. Your identity, like the ships, has changed." He paused, his eyes never leaving mine. "I'm offering you a chance to reclaim what's yours, but I warn you, the waters between us have grown treacherous. You'd do well to prepare yourself for the journey ahead."

I swung my legs over the edge of the bed, the motion sending a jolt of pain through my body. The room spun, and for a moment, I thought I would collapse. Tatho's hands grasped my arms, holding me steady. "Easy, Deucalion," he whispered. "You're not ready for this yet." His grip was firm, but his voice was laced with a thread of uncertainty.

I pulled away from him, my movements slow and deliberate.
