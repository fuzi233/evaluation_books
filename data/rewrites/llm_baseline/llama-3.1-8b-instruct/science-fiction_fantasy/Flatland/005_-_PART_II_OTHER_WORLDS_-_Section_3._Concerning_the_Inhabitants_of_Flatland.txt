Yet, within the hierarchy of Elyrian society, these triangles held a special place, their angular strength a testament to their unyielding resolve.

At the pinnacle of this hierarchy stood the priests, their forms so fluid, so circular, that they seemed to embody the essence of Elyria itself.

This principle governed the lives of the nobility, a steady progression upward, a testament to the power of lineage.
