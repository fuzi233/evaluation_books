In the mist-shrouded dawn, the trees of the estate stood like sentinels, their limbs etched against the grey sky like the skeletal fingers of the past. Gwendolen emerged from her bedroom, her footsteps echoing through the deserted corridors as she made her way to the breakfast table. The fire had long since died out, leaving behind a chill that seeped into her bones, but the warmth of the sunbeams streaming through the windows promised a day of unseasonal beauty. Her mother, Mrs. Davilow, sat at the head of the table, a faint smile on her face as she poured Gwendolen a cup of coffee.

As Gwendolen sat down, she noticed the letters scattered across her plate. Among them was one from Mr. Grandcourt, his handwriting bold and confident, speaking of the upcoming roving archery match at Cardell Chase. A shiver ran down her spine as she read the words, her heart quickening with anticipation. She foresaw herself gliding effortlessly through the woods, her arrow flying straight and true, with Grandcourt by her side. The thought sent a flutter through her chest, and she felt a surge of determination. This was the life she wanted – one of ease, of leisure, of being the center of attention.

Her mother's voice broke into her reverie, asking if she felt inclined to travel to the other side of the country. Gwendolen shook her head, her mind already fixed on the match. "Not exactly so far," she replied, trying to sound nonchalant despite the excitement building inside her. Her mother smiled, noticing the change in her daughter's demeanor, and suggested writing to Grandcourt to confirm their plans. Gwendolen hesitated, feeling a twinge of guilt, but then brushed it aside. "To-morrow will do," she said, her voice firm.

As they finished their breakfast, Gwendolen felt a sense of restlessness growing inside her. She couldn't shake the feeling that she was on the cusp of something momentous, something that would change the course of her life forever. The roving archery match loomed large in her mind, a symbol of freedom and adventure. And Grandcourt, with his steady gaze and confident smile, represented the security and stability she craved. Together, they would create a life of ease and luxury, one that would be hers to command.
