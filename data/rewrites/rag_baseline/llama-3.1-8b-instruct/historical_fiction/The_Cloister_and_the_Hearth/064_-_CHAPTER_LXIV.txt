In the opulent salon, where the scent of exotic spices wafted through the air, a woman sat poised, her slender fingers drumming a staccato rhythm on the intricately carved armchair. Her raven-black hair cascaded down her back like a waterfall, framing a face both pale and flushed, as if infused with the delicate hues of a just-picked apricot. The flickering candles danced across her features, casting eerie shadows that seemed to mock her very being.

As she waited, her maid, a timid creature with a look of abject fear, drew back the curtains, allowing a sliver of moonlight to illuminate the room. The lady's eyes snapped towards the entrance, her gaze fixed intently on the figure that emerged from the shadows.

He was a man of unremarkable appearance – short, with a rounded face, plain yet neat attire, and an air of quiet deference. Yet, it was his eyes that drew her attention – restless, like a wild animal caged and waiting to burst free. As he bowed low, his dark hair bobbing with each movement, the lady's agitation intensified, her breathing quickening like the sizzle of a skillet on the stove.

She rose, her movements fluid and deliberate, like a chef expertly preparing a dish. With a few swift gestures, she donned a small, black mask, its delicate lacework a stark contrast to the alabaster smoothness of her skin. Her complexion, once a map of emotions, now seemed chiseled from marble, as if carved by the skilled hands of a master artisan.

The man entered, his eyes never leaving hers, and stood before her, awaiting her pleasure. "You have been informed of the purpose of your visit?" she asked, her voice as sweet as a ripened fig.

"Yes, Signora," he replied, his deep voice like the gentle simmer of a stew on the back of the stove.

"Those who spoke to you agreed on the terms?" she pressed on, her eyes glinting like polished onyx.

"Yes, Signora. It is the full price; the greater vendetta, unless you choose to show mercy and accept the lesser."

The lady's expression was a mask of confusion, a mixture of concern and curiosity. "I do not understand," she said, her words as enigmatic as a recipe passed down through generations.
