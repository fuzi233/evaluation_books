The darkness was absolute, a heavy blanket that enveloped me like a shroud as I stepped out into the night, the spade clutched tightly in my hand. Adam's words echoed in my mind, guiding me towards the dry rock, its ancient cataract long since silenced. The air was crisp, with a hint of dampness that spoke of the distant river, its voice a gentle whisper in the darkness. I walked with purpose, the pale light that broke from the ground at each step illuminating the path ahead, a reassuring glow that banished the shadows.

As I traversed the heather and low rocks, the silence was almost palpable, broken only by the soft crunch of gravel beneath my feet. The bad burrow loomed ahead, its stillness a testament to the peacefulness of the night. A faint breeze rustled the leaves, and I caught a glimpse of a distant moon, its gentle light casting an ethereal glow over the landscape.

Time lost all meaning as I walked, the world narrowing to a single focus: the task at hand. The moon climbed higher in the sky, casting long shadows across the rocky terrain, and I pressed on, my footsteps steady and deliberate. The dry channels of the first branch of the river-bed came into view, and I judged I was not far from Mara's cottage.

As the moon dipped lower, its light cast an eerie glow over the landscape, and I saw a figure standing in the path ahead. It was shrouded in mist, a veil of silver that seemed to shimmer and dance in the moonlight. I felt a pang of unease, but Adam's words echoed in my mind, reminding me of the task at hand. I walked steadily forward, my eyes fixed on the figure, which slowly lifted its veil, revealing a face I knew all too well.

"Lila," I said, my voice barely above a whisper.

The princess's eyes flashed with anger, and she took a step closer, her hand extended. "Take my hand," she said, her voice husky with emotion. I hesitated, my heart pounding in my chest, and then I continued walking, my eyes fixed on some point beyond her. The princess's face contorted in a mixture of pain and anger, but I did not falter, my determination driving me forward.
