As the trio of travelers—Amyas, Cary, and Brimblecombe—settled beside the tranquil waters of the Meta, their weary bodies seemed to embody the cumulative effects of three arduous years of searching for the fabled Golden City. The notion of Manoa, with its fabled lake and resplendent palaces, had long become an all-consuming obsession, driving them forward through the unforgiving vastness of the New World. Yet, with each successive failure, their determination only intensified, fueled by the tantalizing whispers of its existence, which had repeatedly lured them down divergent paths, only to leave them bereft of satisfaction.

Their initial departure from the venerable tree of Guayra had been marked by an air of hope and camaraderie, as they endeavored to decipher the cryptic language of the region's indigenous peoples. However, as the months passed, their numbers dwindled, and the losses mounted. Drew, their trusted leader, now lay on the banks of the Rio Negro, his life extinguished by the poisoned arrows of an unforgiving enemy. Five companions had fallen alongside him, their bravery in the face of overwhelming odds a testament to the indomitable spirit that defined their expedition.

As the survivors gazed up at the tropical moon, their rugged visages seemed to tell the tale of countless hardships endured. Their beards had grown long and unkempt, their hair tied back in knots to shield their faces from the scorching sun. The remnants of their once-sturdy equipment now hung tattered and worn, a poignant reminder of the unyielding forces that had conspired against them. Despite these trials, however, their eyes sparkled with a fire that refused to be extinguished, a testament to the enduring legacy of English resolve and humor, which continued to buoy them through the darkest of times.

In this moment, as they sat amidst the primordial silence of the forest, their laughter carried on the breeze, a defiant rejection of the perils that had beset them. Though their quest remained elusive, their bond remained unbroken, a thread of fellowship that had weathered the unforgiving landscape of the New World, and would continue to propel them forward, undeterred, into the unknown.
