The village of Saint Bertrand de Comminges lay shrouded in the golden light of late afternoon, its stone buildings worn smooth by the relentless passage of time. As I walked through its narrow streets, the scent of woodsmoke and roasting chestnuts wafted from the market square, mingling with the distant tang of the Garonne River. It was a smell that stirred something deep within me, a longing for the rich, slow-cooked stews that simmered all day over the hearths of the villagers.

My stomach growled in anticipation as I made my way towards the local inn, where a sign creaked in the gentle breeze, bearing the image of a golden duck. Inside, the air was thick with the aroma of roasting meat and the murmur of conversation. I pushed open the door, and a warm light spilled out onto the cobblestones, beckoning me to enter. But as I stepped inside, a gruff voice cut short my hopes.

"No room. Full up," he growled, slamming the door shut behind me.

I stood on the threshold, feeling the chill of the evening air seep back in. The drizzle that had begun to fall earlier in the day had turned into a steady downpour, drumming a rhythmic beat on the pavement. I pulled my cloak tighter around me, trying to shake off the dampness that seemed to seep into my very bones.

As I stood there, I couldn't help but think of the dinner that lay ahead. The inn's kitchen would soon be serving up a hearty Bouillabaisse, its broth simmered with vegetables and fish from the Garonne, and infused with the subtle flavors of saffron and thyme. My mouth watered at the thought, but my stomach growled louder, reminding me that I had yet to find a place to stay.

I continued on, my feet carrying me towards the old monastery that loomed above the village. Its stone walls seemed to hold secrets and stories of their own, and I felt an inexplicable pull towards the ancient stones. As I climbed the winding path, the rain grew heavier, drumming against my cloak and threatening to soak me to the skin. But I pressed on, driven by a hunger that went beyond mere physical need.
