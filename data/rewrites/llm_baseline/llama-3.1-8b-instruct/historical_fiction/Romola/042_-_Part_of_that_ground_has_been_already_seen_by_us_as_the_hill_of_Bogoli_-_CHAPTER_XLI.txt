As the first light of dawn crept over the rolling hills, Fra Girolamo rose from his prayerful stance beside Romola, his eyes shining with a deep sense of conviction. "Rise, my daughter," he said softly, "the time has come for me to continue my journey to Florence." The words hung in the still morning air, a gentle reminder of the path ahead, as Romola slowly stood up, her movements deliberate and measured.

In the moments since her decision to abandon her resolve to leave her husband, Romola had felt a sense of surrender wash over her, a quiet acceptance of the unknown. Her will, bruised and battered, seemed to crave guidance in even the smallest of things. She lifted the edge of her cowl, revealing a glimpse of her face, and her eyes met those of Maso and the Dominican friar standing some distance away, their backs turned towards her. Yet, her gaze lingered on Fra Girolamo, seeking the reassurance of his guidance, before turning to follow his suggestion.

"I will go and call them," Fra Girolamo said, responding to her unspoken plea. "And I will recommend you, my daughter, to the care of Fra Salvestro, a brother of San Marco, in whom I place great trust. His wisdom and experience will be a guiding light for you, especially in the sacrament of confession." Romola's anxiety flared, her heart aching at the thought of losing the close bond she shared with Fra Girolamo.

"But, Father, I would rather have your guidance," she said, her voice barely above a whisper. Fra Girolamo's expression softened, his eyes filled with compassion. "My daughter, I am called to a different path, one that requires me to focus on my duties and avoid the distractions of the world. I can offer you my counsel, but I must entrust your spiritual well-being to Fra Salvestro's care."

Romola's uncertainty grew, her mind racing with doubts and fears. What if she lost the personal connection she shared with Fra Girolamo? Would she be able to find the strength to follow her new path without his guidance? But Fra Girolamo's words were laced with kindness, and she knew that he spoke from a place of love and concern. She took a deep breath, steeling herself for the change that lay ahead.
