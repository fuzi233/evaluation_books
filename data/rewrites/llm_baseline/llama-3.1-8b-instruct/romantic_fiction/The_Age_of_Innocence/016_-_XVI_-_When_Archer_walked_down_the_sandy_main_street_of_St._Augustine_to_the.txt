The sun's rays danced across her face, casting a soft, ethereal light upon her features, and Newland felt his tension ease as he took in the serene beauty before him. Her eyes sparkled with a youthful brightness, and her skin glowed with a subtle warmth that seemed to radiate from within.

Without a word, she reached out and took his hand, her touch igniting a sense of calm within him. "Newland – has anything happened?" she asked, her voice barely above a whisper, and he smiled, knowing that he could reveal his true intentions without fear of judgment.
