As the morning light seeped through the windows of the Hollow, Moore began to weave a delicate dance of defiance. His plan was a symphony of subtle resistance, carefully orchestrated to evade the watchful eye of Mrs. Horsfall. Each morning, he surprised her with a new affront, a gentle yet insistent rebuke that tested the limits of her patience. He refused her valet services, choosing to dress himself in the silence of the dawn. He rejected her coffee, opting instead for the warmth of the family's breakfast table. And finally, he barred her from his chamber, a small but significant triumph in the battle of wills.

The household erupted into a cacophony of protest, with every woman in the place weighing in against his audacity. Undeterred, Moore pushed forward, his resolve strengthened by the morning's victories. As he stepped outside, the crisp air invigorated him, and he felt a sense of liberation wash over him. He requested Mr. Yorke to send for a chaise from the Red House Inn, determined to return to the Hollow that very afternoon. The chaise arrived, and Moore, ever the diplomat, used the clinking of coins to convey his gratitude to the servants and Mrs. Horsfall. Her gruff demeanor softened, and she accepted his peace offering with a nod.

With the kitchen soothed and the household mollified, Moore made his way to the parlour, where Mrs. Yorke awaited him. Her gloomy countenance was a testament to the turmoil brewing within, her thoughts consumed by the bitter taste of ingratitude. Moore approached her, his pale features set in a determined smile, and bent over her chair. She was forced to look up, if only to bid him depart. There was still a beauty to his wasted form, a lingering hint of sweetness in his eyes.

"Good-bye!" he said, his voice barely above a whisper. The smile flickered, and for an instant, his emotions threatened to overwhelm him. He had no mastery over his feelings now, no shield to protect him from the vulnerability that lay exposed.

Mrs. Yorke's question hung in the air, a challenge to his resolve. "And what are you leaving us for?" she asked, her voice heavy with concern. "We will keep you, and do anything in the world for you, if you will only stay until you are stronger."

"Good-bye!" he repeated, his words a soft benediction.
