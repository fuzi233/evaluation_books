In the hazy light of autumn, as November's chill begins to seep into the bones, memories stir like embers from a long-dormant fire. Fragments of verse, learned in youth, linger in the mind like the faint scent of smoke. I recall the first poem I ever mastered, the one that earned me fourpence, a paltry sum that vanished into the morning mist like a whispered secret.

The lines, now lost to me, began with "I remember, I remember," a phrase that seemed to hold the weight of eternity. But my memory, a capricious companion, plays tricks on me. I recall tumbling into a dusty abyss as a child, yet the sensation of emerging, of escaping the suffocating darkness, remains shrouded in mystery. If I were to rely solely on memory, I would be convinced that I remain trapped within those dusty recesses, forever lost.

Years later, I found myself caught up in a tender scene, a moment of intimacy that now eludes me. All I can recall is the jarring interruption – a voice, sepulchral and detached, calling out "Emily, you're wanted" – a phrase that shattered the delicate tension like a dropped glass. The words exchanged between us, the whispered promises, the tender touch, are reduced to nothing more than a faint echo, a whispered rumor of what might have been.

Life, viewed through the lens of memory, is a tapestry of broken threads and frayed edges. A crumbling ruin, overgrown with ivy and lichen, where once stood a grand edifice. The passage of time reduces even the most painful moments to a nostalgic glow, a warm ember that casts no shadows. Our youthful days, filled with the promise of adventure and the ache of heartache, now seem idyllic, a golden haze that veils the rough stone beneath.

We tend to remember the laughter, the joy, and the warmth, while the difficulties, the disappointments, and the heartbreak fade into the background. It is as if the sunshine of nostalgia casts no shadows, and the road we've traversed is smooth, unblemished, and free of sharp stones. We recall the roses that bloomed by the wayside, the gentle breeze that rustled their petals, and the tender moments we shared, while the thorns that pierced us are reduced to mere whispers in the wind.
