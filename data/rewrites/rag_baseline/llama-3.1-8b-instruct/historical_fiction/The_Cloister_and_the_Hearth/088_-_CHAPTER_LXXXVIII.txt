Supper-time arrived, casting a warm glow across Eli's humble abode. His family gathered around the wooden board, the tantalizing aromas of roasting meats wafting through the air, making their stomachs growl in anticipation. However, Margaret was nowhere to be seen, and Catherine's brow furrowed in concern. Eli's response surprised her – he announced he would wait for Margaret's arrival, much to the dismay of the household's patriarch, who had anticipated an early meal.

"Why, I told her you wouldn't wait for the duke," the older man grumbled, his eyes clouding with frustration.

"She is not the duke," Eli replied, his voice firm, "but a poor, kind-hearted girl who has waited years for my return. We can still eat without further delay, and she will join us soon enough." He gestured for Catherine to take her seat, the scent of sizzling meat proving too enticing to resist.

As they sat down, a sudden commotion erupted outside, and a Dominican friar burst into the room, his face a deep shade of crimson with rage. He strode to the center of the table, his eyes blazing with fury, and hovered over Cornelis and Sybrandt, his hands suspended above their heads like a hawk ready to strike. The air was heavy with tension as he unleashed a torrent of curses, each word dripping with malice and venom.

"Cursed be the lips that spoke the lie that Margaret was dead!" he thundered, his voice echoing through the small cottage. "May they rot before the grave, and kiss white-hot iron in hell thereafter! Doubly cursed be the hands that changed those letters, and may they be struck off by the hangman's knife, forever bound to hellfire!" His words were a maelstrom of hate, each phrase a blow aimed at the very souls of Eli and his companions.

The friar then produced a letter from his breast pocket and flung it onto the table, his eyes flashing with accusation. "Read this, you hard-hearted man, and see the monsters you have created in the world!" The memory of past wrongs seemed to sear itself into the faces of the frozen figures, their eyes wide with terror.
