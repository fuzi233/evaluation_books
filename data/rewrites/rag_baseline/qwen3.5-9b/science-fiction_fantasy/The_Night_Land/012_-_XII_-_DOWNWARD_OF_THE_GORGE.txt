The descent below the Gorge was measured in hours, a slow erosion of energy. By the time I reached the lower reaches, my legs had ceased to be instruments of motion and became merely weight-bearing structures, prone to stumbling. My grip on the path faltered; the terrain offered no purchase, only a relentless demand for balance that I could no longer sustain. Sleep threatened to claim me mid-stride, a dangerous oblivion that would drop me—and the Maid—with me.

Consciousness returned in fragments, sharp and disorienting. The Gorge, previously a blur of nightmare, suddenly resolved into distinct, jagged lines. I realized with a jolt that I had traversed this distance in a trance, a somnambulist walking through a landscape that now seemed alien and newly constructed. Memory reasserted itself: the great ruddy fire-hole nearby, the specific caves I had passed during our ascent. A surge of relief washed over me. This was the sanctuary I had envisioned—a place of refuge where we could finally rest.

I pressed forward, and there they were. Seven small cavities carved into the left flank of the cliff, clustered just beyond the fire-hole. One stood out, perfectly formed, offering a promise of shelter and safety. We had escaped the immediate threat of the Evil Forces, though the danger was not entirely absent. A monster could still descend from the depths above, finding us vulnerable in our sleep. Ideally, we should have sought higher ground, closer to the Diskos, where the beasts would hesitate before attacking. But fatigue held me fast; I was adrift, unable to push further.

I knelt beside the Maid, kissing her forehead as she leaned against me. She responded instinctively, a reflexive caress in her sleep, unaware of the gravity of the situation. I loved her with a ferocity that bordered on desperation. Shaking her gently, I whispered the news of our arrival at the haven. Her eyes fluttered open, clouded with grogginess. She blamed herself for falling asleep during our labor, her guilt evident in her posture. I kissed her again, watching the residual sleep linger in her lashes, and she returned the gesture with a tenderness that stripped away my defenses. In a voice barely audible, she confessed her eternal devotion.

I guided her to her feet, then ascended alone to scout the entrance. As she paced nervously behind me, ensuring she regained full alertness, I entered the cave. It was dry, warm, and bathed in the reflected glow of the distant fire-hole. It was a fortress. Calling to her, I helped her climb the final stretch, and together we settled inside, feeling a profound sense of security.

Before succumbing to sleep, I secured the perimeter. Releasing the straps of my pack, I descended into the dark throat of the Gorge. I selected a massive boulder, heavy enough to serve as a barrier, and hoisted it back up. With practiced precision, I balanced it on the cave's lip, creating a precarious fulcrum. Any creature attempting to scale the cliffs would disturb the equilibrium, sending the stone tumbling down—a loud alarm that would wake us instantly.

Inside, the Maid had already spread her cloak across the floor. The cave's heat rendered it unnecessary for warmth, but she had fashioned a bed for us both. It was intimate, natural, a testament to her desire for closeness, yet she maintained a delicate modesty, aware of the unspoken boundaries between protector and protected. She indicated where I should lie, then knelt to kiss me, her expression sober and deeply loving. Lying beside me on the cloak, we drifted into sleep almost simultaneously.

Twelve hours later, the silence broke with the fizzing of water from the fire-hole. I opened my eyes to find the Maid preparing our meal. She laughed softly, a sound of pure delight at having me awake. She approached, kissing me with a brightness that illuminated the cave. I sensed she had been watching me sleep, her vigilance maternal and protective. I reached for her, pulling her into my embrace. She yielded willingly, snuggling against the cold steel of my armor. The hardness of the metal was a stark contrast to her softness, yet she seemed to find comfort in the juxtaposition, perhaps seeking the solidity of protection amidst the vulnerability of intimacy.

She soon withdrew, respecting my role as the sentinel. I checked the boulder; it remained undisturbed. No monsters prowled the darkness outside. She insisted I stay lying down, refusing to let me rise. Taking my head onto her lap, she kissed me delicately. She handed me a tablet, tasting it herself before offering it to me, sharing the sustenance. We ate and drank, our hearts light, finding a childish joy in the simplicity of survival.

Afterward, I attempted to stand, only to cry out in pain. My body was stiff, bruised from the previous night's fight with the Four-Armed Man. The injuries were severe, a reminder of the violence that had preceded our respite. The Maid anticipated my distress, producing a pot of ointment. With skilled, gentle hands, she removed my armor and treated my wounds. Her touch was tender, transforming my agony into a sensation of being cared for by a mother. For a long hour, she worked until my body was refreshed.

As she finished, she offered me her hands to help me rise. My limbs responded, the stiffness melting away, restoring my confidence as the Protector. I searched for my spare vest, but she intercepted me. Standing before me, she gazed at my muscular arms with an expression of awe and admiration. I explained the discipline of the Pyramid's upbringing, the foundation laid by my mother, but her reaction transcended logic. She dropped the vest and stepped into my reach.

I embraced her, overwhelmed by a mixture of gladness and humility. She rested quietly against my chest, kissing the muscle beneath my tunic. Soon, she pulled away to assist me with dressing. Once clad, she stood back, her demeanor shifting to a mix of shyness and playful defiance. She raised her hands to my shoulders, lowering her eyelids to peek at me, stealing glances that spoke volumes. Suddenly, she dropped to her knees and wept.

I knelt beside her instantly. I asked no questions; I understood the complex tapestry of her emotions—the worship, the love, the need for connection, and the trembling realization of my significance to her. To her, I was a hero, unique and vital, whereas to the world I was merely ordinary. Pride and unworthiness surged within me simultaneously. I accepted her reverence without pretense, knowing that to deny it would be to betray her trust.

Time passed, and she composed herself, wiping her eyes and nesting closer to me. I slipped her shoes off her bare feet, chiding myself for the delay, then replaced them. Gathering our gear, we prepared to leave the cave. The climb back up the steep cliff face awaited, demanding every ounce of our renewed strength.
