The scent of ripe melons wafted through the crisp Martian air as Carthoris, Xodar, Tars Tarkas, and I stood before the majestic vessel, its sleek hull glistening in the golden light of the twin suns. We watched in awe as three sister ships crested the nearby hills, their descent a testament to the ingenuity of the Heliumian engineers. As they landed softly on the ground, a swarm of one-man air scouts burst forth from the upper decks, their whirring propellers a symphony of sound.

Before we could react, a dozen armed sailors surrounded us, their eyes scanning our group with a mixture of curiosity and caution. An officer stepped forward, his gaze locking onto Carthoris, and his expression transformed from stern to ecstatic. He rushed forward, grasping the young prince's shoulder, and called out his name.

"Carthoris, my Prince!" he exclaimed, his voice carrying across the Martian plain. "Kaor! Kaor! Hor Vastus greets the son of Dejah Thoris, Princess of Helium, and her noble husband, John Carter. Where have you been, O my Prince? The people of Helium have mourned your departure, and the land has suffered greatly in your absence."

Carthoris's smile lit up the landscape as he replied, "Grieve not, dear Hor Vastus, for I return not alone. I bring with me one who has captured the hearts of Barsoom – the greatest warrior and savior of our great nation, John Carter, Prince of Helium!"

As Hor Vastus's eyes locked onto me, he stumbled backward, taken aback by my unexpected return. "John Carter!" he breathed, his face etched with a mix of wonder and trepidation. His gaze clouded over, and he began to speak, but the words caught in his throat. The loyal warrior knew better than to pry into the secrets of the past, the mysteries of the Iss, the River of Mystery, and the Lost Sea of Korus.

Instead, Hor Vastus focused on the present, his expression clearing as he addressed Carthoris. "Ah, my Prince, your return is a blessing indeed. Let Hor Vastus's sword be the first to welcome you back, and may it serve as a symbol of our unwavering loyalty." With a flourish, he unbuckled his scabbard and cast his sword onto the ground before me.
