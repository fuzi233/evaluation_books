Mordecai's eyelids drooped, and he began to speak in a soft, measured tone, as if lost in thought rather than seeking to convey a message.

"In the mystical teachings of the Kabbalah, souls are reborn, each time entering new bodies until they are refined and perfected. A soul freed from its worn vessel may join its companion, that together they may complete their earthly endeavors and depart, making way for the birth of new souls and the dawn of the Messianic era. It is the lingering imperfections of souls already bound to the earth that hinder the emergence of new souls and the unfolding of destiny. Thus, the mind, in its wisdom, has given shape to the unknown, speaking truth through parables. When my wandering soul is finally set free from this frail body, it will reunite with yours, and our joint endeavors will be brought to completion."

The pause that followed seemed a summons, one that Deronda's compassion could not ignore. Though his words sought to be sincere, for Mordecai, they were infused with an unspoken depth. "Everything I can do in good conscience to ensure your legacy endures," Deronda vowed.

"I am aware," Mordecai replied, his voice steady and reassuring, dispelling the need for further assurances. "You see all; you stand beside me on the mountain of prophecy, beholding the paths of fulfillment that others refuse to acknowledge."
