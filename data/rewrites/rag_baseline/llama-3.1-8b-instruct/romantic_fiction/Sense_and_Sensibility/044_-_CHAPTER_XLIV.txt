Elinor stood frozen, her gaze locked on the stranger who had burst into the quiet morning hours, his presence as jarring as the clanging of a kitchen bell summoning the family to gather for Sunday lunch. The scent of freshly baked bread wafted from the kitchen, mingling with the faint tang of woodsmoke carried on the crisp air. It was a scent that typically filled her with warmth and comfort, but now it only served to heighten her unease.

As he advanced, his words tumbled forth in a rush, a litany of entreaty and supplication that sent a shiver down her spine. "Miss Dashwood, for half an hour, just ten minutes, I beg of you, stay." His voice, though urgent, lacked the warmth of genuine appeal, and Elinor's instincts recoiled in response.

"No, sir," she replied firmly, her hand reaching for the door handle. "I shall not stay. The servants, I'm sure, forgot to inform you that Mr. Palmer is not in the house." Her tone was polite, but firm, a clear signal that she would not be swayed.

The stranger's response was immediate and vehement, his words tumbling forth like a sudden storm. "Had they told me that Mr. Palmer and all his relations were at the devil, it would not have turned me from the door. My business is with you, and only you."

Elinor's eyes narrowed, her mind racing to comprehend the stranger's motives. "With me?" she repeated, her voice incredulous. "Well, sir, be quick, and if you can, less violent." She paused, weighing her options, the prospect of Colonel Brandon's arrival and finding her in this awkward situation flashing through her mind.

After a moment's deliberation, she decided that prudence dictated a swift resolution, and that acquiescing would best serve her interests. With a silent step, she moved towards the table, taking a seat. The stranger followed suit, his movements economical and deliberate.
