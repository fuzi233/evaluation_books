The scent of jasmine wafted through the windows of the old mansion, mingling with the faint tang of dust and forgotten memories. Harry Warrington wandered the halls, his footsteps echoing off the polished woodwork. He stopped before a portrait of his ancestors, the faces stern and unyielding. His fingers brushed against the velvet drapes, releasing a whispered sigh. He recalled the night he'd gathered wildflowers for Maria, their innocence and beauty a fleeting respite from the monotony of life. The memory still lingered, a ache within him like a bruise that wouldn't heal.

"Why did you refuse to go with our aunt, Maria?" he asked, his voice barely above a whisper.

Maria's gaze lifted from the embroidery on her tambour frame, her eyes meeting his with a hint of amusement. "I bid you stay, Harry. You took it as a command."

"You said—when we walked on the veranda two nights since—that I would forget you if I left," Harry's voice trembled, the words tumbling forth like a confession. He pulled out a wilted flower from his waistcoat pocket, its petals crumpled and worn. "This was the token you gave me. You said, 'If your aunt asks you to go away, you will go, and if you go, you will forget me.'"

"All men forget," Maria replied, her voice laced with resignation.

"Not in our family, Maria," Harry countered, his words spilling out in a fervent rush. "We're not like those people in the city, where life is a series of transactions and obligations. We Virginians, we have a different code. We value loyalty, duty, and love."

Maria's gaze drifted back to her needlework, her fingers moving deftly as she worked. "I suppose that's true, Harry. But sometimes, I wonder if our ideals aren't just a luxury, a indulgence we can't afford in the real world."

Harry's face contorted in frustration, his words tumbling forth in a jumbled mess. "I want to prove myself, Maria. I want to be worthy of you. I want to be more than just a foolish boy who's infatuated with a beautiful face. I want to be a man who can make a difference, who can change the world."
