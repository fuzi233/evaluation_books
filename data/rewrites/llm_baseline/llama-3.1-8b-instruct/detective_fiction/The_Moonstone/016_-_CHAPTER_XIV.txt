As I strolled alongside Sergeant Cuff through the shrubbery, the scent of blooming roses and freshly cut grass wafted through the air, transporting me to a moment of tranquility amidst the turmoil that had beset our household. The shrubbery path, a familiar route that Mr. Franklin often took, wound its way gently through the gardens, a testament to the estate's carefully tended beauty. It was here that I had often sought him out when he couldn't be found elsewhere, and I couldn't help but feel a pang of nostalgia for the days when life was simpler.

Sergeant Cuff's taciturn nature was beginning to get the better of me. The more he refused to share his thoughts, the more I found myself probing him with questions. As we walked, I tried a different tack. "Considering the situation, I'd be at my wit's end if I were in your shoes," I said, attempting to gauge his reaction.

The Sergeant's expression remained inscrutable. "If you were in my position, you'd have formed a conclusion, Mr. Betteredge. Your doubts would be settled, one way or the other." He paused, his eyes scanning the surrounding gardens. "I've brought you out here to ask for some information, not to indulge in speculation. Doors and listeners have a knack of getting together, and in my line of work, I prefer the open air."

I couldn't help but wonder what secrets lay hidden behind his words. I gave in, deciding to wait and see what he had in store for me.

"We won't pry into Miss Halcombe's motivations," he continued. "Let's focus on the mystery of the smear on the door. It's a puzzle, and I believe it holds the key to solving the larger enigma. I've decided to speak with the servants, to understand their thoughts and actions, rather than searching their rooms. Before I do, I need to ask you a few questions. As someone who's been observant of the household, did you notice anything unusual among the staff after the diamond went missing? Any signs of discord among them? Anyone acting strangely or out of character?"

My mind flashed back to Rosanna Spearman's sudden illness at yesterday's dinner, but I hadn't a chance to respond before Sergeant Cuff's gaze darted towards the shrubbery, and he muttered under his breath, "Hullo!"

"What's wrong?" I asked, concerned.
