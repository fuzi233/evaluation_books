As I stood at the edge of the Ark, the salty sea air caressed my face, carrying with it the whispers of the past. The vessel, once a marvel of the Ancients' craftsmanship, now drifted listlessly on the waves, its wooden hull strong and unyielding against the relentless tides. I wrapped my arm around Nais, feeling the warmth of her body as we clung to each other in the darkness. Our eyes had long since adjusted to the faint glow of the dim lanterns, but the shadows still danced across the walls, like specters of a forgotten world.

The memory of Atlantis lingered, a bittersweet reminder of what had been lost. The city's downfall had left behind a trail of debris, a testament to the fury of the sea. Trees, splintered planks, and the occasional, bloated corpse floated on the surface, their presence a poignant reminder of the devastation that had ravaged the land. Our Ark, once a proud vessel, now rode the swells like a ghost ship, its cargo hold filled with the remnants of a bygone era.

I closed my eyes, letting the rhythm of the waves lull me into a state of semi-consciousness. The sound of Nais' gentle breathing was a soothing melody, one that seemed to match the cadence of the sea itself. As I drifted off, I felt a sense of peace settle over me, a sense of acceptance that this was our new reality – a life adrift on the bosom of the deep.

Hours passed, or perhaps it was days – time had lost all meaning in this desolate expanse. I awoke to find Nais watching me, her eyes shining with a quiet compassion. We shared a moment of understanding, a recognition that we were in this together. Our hunger and thirst were momentarily forgotten as we basked in the warmth of each other's company.

With a newfound sense of purpose, we ventured back into the Ark's belly, seeking sustenance and comfort in the provisions that had been left behind. The air was stale, but the stores were plentiful, and we ate and drank with a quiet gratitude. As we sat amidst the dimly lit cavern, surrounded by the relics of a forgotten world, I couldn't help but wonder at the secrets that lay hidden within the Ark's ancient timbers.
