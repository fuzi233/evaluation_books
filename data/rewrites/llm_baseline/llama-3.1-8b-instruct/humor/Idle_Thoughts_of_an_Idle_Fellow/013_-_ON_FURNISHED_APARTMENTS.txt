A stout woman, her face creased with years of hard work, descended the kitchen stairs, her apron tied loosely around her waist. She called out to someone below, her voice muffled, as she continued down the stairs. Her footsteps echoed through the hallway, punctuated by the occasional clink of a pot or pan being put away.

As she reached the bottom of the stairs, she turned to a young man standing in the doorway, his eyes scanning the space. "Good morning, sir," she said with a forced smile, her eyes squinting slightly from the bright light. "Would you care to see the rooms?" 

He hesitated, glancing at his watch, before nodding and following her up the stairs. At the first landing, they encountered a pail and a broom, which the woman began to lament about the unreliability of her maid. As they ascended further, she paused at the door to the rooms, smoothing her apron before speaking. "I'm afraid they're a bit of a mess, sir.
