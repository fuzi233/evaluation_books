Gerard's legs wobbled beneath him as he tumbled off the tree branch, his stomach churning with the stench of the dying bear. Denys swiftly caught hold of him, his grip firm but gentle. Though Gerard's friend's timely intervention likely saved him from a broken neck or limb, it was the bear's soft fur that cushioned his fall. As Denys tore him off the bear's carcass, Gerard's head spun, his vision blurring.

"Courage, little one," Denys called out, his voice steady and reassuring. "The devil is vanquished."

Gerard stumbled away from the bear, his senses reeling, and let out a cry of dismay. "Is it quite dead?" he asked, his voice shaking.

Denys chuckled, his eyes crinkling at the corners. "Behold, I'll show you." He playfully tugged on the bear's ear and pried open its jaws, inserting Gerard's head into the gaping maw. Gerard's stomach heaved, and he vomited onto the forest floor.

Denys laughed good-naturedly. "What's wrong now, my friend? Why did you take such a tumble?"

Gerard wiped his mouth with the back of his hand, his face pale. "I think it was the smell of the blood," he said, his voice barely above a whisper. "I'm not usually one to faint, but... Ugh!"

Denys nodded sympathetically. "I think you're right. Blood can be overwhelming." He reached out and gently brushed a strand of hair from Gerard's forehead.

Gerard's eyes widened as he took in Denys's gesture. "Why do you always tease me so, Denys?" he asked, his voice tinged with a mix of annoyance and affection.

Denys's expression softened, and he wrapped his arms around Gerard's waist. "Because I care for you, little one," he said, his voice low and gentle. "And because you're always so brave, even when things get scary."

Gerard's face lit up with a smile, and he leaned into Denys's embrace. "We should thank God for sparing our lives," he said, his voice filled with gratitude.
