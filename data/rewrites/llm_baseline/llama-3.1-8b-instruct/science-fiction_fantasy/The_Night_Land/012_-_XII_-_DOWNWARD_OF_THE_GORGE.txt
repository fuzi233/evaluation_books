As I descended the Gorge, the Maid slumbering in my arms, I found myself growing increasingly weary. My footing faltered, and I stumbled more frequently, the darkness seeming to close in around us like a shroud. I dared not risk a fall, lest I harm the one I protected, and so I pressed on, my senses dulled by exhaustion.

The Gorge seemed to stretch on forever, a seemingly endless tunnel of blackness punctuated only by the faint glow of a distant fire-hole. I strained my eyes, hoping to catch sight of a landmark or a feature that would guide me through the treacherous terrain. As I walked, I felt my consciousness slipping away, my thoughts growing disjointed and fragmented.

But I fought to stay alert, drawing on every last ounce of willpower to keep my feet moving. And then, suddenly, I was awake, my senses snapping back into focus as I took in my surroundings. The Gorge seemed fresh and new, the walls rising up around me like sentinels. I remembered the path we had taken earlier, and my heart leapt with hope as I spotted the familiar outline of the caves we had seen on our ascent.

One in particular caught my eye – a small, cozy opening in the face of the cliff, partially hidden behind a screen of rock. It seemed like a haven, a place of safety from the dangers that lurked in the depths of the Gorge. I knew that we still had far to go before we reached the top, but I dared not press on, not yet. We needed rest, and this cave offered us a refuge from the perils that surrounded us.

I gently roused the Maid, kissing her softly as I set her to her feet. She smiled up at me, still drowsy, and I couldn't help but smile back. I kissed her again, feeling a pang of love for this fragile, vulnerable creature who had become my everything. As I held her close, I knew that I would do anything to keep her safe.

I helped her steady herself, watching as she looked around, taking in our surroundings. Her eyes were still heavy with sleep, but she managed a faint smile, and I knew she was starting to come awake. I encouraged her to move around, to shake off the lingering fatigue, and she complied, her movements slow and uncertain at first, but growing more confident with each passing moment.
