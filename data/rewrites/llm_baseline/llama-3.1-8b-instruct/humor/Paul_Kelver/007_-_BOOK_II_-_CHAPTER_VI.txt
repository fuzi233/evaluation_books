The kitchen was abuzz with the scent of roasting meats and simmering sauces, the aroma wafting up from the pots and pans that lined the stovetop. My mother, a look of distress etched on her face, surveyed the spread before her. "There's nothing missing, I've accounted for every ingredient," she said to my father, who stood nearby, decanter in hand.

"What could have frightened her off?" he wondered aloud, his brow furrowed in concern. My mother shook her head, her eyes welling up with tears. "It was the thought of serving a proper meal, something beyond our usual simple fare. She was nervous about it last night, I remember."

The clock on the wall ticked away, counting down the minutes until their guests would arrive. My father set the bottle down, his mind racing with solutions. "We can't very well serve dinner without her," he said, his voice laced with frustration.

My mother sank into a chair, her shoulders slumping in defeat. It had been a long and trying week, with endless preparations for this special dinner. The house had been scrubbed and polished, the silverware shined, and the linens ironed to perfection. The kitchen range had cooperated, finally, after days of temperamental behavior, and the cookbooks had yielded up their secrets. But now, just as they were about to reap the rewards of their labor, disaster struck.

"We must think of something," my father urged, but my mother just shook her head. "There's no one else to turn to," she said, her voice barely above a whisper. The clock ticked on, each passing moment bringing them closer to the hour when their guests would arrive, empty-handed and hungry.

Just then, a light tap at the front door broke the silence. My father's eyes lit up with hope. "Perhaps it's Barbara," he exclaimed, hastening to answer it. But as he opened the door, a vision of loveliness stood on the threshold, her dark hair shining in the sunlight.

"It's Barbara," she announced, as if anyone could doubt it. She swept into the room, a whirlwind of energy and laughter, and the atmosphere in the kitchen changed instantly. My mother's tears dried, and a plan began to form.
