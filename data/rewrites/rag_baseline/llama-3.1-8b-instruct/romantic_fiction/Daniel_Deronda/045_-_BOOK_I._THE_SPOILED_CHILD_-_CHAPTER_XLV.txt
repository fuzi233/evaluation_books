Though her thoughts were elsewhere, her trained smile remained firmly in place, a mask hiding the turmoil brewing within. Her husband, Sir Hugo, had warned her that their nephew, Deronda, would be attending the musical party at Lady Mallinger's, and Gwendolen's mind was fixated on the prospect of encountering him again. She had been pondering the words he had spoken about Mirah, the young woman who had captured his attention, and the phrase that echoed in her mind like a bitter refrain. As she settled into her seat, Gwendolen couldn't help but feel a sense of disquiet, her submission to her husband's will a heavy burden, weighed down by the strength of her own selfish desires.

The drawing-rooms, adorned with white, gold, and pale crimson, pulsed with the gentle hum of conversation and the soft strains of instrumental music. Gwendolen's gaze swept the room, her long sight taking in the familiar faces, before coming to rest on Mirah, standing resplendent in her beauty and talent. A flicker of resentment danced within Gwendolen, a reminder of the ambitions she had once harbored, only to be eclipsed by her current role as a society matron. As she exchanged a bow with Klesmer, the memory of that earlier aspiration flashed across her mind, a bittersweet pang that threatened to undermine her carefully constructed facade.
