Lady Verinder reclined on her plush velvet sofa, the afternoon sunlight streaming through the window casting a warm glow on her pale face. The Will was signed, and with it, a sense of finality settled over the room. My aunt's eyes met mine, a fleeting glance of understanding passing between us before Mr. Bruff folded up the document and stood expectantly, awaiting his dismissal. I chose that moment to retrieve my bag from beside me, the worn leather creaking softly as I pulled out a delicate porcelain box adorned with a sprig of rosemary.

"A special gift for you, Aunt," I said, offering her the box with a gentle smile. Inside, a fragrant bundle of fresh herbs awaited her acceptance – a pinch of lavender, a sprig of thyme, and a few dried rose petals, carefully selected from the garden. The scent wafted up, transporting her back to memories of summers spent strolling through the estate's gardens, the scent of blooming flowers and fresh earth filling the air.

My aunt's gaze wandered to the box, her fingers hesitating before she took it, inhaling deeply as she lifted the lid. A hint of recognition danced across her face, and for an instant, I dared to hope. Perhaps, just perhaps, this simple gesture would unlock a door in her mind, allowing her to recall the long-forgotten recipes passed down through generations of our family.

But alas, it was not to be. Her thoughts were elsewhere, her eyes clouding over as she mentioned the doctor's name. I sensed the familiar resistance rising within her, a barrier erected against the influences she deemed 'unwholesome.' I recalled the countless times I'd encountered this obstacle, the medical profession's zealotry standing between me and my goal of nourishing her soul.

"The doctor advises you to rest, dear Aunt," I said gently, choosing my words with care. "Perhaps, in these quiet moments, you might find solace in a soothing tea, infused with the very herbs I've brought for you?" I produced a small pouch from my bag, filled with dried chamomile and lemon balm, expertly blended to calm the senses. The fragrance mingled with the rosemary, creating a heady bouquet that seemed to transport us both to a place of serenity.
