"Where has he been?" Jorian's voice was barely above a whisper, but Margaret remained oblivious to his presence. He approached the empty chest, his mind racing with possibilities. The sight of Gerard's pale face and Margaret's anguish stirred something within him. This was no ordinary situation – it was a desperate bid to save a life.

"This is a sorry state of affairs," Jorian muttered to himself. "All for a few precious furs. Better he had stayed with us." He gently took down a small, delicate mirror from the chest and held it to Gerard's mouth and nostrils. The mirror revealed nothing, but Jorian's trained eyes detected a hint of life beneath the surface. He breathed a silent prayer as he placed the mirror back, his heart pounding in his chest.

Margaret's eyes snapped into focus, her face lighting up with a mix of relief and gratitude. She flung her arms around Jorian's neck, her touch warm and urgent. "Oh, thank the heavens you're here!" she exclaimed, her voice trembling with emotion. Jorian smiled, his rugged demeanor softening as he wrapped his arms around her.

"We need to get him warm," he said, his voice gentle. He carefully lifted Gerard onto the bedclothes, his movements deliberate and calculated. From his pouch, he produced a flask filled with Schiedamze, a potent liquor brewed in the nearby town of Schiedam. With a swift motion, he splashed the liquid onto Gerard's face, the pungent aroma filling the air. Gerard's eyelids fluttered open, and a faint sigh escaped his lips. Margaret's eyes welled up with tears as she hesitated, unsure of how to react.
