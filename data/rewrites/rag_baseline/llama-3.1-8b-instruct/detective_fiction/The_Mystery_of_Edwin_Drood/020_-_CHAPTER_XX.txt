Silence crept over the abandoned courtyard, a stillness broken only by the faint hum of the city. Rosa's eyes fluttered open, her mind racing with the echoes of a recent conversation. Every word, every glance, every inflection seemed to reverberate within her. She lay motionless, weighing the fragments of their discussion, searching for a thread to guide her through the labyrinth of her thoughts.

Fear wrestled with reason as she replayed the events by the sundial in the garden. Jasper's words hung in the air, a jarring melody that refused to resolve into harmony. His persistence in treating Edwin's disappearance as murder had left her unsettled. Why wouldn't he encourage the idea of a voluntary departure, if that's what he truly believed? His declaration that he might have taken Edwin from her side, had their bond been weaker, seemed a cruel jest. And the sacrifices he spoke of making for her sake – his fidelity to Edwin after death, his devotion to the truth – did those ring true?

Rosa's mind was a maelstrom of doubts and uncertainties. She had been Helena's rock, her confidante, her steadfast supporter. Yet, she had kept her distance from Jasper since the disappearance, never once visiting him, never once speaking of his avowal to Mr. Crisparkle. To her, Jasper was Helena's brother, nothing more. The memory of his words, however, lingered, refusing to be silenced. She felt a shiver run down her spine as she recalled the weight of his gaze, the intensity of his voice.

A faint glimmer of suspicion flickered within her, a spark that refused to be extinguished. Had Jasper's actions been guided by a desire to gain her trust, to manipulate her into believing in his innocence? The thought sent a pang of guilt through her, as if she were complicit in some dark design. Rosa's breath caught in her throat as she realized the depths of her own uncertainty. What did she truly know of Jasper's nature? What did she truly believe? The silence in the courtyard seemed to grow thicker, a palpable presence that wrapped itself around her, suffocating her.
