As the last rays of sunlight surrendered to the horizon, a vision materialized on the western skyline – a sliver of land, its contours etched against the fiery backdrop of the setting sun. It was a sight to stir the souls of those who had traversed the unforgiving vastness of the ocean, weary and worn by the trials of their journey. The promise of terra firma beckoned, a haven of greenery and freshness, a place where the parched earth and the salty spray of the sea might converge.

"Land ho!" Drew, the seasoned navigator, declared, his voice carrying across the waves as he scrutinized the horizon. "Unless my reckonings have led me astray once more, that should be Barbados."

Frank's eyes, sunken and red-rimmed from the hardships they had endured, shone with a mixture of excitement and trepidation as he strained to discern the outline of the island. "Barbados?" he repeated, his voice barely above a whisper. "I've never heard of it."

Drew's weathered face creased into a knowing smile. "Ah, sir, I've been there before, with Captain Drake and later with Captain Barlow. There's a sheltered harbor to the south and west, perfect for our needs."

Yeo, ever the devout, chimed in, "A haven of peace, a garden of the Lord, hidden away from the world, awaiting those who seek refuge." He recalled the tales of Captain Drake's ambitions to establish a foothold on this very island. "If only he had followed his heart, instead of venturing north to Newfoundland."

Amyas, lost in thought, nodded in agreement. "Indeed, 'twould have been a different fate for him, had he chosen this path."

The crew's voices rose in assent, except for Frank, whose silence was a testament to his unyielding longing for a destination yet unknown. Cary's gentle prodding roused him from his reverie. "Come, Frank, we need your voice in this decision."

"I fear I'm but a prisoner of my own desires," Frank confessed, his words barely audible over the lapping of the waves. "But in this instance, I concur with our captain's counsel. A brief respite here, to tend to our wounded, would be a wise decision."
