The stormy air clung to me like a damp shroud as I waited anxiously by the window, my eyes fixed on the garden beyond. The news of Rosanna's disappearance had spread quickly among the outdoor staff, and I could see them huddled together, whispering among themselves. One of them, a small, wiry boy named Duffy, had caught my attention. He claimed to have seen Rosanna only a short while ago, running towards the sea-shore in a frantic dash. The image of her fleeing form lingered in my mind as I watched Duffy scurry off towards the Shivering Sand, followed closely by the determined Sergeant Cuff.

My mind began to reel with the implications of what I had just heard. I felt a growing restlessness, a sense of unease that seemed to grip me by the throat. I tried to shake it off, but my thoughts were a jumble of confusion and fear. I found myself pacing back and forth across the room, my feet carrying me on a wild goose chase through the house. I couldn't recall what I was doing, or why, but I knew I was trying to distract myself from the turmoil brewing inside.

It wasn't until Duffy burst back into the house, breathless and excited, that I was brought back to reality. He held out a piece of paper, torn from the Sergeant's pocket-book, with a hasty message scrawled on it: "Send me one of Rosanna Spearman's boots, and be quick about it." I dispatched one of the women to fetch the boot from Rosanna's room, and then I followed Duffy back outside, determined to see what new developments were afoot.

As I emerged into the stormy air, the wind and rain hit me like a physical blow. The clouds hung low and grey, and the sea thundered against the sand-bank at the mouth of the bay. I spotted Duffy cowering under the lee of the sandhills, and then my eyes locked onto the solitary figure of Sergeant Cuff standing farther down the beach. He waved his hand towards the north, shouting above the din of the storm: "Keep to that side! Come on down here to me!"

I stumbled towards him, my heart racing and my breath coming in ragged gasps. The Sergeant's face looked twisted with a mixture of horror and concentration. He snatched the boot from my hand and placed it in a footprint on the sand, pointing due south towards the rocky ledge known as the South Spit.
