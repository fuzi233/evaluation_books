As the warm scents of roasting meat wafted through the air, Eli's family gathered around the supper table, awaiting the arrival of their missing member, Margaret. To Catherine's surprise, Eli suggested waiting a short while longer for her. "Why should we not wait for the one who has patiently awaited us for years?" he asked, his voice filled with a deep affection. His words were laced with the tender care of a devoted father, and Catherine couldn't help but notice the loving glint in his eye.

The savory aromas from the dishes tantalized Eli's senses, and he yielded to the hunger pangs, saying, "She will arrive soon enough. Let us begin without delay." He gestured for Catherine to take her seat, assuring her that Margaret would join them shortly. As the covers were lifted, a sudden commotion erupted from outside. In burst a Dominican friar, his face red with indignation, and his eyes blazing with righteous fury.

He strode swiftly to the table, his long frame towering over the seated family, and his hands hovered above their heads like a bird of prey sensing its prey. With a voice that thundered through the small space, he unleashed a torrent of curses, his words pouring forth like a tempest. The family cowered in fear, shrinking away from the torrent of abuse. The friar's anger was so intense that it seemed to fill the room, making the air thick with its presence.

As the friar finished his litany of curses, he flung a letter onto the table, his chest heaving with exertion. "Read this, you hard-hearted man," he spat, his voice dripping with venom. "Behold the truth of your actions!" Eli's eyes widened as he scanned the letter, his expression changing from shock to comprehension. The family's faces remained frozen in terror, their eyes fixed on the friar's retreating figure.

In a flash, the friar vanished as suddenly as he appeared, leaving behind a stunned family. Margaret and Jorian, who had been watching from the doorway, gasped in unison as they took in the scene before them. Margaret's eyes welled up with tears as she beheld the petrified figures, their faces pale and their bodies rigid. She rushed forward, her heart heavy with sorrow, and exclaimed, "Oh, joy! Everyone is safe! No harm has been done."
