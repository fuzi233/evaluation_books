It became clear that moderation was essential, for the path ahead was long and treacherous, with no guarantee of a clear endpoint. I decided to maintain a steady rhythm, allowing myself to eat and drink at set intervals, and to rest at designated times, thereby conserving my strength for the challenges that lay in store.

My plan was to eat and drink every six hours, and to rest from the eighteenth to the twenty-fourth hour. This routine would enable me to cover a significant distance while minimizing the strain on my body. However, I knew that such a regimen would not always be feasible, as the Night Land was fraught with peril, and I would often need to remain vigilant, sacrificing rest for the sake of caution.

In the beginning, I found it difficult to adhere to my schedule, and I broke my rule almost immediately. For twenty-one hours, I walked, hiding and creeping whenever the landscape seemed to pose a risk of detection. My hunger and fatigue eventually took their toll, and I sought refuge in a small fire-hole I spotted in the distance. The faint glow emanating from within beckoned me closer, promising warmth and a respite from the cold, dark vastness of the Night Land.

As I approached the fire-hole, I noticed that it was a relatively small opening, roughly a few paces wide, with a dull, smoldering flame that cast a faint light on the surrounding rocks. I sat down nearby, careful not to disturb the embers, and placed my Diskos within easy reach. Though exhausted, I hesitated to eat or drink, preferring instead to gaze back at the distant pyramid, feeling both a sense of accomplishment and trepidation. Its proximity, though a source of comfort, also served as a poignant reminder of the enormity of my task, leaving me shaken and uncertain.
