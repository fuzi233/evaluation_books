In the hushed stillness of the Great Library of Flatland, the soft whisper of rain pattered against the leaded panes, casting a mesmerizing rhythm that seemed to mirror the pulse of the land itself – a land where life was a canvas of monotony, a world of lines and shadows. For Aenigma, a Square of some renown, the day's arrival brought little excitement, save for the prospect of reviewing the letters that had accumulated by the morning post. Amidst the stack of worn parchment, one letter stood out – a missive from the celebrated Square, Chromatistes, detailing his pioneering work in the art of painting.

Aenigma's thoughts wandered back to a time when Flatland was a realm of vibrant hues, where the ancestors of their kind had once reveled in the splendor of color. According to tradition, a private individual – a enigmatic figure known only as Chromatistes – had unlocked the secrets of the simplest colors and created a rudimentary method of painting, transforming his own form and that of his kin. His variegated frame had been a marvel, captivating the attention and respect of all who beheld it. No longer did they need to rely on mental calculations to distinguish one individual from another; their movements became effortless, their interactions a symphony of understanding.

As Aenigma delved deeper into Chromatistes' letter, he recalled how the practice of painting had swept through Flatland like wildfire, spreading from the district of Chromatistes to neighboring regions. Within a single generation, the custom had become an integral part of their society, with all but the highest nobility and the Priests and Women holding onto their colorless forms. Nature itself seemed to have erected a barrier, pleading against extending the innovation to these two classes, citing the distinction of sides as a pretext for the innovators. Yet, Aenigma couldn't help but wonder – what if the axiom about "distinction of sides implying distinction of color" was not as absolute as they thought? What if, in fact, it was a limitation imposed by their own narrow perspectives, a constraint that had been passed down through generations, stifling the true potential of their world?

As the rain continued its soothing melody, Aenigma felt an inexplicable restlessness, a sense that there was more to their world than the dull, monochromatic landscape that lay before him.
