The pain of confronting her fears was exhilarating, a catalyst for growth, rather than a numbing despair.

With her belongings unpacked and her space tidy, Sally turned her attention to reconnecting with Ginger, a friend whose steady presence would offer a comforting anchor. She yearned to see him, to bask in the warmth of their friendship and draw strength from his unwavering optimism. A pang of regret struck her for not sending a telegram to let him know she'd be arriving, and she recalled the disorienting emptiness she'd felt at the dock, surrounded by the echoes of customs sheds.
