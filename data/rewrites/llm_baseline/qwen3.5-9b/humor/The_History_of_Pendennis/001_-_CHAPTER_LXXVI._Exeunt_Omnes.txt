My Dearest Doctor,

Thirteen long moons ago, just as it looked like our little drama was about to hit "The End" with a tragic flourish, a very nice chap dragged you over to my bedside. Honestly, without your round-the-clock vigilance and your medical wizardry, I'm pretty sure I'd still be lying there, staring at the ceiling tiles until the heatstroke took me.

I simply must pause to thank you for your immense goodness—and by extension, the surprisingly high level of sympathy shown by everyone else during that dark hour. It's funny how people suddenly become angels when they're actually useful; usually, we just see them as obstacles or bad company. But back then, kindness wasn't just polite; it was life-saving.

Since you seem to operate on a strictly non-monetary basis (refusing any fee other than gratitude), allow me to put these thanks down permanently in black and white. Consider this my official receipt, signed on behalf of me and my entire family.

Yours most sincerely and gratefully,
