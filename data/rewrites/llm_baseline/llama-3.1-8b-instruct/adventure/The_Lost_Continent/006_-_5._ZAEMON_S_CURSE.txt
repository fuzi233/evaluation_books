As we dismounted from the golden carriage, the glittering procession drew to a halt before the grand entrance of the royal pyramid. The air was heavy with anticipation, the only sound the soft murmur of the crowd. Our group, cloaked in the majesty of the serpent-adorned canopy, stepped down onto the paved square, the weight of history settling upon us like a shroud. I felt the familiar tug of reverence as I turned towards the imposing silhouette of the Sacred Mountain, my eyes drawn to the spot where the ancient rocks seemed to whisper secrets to the wind. Without thinking, I inclined my head, offering a silent prayer to the forgotten gods of old, the words echoing through my mind like a distant melody.

But as I stood, I sensed a ripple of amusement emanating from the gathered crowd. I scanned the faces, my gaze locking onto Phorenice, expecting to see her standing in obeisance beside me. Instead, she stood tall, her expression enigmatic, her eyes gleaming with a knowing light. I felt a flutter of unease as I realized she had not joined me in my silent devotion. Her eyes met mine, and I saw a spark of understanding flicker between us.

Without a word, she beckoned me to follow her, her voice carrying across the square as she called out the secret password, the stone doors creaking open to admit us into the pyramid's hallowed halls. As we stepped inside, the warmth of the sun gave way to the cool, damp air within, and I felt a shiver run down my spine. The fan-girl trailed behind us, her delicate steps echoing off the stone walls.

We paused in the grand hall of waiting, the air thick with the scent of incense and something else – a fragrance I couldn't quite place. The paintings on the walls seemed to loom over us, the images of long-forgotten kings frozen in time. The fountain of earth-breath cast a warm glow over the space, but it seemed fainter than I remembered, its light struggling to penetrate the heavy atmosphere. I felt a pang of nostalgia wash over me, remembering the days when the air had been crisp and clean, the scent of the pyramid a sweet reminder of the old ways.
