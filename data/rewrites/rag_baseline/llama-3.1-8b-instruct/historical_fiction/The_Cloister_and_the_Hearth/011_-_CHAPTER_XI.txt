As Gerard descended the winding path, the shadows of the cloister seemed to deepen, their silhouettes twisting into grotesque forms that danced in the flickering torchlight. The air was heavy with the scent of damp stone and decay, a morbid perfume that clung to his skin like a shroud. The burgomaster's parting glance still lingered in his mind, a cold, calculating gaze that had left him with a sense of foreboding.

Gerard's footsteps echoed off the walls as he navigated the narrow corridor, his heart pounding in his chest like a drum. He had expected to find some treachery waiting for him, a cruel trick devised by Ghysbrecht to ensnare him within these cold, unforgiving stones. But as he turned a corner, a gentle hand brushed against his, and Margaret's voice whispered in his ear, "Come, Gerard, let us face this darkness together."

As they emerged from the shadows, a sight unfolded before them that took their breath away. A creature with a fiery head, like a lantern in the darkness, ascended the tower's wall, its presence both mesmerizing and terrifying. Gerard and Margaret stood transfixed, their senses reeling from the sheer wonder of it.

Margaret's grip on his hand tightened, and Gerard felt a surge of protectiveness course through him. "The rope!" he whispered, his voice barely audible above the wind. "It's climbing the rope!"

The glowworm's light vanished into the cell, casting an eerie glow over the interior. The white figure remained motionless below, a haunting apparition that seemed to embody the very essence of their fears. Gerard felt a shiver run down his spine as Margaret's hand slipped from his, and she stepped forward, drawn to the specter with a wild, unholy fascination.

Gerard's instincts screamed at him to stop her, but he remained rooted to the spot, his voice frozen in his throat. It wasn't until he heard Margaret's cry of despair that he sprang into action, falling to his knees and invoking the sacred words of exorcism. The air seemed to vibrate with the power of his incantations, and to his amazement, the specter responded, its eerie glow faltering as it whispered a pitiful plea for mercy.
