As the Golden Hind sailed back towards the familiar shores of England, its crew couldn't help but ponder the tales of old. The words of the poet Campbell echoed in their minds: "The spirits of your fathers shall start from every wave; for the deck it was their field of fame, and ocean was their grave." 

Mrs. Hawkins sat with Mr. Gilbert, her eyes fixed intently on the glittering ore samples spread before them. "With these silvers, lead, manganese, and copper, alongside this gossan, which the Cornish call the matrix of the ore, I suspect it may hold the key to refining the very essence of metals," Mr. Gilbert said, his words dripping with excitement. "If my recipes, gleaned from the wisdom of Doctor Dee, prove successful, I'll refine out the silver, set it aside, and transmute the remaining ores into gold. Imagine, my dear madam, the implications – Peru and Mexico would be mere afterthoughts, and England would reign supreme."

Mrs. Hawkins sighed, her expression a mix of concern and resignation. "But what use would it be to you, Mr. Gilbert, even if you could conjure gold from thin air? It's not as though it could wash away the stains of our conscience. God's mercy comes slowly, and often at a steep price." She paused, her gaze drifting upwards to the ribbed oak roof, where the crest of her late husband's family emblazoned a haunting reminder. "When that crest was first hung in our grand new house, the parson's words struck a chord within me – 'Woe to him that buildeth his house by iniquity, and his chambers by wrong.' Those negroes still weigh heavy on my heart, and I fear the Lord will exact payment for our transgressions."

Mr. Gilbert's countenance turned thoughtful, his eyes narrowing as he weighed his words. "My dear madam, you speak of the weight of our actions, but what of the world beyond our shores? The Spaniards wield power through gold, the very instrument of their might. Can we not outwit them, outmaneuver them, by securing the same advantage without spilling blood? Gold, not just a commodity, but a tool for securing our place among nations."
