The sun rose over the small cottage, casting a warm glow through the windows as Marianne slowly stirred from her slumber. Though she claimed to be feeling somewhat better, her eyes told a different tale – a tale of weariness and lingering discomfort. She lay back against the pillows, trying to muster the energy to engage in her usual activities, but the effort was futile. A day spent shivering by the fire, clutching a book she couldn't focus on, or lying listlessly on the sofa, spoke volumes about her condition. Colonel Brandon, observing her sister's fragile state, couldn't help but feel a sense of concern, while Elinor, though trying to appear composed, was secretly worried.

As the day drew to a close, Marianne's fatigue became increasingly apparent, and she eventually surrendered to her exhaustion, retreating back to bed. It was then that Elinor, persuaded by Mrs. Jennings's words of wisdom, decided to seek the expertise of the local apothecary, Mr. Harris. He arrived shortly, examined Marianne with a discerning eye, and while offering words of encouragement, hinted at the seriousness of her condition. His mention of infection sent a ripple of alarm through the room, particularly among Mrs. Palmer, whose concerns for her baby's safety were palpable. Mrs. Jennings, who had always suspected Marianne's illness to be more severe than initially thought, now looked on with a grave expression, urging the need for Marianne's immediate removal to a safer location.

Mr. Palmer, though initially skeptical, found his wife's anxiety impossible to ignore, and eventually relented, agreeing to follow her to the home of a distant relative on the outskirts of Bath. As the preparations were underway, Colonel Brandon, too, began to contemplate leaving, but it was Mrs. Jennings who intervened, gently suggesting that he stay behind and care for Marianne, whose spirits were already low. With a heavy heart, Colonel Brandon acquiesced, and the party was thus reduced to just Elinor and Mrs. Jennings, who vowed to tend to Marianne with the utmost care.

The prospect of missing this milestone, and the surprise visit they had intended to make on their mother, filled her with a deep sense of disappointment.
