With measured steps, they escorted us to the grand hall of the temple, a majestic structure that seemed to embody the very essence of Barsoom's storied past.

As we entered the great hall, the air was filled with the savory aroma of roasting meats wafting from the kitchen, mingling with the scent of exotic spices and herbs. The chefs, donning crisp white aprons, busied themselves with the final preparations for the day's main course – a traditional Barsoomian feast, featuring the finest delicacies the planet had to offer.

We were led down the broad Aisle of Hope, flanked by ranks of stern-faced guards, towards the central platform. The judges, thirty-one esteemed members of the noble class, sat in a circle about the periphery, their faces stern and unyielding. As I gazed upon them, I noticed a subtle absence of familiar faces – none of my friends or allies were present among them.

The vast circular coliseum was packed to its full capacity, a sea of diverse faces representing all walks of life on Barsoom. The hum of subdued conversation gave way to an expectant silence as we took our seats upon the platform, facing the judges and the audience. Upon a smaller platform at the center of the larger one, a steaming hot dish was being presented to the judges – a traditional Heliumian stew, rich with the flavors of the planet's bounty.
