Lady Rockminster's sharp tongue had been the primary culprit, her words slicing through the air like a scythe, leaving in its wake a trail of discomfort and unease. But it was not just her acidic wit that had cast a pall over the evening; Arthur's arrival, his face ashen and his eyes haunted, had sent a ripple of concern through the assembled guests.

Laura, ever the empathetic soul, had intervened, her voice a gentle balm to the anxious atmosphere. "Arthur, dear cousin, what's wrong?" she asked, her eyes locked on his troubled visage. Arthur's response, a hasty glass of sherry, only served to heighten the sense of foreboding that hung in the air. "I've received some extraordinary news," he said, his voice barely above a whisper. "I'll tell you later."
