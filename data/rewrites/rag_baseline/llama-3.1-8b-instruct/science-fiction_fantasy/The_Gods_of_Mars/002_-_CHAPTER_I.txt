My heart yearned to be reunited with my lost love, to feel the thrum of his power coursing through me once more.

As I raised my arms to the starry sky, the red glow of Mars a beacon above, I felt the familiar, intoxicating pull of the divine. It was a sensation I'd known on countless nights, since that fateful evening in 1866, when I'd lain still and lifeless in the Arizona cave, masquerading as a corpse. But tonight, the longing was almost unbearable. My senses reeled, my legs buckled, and I stumbled, pitching forward onto the dewy grass at the edge of the bluff.

The horror, the terror, the feeling of being trapped and helpless – it all returned, as if I'd never escaped. My muscles refused to obey me, and I felt the same paralyzing anesthesia wash over me. I strained against it, and for an instant, I was free. Then, like the snapping of a wire, I was plunged into darkness, my body numb, my soul adrift.

I pushed myself up, my eyes locking onto the star above, and my hands reached out once more. The moment passed, and I felt the familiar jolt of departure. The world around me dissolved, and I was hurtled into the void. An instant of cold, crushing darkness enveloped me, and then I opened my eyes to find myself in a realm both familiar and yet utterly alien.

Beneath the scorching sun, I lay amidst a sward of crimson grass, surrounded by trees with blooms as vibrant as the stars, their branches heavy with an otherworldly beauty.
