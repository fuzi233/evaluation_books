My thoughts, however, were soon interrupted by a sense of constriction in my chest, and I found the blanket draped over my face. It was then that I realized another presence pressed against me, and my initial assumption was that my companion had inadvertently shifted onto my mattress during the night. I shouted louder, but my companion remained unresponsive, and I noticed the flap of the tent hung loose, an egregious mistake. I hastily exited the tent into the darkness, intent on securing it properly. It was then that I comprehended, with a growing sense of unease, that the Swede was nowhere to be found. He had vanished.

A rush of panic swept over me, and I sprinted into the darkness, the sound of the Swede's snores now replaced by an unsettling stillness. As I emerged into the open, I was enveloped by a maddening hum, a disorienting murmur that seemed to emanate from all directions at once. My concern for the Swede's safety propelled me forward, despite the trepidation that gripped my heart.
