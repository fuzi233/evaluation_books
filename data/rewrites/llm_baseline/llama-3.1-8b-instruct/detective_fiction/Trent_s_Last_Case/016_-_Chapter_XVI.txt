As they emerged from the grand entrance of the sprawling flats, Mr. Cupples turned to Trent with a curious expression. "Forgive me, Trent, but I believe you mentioned an appointment at seven-thirty?" His words trailed off, inviting clarification.

Trent's countenance lit up with a radiant smile. "Indeed, we do have an engagement. You're to join me for dinner. I've taken the liberty of securing a table at Sheppard's, and I daresay, nothing would bring greater joy to this evening than sharing it with you. After all, when one has finally unraveled a puzzle that had beset me for nigh on a year, what better way to celebrate than with a convivial meal? The prospect of indulging in a fine repast without the tedium of our club's routine offerings is almost too enticing to resist."

Mr. Cupples' eyes narrowed slightly as he fell into step beside Trent. "Sheppard's, you say? Forgive my ignorance, but who might that be?"

Trent's glee seemed to border on the effervescent. "Ah, Sheppard? A name that might as well be a cipher, a riddle waiting to be deciphered. I care not who Sheppard is or was; what matters is that their establishment serves a saddle of mutton that has beguiled even the most jaded of palates, including those of certain American visitors who have since cursed the very name of Christopher Columbus."

A hansom cab drew up alongside them, and Trent signaled for the driver to halt. As they settled into their seats, he lit a cigarette with an air of frenzied excitement. "And another reason I chose Sheppard's, my dear Cupples, is that I find myself on the cusp of a most momentous occasion – my forthcoming union with the incomparable Mabel. I daresay, the connection between these disparate thoughts should be clear."

Mr. Cupples' face split into a warm smile. "By Jove, Trent! I had heard whispers of your intentions, but to hear it confirmed is music to my ears! Congratulations, my dear fellow! May I extend my felicitations from the bottom of my heart? You two are a match made in heaven, and I daresay, Mabel's presence in your life will be a balm to your soul.
