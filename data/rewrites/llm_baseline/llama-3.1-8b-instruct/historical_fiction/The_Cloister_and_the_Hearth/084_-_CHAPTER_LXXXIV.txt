The aroma of freshly baked bread wafted through the air as Catherine busied herself in the kitchen, preparing for the family supper. Her son Cornelis and his brother Sybrandt were set to open their new shop the following day, and she was eager to celebrate their newfound independence. As they sat in the garden, basking in the warm sunlight, they couldn't help but feel a sense of excitement and purpose. The sign above the door, a white lamb sucking a gilt sheep, seemed to mock them, reminding them of their commitment to their new venture.

Their mother's ears, ever attuned to the conversations of her children, picked up on their conversation from the open window. She smiled to herself, knowing that they were finally embracing the idea of making a living from their own endeavors, rather than relying on the land. The family supper was to be a grand affair, with Margaret and her young son joining them, and Eli, their friend and supplier, contributing to the feast with fresh produce from his store.
