The afternoon sun cast a warm glow across the piazza at Belleville, where a well-dressed woman sat engrossed in a novel, her eyes scanning the page with a mixture of intensity and distraction. Her gaze wandered from the vibrant colors of the sailboats dotting the horizon to the lively groups of young people strolling along the beach, their laughter carrying on the breeze. She barely acknowledged the children playing nearby, their carefree shouts and giggles lost on her. It was as if the world around her existed in a state of suspension, and she was merely a spectator to the passing scenes.

At the other end of the piazza, an elderly man sat absorbed in his own reading material, a newspaper spread out before him. His eyes sparkled with amusement as he devoured the headlines, his benevolent face creasing into a series of gentle smiles. But as he read, he suddenly set aside his paper and rose from his seat, his movements fluid and unhurried. As he walked towards the edge of the piazza, he caught sight of two gentlemen emerging from the grand entrance behind him.

The woman, sensing their presence, quickly closed her book and rose to her feet, her expression a mixture of wariness and curiosity. Her eyes darted towards the approaching men, and for a moment, it seemed as though she might turn away, but instead, she stood frozen, her hand absently stroking the head of a small dog that sat by her side. The dog, sensing her tension, whined softly, its eyes fixed on the approaching strangers.

The two gentlemen, oblivious to the woman's unease, continued their leisurely stroll, their conversation hushed and indistinct. They paused at the far end of the piazza, turned, and began to retrace their steps, their faces a mask of indifference. As they drew closer, the elderly man shifted in his seat, his eyes flicking towards the pair, his expression a mixture of curiosity and interest. One of the gentlemen, an elderly man with a rugged, weathered face, caught his eye, and the old man's gaze lingered on him for a moment, taking in the lines etched on his face, the quiet strength in his posture. The younger man, on the other hand, seemed withdrawn, his eyes cast downward, his entire demeanor a picture of melancholy.
