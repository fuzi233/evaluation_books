Silence enveloped the city like a damp shroud. I descended into the streets, my footsteps echoing off the buildings. The moon hung low in the sky, casting an eerie glow over the deserted alleys. My thoughts drifted to Nais, lying asleep in the throne of granite, her presence a distant hum in the stillness. Old Zaemon's words lingered, a promise of redemption tied to the fragile thread of her release.

I navigated the narrow streets, avoiding the clusters of Phorenice's soldiers huddled around their campfires. Their unease was palpable, a nervous energy that seemed to seep from their very pores. I moved unseen, a ghostly figure weaving between the shadows. The weight of the task ahead pressed upon me, a slow-building pressure that threatened to consume me whole.

My destination lay hidden behind the great stone circle of the Lord the Sun, a place where the ancient powers slumbered, waiting to be stirred. Old Zaemon's plan was a mystery, a puzzle yet to be solved, but I was willing to risk everything for Nais. The cost of her freedom was unclear, but I was prepared to pay it.

As I approached the Ark of Mysteries, a sense of trepidation settled over me. The door creaked open, revealing a dimly lit chamber filled with the scent of old dust and forgotten knowledge. I stepped inside, my eyes adjusting slowly to the darkness. The air was heavy with anticipation, a sense that something was about to be unleashed.

Old Zaemon's voice echoed through the chamber, a low murmur that seemed to come from everywhere and nowhere at the same time. "The time is near," he whispered. "The earth tremors will soon begin. Are you prepared to face what is to come?"

I nodded, my heart pounding in my chest. The weight of responsibility settled upon me, a burden I was ill-equipped to bear. But I steeled myself, drawing on a reserve of determination that I never knew I possessed. The night ahead would be long and fraught with danger, but I was ready to face it head-on, for Nais' sake.
