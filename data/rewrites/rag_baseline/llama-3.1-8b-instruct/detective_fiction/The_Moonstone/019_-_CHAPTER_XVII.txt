Sergeant Cuff lingered, his presence a constant reminder of the weighty mystery unfolding beneath our feet. I expected him to depart for Frizinghall, but instead, he seemed to deliberate on some unspoken matter. Mr. Franklin, strolling along the shrubbery path, caught my attention, and I joined him, exchanging a brief greeting.

Before our conversation could gain momentum, the Sergeant appeared at our side, his eyes narrowing as he approached Mr. Franklin. A haughty silence settled between them, with Mr. Franklin responding curtly to the Sergeant's polite inquiry. The air was heavy with tension, the unspoken question hanging like a challenge.

Sergeant Cuff's words dripped with a calculated patience, his tone measured to provoke a response. "I merely wish to remind you, sir, that I have not yet been proven wrong in my inquiry. In light of this, I implore you to consider your position and whether it is not your duty to provide any information you might possess that could aid me in my investigation."

Mr. Franklin's sharp retort cut through the air, his words laced with a defensive edge. "I possess no special information." The Sergeant's expression remained inscrutable, as if he had not been addressed. His gaze, however, betrayed a hint of calculation, his eyes flicking towards the shrubbery path where Rosanna Spearman and Penelope had momentarily appeared.

A fleeting glimpse of the scene flashed before my mind – the movement in the swing-door, the coat-tails disappearing down the passage – and I sensed the Sergeant's suspicion: Rosanna had confided in Mr. Franklin the previous evening. This notion sparked a chain reaction in my thoughts, but before I could grasp its full implications, Rosanna emerged from the shrubbery, accompanied by Penelope, who urged her to rejoin the others.

The girls paused at the threshold, their faces etched with uncertainty. Mr. Franklin's eyes met mine, and I shared his confusion. The Sergeant, meanwhile, adopted an air of nonchalance, feigning obliviousness to their presence. His words, however, were laced with a subtle intent, designed to draw Mr. Franklin into the conversation.
