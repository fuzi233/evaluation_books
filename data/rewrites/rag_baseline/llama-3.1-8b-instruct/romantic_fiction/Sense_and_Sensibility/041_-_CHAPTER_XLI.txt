Her spirits soared as she envisioned the three of them sharing meals together, surrounded by the lush greenery of the estate. The prospect of their impending union filled her with gratitude towards Elinor, whose selflessness and kindness had earned her a special place in Lucy's heart.

As Mrs. Jennings inquired about Elinor's role in securing Edward's happiness, Lucy's words overflowed with sincerity. She spoke of Elinor's unwavering dedication to those she loved, her unshakeable resolve to support them in times of need. Colonel Brandon, too, was held in the highest esteem, his integrity and generosity inspiring Lucy's admiration. She harbored a deep respect for the Colonel, not merely as a benefactor, but as a guardian of the community, whose influence extended far beyond the boundaries of his estate.
