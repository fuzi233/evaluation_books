As the post-chaise rattled to a stop before the door of Aswarby Hall, a young boy named Stephen Elliott peered out at the unfamiliar surroundings with wide-eyed curiosity. It was late September 1811, and the crisp autumn air carried the scent of ripe fruit and damp earth. The hall itself stood as a testament to the architectural styles of bygone eras, its red-brick façade dating back to the reign of Queen Anne, while the stone-pillared porch added a touch of classical elegance from the late 1700s. Tall, narrow windows with small panes and intricately carved white woodwork glinted like a string of fireflies in the fading light, while a pediment above the entrance boasted a delicate round window, casting a warm glow over the scene.

Beyond the hall, a tranquil expanse of parkland stretched out, dotted with ancient oaks and fringed with fir trees that stood sentinel against the deepening sky. The distant chime of the church bell, its golden weathercock catching the last rays of sunlight, tolled the hour of six, its gentle cadence carried on the breeze. The atmosphere was one of serene tranquility, tinged with a hint of melancholy, as if the passing of summer's warmth was being mourned.

Stephen's thoughts, however, were far from somber. Just six months prior, he had lost his parents in Warwickshire, leaving him an orphan. But fate had brought him to this new home, courtesy of his elderly cousin, Mr. Abney, who had extended a generous invitation to live at Aswarby Hall. The prospect of a new life under Mr. Abney's care filled Stephen with a mix of excitement and trepidation. Little was known about his reclusive cousin, who was said to possess an extensive knowledge of ancient Greek and the mystical practices of the past. Mr. Abney's library, rumored to contain a vast collection of rare texts on the Orphic poems, the worship of Mithras, and the Neo-Platonists, only added to the enigma surrounding his character.

As the post-chaise door creaked open, Stephen stepped out onto the porch, his eyes scanning the surroundings with wonder.
