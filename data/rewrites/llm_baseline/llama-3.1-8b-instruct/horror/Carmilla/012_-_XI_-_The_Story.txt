As I sat listening to the General, I felt the threads of time begin to weave together, drawing me into the tapestry of his tale. With a deep breath, he began to speak, his voice laced with a hint of melancholy. "My dear child had been eagerly anticipating the visit you had arranged for her to your enchanting daughter," he said, his eyes locking onto mine with a gallant bow. "In the meantime, we received an invitation to join my old friend, Count Carlsfeld, whose grand schloss lay six leagues to the east of Karnstein. We were to attend the lavish festivities in honor of his illustrious guest, the Grand Duke Charles."

"Yes, I recall they were quite spectacular," my father replied, his eyes sparkling with reminiscence.

"Indeed, they were," the General continued, his voice filled with awe. "Count Carlsfeld's hospitality was nothing short of regal, reminiscent of Aladdin's lamp. The night of the masquerade was a night I shall never forget. The grounds were ablaze with colored lamps, the trees twinkling like stars in the darkness. The fireworks that lit up the sky were a wonder to behold, rivaling anything Paris had ever seen. And the music! Oh, music is my weakness, and that night it was as if the heavens themselves were singing in harmony with the finest instrumental band in the world, accompanied by the most exquisite singers from across Europe. As I wandered through the enchanted gardens, the moonlight casting a golden glow over the chateau, I felt myself transported back to the romance and poetry of my youth."

As the fireworks came to an end and the ball began, we made our way back to the grand salon, where the nobility had gathered. A masked ball is always a wondrous sight, but this one was unlike any I had ever seen. I was struck by the opulence of the assembly, and yet, I felt like an outsider, a mere nobody among the aristocracy.

My dear child shone brightly, her beauty unencumbered by a mask. Her excitement and joy added an extra layer of radiance to her already lovely features. I noticed a young woman, resplendent in her finery, but veiled behind a mask, watching my ward with an intensity that seemed almost...unnatural.
