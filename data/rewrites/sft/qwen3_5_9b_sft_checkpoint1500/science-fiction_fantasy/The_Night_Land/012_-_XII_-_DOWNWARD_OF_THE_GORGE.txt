I bore the girl an hour further down from the Gorge, as I mentioned. By then, exhaustion had seized me so completely that I nearly collapsed with every step, stumbling constantly as the guidance in my feet failed, revealing my utter weakness.

I realized I needed a place for sleep urgently, or I would tumble headlong with her; for I was nearly asleep while walking. My consciousness faded, and I drifted into a trance-like state regarding the sides of the Gorge. Suddenly, I woke as from a nightmare, my will sharpening to perceive reality. It struck me then that I had traversed this strange, narrow place like a somnambulist; the Gorge appeared entirely fresh to me, as if I had awakened abruptly to find myself walking there.

Presently, I recognized my location. I recalled the route of my outward journey, trusting in my ability to remember paths traveled. There, near me, burned a great, ruddy fire-hole. I was certain I had passed caves in the wall of the Gorge nearby during my ascent, and a mighty longing had gripped my heart then—the desire to safely bring Mine Own out of peril and secure her a place for slumber on our return journey.

Truly, I recount this with the joy of a child in play, for it seemed a wondrously happy prospect that my deepest desire might finally be fulfilled, if my memory served me correctly.

I pressed onward. Lo! I found the small caves there, just past the great fire-hole. Seven of them lined the left side of the great cliff of the Gorge, and one appeared particularly cozy—a sanctuary of sure safety if we could reach it.

Surely, as I conceived, we would escape the Evil Forces of that Land safely. Yet, I felt no absolute security. A Monster might descend from the Gorge, working our deaths while we slept, unless we secured a refuge. Indeed, I wished we had descended further, but I was adrift with weariness. If we reached the topmost cave, however, few Monsters would have the power to breach us before we were warned, and they would likely struggle to ascend against the Diskos thereafter—a difficult undertaking.

It became necessary to wake the Maid. I kissed her, as she lay in my arms; surely, the dear one kissed back in her sleep and remained unaware. Truly, I loved her with all my being. I kissed her again and shook her gently, drawing her into wakefulness, and told her we had arrived at a fitting place for slumber.

She looked around, very sleepy, as I helped her to her feet, then upbraided herself for having fallen into such a deep sleep while I labored carrying her. In verity, I kissed her again as she steadied herself, looking so pretty with the lingering sleep in her eyes. She returned the kiss, very dear and wholly devoted, and even then, scarcely fully awake, she spoke with a slight, tender abandonment, declaring that she loved me utterly and forever.

Afterward, I climbed to the topmost cave, telling Mine Own as she paced slightly, ensuring she achieved full wakefulness. This was vital, for she would need all her faculties to navigate safely upward to the shelter.

When I reached the cave, lo! it was sweet and dry, bringing gladness to my heart. A warmth permeated the space, suggesting a fire burning somewhere within the rocks nearby. Light reflected from the fire-hole inward, rendering the place safe and fit for our slumber.

I descended to Naani, calling out that the cave was perfect for our needs. Presently, I offered her assistance in climbing, and together we entered safely. We felt a profound sense of safety and happiness.

Yet, before sleep, I released the scrip and pouch, taking the straps and returning downward into the Gorge. I retrieved a large boulder, heavy as I could manage, strapped it to my back, and ascended again to the cave. The Maid, grave and anxious, watched lest I slip and injure myself. Upon reaching the cave, I placed the boulder at the mouth, balancing it lightly upon the edge so that a mere touch would send it rolling downward.

By this device, I reasoned that any Beast or Monstrous Thing attempting to climb upward while we slept would likely dislodge the rock. While it might harm the intruder, the chief purpose was to ensure I was swiftly woken by the noise.

Then, we retired to slumber. The Maid had spread her cloak upon the rock for us to sleep upon, though it was no longer needed for covering, given the cave's warmth. Surely, there was no need for me to use the cloak as a bed, for how could I feel its softness beneath my stern armor? Yet, I observed that she had fashioned a couch for the two of us, sweet and natural, allowing me to lie by her. Still, to preserve her modesty, I approached the arrangement with no thought other than what was natural to our hearts; she longed always to be near me, perhaps scarcely conscious that her heart prompted her in this.

In verity, I loved her dearly.

Naani showed me where I should lie upon my side. When I obeyed, she knelt, kissing me on the lips, sober and loving. She lay down upon the cloak beside me, and truly, within a minute, both of us had sunk into sleep.

Twelve great hours later, I was awakened by the fizzing of water. Looking around, the Maid was not beside me but preparing our simple meal and drink. She laughed at me, sweet and tender, for she loved me so and was glad I had awakened to her. She came over, kissing me brightly and lovingly upon the lips.

Afterward, she knelt beside me, looking at me with great tenderness. I knew instantly that she had awakened some time prior and had watched me, somehow motherly, as I slept. How I knew this, I am unsure, save that my spirit sensed it, or her thoughts spoke directly to mine.

Truly, I needed to be so loved, and I raised my arms to her. She did not deny me; she came into my embrace, snuggling there sweetly and happily, with such true delight that it was plain she loved me in all her body and spirit. Yet, truly, as you recall, the armor was upon me. I feared to hold her strongly, lest I hurt the dear Maid; the armor was stern, unsuitable for nesting. However, perhaps the sternness pleased her woman's heart, or perhaps it lacked appeal.

Presently, she made to depart, and I loosened my arms readily, mindful always that she retain the full, sweet liberty of her maidenhood. I remained watchful, honest, and wholesome, avoiding over-pondering. You shall perceive this proper intention if your hearts strive to hearken to mine, which speaks always before you.

The Maid departed toward the cup of water and the tablets. I rose to check that the boulder remained undisturbed at the cave's mouth. But she called to me to lie back, meaning gently to spoil me, and told me to heed neither the boulder nor any potential Monster or Beast in the Gorge, for the boulder was safe as I balanced it, and no Creature was visible, for she had looked often since waking.

Truly, I obeyed, lying back and enjoying the spoiling. Naani brought the cup and tablets, retrieving them from the scrip before I could reach my pillow. Even as she brought the cup, she refused to let me rise; instead, she took my head onto her knees, kissing me daintily on the lips, and I was very happy to be so loved, wishing for no other.

She took a tablet, kissed it, and gave it to me. Then she touched another to my lips, keeping it for herself. Thus, we ate, very glad and happy, somewhat like children, our hearts at ease.

We each consumed a second tablet in the same manner. Truly, I was kissed more than once. Afterward, we each drank the water.

When we finished, Naani told me to stand. I wondered, and rising, I nearly cried out with the pains of my bruises. I was stiff from sleep, and more sore than ever, owing to the fight I had engaged in—surely injured more by the Four-Armed Man than I had realized.

I perceived then that Naani had anticipated my pain, giving dear thought to the matter. She had the pot of ointment ready, should I require it. She assisted me with removing my armor and garments. Truly, I was utterly bruised by the violence of the Yellow Beast-Man. The Maid's eyes were tender and sweet as she looked upon my wounds, filling me with happiness and contentment.

She made me lie down and arranged the cloak for my comfort, so loving and grave that I felt like a child cared for by his mother. She rubbed me skillfully and gently for a great hour until I was refreshed. In verity, she was a lovely, wise maid.

While she ministered, I lay restfully, listening to the low muttering of the fire-hole at the bottom of the Gorge. Always, I felt like a happy child clothed in love and guided in wisdom.

When the Maid finished her care, she set aside the ointment and offered me her hands daintily to assist me in rising. When I stood, I was eased, able to move with little pain, which pleased me wondrously and renewed my courage. I had been troubled by the thought of being helpless, the Protector of Mine Own.
