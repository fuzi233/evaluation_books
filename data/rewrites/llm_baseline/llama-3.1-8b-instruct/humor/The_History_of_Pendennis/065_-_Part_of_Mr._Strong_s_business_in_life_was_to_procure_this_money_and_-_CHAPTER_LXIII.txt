As Major Pendennis pondered the intricacies of the Clavering family's affairs, gleaned through his connections with Strong and his own discreet inquiries, a sense of unease began to settle within him. The prospect of marrying Arthur to a young woman with two such unsavory fathers-in-law, as the unfortunate Lady Clavering had managed to entangle herself with, was a daunting one indeed. The thought of Amory or Altamont making public appearances, with the former's propensity for violence and the latter's fugitive status, only served to heighten the sense of unease. The old gentleman knew that neither of these men would ever willingly submit to British authority, and yet, their very presence would be a constant reminder of the precarious nature of their family's circumstances.

In particular, Pendennis was troubled by the notion that Altamont, with the specter of the gallows hanging over him, would inevitably seek to avoid detection, while simultaneously using the threat of his discovery to hold Clavering hostage. The Baronet, with his reputation in tatters and his fortune dwindling, would be nothing more than a pawn in Altamont's game, forced to dance to the tune of his benefactor's whims.

Furthermore, Pendennis couldn't shake the feeling that the Begum's wealth, were she to settle Clavering's debts, would ultimately be squandered on the profligate Amory, leaving his heirs to inherit a depleted estate and a tarnished family name. Miss Amory, far from bringing a respectable income and a seat in Parliament to her future husband, would merely bring her own dubious reputation and a legacy marked by scandal.

As Pendennis turned these thoughts over in his mind, another, more calculated strategy began to take shape. It was a plan that would require finesse, diplomacy, and a deep understanding of the complex web of relationships that bound the Clavering family together. This scheme, which would soon become apparent to the reader, would involve a delicate conversation between Pendennis and the Honourable Baronet, and would prove to be a crucial turning point in the unfolding drama.
