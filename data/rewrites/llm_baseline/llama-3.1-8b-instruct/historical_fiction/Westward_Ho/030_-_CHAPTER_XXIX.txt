As the days passed, Amyas settled into a tranquil routine, his thoughts occupied by the lessons of the past year. Yeo, now installed as major domo, wandered the halls with the quiet confidence of a loyal companion, his evenings spent by the fire, hands moving deftly as he crafted intricate trinkets for the family. His Bible lay open on his lap, a testament to his unwavering faith. Each evening, he presented Ayacanora with a small token, an act that only seemed to elicit a pout, followed by a stern rebuke from Mrs. Leigh, and finally, a hasty acceptance. The gesture, though well-intentioned, was lost on Ayacanora, whose heart remained fixed on emulating Mrs. Leigh's mastery of domestic arts. She spent her days trailing behind the older woman, absorbing the intricacies of cooking and brewing, and the art of sewing garments for the family. Her ultimate goal, however, was not merely to be a capable homemaker, but to extend her kindness to those in need. Inspired by Mrs. Leigh's tireless charity work among the poor, Ayacanora began to grasp the value of selfless giving. She observed Amyas, too, as he distributed his wealth among the people of Northam, Appledore, and Bideford, establishing almshouses for weary sailors. Yet, her understanding of charity differed greatly from his. In her native land, gifts were often made to appease the spirits, and she struggled to reconcile this concept with Amyas's actions. When Mrs. Leigh gently explained that kindness to the poor was a way of honoring the divine presence within them, Ayacanora's perception shifted. She began to assist Mrs. Leigh in her charitable endeavors, her hands moving tirelessly as she labored for the benefit of Northam's elderly women. As she accompanied Mrs. Leigh on her visits, Ayacanora's gaze would occasionally flicker towards Amyas, seeking his approval. And when, on occasion, she secured a smile from him, her behavior was transformed, only to lapse back into mischievousness when her efforts went unrewarded. In church, however, her true nature shone through, as she entered, resplendent in her vibrant Spanish attire, drawing the attention of the entire congregation.
