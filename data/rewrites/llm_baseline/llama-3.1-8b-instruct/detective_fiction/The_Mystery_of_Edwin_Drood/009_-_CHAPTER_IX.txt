In the tranquil hours before dawn, a subtle murmur began to circulate within the walls of the Nuns' House, where Rosa had found a home since her seventh year. The gentle hum of whispers, like the soft lapping of waves against the shore, carried the news that had shaken the quiet evening hours – a quarrel between Edwin Drood and Neville. The exact origin of the rumor was unclear, as if it had seeped into the air itself, carried by the morning breeze that rustled through the trees outside. Perhaps it was the baker, kneading the news into the dough, or the milkman, adding it to the creamy liquid he delivered each day. Whatever the source, the tidings spread quickly, infusing every corner of the old building with a sense of unease.

Rosa's life had been a tapestry woven from threads of love and loss. Her memories of her mother were etched vividly in her mind – a beautiful young woman, brought home by her father in a tragic accident at a summer party. The image of the wet hair, tangled with flowers, and the delicate summer dress, had remained indelible. Her father's grief had been a palpable presence, a weight that had settled over the household, until his own passing on the first anniversary of the tragedy.

As Rosa grew older, the whispers about her past continued to surround her, casting a soft glow over her life. The nuns and the townspeople had treated her with a mixture of pity and affection, creating a sense of dependence she had grown accustomed to. She had become an amiable, vivacious, and determined young woman, cherished by all around her. However, beneath the surface, a subtle undercurrent of rivalry simmered, as each person vied for her attention and affection.

Rosa's nature was a wellspring of love, and its gentle waters had brought joy to the Nuns' House for years. Yet, her heart remained untested, its depths untouched. As she navigated the complex web of relationships, it was unclear what would happen when the calm surface of her life was disrupted. Would the ripples of change bring forth hidden strengths or reveal unseen vulnerabilities? Only time would tell.
