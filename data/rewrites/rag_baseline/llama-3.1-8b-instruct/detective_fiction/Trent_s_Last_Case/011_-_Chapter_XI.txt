In the realm of forensic inquiry, the intricacies of human motivation often prove far more enigmatic than the most complex cryptographic cipher. As I sit down to pen this, my third and final dispatch on the Manderson murder, I am met with a mix of emotions. A sense of relief washes over me, for I have been compelled to withhold crucial information in my previous reports, lest it inadvertently alert the culprit and facilitate their escape. Yet, I must confess that the tale of deception and cunning that unfolds before me leaves an unpalatable taste, a morbid fascination with the darker aspects of human nature.

Let us revisit the events leading up to the discovery of the body. My initial report detailed the scene as I found it upon arriving at the location early on Tuesday morning. The particulars of the crime scene, the mysterious circumstances surrounding Manderson's death, and the local hypotheses that emerged in its wake were all meticulously documented. I also shared a seemingly inconsequential detail: the disappearance of an unusually large quantity of whisky from Manderson's private decanter, an anomaly that would later prove pivotal to unraveling the mystery.

As I delved deeper into the investigation, I discovered an intriguing paradox. While the residents of the house swore they had heard no disturbance during the fateful night, the physical evidence suggested otherwise: Manderson's wrists bore signs of a struggle, and at least one pistol-shot had been fired. This incongruity piqued my interest, particularly in light of Martin, the butler's, reputation for being a light sleeper with acute hearing. His bedroom, with its window open, offered a direct line of sight to the shed where the body was discovered.

A second peculiarity soon came to light: Manderson's dental plate, typically worn to bed, lay abandoned by his bedside. This seemingly trivial detail took on greater significance as I continued to investigate. It suggested that Manderson had risen and dressed himself, down to his nightwear, before meeting his untimely demise. The implications of this discovery only deepened the enigma, raising questions about the true nature of the events that transpired on that fateful evening.
