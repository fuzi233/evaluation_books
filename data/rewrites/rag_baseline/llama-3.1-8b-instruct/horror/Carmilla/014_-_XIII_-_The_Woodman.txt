The sensations that followed were equally unsettling – a chill breeze against her skin, a stinging pain below the throat, and finally, a crushing sense of strangulation that left her breathless.

His words conjured images of our own beautiful guest, Carmilla, whose habits and peculiarities seemed to echo those of the afflicted Millarca. The General's words hung in the air like the scent of wet earth, as we drove past the abandoned village, its chimneys and gables a testament to a history both grand and troubled.
