As the fire crackled in the hearth, the small gathering at the hosier's house huddled around the dinner table, their conversation swirling around the recent tumultuous events. Their mother, her face a mask of controlled fury, swept into the room, a letter clutched in her hand. With deliberate slowness, she laid it flat on the table, her eyes scanning the faces of Cornelis and Sybrandt as if daring them to meet her gaze. Then, with a sudden jolt, she lifted her head, her eyes blazing with a fierce intensity that made the air vibrate with tension.

Catherine, her words spilling forth like a torrent, unleashed a verbal barrage that left her siblings reeling. Her words were a maelstrom of anger, a whirlpool of hurt and betrayal that threatened to consume them all. "You," she spat, her voice venomous, "have long harbored suspicions that you fueled the flames between Gerard and Father, and now, with Gerard's own words, it's clear – you've driven your own flesh and blood to distant shores, leaving me to nurse the pain of your treachery."

As she spoke, her voice wove a tapestry of memories, each thread embroidered with the ache of their childhood. "When we were young, Gerard was a comfort to me, but you, Cornelis, were a constant thorn – mischievous and sly, always getting into trouble, always testing the limits. Your carelessness with your clothes was legendary; it was as if honesty and industry were foreign concepts to you." Her words dripped with a deep sadness, a sense of disappointment that had been festering for years.

Her eyes flashed with a hint of wonder, a question that hung in the air like a challenge. "Where did you come from, exactly? You don't take after Father, and you're not like me either – you're a cipher, a puzzle I've struggled to solve. Sometimes I think you must have been switched at birth, or perhaps the cuckoo dropped you on my doorstep." Her voice rose, a crescendo of outrage. "You've always mocked those who are different, who don't fit your narrow standards – Giles, because he's smaller, Lily Kate, because she's not strong enough. And now, you've sunk to a new low, stealing from the very people you claim to love."

The room fell silent, the only sound the crackling of the fire and the heavy breathing of Catherine's children.
