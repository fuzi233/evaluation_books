A Learned Squabble.

The sun-drenched gardens of the Gherardesca palace glowed like a verdant tapestry, woven from threads of emerald and sapphire. Beyond the loggia, the gentle rustle of leaves and the sweet scent of blooming flowers mingled with the distant song of a lutenist, carried on the warm breeze of a May afternoon. Bartolommeo Scala, secretary of the Florentine Republic, reclined upon a cushioned chair, his gouty heel propped on a velvet stool. He was a man of considerable wealth and influence, as attested by the azure ladder on his family crest – an emblem of his ascension through the ranks, unaided by fortune or patronage. Yet, despite his many accomplishments, Scala's felicity was tempered by the contents of certain letters laid before him.

A correspondence with the renowned scholar Politian had become the subject of a most...lively discussion. The two men had engaged in a spirited exchange, their words dancing across the page in a playful tone, yet beneath the levity lay a deep-seated animosity. Politian, once a suitor for Scala's daughter Alessandra's hand, had been rebuffed, and now sought to exact a measure of revenge through the written word. The secretary's response, penned in the midst of a particularly grueling bout of gout, was a masterpiece of defensive verbosity, replete with clever turns of phrase and witty ripostes.

As Scala listened to the opinions of his friends, he couldn't help but feel a twinge of annoyance. Ficino and Landino, those esteemed scholars and fellow browsers of the Medicean park, praised his writing with lavish enthusiasm, while the formidable Joseph Scaliger reserved his judgment, his silence a testament to the depth of his scorn. The secretary's eyes narrowed, his mind racing with the implications of his friend's words. Was he truly as inept a writer as they claimed, or was it merely a matter of taste? The question nagged at him, a persistent itch he couldn't scratch.

In the midst of this intellectual tumult, the aroma of roasting meats wafted from the kitchen, mingling with the scent of fresh bread and herbs. Alessandra, her dark hair styled in the fashionable knot of the day, smiled brightly as she poured a cup of wine for her father.
