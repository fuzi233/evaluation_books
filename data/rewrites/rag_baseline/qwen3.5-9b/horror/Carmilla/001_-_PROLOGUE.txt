Upon a sheet of aged parchment affixed to the narrative that follows, Doctor Hesselius has penned a note of considerable elaboration, accompanied by a referral to his treatise on the singular phenomenon illuminated by this manuscript. In that scholarly inquiry, he addresses the matter with his customary erudition and piercing insight, stripping away ambiguity with remarkable directness and compression. Were I to present the full scope of his findings, it would constitute but a single volume within the vast series of that extraordinary man's collected works. Yet, since my sole intent in publishing this case is to engage the layperson, I shall defer to the intelligent lady who recounts it, allowing her voice to stand unobstructed. After due deliberation, I have resolved to withhold any précis of the Doctor's intricate reasoning or extracts from his assertions regarding a subject he deems to involve, not improbably, some of the most profound arcana of our dual existence and its elusive intermediates. My initial impulse upon uncovering this paper was to reignite the correspondence commenced by Doctor Hesselius so many years prior with an informant of such formidable intellect and meticulous care. Regrettably, however, I discovered that she had passed away during the intervening decades. Had she lived, she might have contributed little to the narrative she now communicates in the following pages, rendered with a conscientious particularity that, to the best of my judgment, remains undimmed by time.
