As I walked, the weight of my departure from home settled upon me like a physical burden. The Master Monstruwacan's words of encouragement still lingered in my mind, a reminder of the trust placed in me. But with each step, the city's warmth and comfort receded, leaving only the stark, unforgiving vastness of the Night Land.

The aether around me pulsed with the collective thoughts and desires of the countless inhabitants I had left behind. Their whispers and yearnings swirled about me like a maelstrom, a cacophony of hopes and fears that threatened to consume me whole. Yet, I pressed on, driven by a sense of duty and a glimmer of hope.
