Silence seeped into the darkness like a cold mist. I stood at the edge of the Great Road, the faint glow of the Home-Call Machine casting an eerie light on the deserted landscape. The air was heavy with the weight of the past, and the Instruments' faint hum seemed to vibrate in harmony with the stillness. Our people had grown accustomed to the Influence, that strange and quiet force that emanated from the House of Silence on the distant hill. Some believed it was directed towards the Youths, who were now drawing near to the treacherous stretch of the Road Where The Silent Ones Walk. Others whispered of a secret power, one that might hold the key to their fate.

I recalled the words of the Master Monstruwacan, his voice barely above a whisper as he spoke of the risks of summoning the Home-Call. Its piercing voice would alert the monsters that lurked in the shadows, putting the Youths in even greater peril. We had witnessed the devastating consequences of its misuse in the past, when the great flying-ships had traversed the skies. The thought sent a shiver down my spine as I gazed out into the darkness, searching for any sign of the Youths.

Days passed, each one a blur of anxious waiting and watching. The Land itself seemed to be holding its breath, as if anticipating the outcome of the Youths' journey. Low roars echoed through the night, a haunting reminder of the terrors that lurked beyond the edges of our world. I found myself drawn to the Instruments, their soft melodies a balm to my frazzled nerves. They seemed to capture the essence of the Influence, a mysterious power that both fascinated and unsettled me.

As the hours ticked by, our hopes and fears hung in the balance. We knew that the Youths had not slept or eaten, their bodies weakened by the unforgiving environment. The Master Monstruwacan's words echoed in my mind: "We cannot act without certainty, lest we risk losing them forever." The weight of those words hung heavy on my conscience, a constant reminder of the delicate balance between action and inaction. And so, I stood there, frozen in indecision, as the night wore on and the Influence continued to weave its subtle spell.
