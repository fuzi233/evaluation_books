Downward of the Gorge

We'd been walking for what felt like hours, the darkness of the Gorge swallowing us whole. I stumbled, my feet heavy with fatigue, my thoughts a jumbled mess of concern for the Maid. She slept fitfully in my arms, her breaths shallow and slow. I was on the verge of collapse, my legs trembling beneath me like a leaf in an autumn breeze. If I didn't find a place to rest soon, I feared I'd falter, and she'd be lost to the dangers lurking within the Gorge.

As I scanned the walls, the flickering torches casting eerie shadows, I thought I'd lost all sense of direction. The Gorge seemed to stretch on forever, a seemingly endless tunnel of blackness. But then, like a whispered promise, a memory stirred within me. I recalled the caves I'd seen on our ascent, nestled in the cliff face like a series of secret chambers. My heart quickened, a spark of hope igniting within me.

If we could reach those caves, we might find a haven from the perils that stalked us. I nudged the Maid gently, trying not to startle her. She stirred, her eyelids fluttering open, and I caught the faint scent of her perfume. My fingers itched to touch her, to reassure myself she was still with me. I pressed my lips to her forehead, feeling the softness of her skin. She kissed me back, her lips barely awake, and I held her close, my love for her a flame that burned bright and true.

As I helped her to her feet, she swayed, her balance uncertain. I steadied her, my hands cradling her waist, and she smiled up at me, her eyes still heavy with sleep. I kissed her again, the taste of her sweetness lingering on my lips. Her response was tender, her fingers brushing against mine, and I felt a pang of joy. In that moment, I knew I'd move heaven and earth to keep her safe.

I guided her to the caves, my footsteps echoing off the stone. The topmost one beckoned, a promise of shelter from the unknown dangers below. I encouraged her to walk, to shake off the remnants of sleep, and she did so, her movements tentative at first, growing more confident with each step.
