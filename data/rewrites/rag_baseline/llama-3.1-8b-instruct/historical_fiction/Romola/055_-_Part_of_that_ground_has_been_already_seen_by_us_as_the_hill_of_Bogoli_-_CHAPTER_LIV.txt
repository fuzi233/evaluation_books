The evening light cast a warm orange glow over the cobblestone streets as Romola hastened through the winding alleys, her feet carrying her with a sense of purpose she couldn't quite articulate. The scent of roasting chestnuts wafted from a vendor's cart, mingling with the acrid smell of woodsmoke and damp earth. She navigated the narrow passageways with an instinctual knowledge, dodging market stalls and clusters of townspeople as she made her way to the Old Palace.

Her thoughts were a jumble of half-formed fears and intuitions, like the intricate patterns woven into the silk fabric of a Tuscan tapestry. She knew she couldn't afford to dwell on the darkness gathering within her, not now, not when every step brought her closer to the Gonfaloniere. Her fingers tightened around the small pouch containing the few coins she had managed to scrape together for a meal, the weight of it a tangible reminder of her own vulnerability.

As she crossed the bridge, the Arno River glinted like polished steel in the fading light, its waters a soothing balm for her racing thoughts. She took the most direct route to the palace, her heart pounding in her chest like a drumbeat. The pillared courtyard loomed ahead, its frescoes glowing softly in the twilight, and Romola felt a shiver run down her spine as she pushed open the massive wooden doors.

She requested an audience with the Gonfaloniere, her voice steady despite the turmoil brewing inside her. The usher's nod sent her through the labyrinthine corridors, past tapestries depicting the city's storied history, until she reached a private chamber. The door creaked open, and Bernardo del Nero stepped forward, his eyes narrowing slightly as he took in her disheveled appearance.

"Romola, my child, what is this?" he asked, his voice low and measured, as he closed the door behind her.

Romola's gaze locked onto his, her exhaustion momentarily forgotten in the face of her urgency. She felt a flutter in her chest as she began to speak, the words tumbling out in a rush. "Dearest godfather, it's about you. I've heard...things. Whispers, really. I don't know if I can trust anyone, but I had to come to you."
