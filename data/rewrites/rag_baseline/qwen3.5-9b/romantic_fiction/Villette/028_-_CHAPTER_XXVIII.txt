Within this system, interruptions were treated as existential threats; the mere act of traversing the aisle during a lesson was perceived by the faculty and student body alike as a violation of cosmic proportions, tantamount to a mortal danger.

Consider the operational protocols of the key actors. Madame Beck, the principal, approached these breaches with a calculated risk assessment, physically retracting her garments and navigating the raised platform with the precarious maneuvering of a vessel fearing treacherous shoals. Similarly, Rosine, the gatekeeper tasked with extracting students for private instruction, operated under extreme duress. Her repeated attempts to enter the sanctum of M. These spectacles served as a potent symbol of authoritarian surveillance, transforming simple administrative duties into moments of profound terror.
