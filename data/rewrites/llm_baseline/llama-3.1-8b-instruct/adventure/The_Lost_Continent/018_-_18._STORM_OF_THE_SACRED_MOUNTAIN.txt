As I sat beside Nais, tending to her gentle needs, the distant rumble of drums and the clash of steel on steel pierced the air. Her eyes fluttered open, and she asked, with a hint of curiosity, about the commotion outside. I recounted the tale of her long slumber, and the events that had transpired in her absence.

Her cheeks flushed with a mixture of concern and amusement. "My lord is a just ruler," she whispered, her voice barely audible over the din of battle. "He has always prioritized the safety of those he loves."

"I knew you would be worried," I replied, trying to soothe her. "But I refused to yield until I had secured your return, safe and sound, to the Sacred Mountain."

Nais's gaze drifted away, her thoughts clouded by the remnants of her long sleep. "Women are often treated as mere pawns in the game of power," she mused. "But I suppose I have grown accustomed to this...this 'traffic' between nations."

I handed her a cup of warm broth, and she sipped it gratefully. "Your strength will return soon, my love," I reassured her. "But for now, rest and let your body heal."

As she drifted off to sleep, a faint smile still playing on her lips, I rose from my chair and made my way to a nearby room. There, I found a selection of instruments and, with a sense of relief, shaved off the tangled beard that had grown during my vigil. A change of clothes, too, was welcome, and I donned a simple yet dignified robe, feeling a sense of renewed purpose.

But the sounds of war outside did not abate. The enemy's assault grew more intense, and I could feel the weight of our kingdom's fate bearing down upon me. Some of our defenses had fallen, and Phorenice's forces had breached the gates in the cliffside passage. The Empress was a formidable foe, driven by a fierce determination to claim the Sacred Mountain for herself.

As I gazed out the window, watching the chaos unfold, I realized that our defenses, though sturdy, relied on the bravery and skill of our men to hold firm. But even the strongest walls can crumble under the relentless onslaught of enemy forces, especially when faced with novel and deadly contraptions like Phorenice's fire-tubes.
