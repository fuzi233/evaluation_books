The air was heavy with anticipation as I gazed up at the sky with Thuvia by my side. She pointed upwards, her slender finger beckoning me to follow her line of sight. There, hovering above the temple, court, and garden, were shadowy bodies flitting hither and yon, their movements swift and unpredictable. The air was charged with tension as flashes of light burst forth from these mysterious objects, accompanied by the deafening roar of musketry. The thern defenders responded with volleys of their own, their shots whizzing through the air towards the swift and agile fliers.

Thuvia's words cut through the din, "The black pirates of Barsoom, O Prince." As she spoke, the air craft of the marauders began to circle lower and lower, their trajectory a blur of speed and precision. Volley after volley of gunfire ripped through the night air, striking true against the temple guards and the fleeing fliers. The thern soldiery poured out of the temples, pouring into the gardens and courts, their numbers swelling the chaotic melee.

As the pirates swooped closer to the ground, a swarm of fliers emerged from all directions, their silhouettes stark against the moonlit sky. The therns retaliated with shielded rifles, firing steadily into the void, but the pirates continued their relentless assault. Some of their craft were small and agile, designed for two to three men, while others loomed larger, their keel batteries unleashing devastating bombs upon the temples below.

With a coordinated rush, the pirates in our vicinity dived recklessly towards the ground, landing amidst the thern soldiery. Their warriors leapt forth from their craft with a ferocity that took my breath away. I had witnessed fierce battles before, but never anything like this. The black pirates seemed to embody a madness, a primal abandon that defied all rational understanding.

Under the radiant light of Mars' dual moons, the scene unfolded in vivid detail. Golden-haired therns clashed with ebony-skinned pirates in hand-to-hand combat, their struggles entwined amidst the lush vegetation. Here, a knot of warriors trampled a bed of vibrant pimalia; there, a curved sword sliced through a thern's chest, leaving its victim at the base of a glittering ruby statue. Everywhere, the air was alive with the clash of steel and the scent of smoke.

Thuvia and I stood apart from the fray, yet still within its reach.
