As Mrs. Lambert stood on the threshold, the soft flutter of her lily handkerchief caught in the gentle morning breeze, a poignant reminder of the departure of her beloved family. Her daughters, too, lingered for a moment, their eyes following the pair of gentlemen as they disappeared from view behind the verdant limes that had witnessed so many farewells. The trees, with their gnarled branches and weathered trunks, seemed to hold secrets of their own, their silences a testament to the passing of seasons and the ebbs and flows of life.

Behind the sheltering boughs of the limes, Mrs. Lambert often found solace in the memories of times past, when her husband had departed for battle, and her sons had set off for school, their futures uncertain but full of promise. The quiet moments at home, with its gentle rhythms and soothing sounds, allowed her to cherish the tenderness and fidelity that defined the women of their household. As she gazed out at the empty road, her thoughts wandered to the letters that would surely arrive tomorrow, bearing news of her loved ones far away.

In the vacant room where her son had slept just hours before, the imprint of his carpet bag remained, a tangible reminder of his presence. His whip, now hung in the hall, seemed to whisper tales of the fleeting joys they had shared. The cherry tart, with its half-eaten portion, brought a smile to her face, a bittersweet recollection of the simple pleasures they had savored together. The evening prayer, usually spoken with his voice leading the way, now felt hollow without his participation. And as the night deepened, Mrs. Lambert's slumber was fitful, her mind preoccupied with the thought of one of her children absent from the nest.

With the dawn breaking, the tranquility of their home gave way to the routines of the day, and the weight of responsibility settled upon her shoulders. The rustling leaves of the limes seemed to form a gentle barrier between their world and the outside, a comforting illusion that shielded them from the uncertainties that lay beyond.
