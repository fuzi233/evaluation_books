Two nights passed since Pietro Vanucci and Andrea had settled into the evening meal, awaiting Gerard's arrival. The atmosphere grew increasingly tense as the hours crept by, with the clock striking nine. Pietro's impatience boiled over, and he dispatched Andrea to Gerard's quarters, a faint hope lingering that his friend might have slipped in unnoticed. Andrea's shoulders slumped in resignation as he departed, returning with a crumpled piece of paper clutched in his hand.

As he handed it to Pietro, a mixture of excitement and trepidation danced across his face. Pietro snatched the note, his eyes scanning the scribbled words with a mixture of amusement and concern. "Dear Pietro, dear Andrea, life is too great a burden," he read aloud, his voice dripping with sarcasm. "So 'tis, my lad,' but that's no reason to be out late for supper. Supper is not a burden."

Andrea's eyes widened as Pietro continued reading, his voice taking on a mocking tone. "'Wear my habits!' Said the poplar to the juniper bush." The words were laced with a dark humor, but Pietro's pale cheeks and chattering teeth betrayed a deeper unease. As he finished the letter, Andrea's face contorted in despair, and he let out a mournful cry. "O, miserable day! O, calamity of calamities! Gerard, my friend, my sweet patron! Help! help!" The words echoed through the house, summoning the attention of the other occupants.

Andrea burst out into the night, his bare head exposed to the elements, imploring passersby to aid him in saving his friend. A small crowd began to gather, drawn by the commotion. However, Andrea's pleas fell on deaf ears, met with skepticism and dismissal. The notion of descending to the riverbank after sundown was deemed foolhardy, as many believed it to be a haunt of restless spirits. Yet, one of the onlookers, a loyal servant of the Princess Claelia, offered to accompany Andrea on his quest, and soon, a handful of younger men joined the cause.

As they made their way to the river, the air grew thick with an unsettling silence. The lackey, whose name remained unknown to the group, questioned Andrea about Gerard's recent behavior, his voice low and measured.
