As I sit here, lost in thought, I am reminded that the tides of life can shift in the most unexpected ways. It had been three weeks since the fateful encounter in the garret, and I found myself in possession of a small collection of letters – five in total, penned by the same hand, bearing the same unmistakable seal. These letters, full of vitality and warmth, had become my solace, a balm to my soul. They were the words of one who had known joy, and in their gentle cadence, I found comfort.

At first, I read them as a draught of ambrosia, a taste of the divine. Time, however, tempered their fervor, and when I revisited them later, I saw them for what they truly were – pleasant, but not overwhelming. Yet, when I first encountered them, their impact was profound. They spoke of a love that knew no bounds, a love that sought to soothe the pain of another's existence.

I recall the question that had lingered in the reader's mind – how did I respond to these letters? Did I heed the dictates of reason or allow my emotions to guide me? The truth is, I walked a fine line between the two. I wrote two responses – one for my own satisfaction, the other for Graham's benefit.

In my initial response, I allowed my emotions to take center stage. I poured out my heart, expressing a deep and abiding affection, a sense of gratitude that bordered on reverence. My words were a testament to the power of feeling, a celebration of the human experience. But, as I finished writing, reason intervened, cautioning me against revealing too much. It was a delicate balance to maintain, one that required great care.

Dr. Bretton's visits were a welcome respite from the turmoil within. He would take me to La Terrasse, regaling me with stories and laughter. His kindness was not merely a gesture; he had a purpose in mind. "To keep the nun at bay," he would say, his eyes twinkling with amusement. He had grown tired of her presence, put off by her austere demeanor and cold, grey eyes. He sought to outwit her, to prove his intellectual superiority. But I sensed a deeper motivation beneath his words – a desire to protect me from her influence.
