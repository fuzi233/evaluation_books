As one settles into the quietude of seclusion, whether within the walls of a school or a secluded dwelling, it's not uncommon for friendships forged in a more vibrant world to gradually fade from memory. The denizens of this freer world, often preoccupied with their own pursuits, might not notice the absence until a prolonged period of silence has taken hold. It's as if the threads of connection, once woven tightly, have begun to unravel, leaving behind a tapestry of uncertainty.

In the stillness of such moments, the mind can become a labyrinth of doubt and speculation. The hermit, ensconced in their self-imposed solitude, may struggle to reconcile the disparity between their internal world and the external reality. They might attribute the silence to their own introspection, choosing to retreat into the shadows, much like a dormouse curling up in its cozy nest, waiting for the seasons to pass.

Yet, unbeknownst to the hermit, their friends and acquaintances continue to navigate the complexities of life, their paths crossing and recrossing like the intricate patterns of a Persian rug. Events unfold, whispers spread, and the currents of fate carry individuals along, often unaware of the impact they have on those left behind.

As the weeks turn into months, the solitary individual may begin to grasp the impermanence of human connections. They learn to accept the ebb and flow of relationships, recognizing that even the most cherished bonds can be swept away by the tides of time. The hermit's thoughts, once a jumble of anxiety and self-doubt, begin to settle into a tranquil acceptance. They come to understand that the silences, though painful, are an inherent part of life's tapestry.
