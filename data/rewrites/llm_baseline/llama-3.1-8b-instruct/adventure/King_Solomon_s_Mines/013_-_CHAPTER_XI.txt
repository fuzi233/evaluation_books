As the first wisps of dawn crept over the horizon, casting an ethereal glow over the kraal, a heavy silence hung in the air, weighing upon the minds of the gathered warriors. Two hours had passed since the gruesome events of the previous night, and the stillness was oppressive, punctuated only by the occasional hoot of a distant owl. It was then that the sound of footsteps echoed through the village, growing louder with each passing moment. The challenge of the sentry at the kraal gate was met with a response, and the soft rustle of approaching figures signaled the arrival of Infadoos, accompanied by a retinue of six stately chiefs.

"My lords," Infadoos began, his voice low and measured, "I have come as promised. I bring with me these esteemed leaders, each commanding three thousand soldiers, sworn to obey their every command. I have shared with them the tale of the horrors I witnessed, and the words I overheard. Now, let them behold the sacred symbol etched upon Ignosi's skin, and hear his account, that they may decide whether to ally themselves with him against the usurper Twala."

With a subtle gesture, Ignosi revealed the serpentine tattoo encircling his waist, its intricate patterns glinting in the faint light of the lamp. One by one, the chiefs approached, examining the mark before moving on to the next. Their faces remained inscrutable, their expressions unreadable in the dim light.

When the inspection was complete, Ignosi resumed his narrative, recounting the events he had shared earlier. His words painted vivid pictures in the minds of the chiefs, who listened intently, their eyes never leaving his face. As he finished speaking, Infadoos stepped forward, his eyes burning with conviction.

"Now that you have heard Ignosi's tale, my brothers, what say you? Will you stand by him and aid him in reclaiming his rightful throne, or will you remain loyal to Twala? The land cries out in anguish, its people suffering under the weight of oppression. You have seen the consequences of defiance – two of your own comrades lie dead, their bodies claimed by the hyenas. Will you suffer the same fate if you refuse to act?"
