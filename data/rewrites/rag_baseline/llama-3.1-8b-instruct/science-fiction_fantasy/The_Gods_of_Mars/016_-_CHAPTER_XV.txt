As I regained consciousness, a warm, golden light enveloped me, banishing the darkness that had shrouded my world for what felt like an eternity. The eerie silence that had accompanied my fall was shattered by the soft murmur of voices, their hushed tones a stark contrast to the cacophony of sounds that usually filled the air. I rose to my feet, assessing my surroundings with a mixture of caution and desperation. The torch in the hand of one of the four green warriors cast flickering shadows on the walls, momentarily blinding me. I slipped into the nearest intersecting corridor, careful not to draw attention to myself. This time, I didn't venture too far from the main passageway, wary of losing Tars Tarkas and his guards once again.

As the party approached the opening of the passageway where I crouched, I held my breath. Their footsteps echoed through the corridor, growing louder with each passing moment. I watched as they passed by, their faces illuminated by the torch's dancing flames. Relief washed over me as they disappeared from view, and I let out a silent sigh. The party was the same one I had followed into the pits, consisting of Tars Tarkas and his three guards. I trailed behind them, careful not to make a sound as we approached the cell where the great Thark had been chained.

The torch remained fixed in its socket, casting an eerie glow over the scene. As I watched, two of the warriors strolled away, disappearing from view beyond a turn in the corridor. I seized the opportunity to approach the entrance to the cell, my plan taking shape in my mind. The thought of freeing Tars Tarkas and escaping together weighed heavily on me, but I saw no alternative. Keeping close to the wall, I drew my longsword, gripping it firmly with both hands, ready to strike down the jailer as he emerged from the cell.

The sound of footsteps grew louder, and I tensed, waiting for the moment to act. My heart pounded in my chest as the jailer approached, oblivious to the danger lurking mere inches from him. In the next instant, Tars Tarkas burst forth from the cell, wearing the metal of a Warhoon chief, and hurried down the corridor toward the spiral runway, the Warhoon's torch casting a faint glow on the walls. I followed closely behind, my sword still poised for action.
