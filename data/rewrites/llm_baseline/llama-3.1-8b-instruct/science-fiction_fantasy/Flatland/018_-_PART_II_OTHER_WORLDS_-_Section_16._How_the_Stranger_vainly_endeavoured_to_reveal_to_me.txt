As the echo of my wife's gentle farewell faded into the stillness, I approached the enigmatic stranger with a mix of trepidation and curiosity. His form seemed to shimmer and shift, defying the rigid boundaries of our Flatland's familiar geometry. The thought of a cunning irregular shape, masquerading as a circle to gain entry into our home, sent a shiver down my spine. Yet, as I drew closer, my doubts were dispelled by the unyielding smoothness of his surface – a perfect, unblemished circle that seemed to radiate an otherworldly calm.

In our sitting room, where the dry air rendered visibility a challenge, I hesitated, unsure if my sight was playing tricks on me. Fear gave way to desperation, and I lunged forward, blurting out an apology as I reached out to touch him. My wife had been right; there was no hint of an angle, no roughness or unevenness – only the soothing curve of a true circle. As I circled around him, beginning from his radiant eye and returning to it once more, I felt a sense of wonder wash over me. This being was unlike anything I had ever encountered, and I knew I had to tread carefully.

The stranger's impatience broke the silence, and he spoke with a tone that brooked no argument. "Have you felt me enough by now? Am I not introduced to you yet?" His words cut through my reverie, and I felt a flush rise to my cheeks as I realized the absurdity of my earlier hesitation. "Most illustrious sir, forgive my awkwardness," I stammered, "which arises not from ignorance of polite society, but from a little surprise and nervousness, consequent upon this somewhat unexpected visit. I beseech you, please, do not reveal my foolishness to anyone, especially not my wife."

As I paused, the stranger's gaze seemed to bore into me, and I felt a sense of unease. "But before your lordship shares any further wisdom," I ventured, "would you deign to enlighten me as to your origins?" His response sent a shiver down my spine: "From Space, from Space, sir: whence else?" I hesitated, unsure how to respond. "Pardon me, my lord, but is not your lordship already in Space, your lordship and his humble servant, even at this moment?"
