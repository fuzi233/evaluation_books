As the sun rose over the tranquil grounds of Castlewood, the air was alive with the gentle murmur of farewells. The departure of Madame de Bernstein had become an event to be savored, and the numerous domestics of the estate gathered in anticipation, eager to bid farewell to their beloved mistress and her charming companion, Gumbo. The warm scent of freshly baked bread wafted through the halls, mingling with the whispers of affectionate goodbyes exchanged between the staff and their departing benefactress.

In the midst of this tender scene, the contrast between the elegant drawing room and the humble kitchen could not have been more pronounced. While the refined ladies of the household wept openly, their hearts heavy with the thought of parting from the charismatic Gumbo, the kitchen maids – Molly and Betty among them – stole away to the quiet solitude of the gardens, seeking solace in the simple pleasures of nature. As they wandered along the winding paths, the soft rustle of leaves and the sweet songs of birds served as a poignant reminder of the transience of life and the bittersweet taste of goodbye.

Gumbo, the object of their collective affection, had captured the hearts of all the women at Castlewood with his captivating wit, charming accomplishments, and genuine kindness. Though some of the men may have harbored jealousy, the ladies of the household saw beyond his humble station, recognizing in him a true friend and confidant. As the years went by, the social conventions that would later come to divide the races had not yet taken hold, and the women of Castlewood treated Gumbo with a generosity and warmth that transcended the boundaries of class and color.

As the morning wore on, the bustle of activity reached its peak, with the staff vying for a final glimpse of their beloved mistress and her nephew. The atmosphere was charged with a sense of nostalgia, as if the very fabric of their lives was being torn apart by the departure of those they held dear. Amidst this emotional turmoil, the sound of laughter and tears mingled in a poignant harmony, a testament to the enduring power of human connection and the indelible mark left by those who had touched their lives.
