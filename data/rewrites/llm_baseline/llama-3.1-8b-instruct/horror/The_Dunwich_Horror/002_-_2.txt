As the darkness receded, the nocturnal creatures of the hills began to stir, their restless calls piercing the air, and the dogs of the surrounding farms responded with a chorus of anxious barks.

In the midst of this turmoil, Lavinia Whateley, a woman of striking appearance, with skin as pale as the moon and hair as white as the driven snow, gave birth to her child. Yet, Lavinia seemed to take pride in her offspring, a dark, goatish-looking infant with eyes that sparkled like the stars on a clear night. She would often murmur prophecies about his future, speaking of powers that would shake the foundations of the earth.

Her isolation had become a self-imposed exile, one that allowed her to indulge in grandiose daydreams and singular pursuits, far removed from the mundane routines of the world outside.
