As the Christmas recess drew near, a tranquil hush began to settle over Miss Twinkleton's establishment. The term was coming to a close, and the young ladies were abuzz with excitement. Though still bound by the rules of the Nuns' House, a sense of freedom had taken hold. In the evenings, club suppers were held in the bedrooms, with marmalade served from delicate curlpaper plates and cowslip wine poured from a small, squat measuring glass. The housemaids, bribed with ribbons and worn-out shoes, looked the other way as crumbs were swept under the bedcovers.

In the midst of this lightheartedness, music made its appearance. Miss Ferdinand, known for her bold spirit, surprised the company with a lively solo on the comb-and-curlpaper, her fingers moving deftly over the makeshift instrument. As the night wore on, laughter and chatter filled the air, and the usual decorum of the establishment was temporarily forgotten.

Boxes began to appear in the bedrooms, filled with a jumble of belongings and treasures. The girls exchanged whispers and confidences, their voices barely above a whisper as they spoke of the golden youths who would soon be visiting them. Miss Giggles, ever the skeptic, raised an eyebrow at the idea of making faces at these young men, but she was vastly outnumbered.

On the eve of departure, the girls stayed up late, reluctant to surrender to sleep. In the dead of night, they talked of ghosts and the supernatural, their imaginations running wild with tales of spirits and specters. It was a tradition that never failed to bring a thrill of excitement to their otherwise structured lives.

The following day, the girls gathered in Miss Twinkleton's drawing room, the globes covered with brown Holland cloth, as they prepared to bid one another farewell. Glasses of white wine and plates of pound cake sat on the table, a simple yet elegant spread. Miss Twinkleton stood at the center, her voice filled with emotion as she spoke of the passing year and the promise of new beginnings.

"Ladies," she said, her words dripping with sincerity, "another year has come to a close, and we find ourselves at the threshold of a new era. May our hearts be filled with hope and our spirits renewed. As we depart, may we carry with us the memories of our time here, and may we return wiser and more compassionate than when we left."
