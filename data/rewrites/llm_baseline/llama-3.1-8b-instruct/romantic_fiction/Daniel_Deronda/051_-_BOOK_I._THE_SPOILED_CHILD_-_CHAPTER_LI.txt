As Deronda stepped into the grand apartment, the familiar scent of his childhood wafted through the air, carrying with it the memories of his mother's absence and the longing that had defined his early years. The two servants in the antechamber exchanged a curious glance, their eyes lingering on the young man whose striking features seemed to elevate even the somber tones of the evening dress. Yet Deronda was oblivious to their gaze, his attention drawn to the figure waiting for him at the far end of the spacious room.

Wrapped in a shroud of black lace, her face and arms the only exposed features, she stood poised like a statue, her elegance heightened by the delicate sparkle of rich bracelets adorning her wrists. As Deronda approached, she extended a hand, which he took, and then lifted to his lips in a gesture of reverence. Her eyes, piercing and expressive, scrutinized him intently, their depths a kaleidoscope of emotions that shifted and danced like the soft breeze rustling the leaves of a summer tree. For an instant, Deronda felt his cheeks flush with a mixture of excitement and trepidation, a sentiment he couldn't quite explain, as if he were caught between the idealized visions of his mother he'd conjured in his mind and the reality standing before him.

Her hand lingered in his for a moment longer, then dropped away, and she placed both hands upon his shoulders, her face breaking into a radiant smile that banished the lines etched on her visage, restoring a youthfulness that seemed almost otherworldly. "You are a truly beautiful creature," she said, her voice a melodic whisper that carried the faintest hint of a foreign accent. "I knew you would be." With a gentle kiss on each cheek, he reciprocated the gesture, the intimacy of the moment suspended between them like a fragile thread.

As she withdrew her hands, her expression cooled, and her words took on a subtle chill. "I am your mother," she declared, her voice firm and detached. "But I fear you cannot love me." Deronda's response trembled with nervous emotion as he spoke, his voice barely above a whisper. "I have thought of you more than any other being in the world."
