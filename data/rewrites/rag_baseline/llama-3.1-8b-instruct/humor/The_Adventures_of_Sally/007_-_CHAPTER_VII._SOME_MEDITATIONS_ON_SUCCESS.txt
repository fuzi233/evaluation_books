The misty veil of morning lifted slowly, like the gentle lapping of waves against the shore. In the dimly lit dressing room, Sally's gaze wandered over the faces of her companions, each one radiating an air of tentative optimism. The previous week's inertia had given way to a newfound energy, as if the collective weight of their hopes had been buoyed by the sudden elevation of their assistant stage manager to the role of proprietor. It was a phenomenon not unfamiliar to them – the Cinderella-like transformation of a previously overlooked individual, and the subsequent euphoria that followed.

As Sally watched, Fillmore's transformation was almost palpable. He stood before a mirror, adjusting his tie, his fingers moving with a newfound deliberation, as if every gesture held significance. A new waistcoat, a fresh supply of cigars, and a more discerning palate for lunch had all contributed to his revitalized appearance. He exuded a sense of confidence, his presence commanding attention as he posed in front of the theatre, his name emblazoned across the posters in bold letters.

However, amidst this whirlwind of positivity, Sally couldn't shake off the feeling of unease that lingered at the periphery of her consciousness. It wasn't until she found herself gliding through the autumnal landscape in a hired car with Gerald, the gentle rocking motion of the vehicle lulling her into a state of introspection, that the threads of her thoughts began to untangle. Gerald, once again, was at his affable best, his enthusiasm infectious as he spoke of the future, his eyes aglow with anticipation.

Yet, as the fading light of day cast long shadows across the landscape, Sally's attention began to wane. It was as if the warmth of the afternoon had given way to an unseen chill, one that pierced to the heart of her being. And then, like a whispered secret, the realization dawned on her – a truth she had been unwilling to confront. The euphoria that had swept over them all, the sense of possibility that had taken hold, was built upon a fragile foundation. Beneath the surface, a discordant note had been sounding, a quiet warning that their newfound success might be nothing more than a fleeting illusion. The world, with all its complexities and uncertainties, still lurked in the shadows, waiting to reclaim its dominance.
