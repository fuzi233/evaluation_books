As Gwendolen settled back into her family's estate, Offendene, she couldn't shake the lingering memories of her adventures abroad, where she had reveled in the thrill of gaming and imagined herself a queen of fortune. Though she'd returned home with a vague sense of disillusionment, a nagging feeling that nothing truly mattered as long as one amused oneself, she couldn't help but wonder if she'd been foolish to pawn her treasured necklace and leave behind the freedom that came with it. Meanwhile, across the Channel, Grandcourt was preparing to set out in pursuit of her, but not in the haste one would expect from a man smitten. Instead, he lingered in Baden-Baden, entwined in a web of social engagements with Russian acquaintances, which he eventually broke free from, driven by his desire to reach Leubronn.

Grandcourt's passions were a slow-burning fire, prone to flickering out, yet still capable of igniting with a sudden intensity. Like many men of his standing, he'd mastered the art of presenting a composed exterior, a façade of refinement and culture that belied the tumultuous inner workings of his mind. He could attend dinner parties, deliver speeches, and propose toasts to esteemed figures without ever truly investing his heart. His obstinacy and persistence were traits he'd honed over the years, and he'd developed a keen ability to conceal his true intentions behind a mask of calm deliberation. Yet, beneath the surface, a maelstrom of thoughts churned, driven by desires both noble and base.

In his quiet moments, Grandcourt couldn't help but feel a twinge of satisfaction at Gwendolen's decision to flee, as if it proved her spirit was worthy of his attention. Doubtless, she'd expected him to pursue her, and indeed, he had every intention of doing so. But for a week, he chose to delay, content to let the wheels of fate turn without interference. His trusted friend, Mr. Lush, sensed a mix of triumph and unease emanating from Grandcourt, as if he'd won a small victory but was unsure what other forces might be at play. As the days passed, whispers began to circulate among the guests at Diplow: where had Gwendolen gone? Had she rejected Grandcourt's proposal?
