The turmoil and passion that had previously coursed through her veins had given way to a tranquil acceptance.

"Denys," she said softly, her voice barely above a whisper, "I've received my instructions. I'm to read my lover's letter to his kin."

"Will you truly do that?" Denys asked, his brow furrowed with concern.

"I will," she replied firmly, her resolve unwavering. A hint of a smile danced on her lips as she continued, "Though my feelings towards them remain unchanged, I wouldn't dare defy my love's request. It's a peculiar comfort to obey, especially when the one I'm pleasing is far away, unable to enforce my compliance, yet trusting in my devotion. Ah, Gerard, my dearest, if you had been here, I might have hesitated to follow your wishes, but now, I'd venture into the depths of the ocean itself to fulfill your command. Denys hastened to share the news with Catherine and the rest of the family, who were gathered around the dinner table.
