The Unseen Madonna.

As Romola walked back from the hospital, she found herself veering off the main road, following a circuitous route towards the Arno River. Her path converged with the Ponte Vecchio, and she intended to hasten to San Stefano in search of Baldassarre. Though she dreaded the prospect of knowing more about him, a part of her felt a deep connection to this enigmatic figure. She quickened her pace, weaving through the crowded streets, as the sound of chanting drifted through the air.

Upon reaching the intersection of the roads, Romola found herself swept up in a sea of people kneeling in devotion. She joined them, her eyes fixed on the majestic procession making its way towards the Ponte Vecchio. The Great Cross of the Duomo led the way, its presence commanding reverence from the assembled crowd. Romola felt a pang of guilt for being late to the spectacle, knowing she would have to wait until the procession had passed before continuing on her journey.

As she rose from her knees, the weight of her exhaustion became more pronounced. A shopkeeper beside her offered her a seat in his home, courtesy of Gian Fantoni, a local merchant who was known for his kindness and devotion to the Frate. Romola was accustomed to being greeted with affection by the people of Florence, whose faces she had grown familiar with from her frequent visits to the Duomo. The concept of home had evolved for her, transcending the confines of her family's towered residence in the Via de' Bardi. Instead, she felt a sense of belonging within the very fabric of the city itself.

She gratefully accepted the shopkeeper's offer, entering the warm and welcoming home through an open door. A stout woman with three children, all dressed in the simple attire of the Piagnoni, made space for her beside a beautifully draped window. From this vantage point, Romola could witness the procession unfolding beneath the Ponte Vecchio, its slow and mournful rhythm a poignant reminder of the city's deep faith and tradition.

As the young men bearing the image of the youthful Jesus passed by, Romola felt a shiver run down her spine. Their footsteps echoed through the stillness, accompanied only by the soft rustle of woolen garments. Following them were the enigmatic Companies of Discipline, their faces concealed behind hoods, their eyes the only visible aspect of their otherwise anonymous presence.
