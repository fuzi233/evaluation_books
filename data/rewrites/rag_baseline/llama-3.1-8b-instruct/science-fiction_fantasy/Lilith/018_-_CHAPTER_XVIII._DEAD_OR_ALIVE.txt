Dead or Alive?

The moon hung low in the sky, a bruised and battered disc, its light struggling to pierce the dense canopy of the forest. I walked alongside it, our paths diverging yet converging in a silent understanding. My eyes remained fixed on the ground, but I sensed the moon's disconsolate gaze upon me. The air was heavy with the scent of damp earth and decaying leaves. As I traversed the forest floor, a flash of white caught my attention beneath the sprawling branches of a ancient tree. My footsteps slowed, and I crouched beside the object, my fingers brushing against the damp soil.

A human form lay before me, shrouded in the shadows. The initial glance suggested a skeleton, but as I drew closer, the contours of a body became apparent. It was a woman, gaunt and emaciated, her skin stretched taut over the skeletal framework. The cold seeped into my bones as I reached out to touch her, my mind racing with the impossibility of her situation. How had she ended up here, abandoned and unclothed? The forest was a place of mystery and terror, where the line between life and death blurred. Yet, despite the overwhelming evidence of her demise, a spark of hope flickered within me. Perhaps, just perhaps, she was not beyond salvation.

I rose to my feet, my eyes scanning the surrounding area for any sign of life. The forest was quiet, the only sound the distant hooting of an owl. I spotted a cluster of grapes hanging from a nearby vine, their juicy flesh a potential lifeline. I plucked a few from the stem and approached the woman, her mouth slightly ajar. I inserted a grape into her mouth, but there was no response. Her chest remained still, her limbs frozen in a pose that seemed both natural and unnatural. I searched for a pulse, my fingers probing the cold skin, but found none. Yet, the warmth of the pine needles beneath her head hinted at a more recent demise. The question lingered: was she truly dead, or merely in a state of suspended animation?

As I pondered this, my gaze fell upon her clenched hand, the fingers wrapped tightly around something small. I gently prised open her fingers, revealing a tiny, withered thing that might have been a flower or a leaf.
