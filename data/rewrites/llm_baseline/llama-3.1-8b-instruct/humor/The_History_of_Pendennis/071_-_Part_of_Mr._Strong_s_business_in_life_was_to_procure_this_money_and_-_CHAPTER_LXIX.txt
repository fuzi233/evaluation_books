As the first light of dawn crept through the shutters, Mr. Morgan entered the Major's chamber with the quiet reverence due to a gentleman of his employer's stature. The old man stirred beneath the covers, his voice gruff yet tinged with a hint of amusement.

"You're back, Morgan," he said, "though I'm afraid I won't be requiring your services again."

Morgan's expression remained as grave as ever, his words dripping with an air of dignity. "Not at all, sir. I merely wished to ensure your comfort during your stay, and to see to your needs as has been my habit." With deliberate slowness, he began laying out the silver dressing-case and razor, a ritual he had performed countless times before.

As he worked, he paused to inform the Major of a development. "I took the liberty of speaking with a young man last evening, one who might be of service to you. He comes from a respectable family and has traveled extensively with Lord Cubley."

The Major's eyes sparkled with interest, and he prodded Morgan for more information. "Ah, yes? Tell me more about this young man."

Morgan's tone turned suitably formal, though a faint blush crept up his neck. "He's a courier and valet, sir, with impeccable credentials. I've vouched for his respectability myself, and I daresay his presence would be a welcome addition to your household."

The Major's grin was laced with a hint of mockery, and Morgan felt a pang of irritation. "You're excessively kind, Morgan," he said dryly. "And I appreciate your efforts to secure a suitable replacement. Pray, bring him in."

With a dignified bow, Morgan announced, "Lord De la Pole's nephew, young Lord Cubley, recommended him, sir. Frosch, at your service."

As the young man entered, Morgan presented him to the Major with a flourish, his words dripping with an air of formality. "He's awaiting your pleasure, sir. His chest is delicate, and he finds the climate in Scotland unconducive to his health."

The Major nodded graciously, his eyes fixed on Frosch. "Ah, thank you, Morgan. You're too kind. Come, Frosch, we'll discuss the details of your employment later. In the meantime, would you be so good as to show Mr. Morgan out?"

Morgan bowed and departed, his face set in a mask
