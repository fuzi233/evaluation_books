In the dimly lit grandeur of the palace, I guided Sola to the spacious dining hall, where the faint scent of exotic spices wafted through the air, mingling with the whispers of ancient traditions. As she greeted her father, her eyes gleamed with a mix of duty and curiosity, a ritual she had mastered since childhood. Her tale unfolded like a rich tapestry, woven from the threads of her observations and intuition.

"Seven suns ago," she began, her voice a melodious accompaniment to the rustling of the silken drapes, "after her audience with Zat Arras, Dejah Thoris attempted to slip from the palace under the veil of night. Though I had not witnessed the outcome of her meeting with the cunning Jeddak, I sensed that something had transpired to leave her in a state of turmoil. When I discovered her attempting to escape, I didn't need to be told of her destination."

With a subtle nod, Sola summoned a dozen of Dejah Thoris' most trusted guards, and together they devised a plan to follow their beloved Princess into the unknown. As they set out, the night air was alive with the sweet fragrance of blooming zith-raks, and the soft chirping of nocturnal creatures provided a soothing background melody. They tracked Dejah Thoris to a nearby glade, where she stood with her loyal hound, Woola, her eyes flashing with a mixture of defiance and vulnerability.

As they approached, Dejah Thoris feigned anger, ordering them to return to the palace, but Sola's companions stood firm, refusing to abandon their charge. Eventually, Dejah Thoris relented, and together they ventured into the night, bound for the Sacred Iss and the Valley Dor. The stars above twinkled like diamonds scattered across the velvet expanse, casting an ethereal glow over the landscape.

The following day, they chanced upon a herd of wild thoats, and with the added mounts, their journey gained momentum. They rode swiftly, the wind whipping their faces as they traversed the vast expanse of the Barsoomian terrain. On the fifth day, the morning sun revealed a fleet of battleships sailing northward, their black hulls glistening like polished obsidian. The pirates spotted them, and soon, a swarm of black warriors surrounded the trio, their cries echoing through the stillness.
