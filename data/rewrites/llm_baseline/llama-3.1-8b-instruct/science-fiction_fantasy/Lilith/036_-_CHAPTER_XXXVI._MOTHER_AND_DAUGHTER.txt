As the sun set over the city, casting a warm orange glow over the crumbling buildings, Lona's frustration with the locals, particularly the women, grew increasingly unbearable. She longed to escape the stifling atmosphere and flee to a place where she could breathe freely. In contrast, I felt a sense of resolve wash over me, a conviction that abandoning the city would be a betrayal of the trust placed in us. If we left now, the princess would surely pursue us, and the prophecy, which held such promise, would slip through our fingers like sand. The thought of Lona's ethereal beauty captivating the princess's heart was a tantalizing prospect, and I steeled myself for the challenge ahead. I knew that if the princess resorted to violence, I would stand between her and Lona, ready to defend my companion at all costs.

Without revealing my suspicions about the princess's connection to the prophecy, I shared my thoughts with Lona, and to my relief, she agreed to accompany me to the palace. As we prepared for our journey, I couldn't help but feel a sense of trepidation. What would we find when we arrived? Would the princess be receptive to Lona's charms, or would she prove to be a formidable foe?

Meanwhile, within the palace walls, the princess was consumed by a growing sense of unease. From the vantage point of the highest tower, she had witnessed the approaching army of the Little Ones, their numbers seemingly endless. Panic set in as she realized that her attempts to eradicate them had been for naught. The prophecy, which had long been a distant threat, now loomed large, and she felt the weight of her failure bearing down upon her.

As the first light of dawn crept into the city, the princess descended to the black hall, seeking solace in the stillness of the morning. To aid her in her reflections, she had set up a small, ornate mirror in a hidden recess of the hall, where the sun's rays could dance across her face. The resulting vision of her own beauty, bathed in the golden light, was a balm to her frazzled nerves. But as the hours ticked by, and the city below erupted into chaos, the princess's composure began to fray. Shadows danced around her, but she refused to acknowledge them, her gaze fixed instead on the fleeting glimpse of herself in the mirror.
