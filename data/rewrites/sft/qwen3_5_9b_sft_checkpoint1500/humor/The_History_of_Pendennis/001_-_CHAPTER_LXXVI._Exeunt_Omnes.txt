To Dr. John Elliotson

My Dearest Doctor,

Twelve months back, just as I thought the curtain was finally falling on my little drama, a true pal rolled up his sleeves and dragged you right into my sickbed. Honestly, I'm convinced I'd still be a permanent resident in that cot today if it hadn't been for your tireless hovering and your magic touch. I simply adore remembering how magnanimous and sweet you were then (along with a whole cast of other folks who showed a baffling amount of loyalty and warmth), especially when those virtues were about as rare as black swans.

Since you refused any payment other than a heartfelt "thank you," allow me to put these sentiments down in ink, signing off on behalf of me and my entire family.

With deepest gratitude and sincere devotion,

W. M. Thackeray.
