The last of the evening's guests departed, leaving behind the faint scent of cigar smoke and the soft hum of laughter. I slipped back into the inner hall, where Samuel was polishing a decanter with a white cloth, the soft clinking of ice against glass the only sound breaking the silence. My lady and Miss Rachel emerged from the drawing room, followed closely by the two gentlemen. Mr. Godfrey wandered over to the sideboard, pouring himself a small glass of brandy and soda water, while Mr. Franklin slumped into a chair, his eyes sunken from fatigue.

My lady paused in the doorway, a hint of concern etched on her face as she glanced at the diamond necklace sparkling around her daughter's neck. "Rachel, where will you keep the Moonstone tonight?" she asked gently.

Miss Rachel's eyes sparkled with mischief, her usual good nature now replaced by a playful obstinacy. "Oh, I'm not sure," she replied, "perhaps on my dressing table with my other treasures." Her gaze drifted to the Indian cabinet in her sitting room, its intricately carved wooden surface glinting in the candlelight. "Or maybe I'll put it in the Indian cabinet, so it can shine bright alongside its native companions."

Her mother's expression turned stern, her voice firm but patient. "Rachel, the Indian cabinet has no lock. We can't leave such a valuable gem unprotected." Miss Rachel's face fell, her voice rising in mock horror. "Good heavens, Mamma! Is this a hotel? Do thieves lurk in every corner?"

As the conversation continued, I couldn't help but feel a twinge of amusement at Miss Rachel's fanciful nature. My lady smiled wryly, her eyes flicking towards Mr. Franklin, who sat quietly in the corner, lost in thought. "Why not let me keep the Moonstone for you tonight, dear?" she suggested.

Miss Rachel's response was immediate and adamant. Her mother's proposal was met with the same resistance she might have shown to parting with a treasured toy. My lady sighed inwardly, her thoughts clearly elsewhere. "Come to my room early tomorrow, Rachel. I have something important to discuss with you." With those words, she bid the gentlemen goodnight and withdrew, her expression thoughtful.
