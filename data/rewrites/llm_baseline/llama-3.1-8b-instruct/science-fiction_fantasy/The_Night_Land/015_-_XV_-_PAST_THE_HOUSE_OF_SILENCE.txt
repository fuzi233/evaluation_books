My companion, the Maid, sat at the bow, her strokes strong and steady, while I paddled from behind, our movements harmonious and well-practiced. Time passed almost imperceptibly as we glided along, the sun beating down upon our faces, its warmth seeping into our skin like a comforting caress.

We stopped to rest and refuel at the twelfth hour, and as we ate and drank, the Maid's eyes met mine with a hint of longing, her lips curving into a subtle smile. But she shook her head, her gaze never leaving mine, and I returned to my paddling, content to let the gentle rhythm of the water soothe my thoughts. As the hours passed, our conversation flowed effortlessly, a gentle stream of words that ebbed and flowed like the tides.

We drew in our paddles, and the Maid wrapped a soft cloak around us, transforming it into a makeshift bed. As we settled in for the night, the sound of the waves lulled us into a deep and restful sleep, our dreams infused with the sweet scent of the sea.

Eight hours later, we awoke, our minds foggy from the long rest. But as we looked into each other's eyes, our memories came flooding back, and we laughed together, our joy infectious and unbridled. We washed ourselves in the cool waters of the sea, and then set about preparing our next meal. As we ate and drank, the Maid's hand brushed against mine, sending a spark of electricity through my body.

With renewed energy, we launched back into our paddling routine, our strokes strong and confident.
