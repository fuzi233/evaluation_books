The narrow streets of Florence, a labyrinth of ancient passageways behind the Badia, rarely traversed by strangers unless driven by a singular purpose, much like the one that had brought me to this spot. I had heard whispers of a humble door, inscribed with an inscription that seemed to reverberate with the poetry of the divine – Qui Nacque Il Divino Poeta. It was said that in the era of fierce battles between rival families, these very streets would echo with the shouts and clangs of steel; yet, in the fifteenth century, they were merely alive with the mundane disputes and good-natured jests of woolcarders in the cloth-producing quarters of San Martino and Garbo.

As I stood beneath the Loggia de' Cerchi, the morning light cast long shadows across the worn cobblestones, illuminating the figure of a grey-haired man, Bratti Ferravecchi, who stood scrutinizing me with an air of importance. His rugged features, honed by the fist and polished by the pickaxe, seemed to contradict the gravity etched into his brow and mouth. A large, well-filled bag made of animal hides lay beside him, accompanied by a pedlar's basket, adorned with small trinkets and fragments of glass. His eyes narrowed as he pointed to the gold ring on my finger.

"Ah, young man," he said, his deep voice rumbling like thunder, "when your beard grows thicker, you'll learn to choose your resting spots more wisely, especially with a ring like that on your hand." His words carried a hint of warmth, tempered by a keen business acumen. "I'm not one to pilfer, Bratti Ferravecchi isn't my name. I wouldn't snatch a mouse from under a cat's nose, nor would I covet gain without a fair bargain. You see, three years ago, San Giovanni sent a blind beggar my way, and I found myself with a sum of money that I'd never anticipated. I paid for a mass, and in doing so, I saw it as a fair trade."

As Bratti spoke, I felt a shiver run down my spine, and my gaze dropped to the ring on my finger. With a mix of instinct and caution, I removed it and tucked it safely inside my doublet.
