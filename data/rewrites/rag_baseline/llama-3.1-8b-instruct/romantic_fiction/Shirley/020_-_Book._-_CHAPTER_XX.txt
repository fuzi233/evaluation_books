The scent of morning dew wafted through the rectory, carrying with it the promise of a new day. As the first light of dawn crept through the windows, Shirley and Caroline slipped back inside, their footsteps quiet on the creaky floorboards. The room, though unfamiliar, offered a fleeting sense of refuge, and Shirley sought out her bed, her eyelids heavy with the weight of the previous night's events. Despite the turmoil that had shaken her, her body seemed to have absorbed the turmoil, and she sank into a deep, restorative sleep, her features softening into a serene mask.

Caroline, however, found no solace in slumber. The mundane routines of tea and school, usually enough to keep her awake, were overshadowed by the raw emotions that lingered within her. The terror she had witnessed the night before seared her mind, refusing to yield to the gentle caress of sleep. She settled beside Shirley, her eyes fixed on the gentle rise and fall of her chest, as the June sun climbed higher in the sky.

As the hours ticked by, Caroline's vigil continued, her thoughts wandering through the desolate landscape of her own heart. Her mind, starved of nourishment, struggled to find sustenance in the meager diet of wishes that threatened to consume her. She prayed, her words echoing the familiar cadences of her faith, begging for patience, strength, and relief. Yet, like the winter wind that howled outside, her prayers seemed to be lost in the void, leaving her feeling forsaken and alone.

In those moments, Caroline's faith wavered, and the darkness of despair seemed to close in around her. She felt the sting of reprobation, the weight of a God who had turned His face away. But like the perishing birds that shivered in the cold, she couldn't grasp the promise of spring that lay hidden beneath the icy blast. Still, she clung to love and faith, the fragile threads that bound her to the world. For in the midst of affliction, she remembered the words of comfort: "Whom He loveth, He chasteneth." A truth she held onto, though it seemed a distant memory, a beacon in the darkness.

As the household stirred to life, the shutters creaking open, Caroline rose from her vigil, her spirit revived by the return of day and action. She dressed with care, weaving a veil of normalcy over the turmoil that churned within her.
