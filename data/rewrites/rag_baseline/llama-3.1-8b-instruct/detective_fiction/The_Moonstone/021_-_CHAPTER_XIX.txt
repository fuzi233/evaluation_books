The rain-soaked air clung to me like a damp shroud as I descended the precipitous path, my heart racing with a mix of trepidation and curiosity. The news of Rosanna Spearman's disappearance had spread like wildfire among the household staff, and I had been tasked with retrieving one of her boots for the enigmatic Sergeant Cuff. I found myself pondering the events of the past few hours, the fragments of conversation, and the cryptic message scrawled on the leaf that Sergeant Cuff had left behind – "Send me one of Rosanna Spearman's boots, and be quick about it."

As I hastened towards the shore, the darkness deepened, and the wind howled like a chorus of restless spirits. I spotted Duffy, the young servant, huddled under the lee of the sandhills, his eyes fixed on the turbulent sea. I quickened my pace, my legs burning with a mixture of exertion and anxiety. The waves crashed against the sandbank, sending sprays of water high into the air, as I emerged onto the windswept beach.

Sergeant Cuff stood alone, his figure silhouetted against the driving rain, his eyes fixed intently on some point beyond the horizon. As I approached, he waved his hand towards the north, his voice carrying above the din of the storm. "Keep on that side!" he shouted. "And come on down here to me!" I stumbled towards him, my heart pounding in my chest, as he snatched the boot from my hand and placed it in a footprint on the sand. The mark was still legible, despite the torrential rain, and I watched in horror as the girl's boot fit perfectly into the impression.

Sergeant Cuff's eyes locked onto mine, and I felt a shiver run down my spine. There was something in his gaze that chilled me to the bone – a look of unutterable fear. He pointed to the boot, his finger trembling ever so slightly, as if beckoning me to follow him. Without a word, he began to lead me down the beach, his footsteps steady and deliberate, as we followed the trail of footprints towards the rocky ledge known as the South Spit. The tide was rising, the waters lapping at the hidden face of the Shivering Sand, as we navigated the treacherous terrain. The sergeant's eyes never left the ground, his focus fixed on some point ahead, as if driven by a singular purpose.
