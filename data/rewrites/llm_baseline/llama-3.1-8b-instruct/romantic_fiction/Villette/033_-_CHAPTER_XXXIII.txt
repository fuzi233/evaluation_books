As the first rays of dawn crept over Villette, our small group of twenty boarders and four teachers stirred to life, rising at the ungodly hour of five o'clock to prepare for our expedition with M. le Professeur Emanuel. By six, we had donned our lightest attire, carefully selected to suit the balmy weather: crisp white dresses adorned with intricate lace, and straw hats artfully trimmed to exude a sense of effortless elegance. The air was alive with the sweet melodies of birdsong, and a gentle mist hung over the gardens like a promise of warmth to come.

Our Professor stood waiting for us in the vestibule, resplendent in a youthful belted blouse and straw hat, his usual severity softened by the morning's gentle light. His warm greeting was met with grateful smiles from most of us, and he began to marshal us into line, moving effortlessly along the row to offer words of encouragement and affection to his favourite students. I, however, preferred to keep a discreet distance, partnering with Ginevra Fanshawe instead, who leaned lightly on my arm, her slender form radiating an otherworldly loveliness. Her presence was a burden I gladly bore, but I couldn't help feeling a twinge of anxiety, knowing that my bright pink dress might draw unwanted attention from our Professor.

As we set off into the quiet streets, the boulevards unfolding before us like a tranquil landscape, I attempted to use Ginevra as a buffer between myself and M. Paul, shifting my position to avoid drawing attention to my attire. However, he proved to be too perceptive, and soon discovered that regardless of the arrangement, Miss Fanshawe remained his constant companion. Their relationship had never been smooth, with M. Paul finding her English accent grating, and she perceiving him as bearish and meddling. He had a tendency to become irritable whenever they interacted, and I sensed a growing tension between them as we strolled along.

For a while, my strategy seemed to work, but eventually, M. Paul grew tired of being thwarted and thrust his head forward, his eyes narrowing as he scanned the line, searching for a way to circumvent my attempts at evasion. I knew I couldn't keep this up for long, and wondered what the consequences might be if he finally discovered my ruse.
