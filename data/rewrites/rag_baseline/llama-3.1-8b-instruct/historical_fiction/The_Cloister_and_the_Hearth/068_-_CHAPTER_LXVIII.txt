As I reflect on the tapestry of my life, I realize that the threads of maternal love are woven with equal intensity as those of paternal devotion. Yet, the trials of the feminine heart often remain shrouded in mystery, leaving us male writers to merely hint at the depth of their suffering. It is up to the perceptive reader to unravel the intricacies of Margaret Brandt's journey, which I have merely alluded to until now.

Now, as the pivotal moment draws near, Gerard's absence weighs heavily on her. Though she barely availed herself of Eli's generosity, Margaret's reserve was rooted in a profound unease – an unspoken terror that gripped her at the mere sight of two men within Eli's household. Their countenances seemed to whisper secrets of wickedness and malice, sending shivers coursing through her veins. To avoid confronting these dark energies, she requested that Catherine and her daughter stay with her at home, where she could shield herself from the sinister undercurrents that lurked beneath the surface.

Catherine, a woman of boundless vitality, embodied the unyielding spirit of maternity. As she navigated the labyrinth of her own existence, she had borne numerous children, each one a testament to her unrelenting fertility. Though her daughters-in-law might view her as a formidable presence, Catherine saw herself as a guardian of life, a midwife to the countless young souls entrusted to her care. As she implored Eli to allow her to stay behind in Rotterdam, she seemed to be protecting something precious, a treasure that would soon unfold.

Eli, however, was resolute in his decision, driven by a practicality that left no room for sentiment. "What use are a couple of soldiers?" he exclaimed. "They're nothing but cattle, best sent packing at a time like this." His words, though harsh, brought Margaret a measure of comfort.

In the days leading up to the birth, Margaret's anxiety reached a fever pitch. Her father's frailty and her own poverty, once sources of worry, now served as a bulwark against the uncertainty that loomed ahead. Adversity had honed her resilience, teaching her to navigate the treacherous tides of life with a fierce determination. Catherine's counsel had been instrumental in shaping her resolve, urging her to persevere through the hardships that threatened to overwhelm her.
