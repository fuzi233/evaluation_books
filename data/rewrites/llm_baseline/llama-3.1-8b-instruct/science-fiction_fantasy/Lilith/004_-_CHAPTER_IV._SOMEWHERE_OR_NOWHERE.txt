As I stood at the breakfast table, the morning sunlight streaming through the windows cast an ethereal glow on the intricate patterns etched onto my grandmother's antique porcelain. I glanced at the milky sapphire ring on my finger, its gemstone veiled in a soft haze, and wondered if the star within would shine brightly today. Rising from my chair, I walked to the window to observe the stone, the gentle morning dew still glistening on the lawn outside. The previous night's rain had left a trail of tiny rivulets, and on the grass, a thrush struggled to free itself from the shell of a snail, its tiny beak poked out, futilely attempting to break free.

As I rotated my ring, attempting to discern the clarity of the star's light, a piercing gaze met mine through the milky blue mist. The sudden appearance startled me, causing me to drop the ring. When I retrieved it, the raven's watchful eye was gone, replaced by the dull gleam of the morning light. The sky, once a brilliant blue, began to darken, shrouded by a thick, grey mist. A gust of wind swept through the garden, rustling the leaves of the nearby trees, and in an instant, a flash of lightning illuminated the sky, accompanied by a low, rumbling thunderclap. The rain poured down in torrents, drumming against the windows and creating a soothing melody that seemed to lull the world into a peaceful slumber.

I stood transfixed, watching the rain, when a sleek raven emerged from the downpour, its black feathers glistening with raindrops. With deliberate, measured steps, it approached the house, ignoring the deluge. My heart skipped a beat as I recognized the raven's familiar, knowing glint in its eye. I breathed a silent sigh of relief, grateful to be indoors, yet a nagging sense of foreboding lingered within me.

The raven drew closer, its eyes fixed intently on me, before executing a low bow and leaping onto the windowsill. It stepped across the ledge, landing with ease inside the room, and began to walk towards the door. I trailed behind, unsure of what lay ahead, but resolved to keep my distance if it ventured upstairs.
