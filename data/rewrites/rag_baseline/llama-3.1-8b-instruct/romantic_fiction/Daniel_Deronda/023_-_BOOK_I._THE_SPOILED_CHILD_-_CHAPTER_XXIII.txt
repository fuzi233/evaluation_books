The morning sunlight seeped through the leaded glass panes of the library, casting intricate patterns on the polished floorboards as it danced across the room. In this tranquil scene, Gwendolen Harleth stood poised, her slender fingers moving with deliberate precision as she sorted her collection of books and sheet music on the piano. The faint scent of old leather and wood polish hung in the air, mingling with the soft hum of the household, a soothing background melody to the quiet morning.

As she worked, Gwendolen's gaze drifted to the glass panel that reflected her image, her dark hair and pale skin set off against the rich tones of the furniture. For a moment, she forgot the gentle rustle of the wind outside, the distant calls of birds, and the soft ticking of the clock on the mantle. Her thoughts were consumed by the memory of Herr Klesmer, his words still echoing in her mind like the sweet, mournful notes of a Chopin nocturne. She recalled the way he had looked at her, his eyes drinking in every detail of her pose, every nuance of her expression. A flutter began to stir within her, a sense of vulnerability she had never before acknowledged, a feeling that she needed someone else's opinion to validate her own sense of self.

As she pondered, the sound of wheels crunching on gravel and the soft thud of footsteps signaled the arrival of the family. Gwendolen's mother, Mrs. Davilow, entered the room, a look of concern etched on her face. "Pray, dear, shall I go to church? You seem to be quite engrossed."

Gwendolen turned, a hint of impatience creeping into her voice. "Yes, mamma, please do. I'd rather be alone with Herr Klesmer." She had written to him, inviting him to join her at eleven, and she couldn't wait to see him again.

Her mother hesitated, her eyes searching Gwendolen's face. "But surely, dear, it would be more proper if Isabel joined us. She could sit quietly in the corner and read."

Gwendolen waved her hand dismissively. "No, no, mamma. She would only fidget and annoy me. I'd much rather be alone."
