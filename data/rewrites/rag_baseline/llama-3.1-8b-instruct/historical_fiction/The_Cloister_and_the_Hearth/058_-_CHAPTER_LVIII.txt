As Gerard strolled alongside Teresa, the rhythmic cadence of their footsteps echoed through the narrow streets of Rome, each step a deliberate dance through the labyrinthine city. He pondered the enigmatic Teresa, her intentions veiled behind an impenetrable mask of serenity, much like the intricate patterns woven into the fabric of a fine silk tapestry. Her gaze drifted toward him, and he sensed a fleeting glimmer of curiosity, a spark that kindled a flame of intrigue within him.

"Madama," Teresa said finally, breaking the silence, "a friend informed me of a working goldsmith's wife who seeks a writer. 'Tis a modest abode, not far from here." Gerard's ears perked up, attuning to the subtle melody of her words, as she led him toward the goldsmith's residence.

Upon arrival, they found themselves standing before a sturdy door adorned with the symbol of the goldsmith's craft – a hammer and anvil emblazoned upon a shield. Teresa knocked, and the door creaked open to reveal a woman of imposing stature, her features etched with a mixture of warmth and wariness. "Madama," Teresa began, "Leonora tells me you require a writer... I've brought one to your attention, a young man who saved a child at sea."

The goldsmith's wife, Olympia, gazed upon Gerard with a mixture of fascination and hesitation, her eyes lingering on his face as if searching for a hidden truth. "Ah, nay," she said finally, "I have no use for a writer. Claelia, the sausage-maker, was the one who sought one, and I recall mentioning it to Leonora." Teresa's expression remained serene, yet a hint of disappointment flickered across her face.

Undeterred, they continued their journey to Claelia's humble abode, situated at a respectable distance from the goldsmith's residence. Upon arrival, they found Claelia out, but Teresa insisted on waiting for her return. As they sat in silence, Gerard couldn't help but notice the intricate patterns etched onto the walls, a testament to Claelia's craftsmanship. When Claelia finally returned, she greeted them warmly and, with a gentle smile, led Gerard forward, introducing him to her husband.
