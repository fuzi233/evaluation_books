Thinking that it was high time to drag the Monarch down from his ecstatic reverie to the grimy floor of common sense, I resolved to attempt a revelation of the truth—that is, the true nature of existence within Flatland. Thus, I commenced my discourse: "Your Royal Highness, by what mechanism do you distinguish the shapes and positions of your subjects? By my own observation prior to entering your Kingdom, utilizing the sense of sight alone, I noted that your populace consists of Lines and Points, varying greatly in magnitude—"

"You speak of an absolute impossibility," the King interjected sharply. "You must be suffering under a delusion. To differentiate a Line from a Point via visual perception is, according to the immutable laws of our nature, utterly futile. Yet, this distinction is readily ascertainable through the auditory sense. Through sound, my precise shape is revealed to me without fail. Observe me—I am a Line, the supreme entity in Lineland, stretching over six inches of Space—"

"Of *Length*," I ventured cautiously.

"Fool!" he barked. "Space *is* Length. Cease your interruptions immediately, or I shall end this audience."

Apologizing profusely, I pressed on, knowing irritation was the only path forward. "Since you remain impervious to logic, allow me to demonstrate via your own preferred organ: hearing. Consider my two wives, currently situated six thousand miles, seventy yards, two feet, and eight inches apart—one to the North, the other to the South. Listen."

He emitted a sharp chirp, then resumed his lecture with smug satisfaction. "My wives, receiving the first vocalization followed closely by its echo, perceive a temporal delay during which sound traverses precisely 6.457 inches. From this interval, they deduce that one mouth lies 6.457 inches farther from them than the other, thereby calculating my exact shape. Naturally, they perform this arithmetic only once, prior to our nuptials, though the capability remains ever-present. Similarly, I determine the dimensions of any male subject solely through acoustic triangulation."

"But how," I challenged, "if a Man mimics a Woman's tone with one voice, or masks his southern resonance so effectively that it passes unrecognized as an echo of the north? Might such deceit not wreak havoc? Furthermore, lack of verification mechanisms seems glaring. Could you not command neighboring subjects to physically verify one another?"

This query was, admittedly, foolish, for tactile sensation holds no utility in a one-dimensional realm; yet I posed it deliberately to provoke the Monarch, succeeding beyond my wildest hopes.

"What!" he shrieked in genuine horror. "Explain your intent!"

"Feel. Touch. Make physical contact," I replied.

"If by 'FEELING' you mean approaching so intimately that no spatial gap remains between two entities, know, Stranger, that this offense carries the penalty of death in my dominions. The rationale is self-evident: the fragile constitution of a Woman, susceptible to shattering under such extreme compression, demands state protection. Since Women are visually indistinguishable from Men, the Law universally decrees that neither shall approach the other closely enough to obliterate the intervening space."

"And what conceivable utility," the King scoffed, "could possibly arise from this illegal, unnatural excess of approximation you term 'touching,' when all objectives of such a crude process are achieved instantly, accurately, and effortlessly through the auditory sense? Regarding your alleged danger of deception—it is nonexistent. The Voice is the very essence of one's Being; it cannot be altered at will. Come, suppose I possessed the ability to pass through solid matter, penetrating my subjects sequentially, numbering in the billions, verifying each size and distance via the clumsy, inaccurate method of 'feeling.' How much time and energy would be squandered on such inefficiency! Conversely, in a single moment of audition, I conduct a comprehensive census and statistical survey—local, corporeal, mental, and spiritual—of every living soul in Lineland. Hark! Only hark!"

As he spoke, he paused, listening with rapturous ecstasy to a sound that appeared to me merely as the incessant chirping of countless Lilliputian grasshoppers.

"Truly," I conceded, "your auditory sense compensates admirably for your visual deficits. However, permit me to observe that your existence in Lineland must be profoundly dreary. To behold nothing but a Point! To be unable even to contemplate a Straight Line! Worse still, to possess no concept whatsoever of what a Straight Line entails! It would be preferable to possess no sense of sight at all than to see so little! I admit I lack your discriminative faculty of hearing; the grand symphony of Lineland, which brings you such intense joy, registers to me only as a chaotic, multitudinous twittering. Yet, I retain the capacity to discern a Line from a Point. Allow me to prove it."

"Just prior to entering your kingdom, I observed you dancing laterally—from left to right, then reversing from right to left—with seven Men and a Woman positioned immediately to your left, and eight Men alongside two Women to your right. Is this accurate?"

"It is correct," acknowledged the King, "regarding the counts and sexes, though I confess ignorance of your terminology 'right' and 'left'."

"I deny that you perceived these details," I countered. "For how could you see the interior of any Man? You must have heard these reports and subsequently dreamt them into sight. Moreover, pray tell, what do you mean by 'left' and 'right'? I presume it is merely your dialect for 'Northward' and 'Southward'?"

"Not so," I replied. "Beyond your linear motions of North and South, there exists another dimension of movement I designate as 'from right to left'."

"KING: Exhibit to me, if you will, this motion from left to right."

"I cannot," I stated firmly, "unless you are willing to step entirely out of your Line."

"Out of my Line?" the King demanded, his voice rising. "Do you mean out of the world? Out of Space itself?"

"Well, yes," I confirmed. "Out of *YOUR* World. Out of *YOUR* Space. Your Space is not the true Space. True Space is a Plane; your Space is merely a Line."

"If you cannot demonstrate this lateral motion by moving within it yourself," the King pleaded, "then I beseech you to describe it verbally."

"If you cannot distinguish your right side from your left, I fear no verbal explanation can clarify my meaning to you. Surely, you are not ignorant of such a fundamental distinction?"

"I comprehend you not in the slightest," the King admitted.

"Alas! How shall I render this clear?" I lamented. "When you advance straight ahead, does it not occasionally strike you that you *could* move differently? Turning your attention sideways to gaze in the direction your flank currently faces? In other words, instead of perpetually moving toward one of your extremities, do you never feel an urge to move in the direction of your side?"

"Never," the King declared. "And what do you mean? How can a man's interior 'face' in any direction? Or how can a man move in the direction of his inside?"

"Well then, since language fails to elucidate the matter, I shall resort to deed. I will gradually withdraw my body from Lineland to illustrate the direction I seek to convey."

At my command, I began to extrude my form out of Lineland. As long as any portion of my body remained within his dominion and visible to his singular line-of-sight, the King exclaimed repeatedly, "I see you! I see you still! You are not moving!"

But once I had fully withdrawn myself from his Line, he screamed in his highest, shrillest pitch, "She is vanished! She is dead!"

"I am not dead," I replied calmly. "I am simply out of Lineland—that is, out of the Straight Line you erroneously call Space—and residing in the True Space, wherein I perceive reality as it truly is. At this very moment, I can see your Line, your side—or whatever interior you choose to call it—and I can also see the Men and Women positioned North and South of you. I shall now enumerate them, describing their sequence, stature, and the intervals separating each."
