The flickering candlelight danced across the worn stone walls of the Cathedral Close, casting eerie shadows on the pavement. Leaves, scattered by the previous day's rain, rustled softly beneath the feet of two men walking together. They moved in tandem, their footsteps echoing off the ancient buildings. The Dean and Tope paused before the entrance, where a low arched door seemed to swallow the fading light.

Tope shifted his weight, his eyes darting to the Dean before returning to the ground. "Jasper, sir?" he ventured, his voice barely above a whisper.

"Yes, Tope," the Dean replied, his gaze fixed on the door.

"He stayed late," Tope continued, "and was... taken poorly."

The Dean's eyes narrowed. "Taken poorly?"

Tope hesitated, glancing at the Dean's assistant, Mr. Crisparkle, who stood a few paces behind them. "B-breathed short," Tope stammered, before correcting himself under Crisparkle's silent guidance. "Breathed to that extent, sir, would be more proper."

The Dean nodded, a hint of a smile playing on his lips. "Yes, Tope, breathed to that extent would be better."

Tope cleared his throat, his words tumbling out in a hesitant rush. "When he came in, his breathing was so... short, sir. Distressed him to get his notes out, which might've caused a kind of... fit. Memory grew DAZED, sir. And a dimness and giddiness crept over him, strange as I've seen. But a little time and water brought him out of his DAZE."

Crisparkle's eyes met the Dean's, a fleeting glance that spoke volumes. The Dean turned back to Tope. "And Jasper has gone home quite himself now, has he?"

Tope nodded, his eyes drifting towards the old stone gatehouse. "Gone home, sir. Fire's lit, and it's chilly after the rain. Cathedral feels damp today, and he was shivery."

As the Cathedral bell tolled the hour, the trio fell silent, their eyes drawn to the warm glow emanating from the gatehouse window. The leaves outside seemed to stir, as if responding to the sound, and the wind rustled through the ivy, creating an unsettling melody that hung in the air.
