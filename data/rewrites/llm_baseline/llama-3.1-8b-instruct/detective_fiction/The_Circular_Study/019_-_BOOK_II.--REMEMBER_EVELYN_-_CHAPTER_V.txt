The memory of that day still lingered within me, a testament to the enigmatic events that had unfolded at Felix's estate. As I recalled, I had promised my young wife, Eva, that we would visit my brother on the appointed day and hour. We walked there on foot, the distance being relatively short from our hotel. An elderly servant, known to be both deaf and mute, greeted us at the entrance, and as I caught sight of him and the grand, dimly lit hall, Eva's countenance paled. Yet, I attempted to reassure her with a forced smile, which belied the trepidation I felt myself. Taking her hand, I followed the servant down the hallway, past the open drawing room, where I had half-expected we might pause, into a room that seemed almost austere compared to the opulent decor of the rest of the house.

It was here that I had first laid eyes on my brother, Bartow, as he was later known. His study was a sight to behold, with its walls adorned by ancient artifacts and dusty tomes that seemed to whisper secrets to one another. Felix, as I had called him, stood amidst this sea of relics, his presence radiating an aura of quiet power that left me, and indeed Eva, in awe. Her eyes widened as she beheld him, and I could sense her fear mingling with curiosity. Though I had spoken little of my brother to her, I had not prepared her for the striking visage that now stood before her – a man of imposing stature and unyielding character.

As I introduced us, Felix's pale features seemed to grow even paler beneath the flickering light of the lantern above us. His greeting was stilted, his words barely above a whisper, and his hand, raised in salutation, dropped heavily back onto the table. Yet, his gaze remained fixed intently upon Eva's face, betraying a depth of emotion that I knew her to be unaware of. That look spoke volumes of the turmoil brewing within him, a turmoil that would soon have far-reaching consequences for her very existence.

My own understanding of the situation was complicated, and as I cast a beseeching glance at Felix, I requested a brief moment alone with him to discuss matters of import. He acquiesced, his expression a mask of sarcasm that left me feeling momentarily diminished.
