> "Be patient, for the world is broad and wide."
>
> That old Flatland proverb never once stirred any deeper thought in me for decades of my ordinary, two-dimensional life—until the day my eyes were opened to realities that stretched far past the planar bounds I had always mistaken for the sum of all existence. This memoir, written by a humble, law-abiding middle-class Square, is split into two clear parts, so that any reader from any plane of existence might follow my journey from unthinking familiarity with my own world to the dizzying, humbling revelation of dimensions I never could have imagined were possible.
>
> ---
> ### Part I: This World
> The first half of my account is dedicated entirely to the laws, customs, peoples, and hidden, suppressed history of Flatland itself, for no outsider could possibly intuit how our two-dimensional lives unfold without careful, step-by-step explanation:
> 1.  *Of the Nature of Flatland*: The foundational physical rules that govern our realm, with no concept of height or depth, only infinite length and breadth stretched out before all who dwell here.
> 2.  *Of the Climate and Houses in Flatland*: How weather functions on a two-dimensional plane, and the strict, elegant architectural rules we follow to keep our households safe and orderly.
> 3.  *Concerning the Inhabitants of Flatland*: The rigid class system that defines our society, where the number of your sides marks your intelligence, your social status, and your prescribed place in our world.
> 4.  *Concerning the Women*: The singular, razor-sharp line-shaped women of Flatland, whose very form demands strict public safety laws and carefully enforced social customs to prevent accidental harm to our triangular, polygonal citizenry.
> 5.  *Of our Methods of Recognizing one another*: The clever, millennia-honed tricks of touch, sound, and subtle deduction that we use to tell one shape apart from another, even when all any Flatlander can ever see of a peer is a straight, uniform line.
> 6.  *Of Recognition by Sight*: The rare, refined skill reserved for our upper classes, of piercing the thin perpetual fog that hangs over Flatland to identify strangers by tiny, almost imperceptible differences in the shading of their lines.
> 7.  *Concerning Irregular Figures*: The great unspoken social taboo of lopsided, asymmetric shapes, and the rigid state policies that frame any imperfection in a citizen's sides as an existential danger to the entire social order.
> 8.  *Of the Ancient Practice of Painting*: The half-forgotten old custom of citizens dyeing the edges of their own sides to make their shape instantly identifiable at a glance, no touch or sound test required.
> 9.  *Of the Universal Colour Bill*: The populist political movement that almost swept Flatland generations ago, to make painting all citizens mandatory and upend the entire long-standing class system overnight.
> 10. *Of the Suppression of the Chromatic Sedition*: The bloody, decisive counter-coup led by our ruling priestly caste that crushed the Colour Bill movement for good, and erased much of the old painting custom from public memory.
> 11. *Concerning our Priests*: The near-perfect Circles who rule Flatland, revered as the wisest of all living beings, the custodians of all our laws and sacred traditions.
> 12. *Of the Doctrine of our Priests*: The carefully guarded official dogma that the Circles enforce across all of Flatland, to keep social order steady and ensure no citizen ever questions the unchallenged "fact" that only two dimensions can possibly exist.
>
> ---
> ### Part II: Other Worlds
> It was in my quiet old age that the unthinkable happened, and my entire view of reality shattered. The second half of this record lays out that impossible, life-altering journey, no part of which I would have once believed possible myself:
> 13. *How I had a Vision of Lineland*: The first strange dream that showed me a realm even narrower than my own: a one-dimensional world of lines and points, trapped on a single narrow glowing thread of existence.
> 14. *How I vainly tried to explain the nature of Flatland*: My desperate, futile attempt to make the king of that tiny Lineland understand the possibility of a second dimension, a reality he could never perceive with his limited senses.
> 15. *Concerning a Stranger from Spaceland*: The day a glowing, shape-shifting visitor materialized right inside my locked house, a being no Flatland natural law could ever account for.
> 16. *How the Stranger vainly endeavoured to reveal to me in words the mysteries of Spaceland*: The Sphere's long, frustrating attempts to describe three full dimensions to me, a man who had never once seen or conceived of height in my entire life.
> 17. *How the Sphere, having in vain tried words, resorted to deeds*: When language failed, my visitor used magic I could not explain to prove his claims, pulling objects right out of sealed cabinets and revealing secrets I had hidden in my own home no other Flatlander could ever know of.
> 18. *How I came to Spaceland, and what I saw there*: The day I was lifted right out of Flatland entirely, to float in three-dimensional space, and see my entire planar world spread out beneath me like a printed sheet of paper.
> 19. *How, though the Sphere shewed me other mysteries of Spaceland, I still desired more; and what came of it*: The awe I felt exploring three dimensions, and the heretical question I dared to ask, of a fourth dimension, a fifth, and endless more beyond that.
> 20. *How the Sphere encouraged me in a Vision*: The dream the Sphere granted me, to see an endless hierarchy of worlds, from the zero-dimensional void of Pointland, to Lineland, to Flatland, to Spaceland, and the faint, impossible hints of infinite higher realms beyond.
> 21. *How I tried to teach the Theory of Three Dimensions to my Grandson, and with what success*: My return to Flatland, and my first attempt to share the truth of the third dimension with my own bright little grandson, with results that broke my heart.
> 22. *How I then tried to diffuse the Theory of Three Dimensions by other means, and of the result*: My final, fateful attempts to spread the forbidden truth of higher dimensions across all of Flatland, and the fate that has left me writing this secret record, hidden away from the eyes of the Circles who rule us.
