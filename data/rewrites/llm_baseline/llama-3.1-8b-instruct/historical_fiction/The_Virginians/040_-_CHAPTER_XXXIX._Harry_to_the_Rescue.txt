It was a crisp Saturday morning in August 1756 when Mr. Warrington penned a letter from Tunbridge Wells, the ink still wet on the page as he wrote the words "My dear Lord March." With a sense of satisfaction, he informed his lordship that he had secured the three horses he sought, having driven them himself at a brisk pace, accompanied by his postilion and Mrs. Betty, who rode in comfort alongside him. They had made excellent time, arriving at Tunbridge Wells at eleven o'clock, where Mr. Warrington attended to his business.

Meanwhile, Lady Maria Esmond, confined to her room by Madame Bernstein's tyranny, waited anxiously for news of her freedom. Little did she expect that her trusted emissary, Mrs. Betty, would return with such haste, carrying a message of liberation. As the clock struck midnight, Lady Maria and the chaplain played cards by candlelight, their game interrupted by the sound of wheels whirring outside. The carriage drew to a halt, and a brief parley ensued before Mrs. Betty appeared at the gate, her face aglow with joy, though her eyes betrayed a deep sadness. Following close behind her, a tall young figure emerged, his features illuminated by the moonlight.

Lady Maria's heart skipped a beat as she beheld her cousin, Harry, striding towards her with purposeful steps. The chaplain, overcome with emotion, seized Harry's hand, tears streaming down his face as he implored God to bless his noble patron. Mr. Warrington, too, felt a surge of pleasure, his heart swelling with pride and generosity. As he walked alongside Lady Maria, who leaned upon his arm, the weight of her gratitude and affection hung in the air like a gentle melody.

As they strolled through the quiet streets, the sounds of the town faded into the background, replaced by the sweet music of their conversation. Lady Maria poured out her thanks, her words tumbling forth like a gentle brook, while Harry listened attentively, his eyes shining with warmth and understanding. The night air vibrated with the tender emotions that passed between them, a symphony of love and loyalty that only they could hear. Together, they walked, their footsteps echoing through the stillness, as the world around them melted away, leaving only the gentle lapping of their words against the shore of their hearts.
