As the warm glow of dusk settled over the hotel, Trent's voice echoed through the sitting-room, beckoning Mr. Cupples to enter. The earlier events of the day still lingered in the air, a somber mood cast by the coroner's jury verdict – a denunciation of an unknown individual or individuals. Trent, absorbed in his work, barely acknowledged Mr. Cupples' arrival, his gaze fixed intently on a photographic negative suspended in a metal dish, bathed in the soft light of the window.

"Please, take a seat," Trent invited, his movements betraying a hint of restlessness. "Those chairs, you see, were acquired during my travels, remnants of the Holy Inquisition's suppression in Spain." His eyes never left the negative as he spoke, his words laced with a mixture of distraction and fascination. "This, on the other hand, is a promising image – washed just enough to reveal its hidden details. Let us allow it to dry and clear the workspace of these... distractions."

As Trent methodically rearranged the cluttered table, Mr. Cupples began to explore the assortment of objects, his curiosity piqued. "That, I believe, is a hypo-eliminator," Trent explained, nodding towards a bottle, "a useful tool for speeding up the development process. Though, I must caution against its use; it eliminates more than just sodium hypophosphite, if you catch my drift." A faint smile played on Trent's lips as he carefully placed the final item on the crowded mantelpiece.

"The beauty of a hotel sitting-room lies in its ability to shield one from the ephemeral allurements of the world outside," Trent mused, settling beside Mr. Cupples on the sofa. "It is a space for the mind, not the mayfly indulgences of the soul. Have you ever found yourself in this very room, Cupples? I, myself, have lost count of the number of times I've occupied this space, tracing the path of my wanderings across England. Observe the tablecloth; that's where I spilled ink during my stay in Halifax. And the hole in the carpet, that's from my time in Ipswich. Even the glass above 'Silent Sympathy' bears the scars of my... creative outbursts. This room has been my sanctuary, where I find inspiration. Just today, in fact, I've made significant progress on several negatives – there's an excellent darkroom downstairs."
