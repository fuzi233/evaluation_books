The scent of damp earth and damp wood wafted through the air as I followed the spotted leopardess up the winding path to the ancient palace. The flickering torches cast eerie shadows on the stone walls, and I caught glimpses of the princess as she vanished into the darkness. When I reached the entrance-hall, she stood poised, a fleeting expression of irritation crossing her face as she hastily wrapped a silk robe around her shoulders. Her wounds, though minor, told a different story – one of desperation and cunning.

As our eyes met, she flashed a forced smile, her words tumbling forth in a practiced cadence. "I've encountered a slight mishap. The cat-woman has returned, and I sought to send her away. However, she brought one of her... associates, and it attacked me before I could defend myself." A shiver ran down her spine, and I felt a pang of empathy, despite knowing she was not entirely truthful.

My gaze drifted to the crimson stains on her dress, a testament to her recent ordeal. I recalled the way she'd fought in the cave, her ferocity tempered by a hint of vulnerability. My heart ached with regret for not intervening sooner. The princess's eyes narrowed, her gaze piercing mine as she spoke. "Foolish child, don't cry. Wait for me here; I'll retrieve something for my scratches."

I hesitated, torn between loyalty and trepidation. As I watched her disappear into the black hall, I sensed a low hum of activity, like the gentle buzzing of a thousand insects. The room began to shift and rearrrange itself, as if the very fabric of reality was being rewoven. I had witnessed this phenomenon before – in the forest-hall, during the evil wood's dark rituals – and I recognized the princess's presence amidst the chaos.

Group after group of figures materialized, only to fade back into the shadows as the light danced across the room. Some scenes were familiar, others a jumbled mix of memory and half-remembered nightmare. The princess stood motionless, her face a mask of calm, even as the world around her churned with life. I felt a growing sense of unease, my feet moving of their own accord as I trailed behind her.
