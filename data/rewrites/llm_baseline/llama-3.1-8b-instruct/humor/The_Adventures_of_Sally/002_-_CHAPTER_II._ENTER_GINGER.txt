The atmosphere was a kaleidoscope of color, with parasols dotting the landscape like flowers, and dogs roaming freely, their tails wagging in joyful abandon. A poodle, its fur a rich, chestnut brown, wandered over to Sally, its eyes fixed on the box of sweets in her hand. The dog's decision to stay was likely motivated by the promise of treats, and Sally smiled, watching it sit patiently beside her.

Despite the tranquility of the scene, Sally's mind was preoccupied with thoughts of departure. Her vacation, which had begun with such promise, was drawing to a close. She had spent the past month drifting from one charming French town to another, but Roville's unique blend of blue skies, elegant casinos, and snow-white hotels had finally captured her heart. However, the call of home was growing louder, and she knew she had to leave tomorrow. A letter from Gerald had arrived, informing her that "The Primrose Way" was scheduled to premiere in Detroit, and she couldn't miss the opportunity to witness the opening night.
