The scent of freshly baked bread wafted through the air as Edward stepped into the warm sunlight of the Barton kitchen. The sound of pots clinking and gentle chatter filled the room, a soothing accompaniment to the rhythmic thud of the bread dough being kneaded on the wooden counter. It was a moment of serenity, one that Edward savored as he made his way towards Elinor, who stood poised by the hearth, her eyes fixed on the simmering stew.

As he approached, Edward's thoughts turned to the reason for his visit – to ask Elinor to join him in marriage. Though he had asked many women before, this moment felt different. Perhaps it was the warmth of the kitchen, the comforting aromas that filled the air, or the gentle hum of domesticity that surrounded him. Whatever the reason, Edward felt a sense of trepidation wash over him, a feeling he couldn't quite shake.

After four years of living under the weight of an unwise engagement, Edward had finally broken free. His errand at Barton was a straightforward one – to secure Elinor's hand in marriage. As he stood before her, his heart swelled with a mix of emotions: excitement, gratitude, and a deep-seated longing.

As they sat down to table at four o'clock, the sun casting a warm glow over the room, Edward's anxiety gave way to joy. Elinor's mother, Mrs. Dashwood, smiled warmly as she nodded her approval, and Edward's heart swelled with happiness. He had left behind the weight of his past, the uncertainty and heartache that had defined him for so long.
