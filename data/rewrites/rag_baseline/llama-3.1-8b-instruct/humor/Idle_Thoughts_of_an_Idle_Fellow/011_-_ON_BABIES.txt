As the last wisps of morning fog lifted off the cobblestone streets of Mayfair, Lady Violet Lebas settled into her favorite armchair by the window, cradling a steaming cup of tea in her hands. The warm sunlight streaming through the panes danced across the intricate patterns etched into the walls, casting a mesmerizing rhythm that seemed to mirror the beating of her own heart. She pondered the curious notion that had been plaguing her thoughts since dawn – the enigmatic world of infants, wrapped in layers of lace and silk, their tiny forms swaddled in an excess of fabric that seemed almost an affront to their nascent dignity.

Her musings were interrupted by the soft knock at the door, signaling the arrival of her trusted nurse, Mrs. Fletcher. The woman entered with a gentle smile, bearing a small, bundled package in her arms. As she placed the infant into Lady Violet's waiting arms, the child's eyes fluttered open, and for a moment, their gazes locked in a fleeting understanding that transcended words. Lady Violet felt an inexplicable pang of connection, as if the tiny being had pierced the veil of her reserve, laying bare the tender spots within her.

Mrs. Fletcher's eyes sparkled with amusement as she observed Lady Violet's reaction. "A bundle of joy, milady," she said, her voice low and soothing, "and a fine specimen of humanity, if I may say so myself." Lady Violet's gaze wandered to the infant's delicate features, searching for a glimpse of the unknown, and her thoughts drifted back to the peculiarities of infancy – the conundrum of clothing, the mystery of the sexes, and the societal expectations that shrouded these fragile lives.

As she gazed into the child's face, Lady Violet found herself pondering the age-old riddle: why did infants wear such an abundance of garments, each layer a testament to the whims of fashion and the insecurities of their caregivers? She recalled a conversation with a fellow acquaintance, who had attributed the phenomenon to the desire to conceal the child's true proportions, to create an illusion of grandeur where none existed. But Lady Violet suspected there was more to it than that – a deeper desire to shield the vulnerable from the harsh realities of the world, to wrap them in a cocoon of protection that would soon be torn away, leaving them exposed to the unforgiving forces of existence.
