As I set out with Fritz on the following day, our objective was to retrieve the sledge, the repository of our handcrafted vessels and containers, from the Calabash Wood. We chose to proceed with a minimal entourage, as I had deemed it prudent to divide our attention between the task at hand and a forthcoming expedition into the uncharted territories beyond the chain of rocky hills. Fritz, however, remained with his mother, content to oversee their domestic endeavors while we ventured forth.

Our traverse through the evergreen oak grove revealed a sow, seemingly unscathed by our previous encounter with the feral canines, now feeding on the abundant acorns with an air of placidity. The birds inhabiting this woodland, too, seemed unperturbed by our presence, their chirping and fluttering a testament to their tranquil existence. Fritz, however, couldn't resist the urge to discharge his firearm, striking down a vibrant blue jay and a pair of parakeets – one resplendent in scarlet, the other in green and gold hues.

As Fritz reloaded his rifle, an enigmatic noise reverberated through the air, sending us into a state of heightened vigilance. The low-pitched thudding resembled the muffled beat of a drum, evoking the possibility of indigenous inhabitants in the vicinity. With caution, we drew closer to the source, concealing ourselves amidst the underbrush until we reached an open clearing. There, perched atop a weathered log, was a magnificent bird – its rich chestnut-brown plumage mottled with dark brown and grey, adorned with velvety black tufts on its shoulders, glistening with emerald sheen. As it preened and strutted about, its wings beating in a staccato rhythm, it created a mesmerizing spectacle.

The surrounding birds, similarly attired, watched with rapt attention, their assembly a testament to the drummer's virtuosity. As the performance reached its crescendo, the bird's wings generated a low-frequency rumble, its whirring creating a whirlwind around it. Our observation was abruptly interrupted by a gunshot, and the drama came to a tragic end. My impulsive son had inadvertently silenced the captivating display, and the gathered audience dispersed in disarray.
