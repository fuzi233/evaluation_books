In the vibrant dawn of Tergou, a tapestry of colors burst forth as horses' hooves pounded the cobblestone streets, their rhythmic cadence weaving an infectious energy through the crowds. Feathers danced on the breeze, and the windows of the townhouses were adorned with faces that seemed to drink in the spectacle. Amidst the festive atmosphere, the French ambassador rode through, accompanied by his retinue, as well as several attendants lent by the Duke of Burgundy to honor his visit and cater to his pleasures. The Duke's tumbler, a dignified figure with an air of gravity, led the procession, his stately demeanor elevating the surrounding nobles to appear almost carefree. Yet, whenever respect and awe threatened to become oppressive, the tumbler would unexpectedly dismount with a comical clumsiness that even the ambassador couldn't resist, bursting into laughter. His antics were a clever display of jest, and as he climbed back onto his horse by grasping the tail, the crowd couldn't help but be charmed by his antics.

At the rear of the procession, a figure drew attention like a magnet – the Duke's majestic leopard. A skilled huntsman, perched atop a Flemish horse of imposing stature, guided the box where the leopard sat crouched, her glossy fur and spots captivating the onlookers. As the people pressed closer, some were tempted to touch her, and the huntsman's voice boomed out, a stern warning: "Beware! In Antwerp, a careless gesture was met with the Duke's wrath – a man was imprisoned, his fate sealed in a dungeon below ground, where the rats devoured his flesh in a single night."

The crowd murmured in a mix of fascination and fear, but their spirits were lifted by the arrival of the Duke's giant, a towering Hungarian standing at an astonishing seven feet four inches tall. His voice, a treble flute-like sound with little resonance, was a trait he was unaware of, and it often led to amusing situations. On this particular day, his attention was drawn to Giles, perched atop a balcony, and he decided to play a prank on the diminutive figure. "Hallo, brother!" he squeaked, offering Giles a massive fist to help him down. "You're easy to spot," Giles retorted in his deep, resonant voice, his tone laced with a hint of annoyance.
