As I stood in the salle-à-manger, Madame Beck's voice called out to me on a sweltering Thursday afternoon, her tone laced with a sense of urgency that belied the leisurely pace of the day. "Meess Lucie, I have one more commission for you, if you wouldn't mind taking it on," she said, her words tumbling out in a rush. I hastened to assure her that I was free, and she handed me a list of the finest wools, silks, and embroidery threads required for the pupils' projects.

With my mission clearly defined, I donned a suitable outfit to brave the cloudy skies, only to be recalled by Madame's voice once more. "Wait, Meess Lucie! I almost forgot something." She vanished into the little salon, returning with a beautiful wicker basket overflowing with succulent, hothouse fruits. The vibrant colors of the fruit were set against a backdrop of delicate, exotic leaves and pale yellow blooms, their sweet fragrance wafting through the air. "Take this, it's not too heavy, and won't sully your neat attire," she said with a smile. "Deliver it to Madame Walravens, who resides at Numéro 3, Rue des Mages. I fear the walk will be lengthy, but you have the entire afternoon ahead of you."

As I departed, the task of selecting silks and wools for the pupils' projects proved a laborious one, but eventually, I completed my list. The patterns for the slippers, bell-ropes, and cabas were all settled upon, and I felt a sense of relief wash over me as the "tripotage" was finally off my mind. The only remaining tasks were to deliver the basket of fruit and convey Madame's felicitations to Madame Walravens.

I found myself looking forward to the stroll through the ancient Basse-Ville, its narrow streets and alleys a world away from the bustling commerce of the city. As the sky darkened, casting a deep blue-black hue over the rooftops, I felt a sense of calm settle over me. A high wind might demand exertion, but the gentle rain or snowfall, on the other hand, brought a serene quietude, a stillness that transformed the city into a tranquil, ancient realm.
