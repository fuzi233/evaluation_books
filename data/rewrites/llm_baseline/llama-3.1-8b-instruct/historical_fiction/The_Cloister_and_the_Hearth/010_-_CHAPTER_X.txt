The banns of marriage had been read, and now all that remained was to wait for the wedding to take place. But fate had other plans. As they stood outside, a stranger emerged from the crowd, his eyes fixed on the couple with an air of disapproval. He forbade the banns, citing the couple's youth and their parents' lack of consent.

Margaret's eyes welled up with tears as she clutched Gerard's arm, her fingers digging deep into his sleeve. Gerard, however, was quick-thinking, and he whispered a plan to Margaret as they stood there. He would offer the stranger a bribe, a small fortune in gold coins, and the man would retract his objection. The stranger, it seemed, was not immune to temptation, and he agreed to Gerard's proposal.

As they walked back to the church, the stranger's steps faltered, and Gerard pressed a small pouch of gold into his hand. The man's expression changed, and he nodded curtly before disappearing into the crowd. Gerard and Margaret returned to the church, where the cure, a jovial man with a warm smile, greeted them with open arms. The wedding was set for the following day, and the couple couldn't contain their joy.

Their eyes gleamed with a sinister light, and Gerard's heart sank as he realized they knew about the bribe. The brothers were notorious troublemakers, always looking for ways to cause chaos and mayhem. They had a reputation for being ruthless, and Gerard knew he had to tread carefully.
