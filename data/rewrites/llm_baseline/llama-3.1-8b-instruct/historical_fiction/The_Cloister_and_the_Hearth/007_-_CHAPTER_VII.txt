As morning sunlight danced across the cobblestone streets of Tergou, the townspeople emerged from their homes to behold a spectacle unlike any they had seen before. The French ambassador, resplendent in his finery, rode through the city gates, accompanied by a retinue of servants from the esteemed House of Burgundy. Among them was the Duke's trusted tumbler, a man whose dignified demeanor belied a penchant for the absurd. He rode ahead, his horse's hooves clattering on the pavement, and periodically dismounting in a comical display of agility, much to the amusement of the crowd. The ambassador himself couldn't help but chuckle at the sight, his laughter mingling with the gasps of wonder from the onlookers.

As the procession made its way towards the forest, a figure drew attention from the rear: the Duke's majestic leopard, perched atop a sturdy Flemish horse, its glossy coat glistening in the morning light. The animal was a prized possession of the Duke, known for its ferocity, and the huntsman who accompanied it was tasked with keeping it secure. A small crowd gathered, admiring the leopard's beauty, but their fascination quickly turned to fear as the huntsman warned them of its notorious temper. A careless gesture could incur the Duke's wrath, and the consequences would be dire – as one unfortunate soul had learned in Antwerp.

The warning served as a reminder to the Tergovians of the Duke's power, and they parted ways, their initial excitement tempered by a sense of caution. However, the mood was soon lifted by the arrival of the Duke's giant, a towering figure from Hungary, standing at an astonishing seven feet four inches tall. His voice, a high-pitched, flute-like melody, was a curiosity in itself, and he seemed oblivious to its limitations. Catching sight of Giles perched on the balcony above, the giant playfully teased him, extending a massive hand to help him down.

Giles, never one to back down from a challenge, responded with a fierce growl, and the two engaged in a verbal sparring match, their vastly different tones creating a comedic effect that left the crowd in stitches. However, Giles soon lost his patience and, with a burst of rage, leapt onto the giant's neck, seizing his hair and pummeling his head with a flurry of punches. The giant's initial reaction was laughter, but the force and speed of the blows quickly subdued him, and he stumbled backward
