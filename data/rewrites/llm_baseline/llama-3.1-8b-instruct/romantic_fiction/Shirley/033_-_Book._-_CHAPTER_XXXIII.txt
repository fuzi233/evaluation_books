As Martin sat in the quiet of the morning, the aroma of freshly baked bread wafted through the house, teasing his senses and heightening his appetite. His stomach growled in protest, protesting the delayed breakfast he had been craving since dawn. With a newfound sense of purpose, he rose from his chair and made his way to the larder, the door creaking softly as he pushed it open. The warm glow of the kitchen beckoned, and the tantalizing scents of roasting meats and steaming vegetables drew him in like a magnet.

His eyes scanned the shelves, taking in the array of preserved fruits and cheeses, the baskets of fresh bread, and the pies cooling on the windowsill. Martin's taste buds danced with excitement as he began to assemble his breakfast. He chose three plump, rosy apples, their sweetness and crunch a perfect foil to the richness of the pastries that lay before him. A delicate apricot puff and a tart filled with succulent damsons caught his eye, and he couldn't resist adding a few currant tea-cakes to his selection. With his clasp-knife at the ready, he sliced a wing of fowl and a slice of ham, pairing them with a creamy cold custard-pudding that seemed to beg to be devoured.

Just as he was about to make his escape, the front door swung open and Matthew strode into the hallway. Martin's plans were momentarily foiled, but he saw an opportunity in the disruption. He recalled the numerous times his mother had swooned into hysterics after witnessing a disagreement between him and his brother. A calculated risk, perhaps, but one that could yield a valuable dividend. The two brothers collided in the hall, their words flying like sparks as they exchanged barbs and jibes. The servants, Mrs. Yorke, and Miss Moore rushed to intervene, but it was too late. The damage was done, and Martin's mother was already envisioning a long afternoon spent in her bed, courtesy of her son's cunning tactics.

As the commotion died down, Martin smiled to himself, his mind racing with the possibilities. His mother's absence would give him the perfect opportunity to pursue his second objective – securing a private moment with his mother to discuss the family recipe book. The old book, passed down through generations, held secrets of traditional dishes and cooking techniques that Martin was eager to learn.
