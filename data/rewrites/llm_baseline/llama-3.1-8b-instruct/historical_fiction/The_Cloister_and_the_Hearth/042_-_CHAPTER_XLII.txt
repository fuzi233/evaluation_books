As the sun rose over the village, a mysterious message materialized on the stone wall of the local church, scrawled in bold letters like a fiery script. The villagers, accustomed to the whims of the divine, gathered around the apparition, their faces aglow with a mix of wonder and trepidation. The message, a query about the absence of the duke's mass during these trying times, seemed to hold a sense of genuine inquiry, as if the very heavens themselves were questioning the actions of mortal men.

As the villagers dispersed, the local cure, a man known for his sharp business acumen, saw an opportunity to capitalize on the situation. He invested heavily in masses, securing a tidy profit for himself. Though this development was hardly remarkable, it served as a reminder that even in times of turmoil, commerce and faith could walk hand in hand.

Denys, still grappling with the weight of recent events, implored his lord for permission to retrieve his arms. His plea was met with a demand: swear fealty to the bastard until the sedition was quelled. Denys, aware of the gravity of his situation, hesitated before making a solemn vow on the cross of his sword. With his liberty restored, he rejoined the ranks of the troops.

As they halted outside the local tavern, "The Three Fish," Marion's bright smile and infectious laughter greeted Denys like a warm welcome. Her words, however, cut short as she spotted the look of distress etched on his face. "What's amiss, my friend?" she asked, her voice tinged with genuine concern. Denys shared the tale of his predicament, and Marion listened intently, her expression a testament to her unwavering optimism. She pointed out that Gerard's charm and good character would undoubtedly serve him well, and that the ladies would rally around him in times of need. If fortune smiled upon him, he might even secure a spot among the entourage bound for Rome. Conversely, if fate turned against him, he would return home, possibly even before his friend Denys.

Her words offered Denys a glimmer of hope, and as she pressed a pouch of gold coins into his hands, he felt a surge of gratitude towards this kind-hearted woman. Though he urged her to take payment, she steadfastly refused, her integrity and generosity touching Denys's heart.
