The morning sun cast its golden light upon Hollow's humble cottage, illuminating the quiet scene within. Moore's optimism lingered, a residual warmth from the previous day's events. He and Joe Scott had spent the night in the mill, taking advantage of makeshift sleeping quarters carved out from the recesses of the counting-houses. As the master of the mill, Moore was known to rise with the dawn, and today was no exception. His morning routine began with a melodic French song, filling the air as he attended to his toilette.

"Rise and shine, Joe," he called out, his voice cheerful and bright. "We'll take a stroll through the mill before the hands arrive. I have plans to share with you, and I want to make sure you're awake to hear them."

Joe rubbed the sleep from his eyes, his rugged features creasing into a grin. "Not a penny to my name, sir?" he asked, using the colloquialism for a coin.

Moore chuckled. "Not a single penny, my lad. I've got something far more valuable in mind – the future of our mill. You've heard of Bruce, I presume?"

Joe nodded, his expression thoughtful. "Aye, and the arrand spider, too. I've read about Scotland's history, and I reckon I know as much about it as you do, sir. You're planning to push forward with the project, aren't you?"

Moore's face lit up with determination. "I am."

As they walked through the mill, Joe asked, "How many of your kin do you have back in your homeland?"

Moore's eyes sparkled with amusement. "My homeland? Ah, you mean Flanders, don't you? No, I'm not Flemish. I was born in Antwerp, which was seized by the French. That doesn't make me French, of course."

Joe's brow furrowed in confusion. "Holland, then?"

Moore shook his head. "No, no. You're confusing Antwerp with Amsterdam. My mother was from Antwerp, but her family was of French descent, which is why I speak French fluently."

Joe scratched his chin thoughtfully. "But your father was from Yorkshire, sir, which makes you a bit of a Yorkshireman, too. And anyone can see you're cut from the same cloth as us – always looking to make a profit and get ahead."
