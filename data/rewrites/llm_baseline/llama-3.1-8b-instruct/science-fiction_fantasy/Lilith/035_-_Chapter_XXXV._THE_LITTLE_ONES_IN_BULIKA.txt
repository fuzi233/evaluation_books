Butterflies and dragonflies flitted about our heads, their iridescent wings glimmering in the sunlight. Four-legged creatures, from majestic bears to agile deer, followed in our wake, their hooves pounding out a lively rhythm on the earth. As night descended, many of these creatures departed, but the birds and insects remained, their presence a constant reminder of the rich tapestry of life that surrounded us.
