As the sun began to set over Florence, casting a warm orange glow across the terracotta rooftops, Romola stepped out onto the loggia of Bardo's house, the sound of her footsteps echoing off the stone walls. The quadrangular structure rose above the surrounding buildings, its columns supporting the roof on one side, while the others were pierced with arched openings that allowed a gentle breeze to rustle the leaves of the nearby olive trees. From this secluded spot, Romola could gaze out over the irregularly built dwellings of the city, their terra cotta tiles glinting in the fading light, and beyond, the hill of Bogoli rising up like a green sentinel.

Tito waited for her near one of the arched openings, his eyes fixed on the ground as he struggled to contain the turmoil of emotions that churned within him. He had never been foolish enough to believe that Romola's passion for him would be strong enough to overcome the revulsion she felt upon discovering his secret. Yet, as he gazed up at her, his heart swelled with a deep longing. He yearned to hold her close, to feel the warmth of her skin against his, to taste the sweetness of her lips. But for now, he could only stand frozen, his eyes locked on hers, as she approached him with a quiet dignity that belied the turmoil he knew she must be feeling.

As she drew near, the air seemed to vibrate with tension, the silence between them thick with unspoken words. Romola's eyes, shining like dark pools of water, met Tito's, and for a moment, they simply looked at each other, the world around them melting away. Then, in a movement both swift and gentle, she was in his arms, her face buried against his chest as she sobbed out her emotions.

The night before, Romola had lain awake, her mind racing with thoughts of the story Tito had shared with her – a tale of death and loss that had left her feeling shaken and uncertain. But even as she struggled to comprehend the depths of his sorrow, she couldn't help but feel a sense of wonder at the way he had reached out to her, offering a glimpse into the hidden recesses of his soul. And now, as she stood in his arms, she felt a sense of peace wash over her, a sense of belonging that she had never known before.

"My Romola," Tito whispered, his voice trembling with emotion as he wrapped his arms around her
