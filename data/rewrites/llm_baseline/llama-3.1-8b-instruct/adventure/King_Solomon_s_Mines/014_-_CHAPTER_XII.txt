The moon cast an ethereal glow over the dark landscape as we traversed the winding paths of Loo, our footsteps quiet on the dusty earth. Infadoos and the chiefs guided us with ease, their intimate knowledge of the terrain allowing us to navigate the shadows undetected. Hours passed, and the world held its breath as the eclipse deepened, the moon's disappearance leaving an unsettling stillness. Yet, even in darkness, beauty remained – a silver streak of light burst forth from the obscured moon, accompanied by a fiery glow that illuminated the blackness like a celestial lantern. The spectacle was both wild and breathtaking, a moment of magic in an otherwise somber world. As the stars began to fade, the returning light revealed our surroundings, and we found ourselves approaching a broad, flat-topped hill, its rugged shape like a horseshoe, its sides precipitous and strewn with boulders. This was a place of strategic importance, a military cantonment where regiments had once stood guard. Tonight, however, its grounds were filled with men, their faces pale with fear as they huddled together in consternation, awestruck by the celestial display above.

We pushed through the throngs, unobtrusive, until we reached a hut at the center of the camp, where two men waited, laden with our belongings, which we had been forced to leave behind in our hasty flight. "I sent for them," Infadoos explained, his eyes crinkling at the corners as he smiled, "and also for these." He lifted Good's long-lost trousers, and Good's face lit up with delight as he snatched them from the hands of the attendant.

As he donned the trousers, Infadoos couldn't help but remark, "And now your beautiful white legs are revealed, my lord!" Good's cheeks flushed, and he hastened to cover himself, aware that the Kukuana people were not accustomed to seeing so much of him. Henceforth, they would have to content themselves with his one whisker, his piercing eye, and his quick, mobile lips.

Infadoos continued, his voice low and measured, "I have instructed the regiments to assemble at dawn, when I shall explain the circumstances of the rebellion and introduce the rightful heir to the throne, Ignosi."
