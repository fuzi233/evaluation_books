Hunger, however, spoke loudest, its pangs gnawing at our bellies like a famished beast. We settled on a course of action, choosing to traverse the fields under the cover of darkness.

We paused at a haystack, our eyes scanning the landscape for signs of movement. We wrapped ourselves in the dry hay, trying to find some respite from the chill of the night. As we lay there, the only sounds were the distant rumble of thunder and the crunch of turnips beneath our teeth. We ate in silence, our hunger momentarily sated, but the emptiness within us remained.

As the sun dipped below the horizon, we emerged from our hiding place, our footsteps quiet on the rain-soaked earth. The night was a living entity, its darkness palpable, its fury unrelenting. We stumbled forward, guided by the faint light of the moon, which struggled to pierce the veil of clouds. Our footsteps echoed through the void, a haunting melody that seemed to draw us deeper into the heart of the storm.

Hours passed, or what felt like hours, as we navigated the treacherous terrain. The wind howled, the rain lashed down, and we pressed on, driven by a hunger that refused to be satiated. Finally, we caught sight of a massive structure looming before us – a convent, its stone walls a beacon of safety in the midst of chaos.

"Courage!" Denys exclaimed, his voice barely audible above the wind. "I think I know this place. Yes, it is. We are in the diocese of Juliers. Cologne has no power here."
