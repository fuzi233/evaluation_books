To Dr. John Elliotson,

My Dear Doctor,

Thirteen months back, when it looked like this tale was about to end on a very low note, a good soul dragged you up to my bedside. Honestly, I probably wouldn't have gotten out of bed again if it hadn't been for your tireless watching and your fancy medical tricks. I just love remembering your huge kindness (along with the surprising amount of sympathy shown by others) during those dark days when kindness was the only thing anyone wanted.

Since you refused any payment but a heartfelt thank-you, let me write it down right here for me and my whole family. And please accept my deepest gratitude.

Yours most sincerely and gratefully,

W. M. Thackeray.
