Their exchange revolved around a forthcoming dinner engagement, which Trent insisted on hosting at the esteemed Sheppard's establishment. When Cupples inquired about the identity of their host, Trent's response betrayed a mixture of exasperation and amusement.

"Really, Cupples," Trent exclaimed, his words laced with a hint of sarcasm, "do you truly require an explanation? Sheppard's is a locale renowned for its exceptional cuisine, particularly the saddle of mutton, which has been known to elicit envy from even the most discerning palates. Its reputation precedes it, and I daresay, it has transcended the realm of mythological significance."

As they strolled up Victoria Street, Trent's animated demeanor seemed almost manic, leading the observant policeman to suspect the influence of intoxication. However, Trent's ebullience stemmed from a more profound source – his impending nuptials to the enigmatic Mabel. Upon learning of the news, Cupples's reaction was one of unbridled joy, accompanied by a hint of paternal warmth, as if Trent's happiness was a validation of his own enduring optimism.

"Mabel's remarkable resilience in the face of adversity has long made her a beacon of hope," Cupples observed, his eyes twinkling with a knowing glint. "Her capacity to inspire those around her is a testament to her innate goodness. I must confess, I have always believed that you, Trent, possess a certain... je ne sais quoi, a spark that sets you apart from the rest."

Trent's expression turned slightly wistful, as if acknowledging the accuracy of Cupples's assessment. "Mabel has always seen through my façade, even when I attempted to conceal my true feelings," he revealed, his voice tinged with a hint of self-deprecation. "And I suppose it's only fitting that she should be the one to bring order to my life, to provide a sense of stability amidst the chaos that often surrounds me."
