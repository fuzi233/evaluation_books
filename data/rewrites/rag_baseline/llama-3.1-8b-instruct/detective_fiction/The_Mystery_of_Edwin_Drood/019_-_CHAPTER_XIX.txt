Rosa sat amidst the tranquil gardens of the Nuns' House, the warm sunlight casting a serene glow on the ripening fruit and the soft, green foliage. The gentle rustle of leaves and the distant hum of insects filled the air, creating a soothing melody that seemed to match the rhythm of her thoughts. Her eyes wandered across the sun-dial, the familiar shape of the sundial a comforting presence in the stillness. The memory of Mr. Jasper's presence lingered, a dark undertone in the otherwise peaceful atmosphere.

As she pondered, a servant approached, her footsteps quiet on the stone path. "Miss Rosa, Mr. Jasper requests your presence," the servant said, her voice low and even. Rosa's heart skipped a beat, and she felt a flutter in her chest. She had not seen him since that fateful night, and the memory of it still lingered, a painful scar.

Rosa's hands trembled as she adjusted her garden hat, a nervous habit she couldn't shake. She knew she couldn't avoid him forever, but the thought of being alone with him in the house was unbearable. The garden, with its open spaces and visible windows, offered a semblance of freedom, a fragile barrier between her and the darkness that lurked within him.

As she stepped out onto the porch, she caught sight of him leaning against the sundial, his figure shrouded in deep mourning. Her heart sank, and she felt the familiar weight of his presence drawing her closer. She couldn't resist, her feet moving of their own accord, until she sat beside him, her head bent in an attempt to shield herself from his gaze.

His eyes, she knew, were fixed upon her, though she couldn't bear to look up. The grass beneath her seemed to stretch on forever, a sea of green that blurred her vision. His voice, low and measured, broke the silence, "I have been waiting, for some time, to be summoned back to my duty near you." The words hung in the air, a promise of secrets untold, and Rosa's heart recoiled in fear.
