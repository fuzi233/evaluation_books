As we sailed into the mouth of the gulf, I couldn't help but feel a sense of trepidation wash over me. The rival navy's sudden appearance, their ferocity in battle, and the unmistakable signs of deliberate intent all pointed to one thing: my role in the fate of the Empress's realm was about to become much more significant than I had ever imagined.

But it seemed that Phorenice was not the only one monitoring our progress. Her scouts, stationed in the mountains, had spotted us and swiftly relayed the news to the capital. The sea arm that led to Atlantis's majestic city varied greatly in width, narrowing in some areas to mere rivers and expanding into vast lakes in others. This serpentine course allowed for an alternative route to convey information – a network of runners who traversed the treacherous terrain, braving swamps, fire streams, and roaming beasts to deliver crucial messages. Twenty such couriers had been dispatched, each taking a different path, and remarkably, seven had returned unscathed with their tidings.

Though we had no knowledge of this at the time, we pressed on, assuming our arrival remained a secret. Our galley was crewed by rowers from Europe, whose pungent aroma made the voyage unbearable, compounded by the constant barrage of fine grit carried by the wind from distant volcanic eruptions. As we approached the entrance to the straits, I couldn't help but wonder how I would be received in Atlantis. Would I be apprehended, put on trial for my actions in Yucatan? Or would I slip ashore unnoticed, only to be summoned by the Empress to assume new duties? The uncertainty gnawed at me like a persistent doubt.

The morning sun broke over the horizon, casting a golden glow over the landscape as we caught sight of the sacred mountain's peak, its eternal fires burning bright. The boatswains' whips cracked in encouragement, urging the rowers to their limits. The wind resisted our progress, but we pushed forward, driven by the rhythmic thud of the oars. The mountain's slopes lengthened, and the towering pyramids and buildings of the city materialized above the water's surface, shimmering like mirages. Twenty years had passed since I'd last set foot in Atlantis, and now, as the city rose majestically from the sea, my heart swelled with a deep longing to tread its streets once more.
