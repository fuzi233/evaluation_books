The town of Cloisterham slumbered, its ancient stones bearing witness to the whispers of centuries past. A place where time seemed to have stood still, its dusty chronicles whispering secrets to the wind. Though once known by various names to different cultures, the name of Cloisterham held little significance to its crumbling façade. The town's history stretched far beyond the reaches of recorded memory, a testament to the impermanence of human endeavors.

As one wandered through its narrow streets, the silence was almost palpable. The sun-blinds of the shops barely dared to stir in the gentle breeze, and the sun-browned travelers who passed through would hasten their pace, eager to escape the oppressive air of tradition that clung to every stone. The town's layout was simplicity itself – a single main street that wound its way in and out of the city, flanked by yards with pumps and the occasional paved thoroughfare. In the heart of the town, the Cathedral Close stood as a testament to a bygone era, its hoarse bell tolling out a mournful cadence that echoed through the stillness.

Fragments of ancient walls, chapels, and monasteries had been incorporated into the very fabric of the town, much like the jumbled memories that lingered in the minds of its residents. Every aspect of Cloisterham seemed frozen in time – even its pawnbroker, who offered a selection of tarnished trinkets and dusty tomes, relics of a forgotten age. Yet, amidst the decay, a sense of life persisted, manifest in the lush gardens that burst forth with vibrant greenery, and the faint scent of vegetables wafting from the town's few vegetable plots.

In the midst of this somnolent landscape stood the Nuns' House, its weathered façade a testament to the passing of time. A brass plate gleamed on the entrance gate, proclaiming the establishment as the Seminary for Young Ladies, under the guidance of Miss Twinkleton. The contrast between the worn, aged building and the shiny, modern sign was striking, evoking the image of a dignified elder sporting a fashionable eyeglass. As one gazed upon the Nuns' House, it was impossible not to wonder what secrets lay hidden within its ancient walls, and what stories the whispers of the past might reveal to those willing to listen.
