In the grand estate of Ashwood Manor, where generations of the Lebas family had lived and loved, the rhythms of daily life unfolded with a predictability that belied the turmoil that simmered beneath the surface. It was a truth that had been passed down through the years, a whispered secret shared among the womenfolk as they stitched and sewed by the fire: that the true test of a man's mettle lay not in his ability to provide or protect, but in his capacity to endure the maddening chaos that seemed to seep into every corner of their lives.

As the morning sunlight struggled to penetrate the leaded glass of the library windows, casting dappled shadows across the polished wood floor, Lady Violet Lebas sat at her mahogany desk, her eyes fixed upon the letter that had arrived by the morning post. It was from Mr. Andrew Lang, his sprawling script detailing his latest tales of adventure and bravery, which had captivated her imagination since childhood. Yet, as she read the familiar words, her mind wandered to the trials of the morning, and the creatures that inhabited their halls.
