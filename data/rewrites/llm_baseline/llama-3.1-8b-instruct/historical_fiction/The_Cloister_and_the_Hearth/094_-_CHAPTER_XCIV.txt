As Margaret stood before the hermit, her delicate frame trembled like a leaf in an autumn breeze. Her cheeks flushed with a mix of emotions – love, reverence, and trepidation – and her hand instinctively sought the wall for support. The hermit, Gerard, gazed at her with a mixture of wonder and wariness, his eyes fixed on the crucifix he grasped tightly in one hand, the candle in the other shaking precariously.

"Aha!" he exclaimed, his voice low and husky. "You've finally arrived, in all your physical glory, just as you did when you first sought out Saint Anthony. But I'm not fooled, my child." Gerard's words dripped with a mix of suspicion and desperation. "I've seen through your guile. I'm prepared."

Margaret's eyes widened in alarm as Gerard brandished the crucifix, his face contorted in a fierce expression. She raised her hands in a placating gesture, her voice trembling. "Oh, please, don't harm me! It's really me, Margaret."

Gerard's face twisted in a snarl. "Liar!" he spat. "You're as lovely as ever, but not as innocent as you once were. You shrank at the mention of my name, and now you're trying to deceive me with your tears and your charm." He took a step closer, his eyes blazing with a mix of anger and fear. "In nomine Dei exorcizo vos."

Margaret recoiled, her breath catching in her throat. "Jesu, have mercy!" she whispered, her eyes welling up with tears. "Please, Gerard, don't curse me. I swear I'll leave, I didn't mean to intrude." Her voice cracked with emotion as she added, "We thought we'd see you again, after all these years. Forgive me, Gerard, for disturbing you."

As Gerard's grip on the crucifix tightened, his expression softened, and he seemed to see Margaret for the first time. He took a step back, his chest heaving with exertion, and tried to reach out to her, but his frail body faltered under the weight of his own exhaustion and the crushing loneliness that had consumed him.

"What...what sorcery is this?" he stammered, his voice barely audible. "Is it really you, Margaret? Have I driven you away with my harsh words?"
