As we entered the room, the eerie silence enveloped us like a shroud, our earlier tumultuous arrival momentarily forgotten. The space, untouched except for our chaotic intrusion, slowly regained its composure, and we began to regain our wits. Mademoiselle's notion that Carmilla might have been startled awake by our commotion, then hastily hidden in a secret recess or behind a tapestry, now seemed plausible. We recommenced our search, calling out her name once more, hoping against hope that she would emerge from her hiding place.

However, our efforts yielded nothing. Perplexity and agitation intensified, and we turned our attention to the windows, checking if perhaps she had slipped out through them. But they were securely fastened, and I implored Carmilla, if she was indeed hiding, to put an end to her cruel game and rejoin us. Alas, my words fell on deaf ears. By this point, I was convinced that she was nowhere in the room or the adjacent dressing room, whose door remained locked on our side. It seemed impossible for her to have escaped through it.

The mystery deepened, and I couldn't help but wonder if Carmilla had stumbled upon one of the long-forgotten secret passages rumored to exist within the ancient schloss. The old housekeeper's whispers echoed in my mind – tales of hidden corridors and secret chambers that only the most skilled navigators knew how to uncover. Perhaps, I thought, time would eventually reveal the truth, though for now, we were left utterly bewildered.

As the night wore on, the hours ticked by with agonizing slowness. I chose to spend the remaining darkness in Madame's chambers, unable to shake off the feeling of unease that had taken hold of me. Daybreak brought no answers, only a sense of desperation that seemed to grip every member of the household. My father, at the helm, directed the search, scouring every nook and cranny of the chateau, while the grounds were thoroughly combed over. Still, no sign of Carmilla could be found.

The morning sun cast its golden light upon the anxious faces gathered in the great hall, but it brought no solace. The stream was about to be dragged, and my father's worry was palpable – the thought of breaking the news to Carmilla's mother on her return was almost too much to bear.
