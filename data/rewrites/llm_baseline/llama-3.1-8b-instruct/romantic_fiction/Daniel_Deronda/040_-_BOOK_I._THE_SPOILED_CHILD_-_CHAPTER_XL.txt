As the last wisps of mist dissipated, revealing the tranquil morning light, Daniel Deronda emerged from the narrow house on the Chelsea riverside, his thoughts entwined with the turbulent emotions of the previous night. He yearned for the invigorating rush of physical exertion to dispel the turmoil within, and the sight of the Chelsea Stairs, with their fleet of waiting boats, beckoned him toward the city. Deronda decided against the confinement of a cab, opting instead for the rhythmic motion of rowing, which would allow him to clear his mind and prepare for the encounter ahead.

His destination was Ram's bookshop, where he had missed Mordecai's midday watch the previous day, and had been informed that the enigmatic figure would return between five and six. Deronda was eager to establish a deeper connection with Mordecai, to unravel the threads of their complicated relationship, and to learn more about the mysterious inmate of the Cohen household. As he began to row, the exertion of using the oar kindled a sense of purpose within him, and he found himself shifting his perspective, no longer solely focusing on the benefits Mordecai could offer, but rather considering the complex tapestry of their lives, and the intricate dance of expectations that had brought them together.

"I often wonder," Deronda mused, "if we are merely pawns in a grand game, our lives intersecting for fleeting moments, only to part ways once more. Perhaps Mordecai's gaze, like mine, has searched for a connection, a spark that would ignite a deeper understanding. Yet, our paths may cross and recross, like two ships navigating the same waters, each carrying secrets and stories that remain unspoken. Still, I find myself drawn to the idea that there is a hidden language, a silent understanding that can transcend the mundane, and speak directly to the heart."

As the wherry approached Blackfriars Bridge, the fading light of day cast a warm glow across the river, imbuing the scene with an air of serenity. The clouds, once a deep grey, now unfolded into layers of rich purple, set against a brilliant saffron backdrop, while the river itself rippled and shone, reflecting the ever-changing beauty of the sky.
