As I pushed open the door, Rachel's fingers stilled on the piano keys. The room fell silent, with the full length of the space between us. Her gaze met mine, and I sensed the weight of her exhaustion. Every muscle seemed to be straining towards the effort of simply looking at me.

Fear crept into my mind – had I startled her too abruptly? I took a few cautious steps forward, my voice barely above a whisper. "Rachel."

The sound of my name brought a faint spark to her cheeks, and her eyelids fluttered open. She shifted her weight, her movements slow and labored. As if drawn by some unseen force, she began to move towards me, her eyes fixed on mine with a mix of wariness and longing. The flush deepened on her cheeks, and her eyes shone with a fragile light. My breath caught in my throat as I forgot the turmoil that had brought me here, the secrets I'd kept hidden. All that mattered was the woman I loved, inching closer with each step.

Her trembling grew more pronounced as she drew near, and I felt my resolve waver. I couldn't resist – I swept her into my arms, covering her face with kisses. For a fleeting instant, it seemed as though she might return them, that we might both forget the weight of our troubles. But before the idea could take root, her body stiffened, and a cry of horror escaped her lips. A wave of strength washed over her, and she pushed me back with a force that left me reeling.

Merciless anger flashed in her eyes, and contempt etched itself on her lips. She regarded me with a cold, detached curiosity, as if I were a stranger who'd committed some unspeakable offense. "You coward!" she spat, her voice laced with venom. "You heartless, miserable coward!"

Her words cut deeper than any blow, leaving me reeling. I'd never heard her speak to me like that before. I searched for a defense, a way to make amends, but my voice caught in my throat.

"I remember when you could have told me of my offense," I managed to say, my voice rough with emotion. "I beg your pardon."
