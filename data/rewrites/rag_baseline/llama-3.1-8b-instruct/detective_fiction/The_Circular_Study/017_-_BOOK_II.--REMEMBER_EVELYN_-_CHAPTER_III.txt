The name 'Adams' would be his shield, his brother Thomas's nemesis, a constant reminder of the debt that bound them together.

As he walked towards the door, his mother's voice called out, a gentle inquiry about his plans for the day. He replied with a curt dismissal, his mind already made up. He had no intention of staying in the stifling atmosphere of their family home, not after his father's passing. The walls, once a testament to their patriarch's legacy, now seemed oppressive, a constant reminder of the weight of expectation.
