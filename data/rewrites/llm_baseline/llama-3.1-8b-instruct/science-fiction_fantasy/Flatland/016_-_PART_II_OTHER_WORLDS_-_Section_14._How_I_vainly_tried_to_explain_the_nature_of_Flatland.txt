As I sat with the Monarch, basking in the radiance of his exalted presence, I couldn't help but think it was time to bring him back down to earth, to share with him the humble truths of our world. So I began, "Your Majesty, how do you perceive the shapes and positions of your subjects? I noticed, upon entering your realm, that some of your people are lines, while others are mere points. Some lines, I observed, stretch farther than others." 

The King's face darkened, his expression a mixture of confusion and disdain. "You speak nonsense, stranger," he said, his voice like a cold wind on a winter's night. "To distinguish between a line and a point by sight is an impossibility. Yet, by sound, I can reveal my shape to my wives, who reside six thousand miles, seventy yards, two feet, eight inches away. Listen, if you will." He let out a series of melodic chirps, followed by a low, rumbling hum.

As the echoes faded away, the King smiled condescendingly. "My wives, you see, can calculate my shape by the interval between the sounds. One of my voices reaches them first, followed by the other, after a pause of precisely 6.457 inches. From this, they infer my shape to be 6.457 inches long. Of course, they don't need to perform this calculation every time they hear me; they did it once, before we were wed. Still, they could, if they chose to. And in the same way, I can gauge the shape of any male subject by the sound of their voice."

"But, Your Majesty," I ventured, "what of those who might deceive us with false voices? A man might mimic a woman's tone with one of his own voices, or alter his Southern voice to disguise it as the echo of his Northern one. Might not such deceptions lead to great inconvenience? And have you no means of checking such frauds, by commanding your neighbors to touch one another?" I asked, knowing full well the absurdity of the suggestion.

The King's face turned ashen, his eyes wide with horror. "Explain yourself," he growled. "What do you mean by 'touch'?"
