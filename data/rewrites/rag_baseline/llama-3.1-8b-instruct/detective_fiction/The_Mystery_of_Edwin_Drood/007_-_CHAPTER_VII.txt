As Neville's footsteps echoed through the deserted streets, the flickering gas lamps cast eerie shadows on the walls. He had come to the Minor Canon's residence with a purpose, though he couldn't quite articulate it himself. His sister, beside him, seemed lost in thought, her eyes fixed on some distant horizon. The night air was heavy with the scent of damp earth and decaying leaves.

"I suppose you know we come from Ceylon," Neville said, breaking the silence.

The Minor Canon's response was measured, his voice a gentle counterpoint to the discordant sounds of the city. "Indeed, no."

Neville's words tumbled out in a rush, a confession of sorts. "We lived with a stepfather there. Our mother died when we were little children. He was a miserly wretch who begrudged us food to eat, and clothes to wear. At his death, he passed us over to...this man." A faint tremble crept into his voice as he mentioned his guardian's name.

The Minor Canon's expression remained neutral, though a flicker of concern danced in his eyes. "That was recently, I suppose?"

Neville nodded, his gaze drifting towards the ground. "Quite lately, sir. Our stepfather was a cruel brute as well as a grinding one. It's well he died when he did, or I might have...I might have..." He trailed off, the words dying on his lips.

The Minor Canon's response was soft, almost a whisper. "I surprise you, sir?" He sounded uncertain, as if he wasn't quite sure where this conversation was headed.

Neville's eyes snapped back to his, a flash of defensiveness sparking within them. "You seem surprised, sir."

The Minor Canon's face remained a mask of calm, but his eyes betrayed a growing unease. "You shock me; unspeakably shock me." He paused, his words hanging in the air like a challenge.

Neville's gaze dropped, his shoulders sagging beneath the weight of his own emotions. For a moment, the only sound was the distant hum of the city, a reminder of the world beyond their small, troubled sphere. Then, in a voice barely above a whisper, he spoke of his sister's tears, of the cruel brutality she had endured at the hands of their stepfather. The words spilled out like a confession, a plea for understanding and validation.
