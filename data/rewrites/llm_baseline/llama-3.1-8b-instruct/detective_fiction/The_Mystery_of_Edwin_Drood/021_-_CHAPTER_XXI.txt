As morning broke, the air was filled with the sweet songs of birds, a gentle melody that accompanied Rosa as she rose from her slumber. The clock struck ten, and with it, the soft chime echoed through the quiet halls of Ma's residence. Mr. Crisparkle arrived, his footsteps light on the floorboards, as he entered the parlor where Rosa and Mr. Grewgious sat. He had risen early, eager to offer his assistance, having received Rosa's note and feeling a sense of urgency to act.

"I must confess, I wished you had come to me," Mr. Crisparkle said, "but I suppose it was best that you came to your guardian." Rosa nodded in agreement, and Mr. Grewgious, who had been listening attentively, spoke up. "I've shared all that Rosa told me last night with Mr. Crisparkle, my dear. His arrival is a timely one indeed."

Rosa's eyes turned to Mr. Crisparkle, her voice tinged with concern. "Have we decided what to do about Helena and her brother?" The room fell silent, and Mr. Crisparkle rubbed his chin thoughtfully. "I must admit, I'm at a loss. Even Mr. Grewgious, with his keen mind, seems uncertain. What can we do?"

Just then, the door creaked open, and the Unlimited poked her head in. "Excuse me, ma'am, sir. A gentleman wishes to speak with a Mr. Crisparkle, if such a gentleman is present. If not, he apologizes for the mistake."

Mr. Crisparkle stood up, a hint of a smile on his lips. "Ah, yes, such a gentleman is indeed here, but he's occupied at the moment." Rosa's eyes darted to her guardian, and Mr. Grewgious nodded discreetly. "It might be wise to receive him, reverend sir. Sometimes, in times of uncertainty, a new perspective can be just what we need."

Mr. Crisparkle nodded, and the Unlimited announced the visitor. A gentleman entered, his features warm and pleasant.
