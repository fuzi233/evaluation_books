Yet, as I drifted between dreams and reality, the distant rumble of the Underground Organs began to echo through the air, their low tones weaving a haunting melody that seemed to reverberate across the vast expanse of the world. The sound swelled and receded, like a far-off wind rustling through the valleys below, carrying with it the faint echoes of a long-forgotten song – the Song of Honour.

In the midst of this sonic tapestry, I sensed a collective rejoicing, as if the countless souls in the Country of Silence were celebrating some great wonder. Though the sounds remained indistinct, their presence filled me with a sense of awe and longing. As my vision began to clear, I caught glimpses of the winding pathways of the Lift, lined with fields of flowers that bloomed in every direction, releasing sweet fragrances that wafted up to me on the gentle breeze.

She took my hand, and I felt life coursing through me once more. Her touch was wordless, yet spoke volumes to my soul, filling me with a deep sense of contentment.
