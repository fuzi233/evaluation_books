As I descended into the darkness, my footsteps echoed softly on the unforgiving earth. The weight of the shadows pressed upon me, making it difficult to discern the terrain beneath my feet. I moved cautiously, aware that the darkness could play tricks on my mind, leading me to stumble and lose my footing. But I pushed on, driven by a steadfast resolve to navigate this seemingly endless night.

Every so often, I would pause to consult the faint glow emanating from the Diskos in my hand. Its gentle light illuminated the path ahead, allowing me to fix my gaze inward and recall the route I had taken. With the Diskos's soft illumination, I felt a fleeting sense of security, only to be shaken once more by the crushing darkness. I would then press on, my heart heavy with the burden of uncertainty.

Time seemed to drag, and I found myself making little progress. Hours ticked by at a glacial pace, and I grew increasingly frustrated with my inability to move freely. My mind began to wander, conjuring up fantastical visions of walking on air, of floating above the earth. But each time, a jarring collision with a rock or a boulder brought me back to reality, reminding me that I was firmly rooted on solid ground.

The hours blended together, a seemingly endless expanse of darkness punctuated only by the occasional flash of light from the Diskos. I rationed its use carefully, reserving it for moments when I needed reassurance that I was still moving forward. Even then, the light seemed feeble against the overwhelming blackness.

Eventually, after what felt like an eternity, I decided to rest. I settled onto the slope, my body weary from the exertion. I ate two tablets and drank from my canteen, trying to focus on the simple act of nourishment. As I lay down, I wrapped my cloak around me and placed my scrip and pouch beneath my head. The Diskos remained clutched in my hand, a comforting presence in the void. My thoughts drifted to Naani, and I felt a pang of longing as I drifted off to sleep.

I slept fitfully, waking after six hours to find myself in an unsettling state of heightened awareness. I rose to my elbow, listening intently, my senses on high alert. For a moment, I thought I detected a presence nearby, but there was nothing – no sound, no movement. I sat up, gripping the Diskos tightly, and reached for my scrip to eat and drink.
