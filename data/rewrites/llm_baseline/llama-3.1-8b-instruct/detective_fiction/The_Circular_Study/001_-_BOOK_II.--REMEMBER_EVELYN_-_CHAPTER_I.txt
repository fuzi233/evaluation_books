As the last wisps of autumn's golden hue dissolved into the grey veil of evening, Mr. Gryce felt the weight of years bearing down upon him. His spirit, once a flame that danced with unbridled enthusiasm, now flickered like a candle in the wind, its warmth and light struggling to penetrate the chill of a life grown stale and uninspiring. The thought of relinquishing his position on the force and retiring to the little farm in Westchester, which he had purchased with such hope and expectation, had been weighing heavily on his mind. Yet, action was his lifeblood, and the exercise of his mental faculties, his greatest joy.

But fate, it seemed, had other plans. Just as the darkness deepened and the shadows lengthened, a message arrived at Headquarters, a spark that reignited the fire within him, and infused the mundane day with a sense of purpose. It came from Carter's well-known drug store, a humble establishment that stood like a sentinel on the corner, watching over the comings and goings of the community. The message was brief, yet its words carried a weight that shook Mr. Gryce to his core: a strange crime had been committed in the venerable mansion on ---- Street, a edifice that stood as a testament to the past, its stone façade bearing witness to generations of laughter, tears, and secrets.

The boy who delivered the message was a waif, a small, fragile creature with eyes that shone like stars in the night sky. He was hesitant to speak of the mysterious lady who had summoned him, but the coins she had given him told a different tale. The boy's story, though fragmented, spoke of a woman with a presence that commanded attention, a woman who wore her wealth and power like a cloak, and whose voice, though soft, was capable of striking fear into the heart of a child.

As Mr. Gryce listened to the boy's account, he found himself drawn into a world of wonder and intrigue. The words "strange crime" echoed in his mind, a refrain that refused to be silenced. He made his way to the house, his footsteps echoing off the pavement, as the darkness gathered around him like a shroud. The building loomed before him, its shut door, clean stoop, and heavily curtained windows a testament to the secrets that lay within. Its brick façade, with its brownstone trimmings, stood like a sentinel, guarding the mysteries that lay hidden behind its walls.
