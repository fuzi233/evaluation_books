As Gerard and Margaret walked hand in hand beneath the golden light of dawn, the memories of their fleeting freedom began to wane, replaced by the weight of their uncertain future. They stood at the threshold of a decision that would forever alter the course of their lives – the decision to part ways, at least for a time. Gerard, an escaped prisoner, knew he was at risk of being retaken and punished, and the thought of leaving Margaret behind weighed heavily on his heart. Yet, his desire to visit Italy, to hone his craft and earn a reputation, had become an all-consuming passion. He envisioned a future where he could support Margaret, free from the confines of their small world.

Margaret's eyes, like two shimmering pools of water, reflected the turmoil brewing within her. She had grown accustomed to the idea of Gerard's departure, but the thought of him leaving without her was almost too much to bear. The memory of the demoiselle Van Eyck's words still lingered, and Gerard repeated them to Margaret, reminding her of the gold pieces meant to facilitate his journey. In Italy, he would find recognition and respect, and their marriage would no longer be hindered by the obstacles that surrounded them in this boorish place.

As they stood together, the morning sun casting long shadows across the landscape, Gerard's words poured forth like a gentle stream. He spoke of the possibilities that awaited him in Italy, of the art and the people who would appreciate his talents. Margaret listened, her heart heavy with the weight of their impending separation. Tears streamed down her face as Gerard, young and loving as herself, wept alongside her. Together, they conjured images of a brighter future, one where they could be together, free from the shackles of their past.

The hours ticked by, each one a fragile thread in the tapestry of their lives. Neither Gerard nor Margaret had the courage to speak the words that would seal their fate – the moment when they would say goodbye. It was Giles, a figure from their past, who brought the harsh reality crashing down upon them.
