As I stirred from my slumber on the storeroom floor, the scent of aged spices wafted through the air, mingling with the acrid smell of gunpowder. I couldn't shake the haze of sleep, but the cacophony of cries and rifle fire shattered my tranquility. The sound waves echoed through the subterranean corridors, like the crackling of flames in a well-stocked kitchen.

I sprang to my feet, my joints creaking in protest. The storeroom was dimly lit, the only light coming from a few flickering candles that danced in the draft. The bodies of my companions lay scattered about, except for Thuvia and Tars Tarkas, who, like me, had managed to avoid the initial hail of bullets.

As I regained my footing, the therns ceased firing, their rifles lowered in a mixture of confusion and trepidation. Their leader, a burly man with a scar above his left eyebrow, took a step forward. "What manner of sorcery is this?" he exclaimed, his voice laced with a mix of awe and fear.

I seized the opportunity to assert my authority. "What treachery is this? Does Sator Throg intend to murder his own vassals?"

A younger thern, his face pale, stepped forward. "Forgive us, Great One," he stammered. "We came at the behest of the Father of Therns. Two strangers, one with white hair and the other a massive green warrior, have infiltrated our stronghold. We sought them, but—"

Thuvia interrupted him, her voice low and urgent. "Ask them about their mission, John Carter."

I turned to the therns. "What brings you to this place?"

The leader, his eyes darting nervously between Thuvia and me, explained, "We were tasked with capturing the intruders. One of them, the green warrior, stands before you. The other...the other lies deceased." He nodded toward the body of the man Thuvia had indicated earlier.

Thuvia's eyes locked onto mine, a hint of warning in her gaze. "It seems Sator Throg has played a double game, John Carter. These men were supposed to capture, not kill. Now, they've made a mess of things."
