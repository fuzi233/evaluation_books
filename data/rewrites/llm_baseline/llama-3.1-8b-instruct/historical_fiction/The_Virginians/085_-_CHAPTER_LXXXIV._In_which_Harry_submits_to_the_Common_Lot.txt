The stillness that had once been pierced by the cacophony of poverty was finally at an end. With the passing of my young cousin, my financial struggles dissipated, and a brighter future unfolded before me. As next in line to inherit a substantial estate, I found myself poised to take on new responsibilities. My uncle and his wife, though still young, had not yet produced any children, leaving me the sole heir to their legacy. My friends and acquaintances whispered among themselves about the potential machinations of my uncle's wife, Lady Warrington, speculating that she might resort to underhanded means to secure the estate for herself. However, I knew her to be a woman guided by her faith and conscience, and her actions were motivated by a desire to find solace in the face of tragedy.

The loss of her son had shaken her deeply, and her clergyman counseled her to view it as a divine retribution for her past transgressions. "While your son lived, your heart was turned away from the path of righteousness," he said. "You prioritized earthly pursuits and material wealth above all else. Now, with but a brief time remaining on this earth, what significance can your titles and honors hold compared to the eternal reward awaiting you?" This message resonated within her, and she began to seek comfort in the teachings of her faith. Her devotion to her church intensified, and she became an active participant in its community, finding solace in the words of scripture and the company of like-minded individuals.

I had the opportunity to visit her in London a year after her loss, and during our meeting, she shared with me her thoughts on my own path in life. Her words were laced with a sense of wonder and dismay, as she pondered the twists of fate that had led to my inheritance. She spoke of her son's disobedience, which had ultimately led to his untimely demise, and how this event had brought about a significant shift in her perspective. Though her words were delivered in a didactic tone, I sensed a deep sadness beneath the surface. She implored me to reevaluate my choices, to distance myself from those she deemed unsavory and to seek guidance from her faith. She pressed upon me the importance of dedicating myself to a life of virtue and devotion, rather than the fleeting pleasures of the theater and its licentious patrons.
