As I lay amidst the tranquil silence of the cave, I awoke to a most disquieting sensation – a throbbing pain in the back of my hand. A triangular wound marred the otherwise smooth surface, eerily reminiscent of a leech's bite. The swelling, however, dissipated as the day wore on, leaving behind only a faint reminder of the discomfort. By evening, the ache had all but vanished, and I was left to ponder the mystery of the unassuming injury.

Days passed, and I found myself drawn to the still figure beside me. Its form, though unchanged, seemed subtly different – the sharp angles of its bones softened, the skin taking on a healthier, more supple quality. I dared to hope that life was stirring within, like a tide that had begun to ebb and flow once more. Twenty times a day, I gazed upon her face, searching for signs of revival, only to be met with uncertainty. Yet, each time I recalled the mental image of her as I first discovered her, hope would surge forth anew.

Weeks went by, and one balmy night, I rose to breathe the cool air outside. The moon cast an ethereal glow within the cave, and I couldn't help but steal a glance at my companion. Her features seemed to shimmer in the soft light, and for an instant, I swore I saw her eyes gleam with a dark, starry intensity. My heart racing, I crept closer, only to be met with disappointment – her eyelids remained closed. I felt a pang of despair, but then a glimmer of doubt crept in. Had she perhaps opened her eyes for a fleeting moment, only to succumb to slumber once more? Or was it possible that she was awake, her gaze hidden from me? The thought brought me solace, and I drifted off to sleep, my heart filled with the possibility of life.

That night, I was bitten once more, and awoke with a parching thirst. In the morning, I scoured the cave, searching for any sign of the elusive creature. The wound was identical to the previous ones, and by evening, it had healed once more. I concluded that a large leech from the nearby stream must be responsible, but I reassured myself that as long as I remained vigilant, my companion was safe.
