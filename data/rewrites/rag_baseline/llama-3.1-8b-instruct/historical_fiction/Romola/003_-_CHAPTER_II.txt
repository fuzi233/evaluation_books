Breakfast for Love


As I strolled through the sun-drenched piazza, the vibrant sounds of the market mingled with the murmur of conversation, drawing my attention to the gathering of townsfolk. The air was alive with the scent of fresh herbs and baked goods, enticing passersby like a siren's call. I had given up trying to discern the cause of the commotion, my mind instead focused on finding a vendor willing to accept a small, foreign coin in exchange for a meal.

My fingers rummaged through the worn leather pouch at my waist, searching for the last remaining obolus. But to my dismay, my hand closed around nothing but a handful of dust. "Not a single coin to my name," I muttered to myself, feeling a pang of hunger. I had walked for hours, and the thought of going hungry was almost unbearable.

A nearby market stall caught my eye – a young girl, no more than sixteen, sat leaning against the neck of a mule, its wooden milk-vessels and panniers overflowing with an assortment of fresh greens and herbs. The girl's red hood framed her face, accentuating the delicate features that seemed almost childlike beneath. Her eyelids drooped, suggesting she had been up since dawn, preparing for her journey to market from a nearby castello.

I approached the stall with a quiet reverence, not wanting to startle the girl from her slumber. My fingers brushed against her elbow, and she stirred, her eyes fluttering open like a flower unfolding to greet the sun. For an instant, we locked gazes, and I felt a jolt of surprise at the intensity of her blue eyes.

"Forgive me, dear one," I said softly, using the Tuscan phrase that had become second nature to me. "I'm famished, and the scent of milk is making my stomach growl with hunger." I chose the words carefully, knowing they would be familiar to her ears. The girl's gaze never wavered, and she slowly lifted the corner of her green serge mantle, revealing a steaming cup of milk.

As I took the cup from her, she offered me a warm smile, and I felt a spark of connection ignite between us. While I drank, she watched me with an intensity that made me feel both self-conscious and grateful. When I finished, she held out a large piece of bread, her eyes sparkling with encouragement.
