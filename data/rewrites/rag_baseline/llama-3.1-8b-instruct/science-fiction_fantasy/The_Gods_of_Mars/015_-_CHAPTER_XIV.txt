As I gazed upon my son's face, the echoes of my memories came flooding back. "Tell me of your mother," I said, my voice barely above a whisper, yet carrying the weight of a lifetime's longing. "Share with me the stories she's told you of our time together, of the love we shared, and the dreams we chased."

His eyes lit up with excitement as he launched himself towards me, wrapping his arms around my neck in a tight, exuberant hug. For an instant, I felt the sting of unshed tears, and my heart swelled with a deep, abiding sorrow. But I pushed it aside, knowing that a lifetime of hard-won wisdom had taught me that a man's vulnerability is not weakness, but strength.

As I held him close, his words poured forth like a balm to my parched soul. "Your stature, your swordsmanship, your unyielding spirit – all these and more have been etched into my mind by my mother's loving hands. Yet, even with such vivid descriptions, I couldn't help but doubt the truth of it all, until you spoke of her, until you spoke of our love."

His words cut deep, and I felt the ache of loss and longing rise up within me. "What was it, my boy, that made you realize the truth?"

He hesitated, his brow furrowed in concentration. "Your first words to me, Father – they were of her. No one but the man who loved her as you did could have spoken of her with such reverence and love."

In that moment, the memories came flooding back – the countless nights I'd spent thinking of her, the unrelenting passion that had driven me to explore the unforgiving vastness of Mars, and the ultimate sacrifice I'd made for the sake of our love.
