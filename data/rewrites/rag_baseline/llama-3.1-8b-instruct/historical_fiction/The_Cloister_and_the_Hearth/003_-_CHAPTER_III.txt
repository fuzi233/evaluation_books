The scent of roasted meats wafted through the air, mingling with the murmur of conversation, as Gerard rose to answer the summons of the Princess Marie. A grave seneschal, his hair as white as the wisps of smoke rising from the kitchen hearth, approached their table, inquiring courteously if Gerard Eliassoen was among their number. Upon Gerard's confirmation, the seneschal bowed low, his eyes glinting with a hint of curiosity.

"The Princess would confer with you, young sir," he announced, his voice carrying across the crowded hall. "I am to escort you to her presence."

As Gerard departed, the faces of those seated nearby turned to regard him with a mixture of envy and curiosity. Margaret, her eyes shining with a quiet intensity, watched him go, her fingers drumming a subtle rhythm on the armrest. Her companion, Peter, stirred, his gaze following Gerard's departure with a drowsy interest.

"You shall not see him again," Margaret murmured, a flush rising to her cheeks.

Gerard's parting words, whispered in her ear, brought a fleeting smile to her lips. "This is my good princess," he said, "but you are my queen." Turning to the others, he promised, "Wait for me, I pray you, I will presently return."

As Gerard disappeared into the labyrinthine corridors, the pair he had left behind became the focus of attention. William Johnson, their host, stepped forward, feigning surprise and claiming kinship. "And to think that there was I at your backs, and you saw me not!" he exclaimed.

Margaret's response was cool, her voice laced with a hint of irony. "Nay, cousin Johnson, I saw you long since." Her words were tinged with a subtle rebuke, and Johnson's face darkened in response.

"I saw you, and spoke not to you?" he pressed, his tone tinged with a hint of defensiveness.

Margaret's retort was swift, her words dripping with a quiet venom. "Cousin, it was for you to welcome us to Rotterdam, as it is for us to welcome you at Sevenbergen. Your servant denied us a seat in your house."

Johnson's flush deepened, his eyes narrowing in annoyance. "The idiot!" he muttered, his face reddening further.
