As I sat at my desk, savoring the aroma of freshly brewed coffee wafting from the nearby café, I pondered the enigmatic tale of the Moonstone. It was a mystery that had been simmering in my mind like a rich, complex sauce, and I was determined to uncover its secrets. A week or ten days had passed since Miss Verinder's departure, and my thoughts turned to the events that would soon unfold.

Just then, my clerk burst into the room, a look of excitement on his face. "Sir, I have a visitor," he announced, holding out a card with a foreign name scrawled on it. Below the name, a message was penned in elegant English: "Recommended by Mr. Septimus Luker." My eyes widened in surprise – what audacity for a man in Mr. Luker's position to presume to vouch for someone in my esteemed office! The clerk noticed my perplexity and offered his observations on the stranger waiting downstairs. "He's quite a striking fellow, sir – dark-skinned, with an air of the East about him."

As I gazed at the card, a connection sparked between the clerk's description and the line on the card. It seemed possible that the Moonstone was at the root of Mr. Luker's endorsement and the stranger's visit. My curiosity piqued, I made an impulsive decision to grant the gentleman an audience. My clerk raised an eyebrow at this unconventional move, but I explained that my extensive experience with the Moonstone gave me a unique perspective on its significance.

Indeed, I had played a pivotal role in the gemstone's history. I had helped Colonel Herncastle devise a plan to escape assassination, received his letters detailing his adventures, drawn up his will leaving the Moonstone to Miss Verinder, and even convinced Mr. Franklin Blake to transport the gem to Lady Verinder's estate. If anyone had a claim to the Moonstone's story, it was I. With this in mind, I decided to indulge my curiosity and meet the mysterious visitor.

As the Indian gentleman entered the room, I was struck by his dignified demeanor and the elegant folds of his European attire. Yet, his swarthy complexion and lithe physique betrayed his Eastern origins. I invited him to sit, and he apologized for intruding upon me before producing a small, exquisitely crafted box wrapped in golden cloth and silk.
