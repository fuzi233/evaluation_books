A gentle lapping of water against the shore accompanied the soft clip-clop of the horse's hooves, weaving a soothing melody that filled the air.

As they entered the town, the sweet aroma of freshly baked bread wafted from the nearby bakery, mingling with the earthy smell of the river. Ethne's thoughts were elsewhere, however, her brow furrowed in concern as she steered the cart up the steep, narrow street. It was her first time hosting guests at Lennon House, and the weight of responsibility settled heavily upon her shoulders. She glanced at Feversham, his eyes fixed intently on the surroundings, drinking in the unfamiliar sights and sounds of the town. His rugged features softened ever so slightly as he took in the dark thicket of trees that lined the riverbank, their leaves rustling softly in the breeze.

It was her twenty-first year, and the world was full of possibilities – or so she thought.
