As the April sun rose over the rolling hills of Somerset, Elinor and Marianne Dashwood set out from London, their carriage wheels crunching along the gravel road. Their journey to Barton was a long one, spanning several days, and they had chosen to travel with a leisurely pace to take in the picturesque scenery along the way. Elinor, ever the practical one, was content with the prospect of a tranquil few months at Barton, where she hoped to help her sister recover from the wounds of her past. Marianne, on the other hand, felt a mix of emotions as they left behind the city, where her hopes and dreams had once been centered on the charming but untrustworthy Willoughby.

As they traveled, Elinor reflected on the various invitations she had received from friends and family, each urging her to visit them at their respective homes. Fanny's warm invitation to come to Norland was particularly poignant, given that it was the very place where her mother had spent so many happy years. Meanwhile, John Dashwood's promise to visit her at Delaford was a welcome respite from the loneliness that had begun to creep in. However, Elinor knew that Delaford held no allure for her, a place where she would be constantly reminded of her new role as mistress of the estate, and the weight of responsibility that came with it.

Their journey was marked by the gentle rhythms of rural life – the chirping birds, the rustling leaves, and the soft breeze carrying the scent of blooming flowers. On the second day, they entered the county of Somerset, a land of rolling hills and verdant pastures that Marianne had grown to love during her summer sojourns at Barton. As they approached Cleveland, the grandeur of the estate began to reveal itself – a spacious, modern-built house perched on a sloping lawn, surrounded by an extensive network of pleasure grounds, shrubberies, and wood walks. The air was filled with the sweet fragrance of blooming trees and the soft chirping of birds, a far cry from the bustling streets of London.

Marianne, still grappling with the memories of her past, found solace in the peaceful surroundings of Cleveland. She stole away from the group, seeking a distant vantage point where she could gaze out at the rolling hills and imagine the familiar contours of Combe Magna, her beloved childhood home.
