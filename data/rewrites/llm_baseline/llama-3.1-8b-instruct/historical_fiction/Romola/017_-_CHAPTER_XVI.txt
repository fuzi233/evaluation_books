As the morning sun cast its golden light upon the narrow thoroughfares of Florence, Tito strolled back from Bratti's shop, his footsteps echoing off the ancient stone buildings. The previous day's events still lingered in his mind, the memory of parting with the onyx ring a nagging presence. Though he'd rationalized it as a minor sacrifice, a trivial loss compared to the potential consequences of staying in Florence, Tito couldn't shake the feeling of regret. His confidence in fortune had waned, leaving him listless and uncertain.

The streets were alive with the gentle bustle of morning commerce, the air thick with the scent of freshly baked bread and roasting coffee. As he navigated the winding passageways, Tito deliberately avoided the Mercato Vecchio, knowing that running into Tessa might only exacerbate his mood. He wasn't in the frame of mind to engage with anyone, not even the lively market folk who always seemed to know his name and his story.

The Piazza di San Giovanni beckoned, its beauty and tranquility a balm to his frazzled nerves. The autumn dew still clung to the cobblestones, casting a mystical glow over the scene. The soft morning light invited him to linger, to soak in the atmosphere and forget his worries. As he entered the piazza, Tito spotted the signs of the upcoming fair – colorful stalls set up around the octagonal baptistery, laden mules waiting patiently for their drivers, and vendors calling out their wares in cheerful tones.

Near the Duomo, groups of people gathered on the broad marble steps, their conversations and laughter mingling with the sweet scent of fruit and flowers. An elderly woman coaxed a giggling infant to try its tiny feet on the warm stone, while a nearby dog watched with skeptical interest. A pair of rough-looking boys leaned against the wall, watching a young cripple intently as he carved a design onto a cherry pit. Amidst the lively scene, the sound of men's laughter and the clinking of cups created a festive atmosphere.

Tito's attention, however, was drawn to the bustling crowd outside Nello's barber shop. In this warm season, with the sun beating down, many men preferred to be shaved under the shade of the red and white awning rather than within the confines of the shop.
