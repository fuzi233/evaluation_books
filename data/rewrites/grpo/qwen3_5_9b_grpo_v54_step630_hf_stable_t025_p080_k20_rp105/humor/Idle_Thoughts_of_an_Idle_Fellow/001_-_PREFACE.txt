Preface

A couple of chums, after peering at these scribbles in manuscript, kindly remarked that they weren't exactly trash, while a few distant relatives have already signed blank checks promising to purchase the volume once it sees the light of day. Consequently, I feel morally obligated to stop stalling its publication. Without this manufactured "public demand," I likely would have been too terrified to serve these mere "idle thoughts" as mental sustenance to the English-speaking masses. After all, what does the modern reader crave? Improvement! Instruction! Elevation! This book, however, lacks the gravitational pull to lift a cow off the ground. I cannot, in good conscience, recommend it for any utilitarian purpose whatsoever. My only suggestion is this: when you've grown weary of devouring "the best hundred books," pick this one up for a fleeting thirty minutes. It offers a delightful change of pace.
