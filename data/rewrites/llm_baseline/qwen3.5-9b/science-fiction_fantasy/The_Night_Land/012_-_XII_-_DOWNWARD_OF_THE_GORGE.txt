I carried the Maid downward along the gorge for an hour, as I mentioned. By then, I was so utterly exhausted that I nearly collapsed with every step, stumbling constantly. My footing had grown unsteady, betraying my complete depletion.

I realized I must soon reach a place to sleep, or I risked falling headlong with her; indeed, I was nodding off while walking.

Then, I turned my attention to the gorge walls. It became clear that I had traversed that hour in a dream. Suddenly, my senses snapped back to reality, and I recognized that I had been sleepwalking. The gorge seemed entirely unfamiliar to me, as if I had just awakened to find myself wandering this strange, narrow path.

Soon, however, I located my position. Recalling my outward journey, I remembered the route clearly. There, near me, burned a great, ruddy fire-hole. I was certain I had passed certain caves in the gorge wall near this fire during my ascent, and at that time, my heart had yearned intensely to lead "Mine Own" safely out of peril to such a spot for rest on our return journey.

I recount this tale with the innocence of a child in play, for truly, it was a wondrously happy prospect that my desire was about to be fulfilled—if my memory served me correctly.

Moving forward, there they were! Just past the great fire-hole lay the little caves. Seven of them lined the left side of the massive cliff. One appeared particularly cozy—a place of sure safety if we could reach it.

Truly, as I surmised, we had escaped the Evil Forces of that land. Yet, doubt lingered. Could not some monster descend from below to kill us in our sleep? Without a secure refuge, death awaited. Indeed, I wished we had traveled further down, but my weariness rendered me adrift. If we reached the highest cave, few monsters would dare approach us before we were alerted; thereafter, they would likely ascend toward the Diskos, a task far more difficult for them.

It was necessary to wake the Maid. I kissed her as she lay in my arms. She returned the kiss in her sleep, still unconscious. I loved her with every fiber of my being. I kissed her again, shaking her gently until she opened her eyes. I told her we had arrived at a fitting place for rest.

She looked around, groggy and sleepy, as I helped her to her feet. Then, she blamed herself for sleeping too deeply while I labored carrying her. In truth, I kissed her again as she steadied herself, looking so lovely with sleep lingering in her eyes. She kissed me back with profound tenderness, barely fully awake, declaring with a hint of childish abandonment that she loved me utterly and forever.

Afterward, I climbed to the topmost cave while she paced slightly, ensuring she achieved full wakefulness. This was vital; she would need all her faculties to navigate safely upward to the shelter.

When I entered the cave, I found it sweet and dry, bringing immediate joy to my heart. A warmth emanated from a fire hidden within the rocks nearby. Light from the external fire-hole reflected inward, confirming this as a safe haven for our slumber.

Descending to Naani, I declared the cave suitable. With her assistance, we climbed in and felt a surge of safety and happiness.

Before sleeping, I released the scrip and pouch, took the straps, and descended into the gorge. I retrieved a heavy boulder—so large I could barely manage it—and strapped it to my back. Returning to the cave, the Maid watched anxiously, fearing I might slip. Once inside, I placed the boulder at the cave's entrance, balancing it precariously on the edge so that a single touch would send it tumbling down.

My plan was simple: any beast or monster attempting to climb up while we slept would dislodge the rock. While the falling stone might harm the intruder, its primary purpose was to wake me instantly with its crash.

Turning to rest, I saw the Maid had spread her cloak upon the rock for us to sleep on. No longer needed for warmth, given the cave's heat, the cloak formed a natural, sweet bedside arrangement. She had created a space for us to lie together, preserving her modesty yet fulfilling a natural desire to be close to me, perhaps unconsciously driven by her heart.

In truth, I loved her dearly.

Naani indicated where I should lie on my side. Upon my compliance, she knelt, kissed me soberly and lovingly on the lips, and lay down beside me under the cloak. Within a minute, I believed, we were both fast asleep.

Twelve hours later, I awoke to the fizzing of water. The Maid was not beside me but was preparing our simple meal and drink. Laughing sweetly and tenderly, she expressed her joy at having me awake. She leaned over, kissing me brightly and lovingly.

Afterward, kneeling beside me, she gazed at me with such tenderness that I knew immediately she had been watching me sleep, almost maternally. How I knew, I cannot say, unless my spirit understood hers, or her thoughts spoke directly to mine.

Truly, I needed such love. I raised my arms to embrace her. She did not refuse, snuggling into my arms with such sweet happiness and genuine delight that it was evident her entire being craved closeness to me. Yet, remembering my armor, I hesitated. Its sternness might hurt her delicate form. Perhaps, however, the hardness appealed to her womanly heart, or perhaps it lacked charm.

Presently, she withdrew, and I released her arms willingly, mindful of granting her maiden freedom. I remained watchful, honest, and careful not to overthink my duty.

The Maid moved to the water and tablets. Rising, I checked the boulder at the cave mouth. She called out for me to lie back, promising to "spoil" me—that is, to indulge me. She assured me the boulder was secure, no creatures were visible in the gorge (she had checked thoroughly), and I need not worry about monsters.

Obeying, I lay back, enjoying the indulgence. She brought the water and tablets. Refusing to let me rise, she took my head onto her lap, kissing me daintily. Happy to be so cherished, I accepted her care. She offered me a tablet, then touched another to her own lips. We ate and drank, happy as children, our hearts at ease.

We consumed our second tablets similarly, followed by water. When finished, Naani urged me to stand. I rose, nearly crying out in pain. My body was stiff and sore from fighting, injured worse than I realized by the Four-Armed Man.

Perceiving my distress, Naani had prepared ointment. She assisted me in removing my armor and garments, revealing bruises across my body from the Yellow Beast-Man's violence. Her eyes held such tenderness as she viewed my wounds that I glowed with contentment. She laid me down, wrapped me in the cloak with loving gravity, caring for me like a mother to a child. For a long hour, she massaged me skillfully and gently until I was refreshed. Truly, she was a lovely, wise maiden.

As she tended me, I listened to the low muttering of the fire-hole at the gorge's base, feeling like a happy child clothed in love and guided by wisdom.

When her care ended, she handed me her hands to help me rise. My limbs felt restored, moving with little pain, filling me with renewed courage. Having been the protector yet feeling helpless due to injury, this relief was immense. Testing my strength, I searched for my garments. The Maid produced my spare body-vest from her pouch. Before giving it to me, she paused, standing before me with sweet admiration at the great, hardened muscles of my arms.

Indeed, I was strong, trained by the exercises of the Mighty Pyramid, shaped by the Mother who bore me, and priding myself on my health throughout my life. Her admiration was sweet, and I felt deceived if I claimed otherwise. In a moment, she dropped the vest and held out her hands for me to embrace her.

Taking her into my arms, I felt great gladness mixed with humility, knowing I was unworthy of holding such a precious girl. My heart was young, loving her dearly. She lay quietly and happily for a moment, pressing her face against my muscular chest. Soon, she slipped free, helping me dress and armor myself.

Once finished, she stepped back, looking half-shy, half-sweet. Suddenly, she approached, placing her hands on my shoulders, lowering her eyelids to peek up at me. In an instant, before I could react, she fell to her knees and wept. I knelt swiftly beside her.

I asked not why she wept. I sensed her joy, her sweet trouble regarding her man. She was a true woman, worshipping me with strange humility and shyness, yet needing closeness and possessing calm wisdom. All trembled together in her heart. I knew I was truly a hero to her, though ordinary to others. My heart swelled with pride and humility simultaneously, raising me up while making me feel dreadfully unworthy. Yet, I made no pretense of self-discredit. Resolved to earn her respect, I remained natural and truthful, accepting her worship without foolish denial, for she was utterly mine.
