His footsteps echoed off the ancient stone walls, a rhythmic accompaniment to the symphony of thoughts playing in his mind. With each step, the remnants of his earlier anxiety dissipated, replaced by an increasing sense of excitement and anticipation. He was on his way to visit Romola, and the prospect of seeing her filled him with an insatiable longing.

Tito's affection for Romola was a deep and abiding thing, rooted in the simple pleasures of their time together. He cherished the way she laughed at his jokes, the way her eyes sparkled when she spoke of her passions, and the way her hands moved deftly as she worked with the dough, shaping it into delicate pastries that smelled of rose petals and honey. As he approached the door to her family's humble abode, he felt his heart swell with emotion, his senses heightened in preparation for the moment they would be reunited.
