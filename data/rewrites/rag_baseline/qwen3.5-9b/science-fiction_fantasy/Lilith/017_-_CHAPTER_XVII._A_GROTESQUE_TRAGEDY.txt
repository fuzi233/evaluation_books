I had not taken ten paces when a grotesque anomaly halted my stride. Drawing closer, I discerned a carriage of archaic design, its wood mouldering yet miraculously upright upon heavy iron wheels. Flanking the central pole lay the skeletal remains of two horses, their grim white skulls pointing skyward as shriveled reins connected to the tattered hammer-cloth of a coachman's skeleton seated behind them. Both doors had collapsed inward, revealing the interior where two additional skeletons reclined in opposite corners.

Even as I observed, the scene shifted with unnatural violence. The figures jerked awake, emitting a rattling cascade of bone-on-bone impacts. Each leaped from its respective doorframe. One collapsed immediately, lying prone; the other stood momentarily, its structure trembling violently before it managed, with agonizing slowness, to creep along the carriage's edge, gripping the rear frame for support. Its leg-bones appeared insufficient to bear its weight, forcing it to kneel beside its partner in a desperate attempt to lift the fallen comrade, nearly toppling itself in the exertion. Eventually, the prostrate figure rallied, rising to a seated position through sheer muscular tension. It rotated its yellowed cranium this way and that, then, ignoring its neighbor, scrambled to its feet by clutching the spokes of the hind wheel. Standing half-erect, back-to-back, it gripped one of its own knee-joints with both hands. With similar difficulty and a series of contortions, the kneeling figure followed suit and addressed its companion.

"Have you injured yourself, my lord?" the voice drifted, indistinct and hollow, as if carried on a spectral gale.

"Yes, I have," the other replied in a matching but coarser tone. "You offered no assistance, and this cursed knee is dislocated!"

"I did my utmost, my lord."

"No doubt, my lady, for the injury was severe! I feared I would never regain my footing—bless my soul, madam, are you also exposed in your bones?"

She glanced at her own form. "I have nothing else to be exposed in," she retorted. "And you, at least, cannot complain! But what on earth signifies this? Am I dreaming?"

"You may be dreaming, madam—I cannot say; but this knee prohibits me from entertaining such illusions. Ha! I too perceive that I possess nothing but bones to walk in! Yet, not entirely unsuitable for a man, provided they are not *my* bones! Every ache surpasses the other, though this loose knee reigns supreme. The bedding must have been damp—and I sufficiently intoxicated to remain unaware!"

"Likely, my lord of Cokayne!"

"What? What! Do you imply I am dreaming amidst these pains? How do you know the title bestowed upon me by my rowdy associates? I recall you not! Regardless, you have no standing to address me familiarly! My name is—I am Lord—tut, tut! What do you call me when I am... when you are sober? I cannot—at the moment. Why, what *is* my name? I must have been profoundly drunk before retiring! I frequently am!"

"You visit so rarely that I do not know, my lord; but I accept your word regarding that!"

"I hope so!"

"If for no other reason!"

"Hmph! I never lied in my entire existence!"

"You never uttered a single truth to me, my lord!"

"On my honor! Why, I never beheld this woman prior to tonight!"

"You knew me well enough to fabricate a lie directed at me, my lord!"

"It appears I am beginning to dream that we have met before, yet upon my oath, I recognize nothing of you! Stripped of attire, how is one to identify identity? One certainty I possess—that I have never seen you so thoroughly undressed! By heavens, I hold no memory of you!"

"I am pleased to hear it; my memories of you are consequently less repugnant. Good morning, my lord!"

She turned away, hobbling with a rhythmic clatter, halting after a few steps. "You are as callous as any other woman, madam! In this infernal locale, where shall I locate my valet? What was the accursed name I used to invoke this fool?"

He twisted his bare skull on its creaking axis, still clutching his knee with both hands. "I shall serve as your valet for once, my lady," she countered, pivoting back to him. "What task requires my attention? It proves difficult to ascertain!"

"Tie my leg back on, naturally, you idiot! Cannot you observe it is nearly severed? Alas, my dancing days are concluded!"

Scanning her surroundings with vacant sockets, she located a fragment of fibrous grass and proceeded to bind the adjacent segments forming the knee. Upon completion, he executed a few cautious, tentative steps.

"You formerly stamped with greater distinction, my lord!" she remarked as she rose.

"Eh? What! Now that I examine you anew, it strikes me I once harbored hatred for you! Eh?"

"Naturally, my lord! You detested numerous individuals—your wife, certainly, among them!"

"Ah, I begin to recall... I begin to remember—but I must have been absent for an extended period! Truly, I forget! There! Your wretched, miserable scrap of grass is fracturing!"

"We functioned quite adequately together—eh?"

"Not that I recall. The sole joyous intervals in your company occurred during the first week of our matrimony."

"Is that the manner of it? Ha, ha! Well, it is concluded now, thank goodness!"

"I yearn to believe it! Why were we seated together in that carriage? It evokes dread!"

"I surmise we were divorced, my lady!"

"Hardly sufficient; we remain united!"

"A tragic verity, yet remediable: the forest possesses considerable expanse!"

"I doubt! I doubt!"

"I regret I cannot offer a compliment without resorting to falsehood. Judging by your silhouette and pallor, you have endured hardship since our last encounter! I dare not claim I am *quite* so naked as your ladyship—I beg your pardon, madam! I trust you accept this as jesting within a dream! Be it dreaming or waking, appearances are identical! Certainty is impossible, rendering knowledge moot! Life instructs every fool in this lesson!"

"It has taught me the folly of loving you!"

"You were not solitary in this error! Women possessed a propensity to fall in love with me; I had simply forgotten you belonged to that category!"

"I did love you, my lord—a little—at one juncture!"

"There lay your mistake, my lady! You ought to have loved me intensely, devotedly, savagely, eternally! Thus, I would have grown weary of you sooner, sparing you subsequent hatred! Let past grievances remain in the past! WHERE are we? Locality constitutes the inquiry! To be or not to be is NOT the question!"

"We reside in the other realm, I presume!"

"Granted—but which realm, or what variety thereof? This cannot be Hell!"

"It must be! Marriage exists herein! You and I are damned within each other's embrace."

"Then I differ from Othello, damned within a virtuous spouse! Oh, I recall my Shakespeare, madam!"

Seizing a broken branch fallen into the underbrush, she stabilized herself, tossing her skull lightly as she departed. "Hand me that staff," commanded her erstwhile husband. "I require it more than you."

She offered no response. "Do you intend to compel me to beg for it?"

"Not at all, my lord. I intend to retain it," she replied, accelerating her slow retreat. "Deliver it to me immediately; I demand it."

"Regrettably, I believe I require it equally!" the lady responded, quickening her pace until the snapping of her joints and the clinking of her bones grew sharper.

He attempted to pursue, nearly losing balance; the grass binding his knee had ruptured, and he halted with an oath, seizing his limb again. "Approach and rebind it properly!" he thundered, though his voice emerged as a high-pitched whistle.

She paused and regarded him. "Come and bind it instantly!" he reiterated.

She advanced a step further. "I swear I shall not touch you!" he shrieked.

"Swear away, my lord! No witness exists here to validate your oaths. However, refrain from losing your temper, lest you dismantle yourself entirely. Furthermore, identifying sufficient cordage to restrain your fractured joints exceeds my capabilities."

Returning, she knelt beside him once more, first placing the disputed staff beyond his grasp and within hers. The instant she completed the rebinding, he lunged, seemingly intending to seize her by the hair, but his rigid fingers slipped off her smooth cranial dome.

"Disgusting!" he muttered, seizing her upper arm-bone instead.

"You will fracture it!" she warned, looking upward from her knees.

"I shall, then!" he answered, straining with force.

"I shall not rebind your leg the next time it loosens!" she threatened.

He twisted her arm viciously, yet fortunately, her skeletal integrity surpassed his. She extended her free hand toward the broken branch. "Correct: extend the staff to me!" he grinned.
