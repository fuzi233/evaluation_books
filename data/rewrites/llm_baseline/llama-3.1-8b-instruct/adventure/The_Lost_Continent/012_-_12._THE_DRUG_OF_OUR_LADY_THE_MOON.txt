As the sun dipped towards the horizon, casting a fiery glow across the turbulent waters, Phorenice wrung the water from her garments, her slender form shivering in the chill evening air. "Deucalion, use your strength to propel us towards the harbour entrance," she called out, her voice carrying above the lapping waves. "I long to return to the warmth and comfort of my quarters, and I fear the merchant who lent me these fine clothes will not be pleased to see them so besmirched."

She shot me a sideways glance, her bright red hair gleaming in the fading light. "Do you think me wise in coming to this place unescorted?" Her eyes sparkled with amusement, and I couldn't help but be drawn in by her infectious charm. "The Empress can do no wrong," I replied, quoting the familiar phrase with a hint of a smile.

Phorenice laughed, her eyes dancing with mirth. "At least I've proven myself capable in a fight," she said, her gaze lingering on mine. "You've seen my skills with a sword, Deucalion. And I must admit, I've found your reserve quite... intriguing." She leaned in, her voice taking on a conspiratorial tone. "I'm glad I decided to test your mettle, for I believe we could make a formidable team, don't you?"

I couldn't help but think of Nais, another woman who had captured my heart with her courage and compassion. But Phorenice was different – her boldness and confidence drew me in, even as I struggled to reconcile her actions with the vows I'd sworn as a priest. As I rowed the fishing boat through the choppy waters, I felt a sense of trepidation wash over me. Was I truly prepared to serve as the Empress's husband, and to defend her rule against all comers?

The beach of the fishers, where we'd earlier engaged in our mock battle, was shielded from the open sea by a jagged chain of rocks that sprouted from the depths like skeletal fingers. The fishers knew the hidden passages, and could navigate the treacherous waters with ease. But I was a stranger here, and I felt a shiver run down my spine as I guided the boat through the darkening waters.
