The Visible Madonna.

As the last of the crowd dispersed, Romola hastened down the worn stone steps of San Stefano, her eyes scanning the crowded Piazza for a glimpse of Cecco. Instead, she spotted Baldassarre standing alone against the church door, his weathered face illuminated by the faint glow of a nearby torch. He clutched a worn horn-cup in one hand, its surface etched with the symbols of a long-forgotten craft. Romola's heart quickened as she approached him, sensing a change in his demeanor – a fierce intensity that seemed to have burst forth like a storm cloud on a summer's day.

Baldassarre's gaze locked onto hers, and for an instant, Romola felt the weight of his fury. She took a deep breath, her thoughts racing with possibilities. Could this be the man who had wronged her husband? If so, perhaps she could finally lay her doubts to rest by confronting him. She extended a gentle smile, hoping to calm the tempest brewing within him.

"If you'll come with me," she said, "I can offer you shelter and nourishment until you've regained your strength. Will you come?"

Baldassarre's voice was low and gravelly, like the rustling of dry leaves. "Yes, I need to gather my strength," he muttered, his words hanging in the air like the sweet scent of freshly baked bread.

Romola's eyes sparkled with kindness as she beckoned him to follow her. Together, they navigated the winding streets, their footsteps echoing off the ancient buildings. As they crossed the Ponte Rubaconte, the Arno River glinting in the moonlight below, Romola asked, "I think you're not a native of Florence."

Baldassarre's eyes darted towards her, his expression guarded. For a moment, Romola thought she saw a flicker of unease, but it was quickly extinguished, leaving behind a mask of stoic indifference.

"I'm a wandering soul," he replied, his voice barely above a whisper.

Romola's gaze lingered on him, her mind whirling with questions. Why did he seem so hesitant to reveal himself? What secrets did he keep hidden behind those piercing eyes? As they walked in silence, the shadows cast by the flickering torches dancing across the cobblestones, Romola's thoughts turned to her husband, her heart heavy with uncertainty.
