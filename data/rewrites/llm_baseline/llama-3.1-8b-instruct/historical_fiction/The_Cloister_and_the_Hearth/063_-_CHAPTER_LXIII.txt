As the sun dipped below the rooftops, casting a golden glow over the cobblestone streets of Rome, a darkness crept over the heart of a young man once consumed by an all-encompassing passion for Margaret. His love, now lost, left him drifting aimlessly, a leaf blown by the winds of despair and frustration. The weight of his self-imposed penance, the trials he had endured, and the virtues he had cultivated, all seemed futile, a cruel joke, as he tormented himself with the thought of what could have been if he had simply stayed by Margaret's side.

The bitter taste of regret mingled with the poison of desperation, fueling a reckless abandon that drove him headlong into the vices of his youth. Wine, women, and excess became his solace, a fleeting escape from the anguish that gnawed at his very soul. The lavish sums he had set aside for Margaret's sake now fueled a life of debauchery, and he soon found himself at the helm of a merry band of companions, bound together by their shared pursuit of hedonism.

As his morals began to crumble, his heart hardened, and he grew increasingly isolated from those who had once cared for him. His old landlady's gentle warnings and remonstrances only served to deepen his resentment, and he eventually moved to a new part of town, seeking refuge from the painful memories and unwelcome counsel. Yet even in his isolation, he couldn't shake the demons that haunted him, and his hand, once skilled in the art of writing, now faltered, unable to capture the thoughts that swirled within.

The passage of time saw him surrender his quill, opting instead for the brush, and taking up the art of capturing likenesses in color. But even this creative outlet was abandoned whenever his companions came calling, beckoning him back into the world of excess and indulgence. In their company, he descended further into the depths of depravity, seeking solace in the fleeting high of oblivion.
