As the Christmas twilight deepened, the festive throngs of Bideford began to disperse, their footsteps echoing off the thatched roofs as they made their way home to warmth and merriment. The evening song had concluded, and the townspeople were already anticipating the night's festivities – the savory aromas wafting from kitchens, the sound of laughter and music drifting from doorways, and the promise of sweet treats to come. Amidst this joyful chaos, however, one figure moved with purpose, her dark muffler wrapped tightly around her face, her maid following closely behind.

As she hastened towards the causeway, Sir Richard Grenville and his wife intercepted her, their voices carrying across the fading light. "Join us for Christmas dinner, Mrs. Leigh," Lady Grenville invited, her words warm with genuine hospitality. Mrs. Leigh's smile was tinged with melancholy as she laid a hand on Lady Grenville's arm, her gaze drifting westward. "I fear I could not partake in the merriment while that sound remains in my ears."

The assembled company turned to follow her gaze, their faces tilted upwards as if searching for a sign in the rapidly darkening sky. The blue above seemed to be surrendering to a soft gray, punctuated by faint stars beginning to twinkle like scattered jewels. However, to the west, where the hills and woods of Raleigh merged with those of Abbotsham, the sky took on a different hue – delicate wisps of cloud were tinted with an ethereal glow, reminiscent of the shimmering colors of a dying dolphin. Below, a broad swathe of emerald stretched across the horizon, a testament to the sun's recent departure. Yet what was the sound that weighed heavily on Mrs. Leigh's heart? The others, their senses dulled by the cacophony of the town, had not perceived it until that moment. Now, though, the stillness was almost palpable – the air vibrated with a low, sonorous hum, a murmur that seemed to emanate from the very earth itself, like the rumble of countless wheels, the tread of endless legions, or – what it was – the thunderous crash of a titanic wave crashing against the rocky shoreline.
