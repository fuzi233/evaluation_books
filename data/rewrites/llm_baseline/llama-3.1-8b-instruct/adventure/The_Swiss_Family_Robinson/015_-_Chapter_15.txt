As the morning sun cast its golden rays upon the tranquil waters of the ocean, Fritz stood poised at the water's edge, clad in his makeshift swimming attire – a hastily assembled ensemble of trousers, jacket, and cap. His mother's design, though laughable in its amateurishness, couldn't conceal the eagerness that burned within him. The cajack, a diminutive vessel of our handcrafted creation, bobbed gently on the waves as Fritz climbed aboard, his paddles at the ready. With a few swift strokes, he set off on his maiden voyage, executing intricate maneuvers with an air of practiced ease. The whirling motion sent his cap askew, and his mother's eyes widened in a mixture of alarm and mirth as she beheld her son transformed into a human pinball, careening across the surface of the water.

Undeterred by his initial clumsiness, Fritz continued to paddle with increasing confidence, his form blurring as he merged with the rhythmic motion of the sea. As he ventured further from the shore, the gentle currents swept him toward the open waters of the Jackal River. My wife's voice rose above the din of the waves, her words laced with a mix of concern and amusement as she exhorted us to keep a watchful eye on our wayward offspring. "That wretched, ephemeral craft will surely meet its demise!" she exclaimed, her words dripping with a mixture of trepidation and jest.

Without hesitation, I launched the larger boat, joining Ernest and Jack in pursuit of Fritz as he vanished into the distance. The ocean's vast expanse stretched before us like an uncharted text, waiting to be deciphered. Our cries of encouragement were met with an answering shout from the cajack, its tiny form dancing across the waves like a sprite. We tracked the sound of Fritz's laughter, our boat slicing through the water with a soothing rhythm, until we reached the spot where the wreck of the previous ship had once lain. Scanning the horizon, we spotted a wispy plume of smoke rising from the direction of the cajack, accompanied by the sharp report of a pistol shot. In response, we fired a signal shot, which was promptly returned, and we altered our course to intercept the errant vessel.
