His brother's departure had left an aching void, like a note played on a forgotten instrument. The sound of his own footsteps seemed to echo off the buildings, a reminder of the emptiness within. He would glance at the door to the small room his brother had occupied, the half-burned papers in the grate a poignant reminder of Harry's presence. A pang of sadness stirred within him, like the faint resonance of a string plucked too softly.

Hetty's sorrow weighed heavily on her, and George couldn't help but feel a twinge of selfishness for his own joy. The fire crackled in the hearth, casting flickering shadows on the walls as they spoke in hushed tones.
