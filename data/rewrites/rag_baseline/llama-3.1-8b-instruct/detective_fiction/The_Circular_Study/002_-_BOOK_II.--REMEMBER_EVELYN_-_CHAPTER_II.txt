As the last wisps of fog surrendered to the morning sun, Lady Violet Lebas gazed out at the tranquil scene unfolding before her in the garden of her family's London townhouse. The air vibrated with the soft murmur of conversation and the clinking of fine china, a symphony of domesticity that belied the turmoil brewing within her. The previous evening's letter from Mr. Andrew Lang still lingered in her thoughts, its words echoing through her mind like a refrain. His words, always so full of wonder and curiosity, had awakened a sense of restlessness within her, a feeling that the world beyond her narrow circle of acquaintances was vast and mysterious.

As she turned back to her correspondence, her thoughts strayed to the intricate web of relationships that bound their social circle together. Her own family's history, with its threads of love and deceit, was a tapestry rich with secrets and silences. Her grandmother, Evelyn, had been a woman of remarkable strength and beauty, her life a testament to the power of determination and cunning. And yet, it was a story shrouded in mystery, a tale of lost love and hidden truths that had been passed down through generations, whispered in hushed tones and veiled in secrecy.

Just then, a knock at the door broke the spell, and Lady Violet's maid, Alice, entered with a hesitant expression. "Milady, I've been instructed to inform you that Detective Gryce has arrived. He requests a private audience with you, milady."

Lady Violet's heart quickened as she rose from her seat, her mind racing with possibilities. What could the detective want with her? As she followed Alice to the study, she couldn't shake the feeling that the world beyond her doorstep was about to intrude upon her carefully constructed existence.

Upon entering the study, Lady Violet found Detective Gryce standing by the fireplace, his eyes fixed on the figure of her great-grandfather, Sir Malcolm, who lay stretched out on the floor. The atmosphere was heavy with an almost palpable sense of stillness, the only sound the faint ticking of the clock on the mantle. Sir Malcolm's face, frozen in a look of profound reproach, seemed to hold a secret, one that only the most discerning observer might uncover.
