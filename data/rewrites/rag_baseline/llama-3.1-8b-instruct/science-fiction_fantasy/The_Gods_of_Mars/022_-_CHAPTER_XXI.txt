Through Flood and Flame

My mind was set ablaze with the urgency of Yersted's words. We had to infiltrate the Temple of Issus undetected before the forces of Tars Tarkus stormed its gates at dawn. Within those forsaken walls, I was certain that I could outwit the guards of Issus and rescue my beloved, for I had assembled a formidable force at my back.

As Carthoris and the others joined me, we began the perilous journey through the submerged passage, navigating the gangways that connected the temple's watery tunnel to the pits of Issus. This arduous task required multiple trips, but finally, we stood together, five thousand strong, at the threshold of our final quest – seasoned warriors of the mighty red men of Barsoom.

Carthoris, the sole expert on the temple's labyrinthine tunnels, knew that we couldn't divide our forces and assault the temple from multiple angles. So, he took the lead, guiding us swiftly through the winding corridors as we made our way towards the temple's central hub.

Just as we prepared to exit the pool and enter the corridor, an officer alerted me to the turbulent waters beneath our submarine. Initially, the disturbance seemed caused by a massive object lurking beneath the surface, and I suspected another submarine was closing in on us. However, as the water level continued to rise, it became clear that the Omean's pumps had been disabled, and the chamber was filling with water at an alarming rate.

Carthoris was the first to grasp the dire implications of the situation. His voice cut through the chaos, urging us to hasten our pace. "We must reach the upper levels of the pits ahead of the flood," he cried. "Delay will mean our doom. We'll be trapped like rats in a drowning cage."

Without hesitation, he sprinted into the corridor, followed by the soldiers in tight formation. Each company entered the passage only at the command of its leader, their discipline unwavering despite the rising waters. By the time the last company departed, the water had reached ankle-deep, and the men's unease was palpable. The red Martians, unaccustomed to navigating such treacherous depths, showed remarkable bravery and composure.
