The morning fog clung to the city like a damp shroud, its tendrils curling around the windows of Deronda's chambers in Park Lane. As he stirred the embers of last night's fire, a faint scent of pipe smoke and damp wool wafted through the air, mingling with the faint tang of coal smoke from the chimneys outside. Deronda's eyes, still heavy from the previous night's reading, slowly adjusted to the dim light, and he made his way to the door, where a note from Hans Meyrick awaited him.

The scribbled message, penned in a hand that danced across the page like a wild vine, spoke of a new life unfolding in Chelsea, of a space to be claimed and a canvas to be filled. Deronda smiled to himself as he read the words, knowing that Hans's zeal for art was matched only by his zeal for life. He recalled their Cambridge days, when Hans's irrepressible spirit had drawn him in, like a moth to flame, and their bond had grown strong enough to weather the storms of distance and time.

As he entered the sitting room, Deronda was struck by the transformation that had taken place. The space, once a quiet refuge, now pulsed with energy, its walls covered in a riot of sketches and artifacts from Hans's Roman sojourn. The lower half of the windows was shrouded in a makeshift screen, casting the room in a warm, golden light that seemed to emanate from the very walls themselves. Hans, perched on a stool amidst the chaos, looked up with a radiant smile, his hair longer and his face more etched than Deronda remembered, his voice rising and falling in a cadence that was both familiar and yet, somehow, new.

"I knew you'd want to see my treasures," Hans exclaimed, as Deronda approached, his eyes scanning the room with a mixture of wonder and trepidation. "I've found a new home in Chelsea, just a stone's throw from my mother and sisters. But I'm not waiting to settle in – the seed of creation is stirring within me, and I must let it bloom."

Deronda's smile faltered for a moment, his gaze drifting to the sketches propped against the bookcase – five rapid, gestural studies of the same face, each one a different facet of the same enigmatic whole.
