In the verdant expanse of the Santa Clara Valley, where the scent of ripening grapes hung heavy in the air, Buck's world was one of unbridled freedom. His home, Judge Miller's estate, sprawled across the landscape like a tapestry woven from threads of emerald and gold. Gravelled driveways meandered through the lawns, leading to a grand veranda that wrapped around the house, its weathered wood a testament to the seasons' gentle touch. Beyond the house, the property unfolded like a canvas of possibility: stables, servants' cottages, outhouses, and orchards, each one a reminder of the bounty that came with living on this fertile land.

As the son of Elmo, a majestic St. Bernard, Buck had grown accustomed to the finer things in life. His mother, Shep, a sturdy Scotch shepherd dog, may not have contributed to his size – he weighed a respectable one hundred and forty pounds – but her bloodline instilled in him a sense of nobility. Over the years, Buck had lived a life of ease, his days filled with leisurely strolls with the Judge's family, hunting excursions, and, of course, time spent by the Judge's side, basking in the warmth of the library fire. He was a king among dogs, his dominion extending far beyond the confines of the house to the gardens, the paddocks, and even the distant berry patches.

Despite his privileged existence, Buck's spirit remained untamed. He possessed a deep-seated love for the outdoors, which his father had instilled in him. Together, they would venture into the wilderness, where Buck's senses would come alive, and his heart would pound with excitement. This connection to nature had kept his body lean and strong, his muscles honed from the countless hours spent by the water's edge. As the seasons passed, Buck's pride in himself grew, and he became a confident, if not slightly self-assured, member of the household.

In the autumn of 1897, a time of great change was brewing, not just for Buck, but for all the dogs of the Pacific Northwest. The Klondike strike had begun, drawing men from far and wide to the frozen wilderness in search of gold. Little did Buck know, his life was about to take a drastic turn, one that would challenge everything he thought he knew about himself and the world around him.
