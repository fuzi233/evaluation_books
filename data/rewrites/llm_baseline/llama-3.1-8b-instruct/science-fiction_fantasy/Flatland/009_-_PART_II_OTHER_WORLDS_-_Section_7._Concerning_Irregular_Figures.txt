In the realm of Flatland, a notion so ingrained it's often overlooked, lies the fundamental truth that every citizen is born with a Regular Figure – a shape of perfect symmetry and balance. This conviction, like a gentle stream, flows through the very fabric of our society, shaping the way we interact, the way we think, and the way we live. Every Woman, for instance, is a straight line, her essence distilled into a single, unbroken stroke. Artisans and Soldiers, with their two equal sides, embody the sturdy foundations of our world. Tradesmen, with three sides in harmony, represent the industry and practicality that drives our economy. And Lawyers, like myself, with four sides in perfect alignment, stand as beacons of wisdom and justice.

As we grow and mature, our shapes expand, but the principle remains the same. A newborn Female measures just an inch in length, while a mature Woman stretches to a foot or more. For males, their combined side lengths, when added together, rarely exceed two feet. Yet, it's not the size of our figures that matters, but their equality. The harmony of our shapes is what allows us to move through life with ease, to navigate the intricate dance of social interactions, and to build the complex tapestry of our community.

Imagine, if you will, a world where our sides are unequal, where our angles are unpredictable, and our shapes shift and twist like living things. In such a realm, Sight Recognition would be reduced to a mere guesswork, and Feeling would struggle to keep pace. We'd be forced to rely on laborious experiments to discern the angles of those around us, and our lives would be consumed by the endless task of verification. Our social bonds would fray, our relationships would crumble, and the very fabric of our society would begin to unravel.

Consider, if you will, a chance encounter with a Tradesman in the street. Normally, a glance at his angles and sides would be enough to establish his identity and invite him into your home for a meal. But what if he were to appear with a shape that defied all conventions, a parallelogram with a diagonal of twelve or thirteen inches? Would you know how to react? Would you be able to trust your instincts, or would you be paralyzed by uncertainty?
