It was my firm intention to remain vigilant throughout the night, a strategic necessity given the volatile environmental conditions surrounding our encampment. However, physiological fatigue imposed a countermanding directive; exhaustion of both nerve and muscle decreed otherwise, allowing sleep to descend with the comforting efficacy of a heavy, oblivious blanket. The rapid onset of somnolence in my companion acted as a catalytic agent, accelerating the transition into unconsciousness. Initially, he exhibited signs of acute anxiety, fidgeting incessantly and maintaining an upright posture while interrogating me regarding auditory stimuli—"Did you hear this?" or "Did you hear that?" He shifted restlessly upon his cork mattress, reporting structural instability in the tent and a rising river level threatening the island's perimeter. Each time I ventured outside to verify these claims, I returned with data confirming the status quo, and eventually, his agitation subsided into stillness. His respiration normalized, culminating in the distinct acoustic signature of snoring—a phenomenon I noted as uniquely benign and soothing in this context. This specific auditory cue marked the final cognitive entry point before my own consciousness faded.

Awakening occurred via respiratory distress caused by a suffocating weight; the blanket had migrated over my face, compounded by an external pressure source. My initial hypothesis posited that my companion had displaced during REM cycles, rolling onto my sleeping position. Upon vocalizing this concern and assuming a seated posture, a realization crystallized: the tent was *encircled*. The previously intermittent auditory input had evolved into a continuous, multifaceted barrage of soft pattering, saturating the nocturnal atmosphere with a pervasive sense of dread. I escalated my vocalizations, but received no response; the rhythmic cadence of his snoring had vanished, and the tent flap remained depressed. This constituted a critical failure in camp security protocol. Crawling into the obscurity to re-secure the flap, I confirmed the absence of the Swedish companion. Seizing upon this deficit, I launched into a frantic sprint, propelled by a surge of adrenaline-induced agitation. Emerging from the canvas, I was immediately immersed in a dense, omnidirectional thrumming that originated from every sector of the celestial sphere simultaneously. This was the familiar frequency, now distorted into madness. The auditory landscape suggested the presence of a colossal swarm of invisible insects, thickening the atmospheric density to the point where pulmonary function became labored. Despite the physiological strain, the imperative to locate my friend superseded self-preservation. Dawn was imminent, casting a pale, whitish luminescence upward through a narrow aperture in the cloud cover. The wind remained static. Through the gloom, I discerned the silhouettes of the bush line, the riverbank, and the pale sandy expanse. Driven by urgency, I traversed the island erratically, screaming his name with maximum decibel output. However, the dense foliage of the willows absorbed the acoustic energy, while the ambient humming dampened transmission, restricting the effective range of my call to mere feet.

Navigating the undergrowth proved treacherous; I stumbled repeatedly, contending with exposed root systems and tearing against resistant branches. Suddenly, emerging onto the island's extremity, I observed a dark silhouette framed against the interface of water and sky. It was the Swede. Already, one foot hung precariously over the river's edge. Without hesitation, I lunged, wrapping my arms around his waist and exerting maximal force to drag him toward the shore. Naturally, he resisted violently, emitting a cacophony identical to the malevolent humming, interspersed with disjointed, foreign utterances concerning "entering inside" to "Them," and adopting "the path of water and wind." These phrases elicited a visceral reaction of horror and confusion, rendering them unintelligible in retrospect. Eventually, I succeeded in maneuvering him into the relative sanctuary of the tent, collapsing him onto the mattress where I maintained physical contact until the episode abated. The abruptness of the event, mirrored by the instantaneous cessation of the external humming and pattering, represented the most disorienting aspect of the ordeal. As he regained consciousness, the morning light filtering through the doorway illuminated his weary countenance. Addressing me with the innocence of a terrified child, he declared, "My life, old man—it's my life I owe you. But it's all over now anyhow. They've found a victim in our place!" He promptly succumbed to sleep beneath my gaze, collapsing into a state of healthy snoring as if the previous attempt at sacrificial drowning had never occurred. When sunlight roused him three hours later—periods of unceasing vigilance for me—he displayed complete amnesia regarding his suicidal impulse. Deeming inquiry dangerous, I refrained from questioning him. He arose naturally as the sun climbed a windless, scorching sky, proceeding immediately to prepare the fire for breakfast. Following him anxiously to the water's edge, I observed that he avoided plunging, limiting himself to a brief immersion while commenting on the increased thermal gradient of the water. "The river's falling at last," he remarked, expressing relief. "And the humming has stopped too," I countered. He regarded me with composure, evidently retaining memory of the surroundings but excluding the act of self-sacrifice. "Everything has stopped," he affirmed, pausing deliberately. Pressing for clarification, I asked, "Because 'They've found another victim'?" He nodded unequivocally. "Exactly," he replied, "exactly! I feel as positive of it as though... I feel quite safe again, I mean." Rising slowly, he surveyed the environment. Sunlight fractured into hot patches on the sand; the willows stood motionless. "Come," he commanded, "if we search, we shall locate it." He proceeded along the banks, probing sandy coves and backwaters with a stick, while I trailed closely behind. Presently, he exclaimed, "Ah! Ah!" The tonal quality of his voice triggered a resurgence of the preceding twenty-four hours' terror, prompting me to hasten alongside him. He indicated a substantial black object lodged partially in the water and partially on the sand, ensnared by tangled willow roots that prevented the current from displacing it. The location must have been submerged only hours prior. "Observe," he stated calmly, "the victim that facilitated our escape." Peering over his shoulder, I witnessed his stick resting upon the torso of a human male. Turning the body revealed a face buried in sediment. The individual had clearly perished by drowning, likely within the last few hours, suggesting the body drifted ashore around dawn—precisely when the trance concluded. "We must accord it a dignified interment," he suggested. "Presumably," I responded, shivering involuntarily despite the heat, struck by a chilling aura emanating from the drowned figure. The Swede glanced upward with an inscrutable expression before descending the bank. Noticing the current had stripped significant portions of clothing, leaving the neck and upper chest exposed, my companion halted midway, raising a hand in warning. Momentum carried me past his gesture; I collided with him, sending him leaping forward to regain balance. We tumbled onto the hard sand, our feet splashing into the water, and impacted the corpse with considerable force. The Swede emitted a piercing cry, and I recoiled as if discharged by a firearm. At the moment of contact, a resonant chord of humming erupted from the body's surface—a composite of multiple frequencies—that surged outward with the chaotic motion of airborne entities before dissipating upward into the sky, fading gradually until silence prevailed. The effect mimicked the disturbance of sentient, invisible organisms engaged in labor. My companion grasped me tightly, and I reciprocated, neither of us having recovered from the shock before observing the current's action. The flow rotated the corpse, releasing it from the willow entanglement. Moments later, the body flipped completely, presenting the deceased face upward, staring vacantly at the heavens. Positioned at the stream's margin, it was destined to be swept away. The Swede attempted to retrieve it, shouting incoherently about "proper burial," before dropping to his knees and covering his eyes. Rushing to his side, I perceived his vision: as the body swung into the current, the face and exposed torso oriented directly toward us, revealing skin and flesh indented with minute cavities, exquisitely formed and geometrically identical to the sand-funnels scattered across the island. "Their mark!" I heard my companion whisper hoarsely. "Their terrible mark!" Turning my gaze back to the river, the current had completed its task; the body was swept into the mid-stream, disappearing beyond our reach, rotating like an otter on the waves.
