The majestic vessel before us was a marvel of engineering, its sleek hull glinting in the fading light. Carthoris, Xodar, and Tars Tarkas stood beside me, their faces aglow with excitement, as they watched the arrival of the second and third ships. Their sister vessels soared above, their aerial scouts launching from the upper decks in a flurry of motion, plummeting towards the ground with deadly precision.

The air was alive with the whir of propellers and the hum of engines as the sailors closed in around us, their faces set with a mixture of curiosity and wariness. An officer stepped forward, his eyes scanning the group before locking onto Carthoris with a look of unbridled joy. With a cry of delight, he rushed forward, grasping the boy's shoulder and calling out his name.

"Carthoris, my Prince!" he exclaimed, his voice carrying across the clearing. "Kaor! Kaor! Hor Vastus greets the son of Dejah Thoris, Princess of Helium, and her husband, John Carter!" He paused, his eyes searching the surrounding area as if seeking some sign of the missing prince's companions. "Where have you been, O my Prince? All Helium has been plunged into darkness since the fateful day you departed our midst."

Carthoris smiled, his face radiant with the news he was about to share. "Grieve not, good Hor Vastus," he said, his voice filled with conviction. "For I bring not just myself back to lift the hearts of my mother and my people, but also one whom Barsoom holds dearer than life itself – John Carter, Prince of Helium!"

Hor Vastus's eyes widened in shock as they landed on me, and for an instant, I feared he might collapse from the weight of his surprise. "John Carter!" he whispered, his voice trembling with emotion. A look of trepidation crossed his features, and he hesitated, unsure of how to proceed. I knew the question he dared not ask, the secret he feared to uncover: the truth of my return from the depths of the Iss, the River of Mystery, and the shores of the Lost Sea of Korus.
