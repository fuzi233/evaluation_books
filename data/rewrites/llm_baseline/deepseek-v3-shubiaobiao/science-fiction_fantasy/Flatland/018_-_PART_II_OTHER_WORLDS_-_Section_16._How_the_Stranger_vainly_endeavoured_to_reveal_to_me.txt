The moment the echoes of my Wife’s Peace-cry faded, I turned to the Stranger, intent on offering hospitality—but what I saw froze me in place. His form defied reason. Though devoid of angles, his shape shifted ceaselessly, flickering in size and brilliance like no figure I had ever encountered. A chilling thought seized me: *Was this some rogue Isosceles, a murderer disguising himself as a Circle to infiltrate my home?*

The sitting room was clear of Fog, denying me the usual aid of Sight Recognition. Heart pounding, I lunged forward without ceremony. "You must permit me, Sir—" My hands confirmed my Wife’s claim. No angles. No imperfections. Only seamless curvature. A *perfect Circle*.

I circled him, my eye tracing his outline in awe. There was no doubt—he was a Circle, flawless and undeniable.

Our exchange began, his voice edged with impatience.

**Stranger:** "Have you felt enough? Or must I endure further inspection?"

**I:** "Forgive my discourtesy, illustrious Sir. My surprise—and this unanticipated visit—unsettled me. I beg discretion, especially toward my Wife. But before we proceed… might I ask whence you come?"

**Stranger:** "From *Space*, Sir. Where else?"

**I:** "My Lord, are we not already *in* Space?"

**Stranger:** "You know nothing of Space. Define it."

**I:** "Space is height and breadth, extended without limit."

**Stranger:** "Precisely your ignorance. You perceive but *two* dimensions. I bring word of a *Third*—length, breadth, and *height*."

**I:** "You jest. We name dimensions doubly—length and height, breadth and thickness—yet still speak of only two."

**Stranger:** "I speak of *three* dimensions, not names."

**I:** "Then show me this ‘Third Dimension.’ Point to it."

**Stranger:** "It lies where you cannot see—*above* and *below*, not North or South. You lack an eye to perceive it."

**I:** "An eye *inside* me? Absurd!"

**Stranger:** "No jest. I come from Spaceland, where I gazed *down* upon your plane. Your so-called ‘solids’—houses, chests, even your stomach—lay bare before me."

**I:** "Words are easily spoken, my Lord."

**Stranger:** "Then let proof suffice. I saw your Pentagonal sons in their rooms, your Hexagonal grandsons. I watched your youngest retire, leaving you and your Wife. Your Isosceles servants supped in the kitchen, the Page in the scullery. How else but from *above*?"

**I:** "A spy might glean such details."

Frustrated, he tried another approach.

**Stranger:** "When you behold a Line—your Wife—how many dimensions does she possess?"

**I:** "The ignorant call women Lines, but we Squares know better: they are slender Parallelograms—two dimensions, length and breadth."

**Stranger:** "Yet visibility demands *three*. A Line without ‘height’ would vanish."

**I:** "Brightness grants visibility. Do you call *brightness* a dimension?"

**Stranger:** "No. ‘Height’ is a dimension like length—only imperceptibly small to you."

**I:** "Then measure mine. Show me this ‘height.’"

**Stranger (muttering):** "How to convince him…?"

With sudden resolve, he declared:

**Stranger:** "You dwell on a Plane. Flatland is but the surface of a fluid. I am no mere Circle—I am a *Sphere*, infinite Circles stacked from Point to breadth. When I intersect your plane, you see only a slice: a Circle. Just as you appeared a Line to the King of Lineland, so I appear a Circle to you."

Before I could protest, he continued:

**Stranger:** "Watch. As I rise into Space, my section shrinks."

To my shock, his circumference dwindled—first to a tiny ring, then to a Point, before vanishing entirely. A voice echoed from nowhere:

**Stranger:** "Convinced? Now I return."

The Point expanded, restoring the Circle to full size. My mind reeled. Was this sorcery? Some trick of light?

**Stranger:** "Still perplexed? Then let Analogy instruct you. If a Point moves North, leaving luminous trail—what forms?"

**I:** "A Line."

**Stranger:** "And if that Line sweeps East-West?"

**I:** "A Square."

**Stranger:** "Now imagine the Square moving *upward*, each Point tracing its own Line *out* of Flatland. What arises?"

**I:** "Some… *Solid*?"

**Stranger:** "A *Cube*, with *eight* corners. Just as a Point (0) begets a Line (2), a Line begets a Square (4), so a Square begets a Cube (8). The pattern is clear!"

**I:** "And its… *sides*?"

**Stranger:** "Six—your ‘insides’ become its bounds."

Madness seized me. "Enough!" I cried. "Juggler, demon, or dream—I endure no more!"

And with that, I hurled myself at him.
