As the tropical dawn broke, casting a fiery glow across the waves, Amyas strode the deck, his tattered clothes and disheveled hair a testament to the turmoil that churned within him. His eyes, red-rimmed from lack of sleep and tears, burned with a fierce intensity, his heart heavy with a grief that seemed to weigh him down like an anchor. How to convey the depth of his anguish? It was a chasm that yawned open, a void that threatened to consume him whole. Those who had known the pain of losing a brother might grasp the contours of his sorrow, though even they could hardly comprehend its magnitude.

Amyas's thoughts careened wildly, fueled by a maddening desire for retribution. He would return to Guayra, raze the villa to the ground, and exact a terrible price from those who had taken Frank from him. "We can do it, lads!" he thundered, his words carrying across the waves as the ship pitched and rolled in the choppy seas. "If Drake could take Nombre de Dios, we can take La Guayra!" The crew's fervent cries echoed his own, a chorus of resolve that seemed to galvanize them all.

Cary's voice cut through the din, his words a gentle counterpoint to Amyas's frenzy. "We will have it, Amyas, and bring Frank home yet," he said, but Amyas shook his head, his expression a mask of despair. He knew, without quite knowing how he knew, that the ports of New Spain would never yield up Frank's lifeless form.

A glimmer of hope flickered within him, however, as he pointed towards the horizon, where three sails had appeared, their sails billowing in the morning breeze. "Behold, the first fruits of our vengeance," he declared, his voice steady, though his eyes betrayed a glimmer of desperation. "Those are the Spanish bloodhounds, the very ships we spotted off Guayra yesterday. Let us welcome them, as if they were a dozen."

The crew's murmurs of approval swelled into a jubilant cheer, and even the youngest among them felt their spirits lift at the prospect of battle.
