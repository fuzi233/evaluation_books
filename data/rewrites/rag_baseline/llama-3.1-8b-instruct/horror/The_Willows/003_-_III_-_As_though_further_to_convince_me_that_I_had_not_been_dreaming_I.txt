Time lost all meaning as I slipped in and out of a fitful doze, my mind oscillating between the realm of dreams and the harsh reality of the wind-swept island. It was only when the gentle rustling of leaves outside my makeshift shelter coalesced into a more insistent patter that I jolted awake, my senses on high alert.

At first, I attributed the din to the relentless wind, which had been buffeting the island throughout the night, its mournful moans and creaking branches weaving a disquieting melody. But as I strained to pinpoint the source of the sound, I began to suspect that something more deliberate was at play. The pattering grew louder, more insistent, and I felt a creeping sense of dread as I realized that I was not alone on the island.

My thoughts turned to the possibility of a falling branch, perhaps one dislodged by the tempestuous wind, which threatened to crush our fragile shelter. I threw off the covers and emerged into the chill night air, calling out to my companion to join me. But as I scanned the darkness, I was met with an eerie silence. The wind howled on, the stars twinkled above, and the river churned and foamed in the distance, yet I detected no sign of danger.

The cold, grey light of dawn was beginning to seep into the world, casting an otherworldly glow over the island. I felt a deep fatigue wash over me, a product of the sleepless night, yet my nerves remained on edge, my apprehensions simmering just below the surface. The memory of the ascending figures I had seen earlier still lingered, haunting me like a specter.
