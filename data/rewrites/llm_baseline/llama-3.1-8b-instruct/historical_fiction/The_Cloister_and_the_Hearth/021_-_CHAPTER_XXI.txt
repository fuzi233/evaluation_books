Dierich's puzzlement was evident on his face as he watched Gerard gain ground. It wasn't until he noticed the mule's sure-footedness that he realized the reason for Gerard's sudden speed. The youth's chaste living and robust health were also beginning to take their toll, and daylight was growing between the hunted and the hunters.

Determined to close the gap, Dierich made a final, desperate lunge, gaining two yards, but Gerard quietly reclaimed the lost ground. The pursuers' curses filled the air, and Martin's eyes lit up with encouragement. "Courage, Gerard! Courage, brave lad! They're straggling!" he called out.

As they approached a gentle slope, Gerard's advantages became even more pronounced. Youth, grit, and sobriety combined to propel him upward, while Dierich's age and fatigue began to weigh him down. "Our cake is dough," he panted, "take him dead if you can't alive." With a final burst of energy, Gerard left the pursuer behind, followed by the others, one by one, until only Eric Wouverman remained, doggedly pursuing despite his growing exhaustion.

Martin's voice rang out once more, "Hand on mane!" Gerard grasped the mule's mane, and the animal surged upward, its strength propelling them both up the steep incline. The sight of this maneuver broke the resolve of the last pursuer, who, now eighty yards behind and with his comrades far behind him, pulled up short and aimed his crossbow. As Gerard and the mule vanished over the crest of the hill, the bolt whizzed through the air, striking the trio with deadly precision.
