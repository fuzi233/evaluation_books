In the heart of the city, where the rain-soaked streets seemed to swallow the last rays of sunlight, Lady Harriet Wynter sat amidst the musty silence of her family's ancestral library. Her slender fingers danced across the pages of an old manuscript, the worn leather creaking softly as she turned the leaves. The scent of aged parchment and the faint tang of wood smoke wafted through the air, carrying with it whispers of the past. Outside, the city pulsed with the rhythm of rebellion, its citizens torn asunder by the great debate over the Colour Bill.

For three years, the agitation had raged, with the Polygons fighting valiantly alongside their fellow revolutionaries. But as the tide began to turn, the Squares and Pentagons, once neutral, found themselves swept up in the maelstrom. The usually tranquil households of the Circles were rent asunder by the ferocity of the conflict, as wives wearied their husbands with prayers to abandon their opposition, and some, in desperation, fell upon their innocent children and spouses, perishing in the carnage. The toll was staggering – twenty-three Circles lost to domestic discord, their once-peaceful lives reduced to ash and sorrow.

As the stakes grew higher, it seemed as though the Priests had no choice but to submit or face extinction. Yet, fate had other plans. In a twist of fortune that would change the course of history, a lowly Isosceles, barely four degrees above the average intellect, stumbled upon an unlikely role. He had, by chance, acquired the colors of a Dodecagon – or so the tale went – and, with a cunning born of desperation, donned them to woo a maiden from a noble Polygon family. The girl, the orphaned daughter of a revered ancestor, was deceived by the Isosceles' feigned charms and ultimately took her own life upon discovering the truth.

As news of the tragedy spread throughout the land, the Women's hearts were stirred to outrage and fear. They saw in the victim a reflection of their own vulnerabilities, and their sympathies turned against the Colour Bill. Not a few openly renounced their support, while others waited for a catalyst to join their ranks. Seizing the moment, the Circles convened an extraordinary assembly, summoning the reactionaries and securing the attendance of numerous Women.
