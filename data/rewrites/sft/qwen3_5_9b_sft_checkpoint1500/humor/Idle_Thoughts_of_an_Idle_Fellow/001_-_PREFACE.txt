Preface

A couple of close pals who got a sneak peek at these scribbles muttered that they weren't exactly trash, and a few distant relatives actually put money down on the promise to buy them once printed. So, feeling a bit like a man standing on a tightrope with his hands full of wet noodles, I've decided I don't have the heart to drag this out any further. If there hadn't been this tiny, faint whisper of public interest, I certainly wouldn't have dared serve up these "idle thoughts" as serious mental nourishment to the English-speaking world. After all, what does a modern reader want? They want a book to make their brains sprout wings, to teach them how to tie a bowline, and to lift their spirits above the clouds. This little volume? It couldn't even convince a cow to stand up straighter, let alone elevate humanity. I'd be lying through my teeth if I recommended it for anything so noble as self-improvement. My only honest suggestion is this: when you're bored stiff after devouring the "best hundred books" and need a palate cleanser, pick this up for twenty minutes. It won't fix your life, but it might just give you a nice, lazy sigh.

Here is what lies within these covers:

The Idle Thoughts of an Idle Fellow.
