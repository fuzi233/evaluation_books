"We discussed...foreign markets," I said finally, hoping the vague answer would suffice. But Penelope's gaze lingered on mine, searching for more information.

Just then, my wife and her sister, Miss Rachel, returned from their walk, and the conversation turned to Mr. Franklin's unexpected departure. "He must have come on the early train, and then decided to take a ride," I ventured, hoping to deflect their curiosity.
