As I lay on the bed, the princess's words swirled through my mind like the gentle lapping of the river against its banks. Her careless phrase had unraveled the mystery of her presence in the forest, revealing the perilous path she'd taken to reach me. I'd saved the life of her most feared adversary, the one being capable of threatening the Little Ones. The weight of this knowledge settled upon me like a shroud, and soon I succumbed to the allure of slumber, lulled by the sweetness of the wine she'd given me.

The next thing I knew, night had fallen, casting a soft, ethereal glow over the room. A lantern hung from the ceiling, its warm light dancing across the shadows. I felt weightless, drifting on a tranquil sea of twilight. Every sensation was a pleasure, and I was free from pain. Or so I thought.

A searing pain pierced my heart, and I gasped, my hand involuntarily clutching at my chest. The agony ebbed and flowed, leaving me paralyzed and helpless. I tried to move, to assert my will, but it was as if an unseen force held me in its grasp. The pain receded, but my body remained still, trapped in a state of limbo.

Suddenly, I felt a gentle pressure on my face, a soft hand guiding my head back onto the pillow. The weight that had suffocated me lifted, and I drew a deeper breath. As I opened my eyes, the princess stood above me, her gaze lost in thought. Her eyes shone like stars in the night sky, calm and serene. A hint of a smile played on her lips, and a crimson streak on her mouth seemed to speak of a recent passion.

Her eyes locked onto mine, and she leaned forward, her handkerchief poised above my face. With a swift, decisive motion, she struck me across the eyes, the touch like a knife's edge. For a moment, all was darkness.

The sound of a large animal landing softly outside broke the stillness. I opened my eyes to see the majestic sweep of a long tail disappearing into the hallway. I leapt from the bed, racing down the stairs into the alabaster hall. The moon cast an eerie glow, imbuing the space with an otherworldly light. The princess was nowhere to be seen, but I knew I needed to find her – only in her presence could I hope to protect myself from her subtle yet potent influence.
