As the sun began to set over the terracotta rooftops of Florence, Tito made his way through the winding streets, his footsteps echoing off the ancient stone walls. He had just concluded a meeting with the Ten at the Palazzo Vecchio, where he had presented his findings from his recent journey to Pisa. His report, filled with meticulous detail and precision, had earned him the admiration of Bernardo del Nero, one of the Ten. However, Bernardo's initial dislike of Tito had resurfaced, particularly after the sale of the library, which he believed had caused a deep wound to Romola's heart.

As Tito walked, the sounds of the city grew louder – vendors calling out their wares, the clanging of pots and pans from the kitchens, and the distant chime of the Duomo's bells. He strolled towards the Piazza del Duomo, drawn by the vibrant energy of the crowd. The procession had ended, but the people remained restless, seeking a more substantial outlet for their joy. If Fra Girolamo were to mount the pulpit and preach to them, perhaps they might find satisfaction. Yet, even with the new emphasis on Christian discipline, the people's desire for celebration lingered.

Tito paused in front of a small, unassuming shop, its sign creaking gently in the breeze. The aroma of freshly baked bread wafted out, mingling with the scent of roasting meats and herbs. He pushed open the door, and the sounds of the street gave way to the warm, golden light within. Inside, the barber's hands moved deftly, trimming the hair of a young apprentice, while the air was filled with the soft hum of conversation. Tito took a seat, feeling the worn wooden chair creak beneath him, and waited for his turn. As he sat, he couldn't help but notice the array of spices and herbs displayed on the shelves – the crimson red of dried chili peppers, the earthy brown of freshly ground coffee, and the delicate yellow of saffron threads. The barber's hands moved with a practiced ease, his fingers dancing across the scalp as he worked, the scissors snipping away with a rhythmic precision that was both soothing and invigorating.
