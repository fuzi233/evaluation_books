This text is based on a 1907 edition published by Cassell and Company, Limited.

This true yet modest account of an extraordinary journey is respectfully dedicated by the narrator, ALLAN QUATERMAIN, to all young readers—both big and small—who dare to dream beyond the ordinary.

The author takes this moment to express gratitude to his readers for their warm reception of this tale across twelve years and many editions. He hopes that in its current form, it may reach even more hearts and continue to stir wonder in those who still carry the spirit of adventure, drawn to stories of hidden riches, fierce battles, and untamed lands.

Ditchingham, 11 March, 1898.

Now, in 1907, with the release of this new edition, I can only say how delighted I am that this story continues to captivate so many. Imagination has met reality—the legendary King Solomon’s Mines, once a dream, have been found, yielding gold—and now, diamonds too. The Kukuanas, or more accurately, the Matabele, have been subdued by modern arms, yet the tale lives on. It remains a joy to see that so many still find delight in these simple pages. May they bring pleasure not just to today’s readers, but to generations yet to come—just as our dear friend Allan Quatermain would have wished.

H. Rider Haggard
Ditchingham, 1907.
