As the late afternoon sun cast its warm glow through the small, lozenge-shaped panes of the window, Giles found himself alone in the cozy sitting room of the Tergou family's humble home. The air was thick with the scent of freshly baked bread wafting from the hearth, where a pot of simmering stew hung suspended over the flames. The room's wooden floor, polished to a high sheen by diligent scrubbing, seemed to glow with a warm, golden light, inviting all who entered to sit awhile and stay a spell.

Giles's eyes wandered to the sturdy oak table, its surface worn smooth by generations of use, where his mother's prized cookbook lay open to a page filled with the recipe for the traditional Hollandish dish, "Stamppot met kool." The recipe, passed down through his family for years, called for a medley of sautéed vegetables, boiled potatoes, and a tangy splash of mustard, all served steaming hot in a hearty portion. As he read through the instructions, Giles's stomach growled with anticipation, and he couldn't help but imagine the flavors melding together in perfect harmony.

Just then, a faint cry echoed from outside, and Giles's thoughts turned to his missing siblings, Catherine and her little daughter, who had set out to search for his brother Gerard. He hoped they would return soon, bearing tales of their adventures and perhaps even some fresh herbs to add to tonight's meal. The thought of his family gathered 'round the table, sharing stories and laughter as they savored the fruits of their labor, filled Giles with a sense of contentment.

As he delved deeper into the recipe, his gaze fell upon the peculiar figure of the family's resident dwarf, stuck to the edge of the table with his teeth, his eyes gleaming with an otherworldly intensity. Giles chuckled to himself, remembering the countless times the dwarf, whose name was Balthazar, had managed to get himself stuck in the most unlikely of places. Despite his awkward appearance, Balthazar possessed a mischievous spirit and a talent for causing trouble, often to the chagrin of the family's matriarch, Dame Catherine.
