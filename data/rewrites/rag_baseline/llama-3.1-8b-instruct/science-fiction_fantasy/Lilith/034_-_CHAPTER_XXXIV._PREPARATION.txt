PREPARATION

As the sun dipped below the horizon, casting the village of Bulika in a warm orange glow, the woman's words wove a spell of enchantment over the children. They spoke of the city, its defenceless walls, and the wickedness of its ruler, Princess Lilith. The children's chatter was like a gentle breeze rustling through the leaves, carrying the whispers of a secret plan. Though they had never seen the city, they sensed its vulnerability, and the tantalizing prospect of claiming it as their own.

In the days that followed, the idea took hold, germinating like a seed planted in fertile soil. Lona's enthusiasm was contagious, and I found myself swept up in her vision of a new world order. The children's rapid growth was a marvel to behold, and I was convinced that with their help, we could achieve greatness. Our research had revealed the one weakness in Lilith's armor – her daughter – and the ancient prophecy that hung over the city like a dark cloud. The risk was palpable, but the reward was too great to ignore.

My own motivations were not entirely altruistic. I envisioned a future where Lona sat upon the throne, with me by her side as her trusted advisor and consort. Together, we would shape the destiny of this new nation, guided by the wisdom of the Little Ones. I dreamed of establishing a thriving trade in precious gems, one that would bring prosperity to both our worlds. Of course, this was a fanciful notion, and I knew it would require careful navigation to avoid causing harm to either realm.

As we prepared for our invasion, Lona and I divided tasks between us. She focused on organizing the commissariat, ensuring that our young warriors were well-fed and equipped for battle. Meanwhile, I drilled the children in the art of warfare, teaching them how to wield slings, bows, and arrows. The bigger boys were adept at stone-throwing, while the older girls proved skilled with aloe-spikes, their sharp tips as deadly as any sword.

One of the greatest challenges lay in getting the children to respond swiftly to the call to action. We practiced until they could assemble quickly, their faces set with determination. Lona, ever the natural leader, moved with a quiet confidence, her long hair flowing behind her like a river of night.
