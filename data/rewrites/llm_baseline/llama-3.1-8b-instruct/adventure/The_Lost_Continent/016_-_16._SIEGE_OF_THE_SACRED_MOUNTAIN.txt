As I traversed the vast expanse of Atlantis, the journey proved to be a grueling test of endurance, beset by countless perils and uncertainties. Though I had traversed many treacherous landscapes, one stop of significance stands out – a moment of respite that was both a necessity and a blessing in disguise. While hunting in the open savannah, I had managed to bring down a majestic stag after a prolonged pursuit. Satisfied with my quarry, I began to prepare the carcass, carefully cutting it into manageable portions and bundling them together for transport.

However, my satisfaction was short-lived, as I soon discovered a malevolent presence lurking beneath the surface. A gargantuan worm, its body a twisted mass of green flesh, lay hidden amongst the tall grasses. Unbeknownst to me, my heel struck against its body, releasing a torrent of venom that coursed through my veins like liquid fire. Panicked, I swiftly dispatched the creature with my trusty knife, but the damage was done. The wound began to throb with an intense, pulsating pain, and I could feel my leg swelling like a balloon inflating with air. As the sun dipped below the horizon, I hauled myself into the safety of a nearby tree, where I spent a sleepless night wracked by agony and fever.

The first light of dawn brought little respite, and I was forced to continue my journey, my wounded leg throbbing with every step. I leaned heavily on my spear, using it as a crutch to support my weight as I limped through the dark, foreboding forest. The moss growing on the northern side of the trees served as my only guide, but even this familiar landmark seemed distorted and unclear, as if my vision was clouded by a thick veil of pain. I pressed on, driven by a stubborn determination to fulfill the High Council's command and unlock the secrets of the ancient city.

My memories of the journey grew hazy and disjointed, until I found myself in a small, makeshift shelter nestled between two rocks. A woman, her face a map of wrinkles and age, tended to my wound with a mixture of herbs and skill. She worked in silence, her eyes never leaving mine, as she coaxed me back from the brink of collapse.
