As Sally sat in her cozy apartment, sipping her morning coffee, she felt a sense of satisfaction wash over her. Thirty hours had passed since the events of the previous evening, and the news of Bugs Butler's defeat at the hands of Lew Lucas still lingered in the air. The champion's inability to withstand the challenger's relentless assault had brought joy to many, including Mr. Lucas himself, who was beaming with pride in the newspaper photograph that adorned Sally's wall.

Despite the general euphoria, however, Sally couldn't help but feel a twinge of disappointment. She had always found Bugs Butler to be a formidable opponent, and his loss was a reminder of the fragility of human endurance. As she gazed out the window, lost in thought, the phone suddenly rang, shattering the peaceful atmosphere.

She picked up the receiver, and a familiar voice echoed on the other end. "Hullo?" she said, a smile spreading across her face.

"Hullo," replied the voice, followed by a faint pause. "I say, I'm awfully glad you're back. I only got your letter this morning. Found it at the boarding-house..."

Sally's eyes lit up with excitement as she recognized the voice – it was Ginger, her dear friend. She listened intently as Ginger explained his absence, his words punctuated by a hint of hesitation. "You poor thing!" she exclaimed, her voice filled with compassion. "How are you?"

There was another pause, and Sally sensed a touch of vulnerability in Ginger's tone. "I'm all right, thanks," he replied, his voice laced with a hint of embarrassment.

Undeterred, Sally pressed on, her voice firm but gentle. "Well, hurry. Come see me." The line went silent, and Sally waited anxiously for what felt like an eternity before the doorbell rang. She flung open the door, and her eyes widened in shock as she took in Ginger's appearance. One eye was hidden behind a swelling, a bruise marred his cheekbone, and his lips were puffed up from the beating.

"Oh, Ginger!" she exclaimed, her concern evident. "What happened to you?" Ginger's gaze met hers, filled with a mixture of apology and embarrassment, and Sally's heart went out to him. She ushered him inside, determined to nurse him back to health and hear the whole story behind his battered appearance.
