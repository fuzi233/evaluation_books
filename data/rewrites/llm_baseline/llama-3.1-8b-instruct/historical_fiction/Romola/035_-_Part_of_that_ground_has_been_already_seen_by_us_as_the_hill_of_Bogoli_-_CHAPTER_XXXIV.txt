The evening air carried the scent of woodsmoke and simmering sauce as Messer Naldo descended the winding road to the hill of San Giorgio, his footsteps echoing off the stone facades of the villas that lined the way. Eleven days had passed since his previous visit, a remarkably short interval considering the distance he'd traveled. Tito, however, had reason to believe that Messer Naldo's haste was not solely driven by the desire to revisit the charming Tessa, whom he'd taken under his wing more than a year and a half ago.

It was true that Tito's initial decision to provide for Tessa had been motivated by a genuine desire to help, rather than any ulterior motive. Her innocent charm and lively spirit had won him over, and he found himself drawn to her warmth and kindness. Yet, his heart belonged to another – a woman whose presence ignited a deep and abiding passion within him. Romola, with her raven tresses and piercing gaze, had captured his imagination, and he found himself lost in the simple beauty of her smile.

Tito's relationship with Tessa was a delicate balance of kindness and indulgence, difficult to separate in his own mind. He'd spun a web of deception around them both, and now felt trapped by his own benevolence. In his moments of quiet reflection, he acknowledged that he'd been influenced by a calculated self-interest, but the line between truth and fiction had grown increasingly blurred. His conscience, now awakened by his love for Romola, nagged at him, reminding him of the secret he kept from Tessa.

To maintain the secrecy of his offense, Tito had taken great care to conceal his true identity from Monna Lisa, who, like many of her social standing, rarely ventured beyond her household, visiting only a few trusted shops and the confessional. She knew only that Tito supported her comfortably, and that she'd grown fond of Tessa, whom she helped care for. The mystery surrounding their situation was palpable, given Tessa's humble origins and Tito's refined status, but Monna Lisa saw no cause for concern, assuming Tito might be a devoted husband.
