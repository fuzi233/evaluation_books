The rain pattered against the panes of the Meyricks' parlour window, casting a soothing melody that wove in and out of the gentle cadence of Mrs. Meyrick's voice as she read aloud to her daughters, Kate and Amy. Beyond the window, the river flowed like a silver serpent, its surface rippled by the wind. The afternoon sunlight filtering through the holland blind danced across the walls, illuminating the old, familiar faces of the family portraits that adorned the parlour, each one a testament to a history woven from the threads of love, art, and tradition. In the adjacent room, Mab sat hunched over her embroidery, the soft glow of the lamp casting a warm, golden light upon her face as she worked tirelessly to create exquisite satin cushions for the "great world" beyond their humble doorstep.

As the afternoon wore on, the scent of woodsmoke and baking wafted through the air, mingling with the sweet, honeyed aroma of beeswax polish that Mrs. Meyrick applied to the antique furniture with such loving care. The family's love for this old house was palpable, its narrow, shabby facade belied by the rich tapestry of memories and traditions that lay within. For in this modest home, the Meyricks had cultivated a world of beauty and refinement, one that defied the coarseness of the outside world and spoke to the beauty of the human spirit.

Kate, seated at her easel, lost herself in the world of her illustrations, her pencils scratching out vivid scenes that seemed to spring to life beneath her skilled hands. Her sisters, Amy and Mab, worked alongside her, their fingers moving deftly as they embroidered the delicate patterns that would soon adorn the cushions. The Meyricks were a family bound together by a triple cord – family love, admiration for the finer things in life, and a deep commitment to industry and hard work. They had resisted Hans' desire to lavish them with luxury, knowing that true happiness lay not in material wealth, but in the simple pleasures of art, music, and literature.

As the evening drew to a close, the family gathered around the fire, their faces aglow with the warmth and contentment that came from living a life untainted by the petty vanities and coarse selfishness of the world outside.
