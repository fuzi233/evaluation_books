As I lay in the darkness, my intention to remain vigilant was thwarted by the cumulative effects of fatigue and the soothing cadence of my companion's slumber. His restless movements and frequent awakenings initially led me to believe that he was merely adjusting to the confines of our makeshift abode—a small, canvas-walled shelter perched precariously on the edge of the river. The constant din of the water lapping against the shore and the occasional snapping of twigs in the willows created an eerie symphony that only served to further heighten my senses. My companion's words, though hushed, carried a sense of urgency, as he implored me to verify the state of our surroundings, his queries hanging in the air like a challenge to the night itself.

However, as the hours crept by, his agitation gradually subsided, and his breathing grew more regular. The soothing sound of his snores, an unusual occurrence given his typically taciturn nature, served as a welcome respite from the oppressive stillness. As I drifted into a state of somnolence, my thoughts were consumed by the enigmatic phrase he had uttered earlier in the night: "going inside to Them." The phrase, imbued with an otherworldly quality, left me with a sense of unease, as if the very fabric of reality was being pulled taut, threatening to snap at any moment.

A sudden, inexplicable constriction in my chest jolted me awake, and I found myself suffocating beneath the weight of the blanket. Panic set in as I realized that my companion was nowhere to be found. The sound of the river, now a deafening roar, was accompanied by an unearthly humming, a cacophony that seemed to emanate from all directions at once. The air was thick with an almost palpable energy, as if the very atmosphere itself was alive and vibrating with an electric tension.

I burst forth from the tent, my footsteps echoing through the stillness as I frantically searched for my missing companion. The willows, their branches tangled and gnarled, seemed to close in around me, their leaves whispering secrets in the wind. The humming grew louder, a maddening drone that threatened to consume me whole. In my desperation, I stumbled through the underbrush, tripping over roots and scraping my skin on the rough bark of the trees.
