The scent of roasting spices wafted through the air as Ethne's fingers danced across the strings of her violin, the sweet melodies echoing through the garden. It was a sound that Mrs. Adair knew all too well, one that transported her back to the evening when Ethne had played the Musoline Overture with such passion and fire. Yet, despite the familiar tune, a sense of unease settled in the pit of her stomach. She had shared a secret with Durrance, a revelation that had left her wondering what the consequences might be.

As she watched the pair interact, Mrs. Adair couldn't shake the feeling that something was amiss. Ethne and Durrance met and conversed as if nothing had changed, but she sensed a subtle shift in their dynamics. The connection between them seemed to have deepened, and yet, at the same time, it felt almost...forced. Mrs. Adair's initial relief gave way to frustration as she realized her intervention had seemingly had no effect. She began to wonder if she had merely delayed the inevitable.

Meanwhile, Durrance had made up his mind. He had chosen to keep his suspicions hidden, to maintain the illusion of a carefree friendship with Ethne. He was aware that his silence offered her a glimmer of comfort, a fleeting sense of solace in a world gone awry. As he pretended to be oblivious to the truth, he watched Ethne's eyes sparkle with a mixture of sadness and hope. His words were laced with sincerity, a gentle reminder that even in the darkest of times, there was always a chance for redemption.

"You see, Ethne," he said with a warm smile, "one can learn to live with blindness, to find beauty in the shadows. But with you, my dear, each time we meet, I discover something new, something that fills my heart with joy." His words were a balm to her wounded soul, and she let herself be soothed by his presence.

As the days passed, Ethne's emotions seesawed between despair and hope. Her pride had been restored, and Durrance's words had kindled a spark within her, a glimmer of hope that Harry might still be alive. Yet, the ache within her refused to subside, a constant reminder of the uncertainty that shrouded his fate. She found herself torn between her longing to know the truth and her fear of what she might discover.
