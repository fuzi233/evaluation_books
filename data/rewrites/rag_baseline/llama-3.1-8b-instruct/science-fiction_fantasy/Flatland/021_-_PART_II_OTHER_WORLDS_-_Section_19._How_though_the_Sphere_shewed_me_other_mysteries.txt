Lady Evelyn Windsor sat at her father's desk, surrounded by dusty tomes and worn leather bindings, her fingers tracing the intricate patterns etched into the pages of an ancient text. The weight of her gaze settled upon a letter, penned by the esteemed Professor Edwin Abbott, its words a gentle breeze on a summer's day, carrying whispers of secrets yet unknown. As she read, the room seemed to fade into the background, and she found herself transported to a realm beyond the confines of her world.

The memory of that fateful day when her brother, Edward, was taken away still lingered, a wound that refused to heal. She recalled the desperation that had driven her to attempt to intervene, to reach out to him one final time. But the Council Chamber, with its cold, unforgiving stone walls, had stood between them, an insurmountable barrier. The words of her mentor, the wise and enigmatic Mr. Langley, echoed in her mind: "Do not concern yourself with your brother's fate; perhaps you will have ample time to make amends in the future."

As she delved deeper into the letter, the room began to shift and distort, like the ripples on a pond's surface. The air seemed to vibrate with an otherworldly energy, and Lady Evelyn felt herself drawn upwards, into the realm of the unknown. The Sphere, a being of immense power and wisdom, stood before her, its presence both captivating and unnerving.

"Now, I shall reveal to you the secrets of the Solids, and the mysteries of their construction."
