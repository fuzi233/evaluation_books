The Baron produced a yellowed parchment from his pocket and unfurled it on the weathered surface of a nearby tomb. He consulted a small, leather-bound tome, its pages yellowed with age, and read aloud from its yellowed leaves. The words dripped with an ancient, mystical power, sending shivers down my spine.

Together, the trio strolled down the chapel's aisle, their footsteps echoing off the cold stone walls. And then, as if guided by an unseen hand, they converged upon a section of wall, their fingers tracing the outlines of a long-lost monument.
