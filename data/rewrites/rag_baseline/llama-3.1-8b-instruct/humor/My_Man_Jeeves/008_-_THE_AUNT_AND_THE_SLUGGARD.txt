The aroma of freshly brewed tea wafted through the stillness of the morning air, mingling with the scent of damp earth and the distant hum of the city. I lay abed, savoring the tranquility of the moment, as Jeeves floated into the room with the silent ease of a ghost. His eyes, like two bright sapphires, sparkled with an unspoken message, and I knew that he had been roused by some urgent news. 

"Bertie, wake up," he whispered, his voice as gentle as a summer breeze. "Rocky Todd is here. He has arrived with a letter from his aunt."

I groggily sat up, rubbing the sleep from my eyes. The last thing I needed was an early morning visit from Rocky, the poet laureate of Long Island. His laconic nature and penchant for lounging in bed till noon made him a fixture in my life, a constant reminder of the leisurely pace that I often envied. His poetry, a fusion of exhortations to live life to the fullest and musings on the meaning of existence, seemed to have found a receptive audience among the American editors who were willing to pay him a handsome sum for his efforts.

As I swung my legs over the side of the bed, Jeeves handed me a steaming cup of tea and a letter, which Rocky thrust into my hands with an air of urgency. "Read this, Bertie," he said, his voice low and insistent. "It's from my aunt, and it concerns me."

I raised an eyebrow, intrigued despite myself. Rocky's aunt was a mysterious figure, known only to us through the letters she sent him from Illinois, replete with advice on how to live the good life and manage his finances. I took a sip of my tea, feeling the warmth spread through my chest, and began to read the letter.

The handwriting was flowing and elegant, the words dripping with a warm, maternal concern. As I read on, I realized that Rocky's aunt was planning a visit to New York, and she had set her sights on meeting me. I felt a shiver run down my spine as I wondered what this might portend. Rocky, sensing my unease, leaned in close. "Don't worry, Bertie," he whispered. "She's all right. She's just concerned about my well-being, and she wants to meet the person who's been influencing me to lead a more... vigorous life."
