A Midsummer Night's Whispers.

As dusk descended, casting a warm orange glow across the rectory garden, Miss Keeldar stepped outside, the scent of blooming laburnums wafting through the evening air. She turned to Caroline, her eyes sparkling with amusement. "There will be just enough light to guide me home, don't you think?" she said, her voice barely above a whisper.

Caroline's brow furrowed in concern. "You shouldn't walk alone, Shirley. Fanny can accompany you."

Miss Keeldar's laughter danced on the breeze. "Why should I be afraid in my own parish? I'd walk from Fieldhead to the church on a moonless night, just for the joy of breathing in the night air and catching a glimpse of the stars." As she spoke, a phaeton rattled past, followed by the quiet clatter of a carriage and the rustle of leaves as people dispersed from the church.

Miss Keeldar seized the opportunity to slip away, disappearing behind the laburnum trees with Caroline in tow. They settled among the flowers, their heads bent together as they watched the commotion unfold. When the throng finally dispersed, a tall figure emerged from the church porch, his eyes scanning the garden until they landed on Miss Keeldar.

"Ah, I'm glad I found you," he said, his deep voice carrying across the garden. "I was worried you'd already left." Caroline trailed behind him as he strode towards the pair. "I've had an unexpected invitation," he explained. "An old friend has arrived, and I've promised to join him. I'll be back tomorrow afternoon, but for tonight, I'll be staying elsewhere."

Miss Keeldar raised an eyebrow. "And you want me to fill in for you, sir? Become the master of the rectory, guardian of Caroline and the maids, all in one night?"

The rector's smile was quick. "Precisely, Captain. I thought you'd be the perfect person for the job. Will you oblige Caroline by staying here tonight? Just for one night?"

Miss Keeldar's eyes sparkled with mischief. "I accept your proposal, Mr. Helstone. We'll keep each other company, won't we, Caroline?" As she spoke, the rector vanished into the darkness, leaving the pair to their evening together.
