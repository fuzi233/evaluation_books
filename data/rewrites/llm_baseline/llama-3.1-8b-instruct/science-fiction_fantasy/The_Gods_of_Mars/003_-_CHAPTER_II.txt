As Tars Tarkas and I stood amidst the carnage surrounding the great boulder, the air grew thick with an otherworldly energy. The eerie call of the figure perched atop the distant cliffs echoed through the valley, summoning an endless tide of twisted creatures from all directions. Their grotesque forms swarmed towards us like a crimson-stained tide, leaving us with little choice but to flee for our lives.

"Tars Tarkas, come!" I urged, as he grasped my arm. "We must reach the cliffs! There, we can find shelter and hold off this monstrous horde." With a shared glance, we sprinted across the scarlet sward, I matching my pace to his to avoid outdistancing my companion. Our goal was to reach the cliffs within three hundred yards, where a narrow ledge or hidden cave might provide us a temporary refuge.

The pursuing creatures closed in, their twisted limbs propelling them forward with unnatural speed. Tars Tarkas signaled for me to forge ahead, to scout out a safe haven amidst the cliffs. His suggestion was a wise one – every precious moment counted, and I summoned every last shred of strength to bound towards the towering rocks. My feet pounded the earth in great strides, carrying me to the base of the cliffs in a mere instant.

The cliffs loomed above, a seemingly insurmountable barrier that rose vertically from the valley floor. The terrain was unlike anything I'd ever seen – no rubble or debris marred the smooth, unbroken surface, except for a few scattered boulders that had tumbled from above and lodged in the turf. A fleeting glance at the cliff face filled me with trepidation, for not a single handhold or foothold offered itself to aid our ascent.

To my right, the forest's verdant canopy stretched upwards, a lush tapestry of emerald hues that seemed to touch the clouds. To the left, the cliff stretched across the valley, merging seamlessly into the distant mountains that hemmed us in. A thousand feet away, the river burst forth from the base of the cliffs, its crystal waters glinting in the sunlight. Yet, it seemed an impossible route – I turned my attention back to the forest, my eyes scanning the dense foliage for a potential escape.
