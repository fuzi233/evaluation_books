As Pen navigated the labyrinthine underbelly of London, he found himself increasingly drawn to the city's most eclectic and marginalized communities. His acquaintanceship with Warrington had instilled in him a profound appreciation for the unbridled authenticity of the metropolis's diverse inhabitants, from the rugged coalheavers of the tap-rooms to the charismatic pickpockets and burglars of the city's seedier taverns. His affinity for these individuals stemmed from a deep-seated conviction that their lives were a testament to the unfettered expression of humanity, untainted by the stifling conventions of his own aristocratic upbringing.

Pen's predilection for the company of working-class Londoners was not merely a quixotic fancy, but a deliberate choice rooted in his desire to transcend the stifling hierarchies of his own social circle. He reveled in the unvarnished camaraderie of the city's public houses, where the tutelage of the tutelary figures – the coalheaving company at the Fox-under-the-Hill, the Brighton Stunner at the Champion's Arms – served as a salutary corrective to the affected mannerisms and stifling conventions of his own class. His friendship with the landlords and landladies of these establishments only served to reinforce his conviction that the rough-and-tumble world of the metropolis was, in fact, the true epicenter of genuine human connection.

Warrington, with his seasoned perspective and easygoing demeanor, proved an ideal guide for Pen's immersion into this vibrant world. Together, they would spend hours listening to the stories of the city's denizens, each one a testament to the boundless diversity of human experience. As they sipped their gin-and-water at the bar, or laughed with the hatters and shopkeepers in the crowded streets, Pen felt his own sense of identity begin to shift, gradually shedding the stifling trappings of his royal heritage in favor of a more fluid, inclusive understanding of self. His companionship with Warrington had become a potent catalyst for self-discovery, one that would continue to shape his worldview for years to come.
