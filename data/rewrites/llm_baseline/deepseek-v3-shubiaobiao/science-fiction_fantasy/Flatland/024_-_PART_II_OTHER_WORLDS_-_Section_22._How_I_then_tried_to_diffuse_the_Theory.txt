My failure to enlighten my Grandson did not extinguish my resolve, but it taught me caution. I could no longer rely on cryptic phrases like *"Upward, not Northward"* alone. The truth demanded a clearer vessel—words, meticulously arranged. And so, in secret, I began composing a treatise: *Through Flatland to Thoughtland*.

Yet the laws of our world bound me. I dared not speak of physical Dimensions outright, so I wove allegory—a realm where Thought might gaze upon Flatland and perceive the innards of all things at once, where a being could be encircled by six Squares and possess eight terminal Points. But how could I illustrate such wonders when our tablets hold only Lines? My diagrams were but variations of brightness and length, crude shadows of the truths I had witnessed. I feared few would grasp my meaning.

Life grew oppressive. The sights of Flatland taunted me, each shape a hollow mockery of the depth I knew existed. I neglected my duties, consumed by visions I could neither share nor fully recall. Months passed, and even the memory of the Cube began to fade behind my closed eyes. The uncertainty gnawed at me—had I ever truly seen it, or had the revelation itself slipped into dream?

Still, the fire within could not be contained. Among the highest Circles, I let slip dangerous words: *"the eye that discerns the interiors of things,"* *"the all-seeing land."* Once, I even uttered the forbidden—*"the Third Dimension."*

Then came my final reckoning. At a gathering of the Speculative Society, some fool proclaimed why Providence had wisely confined us to Two Dimensions. The absurdity broke me. I spoke openly—of my journey with the Sphere, of the Assembly Hall, of Space itself. At first, I feigned fiction, but passion tore the mask away. I pleaded with them to *see.*

The Council arrested me at once.

Before them, I stood where the Sphere once had, but no mercy followed. The President replaced the guards with cheaper, blunter Angles—a silent death warrant. My fate was sealed.

They asked only two questions:
1. Could I define *"Upward, not Northward"*?
2. Could I describe a Cube beyond mere angles?

I had no answer but the Truth.

Perpetual imprisonment was my sentence. *"If Truth wills your release,"* the President said, *"Truth will free you."* A hollow comfort.

Seven years have passed. My brother visits, faithful and kind, yet his mind remains closed. He *saw* the Sphere’s sections, heard its proofs—yet still denies the Third Dimension. I am a voice howling into a void, a martyr without converts.

Some days, hope flickers—that these words might escape, might ignite rebellion in *some* Dimension. But darker hours come, when even the Cube dissolves into doubt, when all of Flatland feels like delirium. The walls, these very tablets, seem phantoms.

*"Upward, not Northward"* echoes like a curse.

I am Prometheus of Flatland, chained not for bringing fire, but for bearing a light none can see.
