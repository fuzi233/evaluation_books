If the sharply pointed Triangles of our Soldier class are formidable, you will readily grasp that Flatland’s Women are exponentially more dangerous. Where a Soldier is a sharp wedge, a Woman is a needle: entirely point, at both of her two extremities, as it were. Add to that her natural ability to turn effectively invisible at will, and you will immediately understand that no one trifles with a Flatland female and walks away unharmed.

Some of my younger Spaceland readers may be asking how a 2D woman could possibly make herself unseen. This should be obvious on reflection, but let me spell it out for the least attentive of you. Take a sewing needle, set it on a table, and lower your eye to the exact level of the tabletop. Look at it side-on, and you will see its entire length, clear as day. Turn it so the very tip points straight at your eye, though? All you will see is a tiny, barely noticeable dot. For all practical purposes, the needle has vanished. That is exactly how it works for our Women. When her full side is turned to us, we see her as a clear, distinct straight line. When the end that holds her shared eye-and-mouth organ faces us, she appears only as a bright, lustrous pinpoint. But when her blunt, far dimmer back end is turned toward us? That dull, almost lifeless point acts as a perfect Invisible Cap, rendering her all but undetectable to anyone who does not know she is there.

The scope of the danger we face from our Women will now be clear to even the slowest Spaceland mind. If colliding with a respectable middle-class Triangle can leave you bleeding out on the pavement; if a brush with a working man will leave you with a nasty gash; if the lightest graze from a private soldier’s sharp vertex is a death sentence – what else could running headlong into a Woman be, except instant, absolute obliteration? When she is entirely invisible, or nothing more than a faint dim speck, even the most cautious among us can never hope to avoid every accidental collision.

Across every nation of Flatland, dozens of laws have been passed over the centuries to minimize this constant peril. In the warmer southern regions where gravity pulls harder, and even stationary people are prone to sudden, involuntary lurches and movements, the laws governing Women are far harsher still. The core shared provisions of most Flatland legal codes boil down to three non-negotiable rules:
1.  Every dwelling must have a dedicated eastern-facing entrance reserved exclusively for Women, who must enter and exit through it in a slow, decorous manner – they are strictly forbidden from using the western, men-only door. [Note: When I visited Spaceland, I learned that some of your own priesthoods have enforced identical rules, with separate side entrances for villagers, farmers, and public school teachers so they may "approach in a becoming and respectful manner."]
2.  No Woman may step foot in a public space without continuously emitting a low, droning Peace-Cry, on pain of immediate execution.
3.  Any Woman formally certified to be suffering from St. Vitus’ Dance, recurrent seizures, chronic violent sneezing fits, or any other condition that causes involuntary, uncontrolled movement, must be put to death at once.

Some states add extra rules: in some, Women are forbidden from standing or walking in public unless they constantly sway their back end from side to side, broadcasting their position to anyone moving behind them. Others require every travelling Woman to be escorted at all times by one of her sons, a male servant, or her husband. A handful of the most restrictive states bar Women from leaving their homes entirely, save for a handful of public religious festivals each year. But the wisest of our ruling Circles and Statesmen have long since learned that piling more and more prohibitions on the Female population does far more harm than good: it weakens the whole population, reduces our birth rates, and stokes such rage in Women that domestic murders skyrocket, to the point a state loses far more citizens than it ever saves from accident. When Women are driven mad by being trapped inside, or hemmed in by endless arbitrary rules, they will lash out and kill every man and child within reach – and in our warmer southern climates, there are recorded cases where an entire village’s male population was wiped out in the space of two hours by a coordinated wave of female rage. That is why the three core laws outlined above are all that the best-run nations enforce, as a reasonable, balanced Female Code.

At the end of the day, our greatest protection is not legislature – it is the Women’s own self-interest. Though a Woman can stab a person to death in a single backwards flick of her sharp end, if she cannot immediately yank her needle-thin body free of the victim’s flailing corpse, her own fragile straight-line form will shatter into splinters. She knows this, and will almost never risk such a fight unless she has no other choice.

Fashion is also a powerful safeguard for us. I mentioned earlier that some less civilized states force Women to sway their back end side to side in public. This custom has been universal among all upper-class, respectable ladies in well-governed Flatland societies for as long as anyone can remember. It is seen as a deep embarrassment for a state to have to legislate a behavior that every decent Woman is supposed to practice as a natural instinct. The smooth, elegant, rhythmical undulation of the back performed by high-ranking ladies of Circular rank is envied and copied by the wives of the Equilateral working class, who can only manage a plain, monotonous back-swing as regular as a pendulum’s tick. That plain pendulum motion is in turn copied by the wives of the upwardly mobile Isosceles soldiers, whose female relatives have not yet developed even the smallest instinct for any kind of back motion at all. In every respected household, this "back-motion" is as constant and universal as the passage of time itself – and the men of these families are almost entirely immune to surprise invisible attacks.

Do not mistake me: our Women are not devoid of affection. But unfortunately, in the Frail Sex, the heat of the moment overrides every other consideration. This is an unavoidable side effect of their unfortunate body shape. Since they have no angles at all, far less sharp than even the lowest born Isosceles criminal, they are almost entirely without brain power: no capacity for reflection, no judgment, no foresight, barely any memory at all. When they fly into a rage, they forget every prior bond, every relationship, every sense of self-preservation. I personally knew of a case where a Woman murdered every single member of her entire household – and half an hour later, once her rage faded and the bodies had been swept away, she was wandering the home asking where her husband and children had got to.

It goes without saying that you never, ever irritate a Woman when she has room to turn around. But inside their own dedicated apartments, which are deliberately constructed to make it physically impossible for them to rotate their full line-body, you can say and do whatever you please. They are completely powerless to hurt you, and just a few minutes later they will not even remember the threat of murder they were screaming at you, nor the dramatic, over-the-top promises you made to calm their rage in the moment.

By and large, domestic life runs fairly smoothly, save for the lowest ranks of the military classes. There, foolish, tactless husbands rely too much on the protection of their own sharp angles, and ignore the far more effective defenses of good sense and well-timed lies. These reckless men often skip building the mandatory non-rotating walls in their wives’ apartments, or make rude, insulting jokes to their wives out in public and refuse to apologize immediately. Worse, their stubborn devotion to literal, blunt truth makes them refuse to spout the flowery, meaningless compliments that a clever Circle can use to soothe his angry consort in ten seconds flat. The result is predictable massacre. This is not entirely a bad thing, our ruling Circles argue: it culls the most violent, troublesome Isosceles men from the population, eliminating the sorts of people who might stir up revolution against the social order before they can organize.

Even in the homes of our most well-regulated, highest-ranking Circles, though, domestic life never comes close to the ideal you enjoy in Spaceland. There is peace, in the narrow sense that no one is being randomly stabbed to death – but that peace is bought at the cost of almost all domestic comfort. In every wealthy Polygonal or Circular household, it has been an unbroken custom since time immemorial that all wives and daughters must keep their eye-and-mouth end pointed constantly towards their husband and his male guests. For a noble lady to turn her back on her husband would be seen as a monstrous scandal, enough to strip her whole family of their status. But this custom that keeps them safe from stabs brings no quiet. The sharp, bright eye and the chattering mouth are always facing the man of the house, nonstop. Nothing in the world is more persistent than the unending stream of female chatter. The same tact that works perfectly well to avoid a Woman’s deadly sharp sting is useless to shut her mouth. Since Women have no sense of wit, reason, or self-restraint, they will say absolutely every trivial, random thought that pops into their heads, unfiltered. More than a few cynical men I know have openly admitted they would far rather risk the silent, invisible death of a Woman’s stab end than endure the nonstop, deafening noise of her mouth end.

To you Spaceland readers, the condition of our Women must seem truly deplorable – and you are not wrong. Even the lowest, most degraded Isosceles man can hold out hope that his angle will narrow over the generations, and his whole family can climb the caste system one day. But no Woman can ever hope for any advancement, ever. "Once a Woman, always a Woman" is an unbreakable law of nature, and the forces of evolution themselves seem to have frozen entirely to hold them in their place. But even so, we cannot help but admire the wise order of the world: since they have no hope for a better future, they also have no memory to dwell on their humiliations, and no foresight to anticipate the miseries of their existence. They do not suffer their fate the way a thinking, reasoning being would – and that, for us, is the greatest mercy of all.
