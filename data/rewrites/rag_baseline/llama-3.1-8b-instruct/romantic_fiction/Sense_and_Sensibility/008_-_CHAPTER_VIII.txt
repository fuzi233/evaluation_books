The opulent mansion of Mrs. Jennings, with its intricate facades and manicured gardens, was a hub of activity, where social connections and alliances were forged over lavish meals and sparkling wine. As a widow with a substantial dowry, Mrs. Jennings was determined to play matchmaker for her acquaintances, particularly the young women in her circle. Her sharp eye for potential pairings had already identified Colonel Brandon as a suitable suitor for Marianne Dashwood, and she was eager to promote their union.

Her keen insight had been sparked on their first meeting, when Colonel Brandon listened intently to Marianne's singing voice, and confirmed the next evening when he listened once more at the Middletons' dinner party. The fact was clear: Colonel Brandon was smitten. A marriage between them would be advantageous, as he was wealthy and Marianne was beautiful. Mrs. Jennings had long wished to see Colonel Brandon settled, ever since her connection with Sir John introduced them, and she was always eager to secure a good husband for each of the young women in her acquaintance.

The benefits to herself were considerable, providing her with endless anecdotes to share with her friends. At the park, she teased Colonel Brandon, and at the cottage, she playfully mocked Marianne. The colonel likely remained oblivious to her jests, but Marianne was perplexed by their implications – seeing them as an insensitive remark on the colonel's advanced age and bachelorhood.

Mrs. Dashwood intervened to defend her friend, pointing out that Mrs. Jennings' words might not be entirely malicious. However, Marianne remained unconvinced, arguing that thirty-five was far too old for a man to be considered eligible for marriage. Elinor, sensing her sister's unease, chimed in, suggesting that a twenty-seven-year-old woman might still be open to marrying a man of thirty-five, if circumstances permitted. Marianne paused, weighing the notion, before responding that such a woman would have long given up on love and comfort, and would likely settle for a comfortable home and a modest income.
