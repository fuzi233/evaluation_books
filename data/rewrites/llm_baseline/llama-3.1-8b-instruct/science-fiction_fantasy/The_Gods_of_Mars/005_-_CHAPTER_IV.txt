The scent of roasting meats wafted through the air, mingling with the pungent aroma of spices, drawing me to my senses. I stood, stretching my limbs, and gazed about the dimly lit chamber. The flickering torches cast eerie shadows on the walls, making it seem as if the very spirits of the ancient ones stirred within these stone confines. Beyond the blank wall beside me, the clanging of pots and pans, the hiss of sizzling meat, and the murmur of hushed conversations filled the air.

As I made my way towards the source of the savory smells, I caught sight of the prisoners and the fearsome creatures they were chained to, their eyes fixed upon me with a mixture of curiosity and hostility. Amidst them, the young Martian woman with skin like polished copper and hair like burnished bronze watched me with a look of concern etched on her features. Her cry of warning had been the catalyst for my sudden return to awareness.

Her unadorned state betrayed nothing of her station in life, but her beauty and intelligence were unmistakable. I wondered what tale her past might hold, and whether she was a captive or a slave in this unforgiving environment. The sounds from the opposite side of the partition pierced through my foggy thoughts, and I realized with a start that they signaled a desperate struggle between Tars Tarkas and some unseen foe.

I threw my weight against the secret door, but it yielded nothing to my strength. My gaze swept the panel, searching for a hidden catch or mechanism, but my search was in vain. Just as I raised my longsword to strike, the young woman called out to me in a voice like music.

"Hold back your blade, mighty warrior," she said. "You'll need it where it can do some good – don't waste it on a door that yields to a gentle touch."

"I see you know its secret," I replied, lowering my sword.

"Yes, I do," she said. "Free me, and I'll show you the way into the next room, if you desire. The keys to my chains lie among the bodies of our enemies. But why would you return to face those beasts again, or whatever other horrors they've unleashed within that dreadful trap?"

"Because my friend fights alone," I said, hastening to find the keys among the fallen creatures.
