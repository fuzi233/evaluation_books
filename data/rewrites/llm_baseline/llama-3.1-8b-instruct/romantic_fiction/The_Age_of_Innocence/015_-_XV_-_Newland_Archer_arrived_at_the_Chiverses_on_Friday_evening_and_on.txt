As Newland Archer stepped out of his carriage and onto the frost-kissed driveway of the Chiverses', he couldn't help but feel a sense of familiarity wash over him. He had spent countless weekends at Highbank, and yet, every time he returned, he found himself enchanted by the tranquil beauty of the estate. On Saturday morning, he joined his hostess and a handful of intrepid guests for a leisurely spin across the frozen lake in the ice-boat, the crisp air invigorating his senses as they glided effortlessly across the water.

Later, he strolled through the stables with Reggie, listening intently as his friend waxed poetic about the finer points of horsemanship. The afternoon sun cast a warm glow over the rustic surroundings, and Archer felt his thoughts begin to drift, his mind wandering to the melodic strains of a long-forgotten waltz. He recalled the way the music had seemed to dance across the room, a delicate balance of notes and rhythm that had captivated him, much like the way the sunlight danced across the frost-covered trees.

After tea, he engaged in a gentle conversation with a young woman who had once confided in him about her own heartaches. Though she had claimed to be broken-hearted upon learning of his engagement, she now spoke of her own marital aspirations with a quiet determination. Archer listened attentively, his eyes locked on hers, as the soft crackle of the fire provided a soothing background hum.

As the evening wore on, he joined in a playful pillow-fight that swept through the nursery and down into the basement, the laughter and shouts mingling with the sound of music still echoing in his mind. Yet, as the night drew to a close, Archer felt a restlessness stir within him, a desire to escape the confines of the estate and lose himself in the stillness of the night.

He borrowed a cutter and set off towards Skuytercliff, the moon casting an ethereal glow over the landscape. The village lay just a mile and a half away, but he knew that Mrs. van der Luyden rarely walked, and so he stayed on the road to await the carriage. As he approached the crossroads, he spotted a figure in a crimson cloak walking towards him, a large dog bounding alongside.
