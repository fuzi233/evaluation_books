As the sun-drenched afternoon wore on, Pen guided Rebecca towards the bustling town of Baymouth, the rhythmic pounding of hooves against the cobblestones echoing through the narrow streets. The mare's spirited energy was a perfect match for Pen's own restless temperament, and he urged her on, his heart fixed on a single destination: Mr. Foker's lodgings. The chemist's shop below, with its enticing aromas of cigar smoke and soda water, seemed to be doing a brisk trade, thanks to the lively company of the young gentlemen residing above.

Upon arriving at the lodgings, Pen was greeted by Mr. Spavin, Foker's companion and partner in the tandem business, who was engaged in a most absorbing game of teaching his diminutive canine friend tricks with a crumb of biscuit. Pen's ruddy complexion stood in stark contrast to Spavin's pale, debauched features, and the latter couldn't help but remark on the disparity. "Who is this fellow?" he thought to himself, "He looks as fresh as a newly plucked bean, his hand steady as a rock even in the morning light."

But Foker was nowhere to be found, leaving Pen with a sense of disappointment. Spavin, however, was more than happy to provide some information, though he couldn't say when his friend would return. Sometimes Foker stayed away for a day, sometimes for a week. Pen's curiosity was piqued, and he asked if the other man was a scholar. Spavin's eyes lit up at the mention of a college, and he revealed that Pendennis was indeed a gentleman of some learning. As they parted ways, Pen took a moment to gaze out at the vast expanse of the sea, its blue waters rolling in with a soothing melody that did little to calm his turbulent thoughts.

Pen's mind was a maelstrom of emotions, and he felt a growing sense of frustration at not being able to locate Foker. He wanted to see his friend, to talk to him, to unravel the tangled threads of their conversation. "Perhaps," he thought, "I'll ride on to the Chatteris road, see if I can catch up to him." Rebecca, sensing her rider's unease, responded eagerly to the reins, and they set off once more, this time towards the Chatteris road.
