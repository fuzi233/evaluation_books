As the autumnal mist swirled through the streets of Barton village, Mrs. Dashwood's visit to Lady Middleton unfolded with the precision of a carefully planned ritual. Two of her daughters accompanied her, their laughter and chatter a gentle accompaniment to the rustling leaves. Meanwhile, Marianne remained at home, citing a pressing engagement with the art of preserving local wild mushrooms for the winter months, a tradition passed down through generations of Dashwoods. Her mother, suspecting that Willoughby had promised to call during their absence, was satisfied with Marianne's decision.

Upon their return from the village, the family discovered Willoughby's carriage waiting outside, its servant attentively standing by. Mrs. Dashwood's initial assumption proved correct – Willoughby had indeed arrived, but not in the manner she had anticipated. As they entered the cottage, they were met with an unexpected scene: Marianne rushed out of the parlour, her eyes red-rimmed and her handkerchief clutched tightly in her hand. Without a word, she hastened upstairs, leaving her family to exchange worried glances.

Their concern deepened as they entered the parlour, where Willoughby stood by the fireplace, his back turned towards them. He turned as they approached, his countenance a mixture of distress and resignation. "Is anything the matter with her?" Mrs. Dashwood inquired, her voice laced with concern. "Is she unwell?"

Willoughby's forced smile faltered, and he cleared his throat before responding. "I fear not, ma'am. It is I who may soon be afflicted with a severe disappointment." His words hung in the air like the scent of wood smoke on a winter's evening.

"Disappointment?" Mrs. Dashwood repeated, her brow furrowed in inquiry. Willoughby's gaze dropped, and he fidgeted with his gloves. "Yes, for I have received news that requires my immediate attention in London. A wealthy acquaintance, Mrs. Smith, has summoned me to attend to her affairs."

"A sudden summons?" Elinor asked, her curiosity piqued.

Willoughby nodded, his eyes clouding over. "I have no choice but to comply. My presence is required in the city, and I must depart at once."
