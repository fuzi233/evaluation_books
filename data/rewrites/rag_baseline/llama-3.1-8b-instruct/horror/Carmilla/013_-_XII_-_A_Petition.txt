The autumnal gloaming settled over the rolling hills, casting a melancholy veil over the tranquil landscape. In the grand manor house, the soft ticking of the grandfather clock punctuated the stillness, a reminder that time, like the seasons, was always in motion. In the midst of this peaceful scene, a sudden stir of excitement rippled through the household, as the arrival of a mysterious visitor announced the prospect of an evening's diversion.

Lady Violet, seated in her favorite armchair, watched with interest as the gentleman in black, resplendent in his formal attire, ushered in the unexpected guest. His stately demeanor commanded respect, and the air of quiet authority surrounding him hinted at a world beyond the confines of their small village. The lady, dressed in somber finery, her dark hair styled in an intricate coiffure, exuded an aura of refinement and poise, her features etched with lines of worry and fatigue.

As she entered, her eyes locked onto Lady Violet, and for an instant, a flicker of recognition danced across her face. Then, like a mask slipping into place, her expression smoothed, and she extended a hand, her voice dripping with courtesy. "Lady...Violet, I believe?" Her tone was measured, each word weighed carefully, as if she navigated a minefield of unspoken meanings.

"I am," Lady Violet replied, rising from her chair to greet the newcomer. "And you are...?"

The Countess's smile was a gentle, enigmatic thing, a whispered secret shared between two old friends. "We have met before, though I fear I have forgotten your name. Forgive me, I am Countess Millarca." Her voice was husky, like the rustle of dry leaves, and it sent a shiver down Lady Violet's spine.
