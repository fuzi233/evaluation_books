The night air clung to the old buildings of Miskatonic University like a damp shroud. I stood outside the Widener Library, the flickering moon casting eerie shadows on the walls. The sound of distant whippoorwills pierced the air, their haunting calls weaving an unsettling melody. It was a night like any other in Arkham, Massachusetts, yet something felt off, a sense of anticipation hanging heavy over the sleepy town.

I recalled the stories I'd heard about Whateley, a scholar consumed by the forbidden knowledge contained within the pages of the _Necronomicon_. His frantic attempts to borrow or copy from the tome had been met with my stern warnings, and I couldn't help but wonder if I'd inadvertently contributed to the events unfolding before me.

The sound of barking dogs and screams cut through the night air, sending a shiver down my spine. I rushed towards the library, joining the growing crowd of onlookers. The moonlit window glared like an open wound, and I knew that whatever was happening inside was not for the faint of heart. Brushing aside the curious onlookers, I unlocked the vestibule door and stepped inside, motioning for Professor Warren Rice and Dr. Francis Morgan to follow.

The air inside was thick with an unholy stench, one that I recognized all too well. We made our way to the genealogical reading-room, where the source of the low whining grew louder. My heart pounded in my chest as I fumbled for the light switch, the sudden illumination bathing us in an unforgiving glare. One of my companions shrieked, and for an instant, I thought I'd stumbled into madness.

Before us lay a twisted, nightmarish form, its body contorted in a fetal position amidst a pool of greenish-yellow ichor. It was almost nine feet tall, its skin a mass of torn flesh and mangled skin. The whippoorwills outside seemed to be singing in eerie harmony with the creature's labored breathing, their mournful calls echoing through the night. As I gazed upon the monstrosity, my mind struggled to comprehend the horror before me – a creature born of darkness, a product of some eldritch force that defied human comprehension.
