As I stood poised on the deck of the black pirate ship, locked in a fierce stare with the man who had held a revolver to my head, the air seemed to thicken with tension. The pirate's dark features twisted into a macabre grin, and his ebony hand slowly rose into view, the cold, hollow eye of the gun trained squarely on my forehead. My own hand shot out, grasping for his throat, just within reach, and the pistol's trigger clicked futilely against an empty chamber.

In the split second before he could fire again, I hauled him over the edge of the deck, forcing him to drop his gun and cling to the railing with both hands. My grip on his throat constricted, silencing any cry that might have escaped his lips. We wrestled in a deadly silence, our bodies straining against each other as he struggled to break free and I fought to drag him over the side.

His face turned a deep shade of crimson, his eyes bulging from their sockets like ripe fruit about to burst. He knew his time was running out, and with a final, desperate lunge, he flung himself backward on the deck, wrenching his hands from the rail to claw at my fingers in a bid to loosen my grip.

That fleeting instant was all I needed. With a powerful downward surge, I sent him tumbling through the air, his body plummeting toward the dark waters below. I held fast, my single free hand grasping the anchor chain, my fingers locked around his throat, choking the life from him. I refused to let go, knowing that a single scream from his lips would summon his comrades to avenge him.

His convulsive struggles grew weaker, slowing until they finally ceased. Only then did I release my grip, and in an instant, he was swallowed by the darkness beneath. I hauled myself back up to the ship's rail, my eyes scanning the deck below, where the light of the distant moon cast an eerie glow on the slumbering forms of six or eight black men. A young white girl, her wrists bound, gazed up at me with a mixture of horror and warning, her eyes fixed on the gemstone glinting in the center of my headdress.
