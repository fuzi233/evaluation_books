I opened my eyes to a landscape of pain and confusion, a fog of forgetfulness clinging to my mind. I tried to lift myself, but a strange, crushing weight held me down, anchoring me to the earth as if by some invisible chain. Lying on my back, I heard a faint, labored breathing nearby. With effort, I turned my head. There, close at hand, was the Maid. She was still naked, her skin pale, and she breathed heavily. In her hand, she gripped a long wooden pole—the very one I had fashioned when the Humpt Men first attacked.

Suddenly, the flood of memory returned. I was on the raft, and she was pushing it away from the shore, driven by desperation. A low groap escaped my throat, but she did not hear me. Her gaze was fixed backward, toward the beach, her expression a mask of terrible anxiety. From the distance came the terrifying, guttural howls of the Humpt Men. Realization struck me: she had regained her senses while I remained unconscious, dragging me onto the raft just in time to save me from their claws. How she had managed it, I would never fully understand, save for the knowledge that her love for me—a man she called "Mine Own"—had granted her a strength beyond human limits.

Later, she would tell me the truth. She had woken on the ground, feeling my heavy head resting on her chest. She believed I was dead, my body so still and drained of blood that it stained everything around it. Her heart nearly shattered, but when she finally laid me down, she found a spark of life flickering within me. As she carried me back to the raft, splashing river water from my helmet onto my face to rouse me, a subtle warning sang in her spirit. Danger approached. She fought with me, wrestling me to safety, carrying me the weary distance to the vessel. Just as she grabbed the pole to launch us, she spotted her torn clothes still clutched in the grip of a surviving Humpt Man. Snatching them back with frantic haste, she pushed the raft free and leaped aboard.

As she prepared to push off, a sound emerged from the woods. Two of the creatures, the ones who had survived, poured out. They tracked her path, moving silently and deliberately toward the shore. Desperate, she worked the pole, driving the raft away with an utter frenzy. Before they could reach us, she had gained significant distance. It became clear that they could not swim, or perhaps they feared some ancient terror lurking beneath the waves; instead, they froze on the bank, staring with stupefied eyes before letting out their mournful cries. These sounds I heard clearly when I finally regained consciousness, drifting safely toward the little island refuge.

For a long time, my mind was a blur of pain and delirium, alternating between waking and a hazy dream-state where reality felt unreal. Yet, a constant thread of love surrounded me, a gentle vigilance that eased the black mists of weakness and banished despair. Eventually, the burden lifted. I woke to find myself lying softly, a sweet quietness enveloping me. Beside me knelt the Maid, her face radiant with love and gladness. She leaned down, kissing me with a tenderness that tasted of healing, her tears falling sweetly upon my cheeks. I returned the kiss, filled with contentment. She cradled my head, offering me sustenance, and as I drank, she kissed me once more, light as a breeze. I sank back into sleep, lulled by her care.

Three times this cycle repeated. On the third awakening, I found a fraction of my strength returning. I moved my hand slightly, and she understood immediately, holding it tightly as I slipped back into a deep, peaceful slumber. By the fifth awakening, I whispered the words "I love you." Instantly, she broke into tears, clutching my hand to her breast, her grief mingling with her overwhelming affection. Waking on the sixth time, I realized I lay wrapped in cloaks and bandages—clever repairs made from her own torn garments using thorns as needles, stitched together with painstaking patience during her long watches.

She saw me looking at her dress and blushed, a gentle, shy flush rising to her cheeks. She kissed me again to hide her emotion, and I wished with all my soul that I could rise to kneel in reverence before her. My strength began to return steadily. She tended to me constantly, bringing broth made from tablets and water at regular intervals marked by my timepiece. She washed me and changed the bandages, reusing them with care due to our scarcity.

By the fifth day, I was entirely eased, wondrously happy, though she insisted I remain quiet due to my lingering weakness. On the sixth, I was allowed to speak, confessing again how deeply I loved her. Her eyes, though weary from the strain of our ordeal, glowed with love and gladness. I asked her to bring me the tablets, and as I ate, I signaled for the Diskos, my great weapon, to be placed near me. I embraced her, laying her head upon my arm, telling her to sleep without fear of disturbing me. For twelve hours, she slept soundly, save for a gentle moan, nesting contentedly in the hollow of my arm. Looking down at her, I felt a quiet, constant glory of love uplift my spirit; her face seemed to imbue my heart with holiness.

As the hours passed, I drank broth and used the Diskos, treating the great weapon as a true comrade, sensing its strange, wondrous connection to me. At the twelfth hour, she awoke suddenly, rushing upward with a sweet haste to see if I was well. Seeing me laugh with quiet joy, she relaxed, though reproached herself for losing track of time. I mocked her sternly, forbidding her to dwell on it, and she threatened me playfully with her fist, laughing until I feigned a worry that my wounds might bleed again.

Curious, I asked if she had brothers, a question born of idle thought. Immediately, guilt seized me, fearing I had caused her pain. She nodded quietly, kissing my hand, and slipped away briefly, lest she weep. Returning soon, she smiled, though I saw the tears she covered while preparing new broth. Before eating, I drew her into my arms, and she submitted gladly, careful not to press too heavily on my wounds. We ate together, happy and talking. Later, I fell asleep, needing her presence even while awake, and we lay in utter contentment.

The seventh day proved wondrous. When I awoke, she was sleeping like a child beside me, her face nestled against my shoulder. She awoke instantly, having watched over me throughout the night. After washing and caring for me, I was allowed full rations of tablets and water. I felt my strength returning rapidly, eager to guard her and continue our journey to the Mighty Pyramid. We kissed the tablets and shared a cup, behaving like children yet bound by the sacred union of man and maid.

She shifted my bandages and washed me properly, keeping me low-lying as I lacked full strength. But the atmosphere was filled with her joyful spirit; she quipped, talked, and sang, making me mend quickly. After her bath in a secluded pool, she returned with her hair a lovely cloud about her shoulders and her feet bare. I teased her about her bare feet, pretending to inspect them closely, and she played the part of the coy maiden. However, I revealed my true plan: I had plucked a single hair from her head and tied her toes together with it. She sat bound to me, a captive in a playful way, hiding her surprise as we laughed like children.

Then came the ritual of hair. She set her abundant hair aside, and I took it down. She draped it over my face, kissing me as I gazed up at her beauty. Cutting a lock from my head and one from hers, she plaited them together, hiding the knot in her bosom. I protested, wanting to do the same, but fatigue prevented me. Undeterred, she cut another pair of locks, plaited them, and gave them to me. I tucked them under the bandage covering my heart. A quarrel ensued over which plait was superior, ending in kisses and forgiveness, a reflection of the foolish, glorious nature of true love.

She gently restrained my large, trembling hands, threatening me playfully if I raised them to touch her hair again. Holding me helpless, she covered my eyes with her hand and kissed me with daring tenderness before releasing me. When I extended my hand, showing it white and shaking from lack of blood, she interlaced her small, delicate fists within it. This simple act brought me immense pleasure, and she looked down, quietly happy.

I gently prodded her about her frail hands, and she responded by wrapping her arms around my neck, kissing me with utter love before stepping back to avoid hurting my wounds. Sitting beside me, I began a tale of an ancient lover and the One Maid Upon All the Earth, of their marriage, her death, and his subsequent awakening in a New Time to find her alive and different yet equally lovely. As I spoke of his reverent, painful love, the Maid grew silent, kneeling to hold my hand. Suddenly, a vision of "Motherhood" and a "shining of Memory" flooded her eyes. A solemn, strange pain touched me as my own memories stirred, and I recounted the fate of their child. An utter quiet settled over us.
