As Gerard and Margaret strolled hand in hand beneath the emerald canopy of the forest, the gentle rustle of leaves and soft chirping of birds created a soothing melody that masked the distant echoes of brutal voices and barking dogs that lingered in their minds. Their footsteps harmonized with the symphony of nature, and the warmth of the sun seeped into their skin, imbuing them with a sense of security and tranquility.

But as they walked, Gerard's gaze fell upon the tender skin of Margaret's ankles, where dark streaks of crimson marred the whiteness. His heart skipped a beat as he grasped her elbow, his voice trembling with concern. "Margaret, what's wrong? What happened?"

Margaret's smile, a radiant beacon of reassurance, illuminated her face as she spoke. "It's just a scratch, Gerard. Don't worry, I'm fine."

Gerard's eyes narrowed, his brow furrowed in distress. "A scratch? I call it a cut—a deep, cruel cut." He gently took her hand, his fingers intertwining with hers as he examined the wound more closely.

Margaret's cheeks flushed with a mix of embarrassment and affection. "It's nothing, Gerard. I just wanted to...distract the dogs. I knew they'd follow me if they saw blood." Her words hung in the air, heavy with the weight of a secret.

Gerard's grip on her hand tightened, his eyes filled with a mixture of sadness and adoration. "You would do that for me?" he whispered, his voice barely audible over the gentle rustle of the leaves.

Margaret's eyes sparkled with love and devotion as she leaned in close, her voice barely above a whisper. "Of course, Gerard. I'd give you everything I have." As she spoke, she wrapped her arms around his neck, her tears mingling with the scent of his skin.

Their entwined bodies swayed to the rhythm of the forest, their love and longing entwined like the branches above them. But amidst the serenity, Gerard's thoughts turned to the bitter reality of their impending separation. One of them would remain in Holland, the other in Italy, their love torn apart by the demands of duty and fate.
