The air was crisp, carrying the scent of damp earth and the promise of spring. I lay still, surrounded by the quiet anticipation of primroses, daisies, and snowdrops patiently waiting for the season to unfold. My body, numb and heavy, felt the gentle caress of the cold seep into its very marrow. I was aware of my existence, yet it was as if I had been born anew, untainted by the memories of warmth and pleasure. The cold was a balm, soothing every ache, calming every sorrow, and restoring balance to my being.

As I breathed in the dampness, I felt my consciousness expand, merging with the stillness of the earth. My thoughts grew distant, yet I was acutely aware of the world around me. I was a part of it, connected to the land, the seasons, and the creatures that lived within it. Time lost all meaning; I existed outside of its bounds, free from the constraints of hours, days, and years.

In this state, dreams began to unfold, like the petals of a flower opening to greet the sun. I found myself standing on a snowy peak, the mist swirling beneath my feet like a turbulent sea. The moon hung low in the sky, casting an ethereal glow over the landscape. I was a figure from a bygone era, waiting for the spark of life to be breathed into my nostrils. But I was not Adam; I was a child cradled in the arms of a mother, her skin radiant with a gentle, otherworldly light. I was a young man on horseback, leaping across the clouds, bound for a destination shrouded in mystery.

Centuries passed, or perhaps eons, or maybe just a single, endless night. Time held no significance; I was suspended in a realm beyond the reach of mortal comprehension. I drifted through the ages, a leaf on the wind, carried by the currents of the universe. I lay within the heart of a melting glacier, the moon drawing closer, its light illuminating the ice crystals. The wind and the torrent sang a lullaby, a melody of redemption, of forgiveness, and of love.

And then, without warning, the weight of my past descended upon me. Every wrong, every hurt, every transgression I had committed, from the depths of my earliest memories to the present moment, rose to the surface of my consciousness. I saw the faces of those I had wronged, and my heart swelled with regret
