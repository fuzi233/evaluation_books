Sola led me to the grand dining hall of the palace, where the air was thick with the aroma of slow-roasted zyth and the soft hum of conversation. As we entered, Dejah Thoris greeted her father with the traditional green men's formalities, her eyes downcast in respect. After the ritual was complete, she began to recount her tale of the fateful events that had transpired seven days prior.

As we sat down at the long, ornate table, Sola poured steaming cups of kah, the fragrant Martian tea, into delicate ceramic vessels. Dejah Thoris' hands trembled as she raised hers to her lips, the memory of her recent ordeal still raw. "It was a night like any other," she began, "when I slipped from the palace, hoping to escape the suffocating constraints of my duties. But little did I know that my heart was already heavy with foreboding, for I had just left an audience with Zat Arras, whose words had left me reeling."

Sola expertly sliced a platter of roasted gondi, releasing the savory scent of slow-cooked meat into the air. "I knew then that something had shaken me to my core," Dejah Thoris continued, "and when I saw Sola and a dozen of my trusted guards emerge from the shadows, I knew I wasn't alone in my desperation. Together, we followed me into the night, determined to reach the Sacred Iss and the Valley Dor, where the ancient secrets of our ancestors awaited."
