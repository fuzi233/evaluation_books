Before me unfolded a tapestry of tiny, shimmering lines, like the delicate threads of a spider's web, dancing across the fabric of the universe. These lines, I assumed to be the essence of femininity, intertwined with smaller, luminous points, all moving in perfect synchrony along a single, unbroken axis.

Finally, I came face-to-face with a majestic line, its presence radiating an aura of authority. "Who are you, and what is the meaning of this gathering?" "I am the ruler of this land," it declared, "and you, a stranger, have entered my domain without permission."
