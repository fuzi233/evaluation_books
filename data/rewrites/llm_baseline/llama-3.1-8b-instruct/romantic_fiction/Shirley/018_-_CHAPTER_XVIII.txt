As the sun began to set, casting a warm orange glow across the landscape, Caroline and Shirley strolled down from the fields, their footsteps quiet on the dew-kissed grass. The air was heavy with the scent of blooming wildflowers, and the sky was painted with hues of pink and gold, reminiscent of a summer evening in the tropics. The church bells had fallen silent, and the crowds had dispersed into the sanctuary, leaving the churchyard deserted.

"It's so peaceful tonight," Caroline said, her voice barely above a whisper.

"Yes, and soon it will be sweltering inside the church," Shirley replied, fanning herself with her apron. "I dread the thought of Dr. Boultby's lengthy sermon and the curates' tedious orations. I'd much rather stay outside and enjoy the evening air."

"But Uncle will be upset if he notices our absence," Caroline warned.

"I'll take the blame, dear. He can scold me all he likes, but I won't be cooped up in that stuffy church. Besides, I want to savor the beauty of this moment. Look, the church and the tomstones are bathed in a golden light, as if the setting sun has consecrated them. Nature is at her most serene, offering a prayer of gratitude for the bounty of the land and the safety of those who travel afar. I see her kneeling before the altar, asking for a gentle night for sailors at sea, for travelers in the wilderness, and for the creatures of the forest and the moors. Caroline, come, let me show you what she looks like. She's like the first woman, Eve, when she and Adam stood alone on earth."

"Eve as depicted by Milton?" Caroline asked, a hint of amusement in her voice.

"No, not Milton's Eve, with her stern beauty and haughty pride. I see a woman who is full of life and passion, a woman who could give birth to a world of giants – Titans, who strove to reach the heavens. Her heart beats with a fire that cannot be tamed, a flame that burns bright with creativity and strength. She's a woman who can nourish the soul, just as Mrs. Gill nourishes our bodies with her delicious pastries and preserves, carefully crafted to delight the senses. Come, let us sit on this bench, and I'll tell you more about the first woman, and the secrets she holds within her."
