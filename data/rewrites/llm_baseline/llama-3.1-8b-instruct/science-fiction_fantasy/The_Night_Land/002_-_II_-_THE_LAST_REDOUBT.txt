Now, I am left to wander through the desolate landscape of existence, a ghost haunting the shadows of what once was.

It is as if my soul has been given a preview of the wonders that await, a taste of the sweet nectar that will one day revive my spirit. I have walked among the ruins of this world, and beheld the promise of a new dawn, one that will bring me reunion with Mirdath, though it may be in a future yet unknown.

But I say to them, "Read, and see if you can deny the beauty of what I have seen."
