As the warm sun dipped below the Tuscan hills, casting a golden glow across the rolling vineyards, Romola settled onto a weathered stone beneath a majestic cypress, its branches stretching toward the sky like nature's own cathedral. The gentle rustle of leaves and the distant hum of bees created a soothing melody, transporting her to a realm of solitude. With each passing moment, she felt the constraints of her life in Florence recede, like the ebbing tide exposing hidden coves and secret beaches. Her husband, Tito, rode westward, leaving her to forge her own path, unencumbered by the expectations of others.

For the first time in her life, Romola felt an unshakeable connection to the land and the heavens above, untethered from the societal strictures that governed her existence. The earthy scent of the soil and the vast expanse of the landscape awakened a deep sense of belonging, as if the very essence of the world was speaking directly to her soul.

A low, resonant voice cut through the serenity, echoing off the stone beneath her feet. "Romola de' Bardi, wife of Tito Melema." The words sent a shiver down her spine, for she recognized the voice – a voice that had whispered secrets in her ear before. Yet, she chose not to turn, nor to raise her gaze, preferring to bask in the warmth of her newfound freedom.

The speaker approached, a black-robed figure with a commanding presence. His words, though firm, were laced with a gentle compassion that struck a chord within her. "You are fleeing Florence, Romola, dressed in the guise of a pilgrim. I have received a message from the divine, instructing me to stop you. Your path, predetermined by fate, cannot be altered."

Romola's ire simmered, her anger fueled by the audacity of this stranger who dared to intervene in her life. She remained seated, her voice steady, yet tinged with defiance. "What right do you have to address me, or to dictate my choices?"

The monk's response was measured, his words dripping with an otherworldly authority. "The right of a messenger, Romola. You have donned the cloak of a devout, yet your heart remains unaligned with the will of the divine.
