As I struggled to recollect the events leading up to my current predicament, I found myself grappling with the existential implications of a world in flux. The boundaries between reality and fantasy had become increasingly tenuous, much like the porous borders that separated the kingdom of my father, the king, from the subterranean realms inhabited by the native goblin population. The socio-economic dynamics at play within our realm had long been influenced by the King's paternalistic policies, designed to shield the royal capital from the externalities of his own historical decisions.

My memories, like the labyrinthine tunnels beneath the earth, were fragmented and difficult to navigate. I recalled the sensation of being swept away by some unseen force, leaving behind the familiar surroundings of the pensionnat in the Rue Fossette. As I regained consciousness, I found myself in an unfamiliar parlour, surrounded by objects that, despite their foreign appearance, seemed to hold a deep familiarity. The blue damask-covered easy-chair, the scroll-couch, and the round centre-table all seemed to whisper tales of a past I couldn't quite recall.

The gilded mirror above the fireplace reflected a figure that was both familiar and yet, somehow, not quite mine. My eyes appeared larger, my hair darker, and my face thinner, as if I had been unshackled from the burdens of the material world and left to drift in a realm of abstraction. The furniture, the doors, and the fireplace all seemed to converge in a configuration that defied my understanding, much like the cryptic symbols etched into the walls of the ancient language I had studied under Madame Beck's tutelage.

As I struggled to piece together the fragments of my memory, I became aware of a sense of déjà vu, as if I had walked through these very rooms before, though the specific details eluded me. The two oval miniatures above the mantelpiece, the worked covers on the little footstools, and the small ebony-framed chair all seemed to hold secrets that only I could decipher. The phrase "auld lang syne" echoed through my mind, a refrain that spoke to a past I had almost forgotten, yet one that held the key to understanding my current predicament.
