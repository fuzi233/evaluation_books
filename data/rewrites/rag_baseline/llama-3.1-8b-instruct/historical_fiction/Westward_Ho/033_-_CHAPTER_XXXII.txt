As the Armada's defeat reverberated across the European continent, a seismic shift in the global power dynamic became palpable. The eternal struggle between the forces of light and darkness, embodied by the archangel Michael and the fallen angel Lucifer, had reached a critical juncture. This apocalyptic conflict, a rare and spectacular manifestation of the spiritual struggle, had spilled onto the terrestrial plane, allowing mortals to participate in the divine reckoning. Simple men, unencumbered by the complexities of dogma, could now choose their allegiances without ambiguity, joining the righteous cause with more than just prayers and supplications, but with the tangible instruments of war.

The outcome of this climactic confrontation was a resounding affirmation of the divine, as the devil's machinations were exposed for the hollow boasts they were. Empires rose and fell, their grandeur dissipating like the fleeting wisps of smoke from a snuffed-out candle. The Byzantine Empire, the Spanish Armada, and the papal thrones were among the numerous edifices that crumbled beneath the weight of their own hubris. As the dust settled, the faithful beheld the vanquished forces, strewn like so many discarded shells along the shores of history.

In the aftermath of this cataclysmic event, a chorus of jubilation erupted from the distant corners of Europe, an outpouring of joy that echoed across the continent. From England and the Netherlands, from Germany and Geneva, and from the embattled Vaudois communities, whose ancestors lay scattered across the Alpine mountains like the seeds of a yet-unripe harvest, a nascent spirit of freedom and progress began to germinate. This nascent force would eventually give rise to the triumphant march of science, commerce, and civilization across the newly discovered world, as the seeds of virtue and greatness took root in the fertile soil of the Americas, Australia, New Zealand, and the Pacific Islands.

In England, Queen Elizabeth herself orchestrated a grand spectacle to commemorate this momentous occasion. By her majesty's command, a nationwide day of thanksgiving was decreed, with churches across the land filled to capacity as people came to offer their gratitude to the Almighty.
