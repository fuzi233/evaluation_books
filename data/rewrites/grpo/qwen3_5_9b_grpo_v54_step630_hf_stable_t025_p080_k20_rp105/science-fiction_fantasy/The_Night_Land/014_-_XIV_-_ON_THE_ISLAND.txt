Consciousness returned to me not as a gentle dawn, but as a violent tearing of the veil. Pain, sharp and blinding, seized my limbs, accompanied by a profound fog that erased the edges of my memory. I struggled to rise, but a strange, invisible weight pinned me to the ground, a force I would later understand was the lingering echo of my own shattered weakness.

I lay on my back, gasping for air. A low, ragged panting sound drifted near my ear. With effort, I turned my head slowly, my neck stiff and uncooperative. There, looming over me, was the Maid. She was still naked, her skin pale in the gloom, her chest heaving as she panted. In her hands, she gripped a long, jagged pole—the very weapon I had severed from the Humpt Men during our earlier skirmish. She pressed it desperately against the water, pushing with a ferocity that belied her exhaustion.

In that moment, the fog lifted. I realized the truth: I was on the raft, drifting away from the shore, and she was the sole engine keeping us moving. I tried to make a sound, a plea, but she did not hear me. Her gaze was fixed backward, toward the darkening shoreline, her face a mask of grim anxiety. From the woods behind her, a distant, guttural howling rose—a chorus of the Humpt Men. She had regained her senses while I remained in the abyss of unconsciousness. She had dragged me onto the raft, saved me from the beasts, and now she pushed us toward safety, driven by a love that had ignited a strength within her that defied nature. I would never know the mechanics of that miracle, nor would she; she simply knew that her man needed saving, and she would burn the world down to do it.

Later, she recounted the horror of the moment she had awakened. She had found herself lying on the cold earth, my head heavy and still upon her breast. She had feared I was already dead, my body so still, so drained of life. Her heart had nearly broken as she looked upon the blood that stained the sand around me. But when she had carefully eased me onto the ground, checking for a pulse, she found I lived. A spark of hope, fragile yet fierce, had ignited in her chest.

She had scanned the area, finding nothing but the corpse of the Humpt Man who had attacked us, and the others lying dead upon the flat-topped rock. With frantic speed, she had run to the raft, filling my helmet with river water and dousing my face to rouse me. But I remained limp. Then, a subtle intuition, a whisper from the spirit, had warned her of approaching danger. Desperation took hold. She had carried me, dragging my useless body across the rough terrain to the raft. As she secured me, she had spotted the torn garments of the Humpt Man, still clutched in the beast's hand—clothes she had slipped off to escape. With a desperate snatch, she had snatched them back, leaped aboard the raft, and began to pole us away.

As she struck the water, a sound emerged from the forest. Two surviving Humpt Men had tracked her scent. They moved silently and swiftly down the shore, intent on capturing her. She worked with utter despair, driving the pole with all her might, managing to push the raft a safe distance before they could reach the water. It became clear that the monsters could not swim, or perhaps they feared the "Dread" of the deep waters. They stopped at the edge, staring stupidly, then erupted in their characteristic howls—a sound I heard clearly as I finally regained my senses on the raft.

By the time I woke fully, the howling had faded into the distance, drowned out by the rhythmic splashing of the pole. The Maid worked with a desperation that bordered on madness. I felt a surge of desire to help her, to lift the burden, but my body refused to obey. Just as I attempted to rise, darkness swallowed me again. Yet, in that final lapse, I knew she had piloted me safely to the little island, the harbor of refuge. She had thought only of me, sacrificing her own safety, her own dignity, her own life, to ensure mine. I had been a helpless man on the brink of death, saved solely by the care of my Beloved.

Yet, in the memory of that struggle, I found a profound joy.

For a long time, my consciousness drifted in a haze of pain and weariness. There were moments of waking, moments of total oblivion, and times when I existed in a state of unrealness, unsure of myself or the world around me. Everything felt strange and vague, yet one constant remained: the presence of Love. A gentle, unwavering watchfulness surrounded me. When the black mists of weakness threatened to swamp my mind, I felt the soothing touch of that love, and when despair crept in, I found a sliver of hope.

Then, the haze cleared. I awoke to find myself laid softly upon something yielding, bathed in a sweet, quiet peace. A healthy drowsiness settled into my bones. Slowly, I opened my eyes to see the Maid kneeling beside me. Her face was illuminated with a love and gladness so pure it felt like drinking sunlight. She leaned down, kissing my lips with a gentleness that washed away my pain, her tears tracing sweet paths down my face. I kissed her back, filled with contentment.

She cradled my head in her arm and offered me a draught. After I drank, she kissed me once more, a light, airy touch like a breeze. She adjusted my head, and I succumbed to sleep, lulled by her tender care.

I recall this cycle happening three times. On the third awakening, I felt a flicker of strength returning. I moved my hand slightly, testing the limits of my recovery. She sensed my need immediately, holding my hand tightly, anchoring me as I drifted back into sleep, gazing into her eyes with all my remaining love.

On the fourth awakening, I whispered the words I had longed to say: "I love you." She broke into sudden, silent weeping, clutching my hand to her breast as if it were the only thing keeping her grounded.

On the fifth awakening, I became aware of my surroundings. I lay naked beneath a cloak, my body swathed in bandages. I learned later that these bandages were woven from the torn garments of the Maid herself. Looking at her, I saw she had been dressed in the clothes I had given her—the outer layer and the inner tunic that the Humpt Man had torn from her. She had repaired them with incredible ingenuity, using threads from her own torn clothes and needles fashioned from the thorns of the island's bushes. The thorns had broken often, requiring her to start anew a hundred times, yet she had persisted until the garments were neat and dainty.

Seeing me gaze at her with such interest, she blushed gently, recalling how I had last seen her in her vulnerability. She kissed me to hide her shame, and I wished with all my soul that I were strong enough to kneel in reverence before her. This was the nature of my love, and it is the nature of all true love.

My strength began to return steadily. The Maid tended to me constantly, feeding me broth made from tablets and water at intervals marked by my timepiece. She washed me, changed the bandages, and reused them, for we were scarce on supplies.

By the fifth day, I was entirely eased, wondrously happy, and she spoke to me with affectionate chatter, though she kept me quiet due to my lingering weakness. On the sixth day, I was allowed to speak freely. I told her how deeply I loved her, a sentiment my eyes had always proclaimed while I lay in silence. She assured me she was in good health, though I saw the thinness in her frame and the weariness in her eyes, despite the radiant love and gladness she held for me.

I urged her to eat and drink, taking the tablets myself before she could. Once she had eaten, I commanded her to bring the Diskos—the great weapon—to my side. I bid her lie by me, resting her head on my arm. I told her to sleep, promising her no weariness, for my own rest would be deeper with her beside me. At first, she worried about being too heavy, but I pressed her gently against me, showing her my recovering strength. She nestled contentedly, falling into a deep sleep, needing it desperately.

She slept for twelve hours, barely stirring except for a soft moan, occasionally turning her face closer to mine in her dreams. I felt no loneliness, only a profound contentment as I looked down upon her sleeping in the hollow of my arm. She was wondrously lovely, her face radiating a holiness that lifted my spirit into a quiet glory of love.

I drank broth at the third, sixth, and ninth hours. My right arm was free to handle the Diskos; I placed my hand upon it twice or thrice, feeling a strange kinship, as if the weapon itself knew and loved me. This feeling stemmed from my uplifted spirit, yet the Diskos was indeed a wondrous thing, always thought to share an oneness with its wielder.

At the twelfth hour, the Maid awoke suddenly, springing up with a sweet haste, relieved to see I was well. She laughed quietly at my joy, though she reproached herself for having slept so long. I mocked her sternly, forbidding her to dwell on the matter, urging her instead to share in our happiness. She threatened me playfully, tapping her fist against my nose, and I laughed so heartily she feared I might reopen my wounds. She reproached herself again, but I was unharmed.

When I could speak clearly, I asked if she had brothers, noting her playful demeanor. Realizing my thoughtlessness, I took her hand to assure her I was not heartless. She nodded quietly, kissed my hand, and slipped away to hide her tears. I called to her gently, and she returned soon, smiling lovingly, though I saw she covered her weeping while preparing my broth. Before I ate, I pulled her into my arms. She submitted gladly, careful not to press too hard on my wounds.

We ate and drank, happy in our talk. Soon, I fell asleep, but insisted she remain near, even while awake. We were utterly content together.

The seventh day was a wondrous time. When I awoke, the Maid was sleeping like a child beside me, her face nestled against mine. She woke instantly, having watched over me through the hours of my sleep. We ate and drank together after she had washed and cared for me. I was now allowed to take my tablets whole, followed by water, a pleasure that made me ache to regain my full strength quickly. I needed to guard the Maid and continue our journey to the safety of the Mighty Pyramid. With my tablets restored, I felt my fitness returning.

She fed me tablets frequently, counting them to ensure sufficiency. I accepted them without refusal, knowing I needed them to rebuild my blood, lest I be lacking when danger approached. We kissed each other's tablets, drank from the same cup, and were as happy children, yet also man and maid.

She shifted my bandages and washed me, keeping me low-lying as I lacked full strength. But she was always there, making sweet quips, laughing, and singing, her joy infectious. She went briefly to dress, promising to be swift. She returned with her hair in a lovely cloud about her shoulders, her feet still bare from her bath in a secluded pool. She teased me about my impatience, admitting she had to dress partially in front of me because she knew I delighted in watching her arrange her abundant hair. She was hungry to be with me, loving that I watched her, though a quaint shyness stirred in her heart.

I asked her to sit near my hand. I scolded her playfully for neglecting her feet, bidding her present them to me. She thought I meant to kiss them, but I had a different plan. I had plucked a hair from her head. When she presented her feet, I bound her pretty toes together with the hair. She was a captive to me, and we laughed like children. She stole her feet back, but I knew she had been careful not to break the hair binding them.

She styled her hair beautifully, then I took it down again, teasing her. She kissed me, asking how she could ever manage it properly if I always teased her. Then, she took her hair and draped it over both sides of my face, kissing me as I looked up at her from the beauty surrounding me.

She cut a lock from my hair and a lock from her own, plaiting them together and hiding the blend in her bosom. I was displeased and tried to do the same, but my arms were too weak. She cut a second lock from me and a second tress from herself, made me kiss hers, and then kissed mine. She plaited them and gave them to me. I placed them under the great bandage over my heart. She was the one discontent now, claiming the second plaiting was kissed while the first lacked it. We quarreled playfully until we kissed for forgiveness. Such glad foolishness is known to all who have loved.

She made me keep my hands down from her hair, threatening me with dreadful punishment if I were not humble. I declared myself humble, and she held my large hands with her tiny ones, her thumbs barely grasping me. She covered my eyes with her free hand, holding me utterly helpless, and kissed me daintily and impudently on the mouth. Then she released me, becoming demure.

We sat in silence for a while. I extended my great, white, trembling hand, lacking blood. The Maid clenched her two small fists and placed them inside my one large hand. I was happy; it was a wondrous pleasure. Her eyelids lowered slightly, and she was quietly happy. I teased her gently about her small hands, and she wrapped her arms around my neck, kissing me with utter love and tenderness before slipping away lest I overexert myself.
