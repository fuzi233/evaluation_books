The recipe, known as "Mama's Elixir," was said to have healing properties, especially effective against fevers and maladies of the mind. As she chopped the fragrant herbs and simmered the broth, Caroline couldn't help but think of her own grandmother, who had taught her the recipe and shared stories of its origins.

Caroline's thoughts were interrupted by a knock at the door. It was Mrs. Pryor, come to visit and offer her assistance. As they sat in the kitchen, sipping the fragrant broth, Caroline felt a sense of comfort wash over her. Mrs. Pryor, sensing her unease, took Caroline's hand and examined her pulse. Her eyes narrowed slightly as she noted the rapid beat, but her expression remained kind and concerned.

"I'll stay with you for a while, dear Caroline," Mrs. Pryor said, her voice soft and soothing. "Your uncle has given his permission, and I'd like to help nurse you back to health." Caroline smiled weakly, grateful for the company and the familiar scent of the broth.

The aroma of the stew filled the air, mingling with the scent of the herbs from Caroline's broth, and for a moment, Caroline felt a sense of peace wash over her.
