I nodded sympathetically, intrigued by the complexity of his situation. "No clear motive?" I asked.

Mr. Godfrey shook his head. "I'm like a child, Miss Clack – I simply don't know why I acted as I did. I proposed to Miss Verinder, only to neglect my duties and abandon my passions. I've apostatized from the very causes I once held dear. It's as if I've lost my way, and I'm powerless to explain it."
