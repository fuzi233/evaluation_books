As Caroline stirred the fragrant mixture of herbs and spices in the simmering pot on the hearth, she couldn't shake the thought that Robert's marriage to Shirley would soon become a reality. Her mind wandered to the upcoming union, and she couldn't help but feel a pang of sadness wash over her. "Of course, I know he'll choose her," she murmured to herself as she carefully measured out the ingredients for the evening's bouillabaisse. "She can bring stability and security to his life, but what about me? Will I be forgotten once they're married?"

The scent of the simmering broth wafted up, transporting Caroline back to a simpler time when she'd cooked with her mother in the small kitchen of their family's cottage. She remembered the way her mother would lovingly chop the fresh vegetables and the way the flavors melded together as they cooked. Caroline's thoughts were interrupted by the soft knock at the door, signaling the arrival of Miss Keeldar. She wiped her hands on her apron and made her way to greet her guest.

Miss Keeldar entered the kitchen, her eyes scanning the space with a discerning gaze. "I see you're busy preparing dinner," she observed, her voice warm and inviting. "May I join you?" Caroline hesitated for a moment before nodding, and Miss Keeldar took a seat at the table, her eyes never leaving Caroline's face.

"I've come to ask you why you didn't visit me as promised this afternoon," Miss Keeldar said, her tone gentle but probing. Caroline shifted uncomfortably, unsure how much to reveal. "I wasn't in the mood," she replied, trying to brush off the question. But Miss Keeldar's eyes saw right through her.

"You're one of those people who gets lost in their own thoughts, aren't you?" she said, her voice tinged with a hint of understanding. "You get so caught up in your own world that you forget about those around you." Caroline felt a flush rise to her cheeks as Miss Keeldar continued, "You have a habit of retreating into yourself when things get difficult, don't you?"

As they sat there, surrounded by the warm scents of the kitchen, Caroline found herself opening up to Miss Keeldar in a way she rarely did with anyone else.
