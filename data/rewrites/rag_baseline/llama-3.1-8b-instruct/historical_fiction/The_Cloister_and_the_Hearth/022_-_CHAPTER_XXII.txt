The sound of a distant shout pierced the air, and I knew that I was not alone. I turned to rejoin my Master, but the cry grew louder, more urgent, and I recognized the panic in his voice.

I hastened back up the slope, my footsteps quiet on the sodden earth. When I reached his side, he was frantically scanning the horizon, his eyes wild with fear. The wind howled around us, threatening to consume us whole.
