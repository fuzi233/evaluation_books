The evening sun cast its warm glow over the terracotta rooftops of Florence as Romola waited patiently in the loggia, her eyes fixed on the winding Arno River below. The air was alive with the murmur of departing footsteps and the clanging of horse hooves on the cobblestones. The French army, which had brought with it an aura of uncertainty and unease, was finally leaving the city. Romola's heart swelled with a mix of emotions – relief, hope, and a hint of superstition – as she anticipated the possibility that the anxieties plaguing her, particularly those surrounding Tito, might dissipate with their departure.

As the last of the troops disappeared beyond the city walls, Romola descended into the family library, her footsteps echoing off the stone floor. The flickering light of a single candle cast eerie shadows on the walls as she settled into her usual spot at the desk. The task of copying the inventory lay before her, a chore she had been putting off all day. Tito had left early that morning, and she didn't expect him to return until later, so she had planned to leave the library and retire to the cozy salon, adorned with delicate frescoes of nymphs and birds, to await his arrival.

However, as fate would have it, Tito appeared sooner than expected, his figure emerging from the fading light like a ghostly apparition. Romola's initial reaction was one of surprise, quickly followed by a surge of affection as she rose from her seat to greet him. Her hands reached out to unwind the scarf from around his neck, a gesture both familiar and intimate.

"Tito, dear one," she said, her voice barely above a whisper, "I wasn't expecting you so soon." Her words were laced with a mixture of concern and delight as she wrapped her arms around him, pulling him close.

Tito's response was characteristic of his usual self – a bright, charming smile that belied the turmoil brewing within. He clasped her tightly, his head tilted back, and Romola's heart skipped a beat as she sensed a glimmer of the old Tito reemerging. "Not welcome?" he teased, his eyes sparkling with mirth, as he gently stroked her hair.
