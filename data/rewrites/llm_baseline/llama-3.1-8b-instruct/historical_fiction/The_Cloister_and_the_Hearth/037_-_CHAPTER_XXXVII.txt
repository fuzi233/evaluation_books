The townspeople, their faces a mixture of curiosity and suspicion, milled about him. Some cast nervous glances, while others openly pointed and whispered among themselves. Gerard's eyes scanned the crowd, searching for a familiar face, but none seemed to recognize him.

As the hours ticked by, the sun beat down upon his skin, warming the wooden beams that held him fast. The smell of roasting meats from the nearby butcher's stall mingled with the scent of bread, creating a savory aroma that made his stomach growl with hunger. Gerard's thoughts turned to the simple pleasures of his youth – the warmth of a hearth fire, the taste of a freshly baked loaf, and the comfort of a hearty meal shared with loved ones.
