I followed him, my arms folded and head bowed, as we navigated the glittering throng of courtiers, soldiers, and dignitaries.

Their warm smiles and gentle nods reached out to me, a stark contrast to the cold formality that had characterized our parting moments. Though I'd once held the reins of power, I'd grown accustomed to concealing my emotions behind a mask of serenity. Yet, as I walked behind Tatho, the kindness in their eyes lingered, a poignant reminder of the esteem they still held for me.

In this tradition-steeped society, it was customary for the people to air their grievances and suggestions during such ceremonies, but today, an unusual silence reigned. Not a single voice spoke out against me or my governance, nor did anyone propose changes for the future. As we completed the prescribed circuit, the assembly's approval hung in the air, a palpable yet unspoken endorsement.

With measured steps, Tatho and I proceeded down the golden tiles of the council hall, accompanied by the great officers of state. At the far end, we reached the entrance to the private chambers that had once been mine. The door swung open at Tatho's command, and we stepped inside, leaving the assembled officials behind.

As the massive stone valves closed behind us, I felt a pang of melancholy wash over me. I had no home in the grand cities of Yucatan anymore, and the thought of returning to the lost continent of Atlantis with my dignity intact was a bittersweet solace.
