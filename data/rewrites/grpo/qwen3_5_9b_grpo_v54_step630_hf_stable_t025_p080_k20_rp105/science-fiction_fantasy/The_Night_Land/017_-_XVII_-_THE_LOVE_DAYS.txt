When consciousness returned to me, I found myself ascending in the Lift, resting upon that same bed where I had once believed I would never lie again, nor ever need to rise from the silent country below. A vague, strange sensation washed over me as the deep, thunderous resonance of the Underground Organs rose from the world's mighty depths. It sounded like a distant music beyond death itself—a rolling chant sung by multitudes across far mountains, sometimes a low-blowing wind from the Deep, and again, the clear, ancient melody of the Song of Honour. I knew, in a dreamlike state, that the millions in that subterranean realm were celebrating this wonder of joy. Yet, everything remained faint and half-hidden; my eyes felt powerless to open, and I seemed to float eternally upon strange waters of unreality.

Then, sweet and lovely odours drifted toward me—scents of reality emanating from the great Fields where flowers perpetually bloomed along the Lift's passages, as the elevator climbed through its vast miles. Perhaps I stirred slightly, for the voice of the Master Doctor came low and gentle, bidding me rest and assuring me that the Maid was well. Soon after, I drifted into a haze, living half-days and half-sleeps, wondering without trouble whether I was truly dead.

Then came days of profound quietude, devoid of thought. The Master Doctor often leaned over me, scrutinizing my face with keen intensity. Finally, after strange intervals, another figure bent down—the dear, lovely face of Mine Own. Her eyes spoke love directly into my soul, though she remained calm and hushed. I began to live within my body again, perhaps making a small, fumbling movement with my hands. She took them, holding them gently, and life flowed from her to me. She was ever wordless and tender; contentment grew within me, leading to a natural slumber.

One day, I was allowed to rise. Those who tended me carried me to one of the Quiet Gardens of the Pyramid, setting me down and seemingly leaving me alone. Then, one emerged from behind a bush, looking at me for a moment with a half-shy expression. Yet the love shining in her eyes rendered that shyness insignificant. Truly, I knew it was Mine Own Maid, though I had never seen Naani dressed so prettily as a maid. Looking at her, I realized she appeared more dainty than I could have imagined. Suddenly, I tried to rise to approach her, but she ran quickly to stop my natural foolishness. She sat beside me, taking my head against her breast, refusing to deny me her lips. In that single moment, she was both maid and mother to me.

Afterward, she kept me very still, and we sat in utter, dumb happiness until the attendants returned. The Master of the Doctors was among them, and I saw a trace of satisfaction on his face. From that day forward, I saw Mine Own Maid every day, recovering health with wondrous speed, for Love healed me. Soon, I was permitted to descend to the Fields, though by private paths, for the Multitudes might otherwise follow me constantly, and I needed silence.

The Maid accompanied me. The Master Monstruwacan and the Master of the Doctors agreed to this arrangement, appointing an Officer of Marriage to wed us quietly and simply. I was still too weak for a public ceremony, which would come later when the Millions erected a Guard of Honour eight miles high, from the top to the bottom of the Mighty Pyramid. That grand ceremonial was reserved for the People, who could not be denied the honor they bestowed upon me.

Surely, the Maid stayed with me always, becoming my wife and my strength, allowing Mine Own to regain perfect health. We entered the Love Days, the most beautiful of all if Love is true. We wandered through the mighty Fields at our will, walking the Love Paths near the villages. We hid our names to avoid being beset by those driven by natural curiosity and kindness, needing to remain utterly together and quiet.

We chose sleeping spots where the beauty of flowers was most wondrous, carrying some food, though we also ate when we reached the villages scattered throughout the Fields—places truly as vast as countries. Mine Own kept her promise a hundred times, preparing me a great, hearty meal. She teased me relentlessly as I ate, calling me a glutton, and kissed me to ensure I never had a chance to defend myself. Truly, she was everything my heart and spirit desired; she companioned me with Love and brought Joy into my spirit.

Once, we descended to the Country of Silence, though we did not stay long, for my memories began to return. In later times, we wandered there often, accompanied by Memory, the holiness of great thoughts, and a Love that holds all. As we left that country, I told Mine Own how, when she had been suspended by the Horrid Force of the House, I had suffered dreadful pain, fearing I would never wake to discover her kissing me while I slept. She blushed most lovely, unaware I had been conscious of her sweet naughtiness. She had spent all her thoughts on my agony during her death, before the Earth-Force's vapour freed her spirit from Silence. She came to me with dear understanding.

She explained that the Doctors said she had been stunned and frozen in spirit, her entire being and life suspended. The great life-force of the Earth-Current had woken her spirit, restoring her body and blood. The Doctors had searched the old Records of their Work, finding mention of one such occurrence in ancient times, but nothing like it had happened through a mighty age of years.

While wandering and resting in the Fields, I often told Mine Own of various matters. I knew she had learned some odd things before I recovered, though not much; she, too, had been utterly wretched, as you might imagine. She had risen from her bed while I lay still, ordered by the Master Doctor who feared I would truly die without something to awaken my spirit. In verity, you must consider the depth of my Love as I know she held my hands so bravely and gently, having scarce power in her own feet. I offered a little holy praise for Mine Own.

Thus, I come to this ending, with one final thing to tell. This happened a while later, after we had undergone the second marriage, the Public Marriage. One day, My Wife, Mine Own, took me with sweet cunning to the Hall of Honour. When I arrived, I saw many of the People standing in silence within that great Hall, waiting for something. My Wife led me to the center of the Hall. Suddenly, I understood why she had brought me there. Standing in the Place of Honour was a statue of a man in broken armour, carrying a maid forever.

I stood dumb. How could this Age know the honour this meant? It was an honour given only to the Great Dead. I was but a young man, far from greatness, save that I loved with all my heart and spirit, making death a small thing before Love. You must understand how Love makes the heart sweet and brave, granting humility, wonder, and natural pride that anyone would think to honour me. Mine Own wept with joy and honest pride beside me. An utter silence of dear sympathy filled the great Hall. Those present let me pass in quietness with Mine Own, a lovely thing of understanding.

I walked lovingly and thoughtfully with my Wife, who stayed very near. I had gained Honour, yet I learned that Honour is but the ash of Life unless you possess Love. I had Love. To have Love is to have all, for truly Love mothers Honour and Faithfulness; and these three build the House of Joy.
