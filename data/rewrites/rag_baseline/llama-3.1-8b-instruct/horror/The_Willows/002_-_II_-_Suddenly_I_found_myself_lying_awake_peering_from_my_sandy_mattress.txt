The moon cast an ethereal glow through the fabric, illuminating the Swede, who slumbered peacefully beside me. My gaze drifted to my wristwatch, its luminescent face gleaming in the moonlight, revealing that it was past midnight – the threshold of a new day. I had managed a couple of hours of rest, yet my mind refused to quiet. A creeping sense of unease settled within me, like a faint tremor that refused to dissipate.

I rose onto my haunches, careful not to disturb my companion, and peered out into the darkness. The willows swayed violently in the gusts, their branches creaking and groaning as the wind buffeted them. Our shelter, nestled in a small clearing, remained secure, the wind passing over it without causing any undue stress. Yet, my trepidation persisted, refusing to be shaken.

A shiver ran down my spine as I spotted movement among the bushes opposite. I froze, my eyes fixed on the shifting foliage. It was as if the leaves themselves were taking on lives of their own, weaving a tapestry of silhouettes against the moonlit sky. I sat back, transfixed, my breath caught in my throat. There, amidst the swaying branches, were forms – indistinct, yet unmistakable. They seemed to coalesce and shift, their outlines blurring and reforming with each gust of wind.

My initial impulse was to rouse the Swede, to share this enigmatic sight with him, but something stayed my hand. A growing conviction, perhaps, that I shouldn't seek corroboration – that to acknowledge the truth would be to invite a confrontation with the unknown. Instead, I crouched there, drinking in the spectacle before me, my mind racing with questions and hypotheses.

As I watched, the shapes grew more defined, their contours emerging from the shadows. They loomed large, their bronzy hue seeming almost otherworldly. I strained my eyes, attempting to discern their features, but they remained elusive, hidden behind a veil of leaves.
