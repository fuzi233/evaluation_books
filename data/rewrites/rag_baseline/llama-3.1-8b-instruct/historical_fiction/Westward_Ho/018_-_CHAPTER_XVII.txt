His comrades, however, remained steadfast in their resolve, buoyed by the gentle caress of the trade-wind, which whispered sweet nothings of hope and redemption. Drew, the seasoned navigator, confidently declared, "That should be Barbados, your worship," his voice laced with a quiet conviction born of experience. Yet, the notion of Barbados was unfamiliar to Frank, a name that conjured whispers of an unknown world, a realm of untold riches and unexplored wonders.
