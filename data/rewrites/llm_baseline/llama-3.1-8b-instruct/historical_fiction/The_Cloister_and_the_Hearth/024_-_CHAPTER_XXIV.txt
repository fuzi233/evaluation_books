As Gerard continued southward, the fading light cast long shadows across the deserted road. He had been walking for hours, and the silence was broken only by the distant rumble of thunder. A faint drizzle had begun to fall, casting a misty veil over the landscape. Gerard hastened his pace, his eyes scanning the horizon for any sign of shelter or a village.

Suddenly, a horse and its riders came into view. Two men stood beside the animal, their faces set in lazy smiles as they conversed in hushed tones. A third figure, dressed in a vibrant yellow cloak, lay sprawled on the ground, oblivious to the commotion. Gerard approached cautiously, unsure what to make of the scene unfolding before him.

One of the men, a burly fellow with a thick beard, caught Gerard's eye and raised an eyebrow. "A bit peckish, friend?" he asked, his voice dripping with amusement. Gerard hesitated, unsure how to respond, but the other man, a lean figure with a mischievous glint in his eye, intervened. "Nay, 'tis not food that troubles our friend," he said, his tone dripping with sarcasm. "Though I daresay, a hot meal might do him some good."

Gerard's confusion deepened as the conversation turned to the matter at hand: the state of the man on the ground. One of the men, who introduced himself as Ulric, claimed that the gentleman was simply taking a leisurely rest, while the other, a jovial fellow named Hans, suggested that the gentleman's condition was more serious. Gerard watched as the two men launched into a heated debate, their words becoming increasingly animated.

As the argument escalated, Gerard noticed that the horse grew restless, its ears pricked and its eyes rolling wildly. Suddenly, Hans struck Ulric across the face, and the two men began to brawl. Gerard winced as the horse, startled by the commotion, bolted, sending the two men tumbling after it. The gentleman, still prone on the ground, sat up with a start, his eyes blazing with fury as he drew his sword.

Gerard quickly retreated, his mind reeling from the sudden chaos. He watched as the horse galloped away, the two men in hot pursuit, and the gentleman careening after them, his sword flashing in the fading light.
