As the winter's chill seeped into the stone walls of their Florentine home, Romola woke to the gentle tapping of the wooden shutters against the wind. The cold light of dawn crept into the room, casting an eerie glow on the sleeping form of the young woman. The soft knock at the door was followed by the arrival of Maso, bearing the request for the traveling-wallet. Romola's eyes fluttered open, and with a mixture of resignation and trepidation, she rose from her pallet.

Her hair, usually neatly braided, now cascaded down her back in loose tangles, and the folds of her grey mantle seemed to swallow her slender frame. The dark cowl cast a somber shadow on her pale face, a stark contrast to the vibrant colors of the frescoes adorning the walls. The image of the Madonna della Misericordia, with her outstretched hands and benevolent smile, seemed to mock her, its beauty a cruel reminder of the world outside, where suffering and hardship reigned supreme.

As she handed Maso the wallet, Romola's thoughts turned to the task ahead. She would have to write two letters, one to Tito and another to her godfather, Bernardo del Nero. The words she would write would seal her fate, marking the end of a chapter in her life and the beginning of a new path, one she could hardly envision. The thought sent a shiver down her spine, and she hastened to complete the task.

The letters were brief, but their meaning was clear. The first one to Tito was a statement of fact, a declaration of the irreversibility of her feelings. She recalled the words they had exchanged during their last meeting, the words that had marked the beginning of her descent into isolation. The second letter, addressed to Bernardo, was a plea, a request to send her bridal chest to him, containing the memories of her family and her past life.

As she folded the ring inside the letter to Tito, Romola felt a pang of sadness. The ring, once a symbol of her love and commitment, now represented a chapter closed, a life left behind. She took a deep breath, steeling herself for the journey ahead. The crucifix, a reminder of her devotion to her faith, lay hidden beneath her mantle, a tangible connection to the world beyond the confines of her home.
