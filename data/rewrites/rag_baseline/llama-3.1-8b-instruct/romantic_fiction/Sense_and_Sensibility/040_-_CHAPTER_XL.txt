Mrs. Jennings' warm smile radiated like a gentle summer breeze as she settled into the plush armchair, her eyes sparkling with excitement. "Well, Miss Dashwood," she began, her voice a soothing melody, "I daresay you've heard the news from Colonel Brandon by now." Her gaze lingered on Elinor's face, searching for a hint of confirmation, before continuing, "Though, I must confess, I tried my best to remain discreet, I couldn't help catching a snippet or two of their conversation. And I must say, I'm overjoyed for both of you."

Elinor's reserved nature allowed a faint blush to rise to her cheeks as she acknowledged Mrs. Jennings' words. "Thank you, ma'am. Indeed, it is a cause for great joy, and I feel deeply grateful for Colonel Brandon's kindness. His compassion is truly one of a kind, and I've never seen anyone with a heart as generous as his."

Mrs. Jennings chuckled knowingly, her eyes twinkling with amusement. "Ah, my dear, I'm not surprised in the least. I've long suspected that Colonel Brandon had a special place in his heart for you. His benevolence is renowned, after all." She leaned forward, her voice taking on a conspiratorial tone, "But, I must admit, I'm intrigued by the speed at which events have unfolded. When a man sets his mind to something, he'll find a way to make it happen, mark my words."

As the servant announced the carriage's arrival, Mrs. Jennings rose to leave, her thoughts already turning to the evening ahead. "I must be off, my dear. We can discuss all the details then, just the two of us. I won't trouble you with company now, as I'm sure your mind is preoccupied with the news. Besides, you must share it with your sister soon – though perhaps not just yet, eh?"

Marianne had slipped out of the room before the conversation began, leaving Elinor to field Mrs. Jennings' questions alone. "Of course, ma'am. I shall tell Marianne, but I'd rather not mention it to anyone else just yet. Not even Lucy, if you please. A day or two's delay won't make a difference, and I need to write to Mr. Ferrars first. He has a lot to attend to regarding his ordination, and I wouldn't want any misunderstandings."
