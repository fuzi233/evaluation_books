The soft glow of the desk lamp cast a warm ambiance in the comfortably appointed room within the offices of the _Record_. Sir James Molloy's pen paused mid-stroke as the telephone on his polished wooden desk emitted a sharp buzz. His secretary, Mr. Silver, arose from his chair, his eyes never leaving the task at hand, and approached the instrument.

"Who is it?" he asked, his voice low and measured. A brief pause ensued before he replied, "Ah, Mr. Bunner, yes. I'm afraid Sir James is rather...indisposed at the moment." A hint of amusement danced across his features as he added, "But I'll let him know you're on the line."

Mr. Silver's gaze lingered on the phone for a moment before he handed it to Sir James, who took the receiver with a hint of trepidation. His deep, resonant voice filled the room as he spoke into the mouthpiece, "Yes?" The words hung in the air like a challenge, awaiting a response.

As Sir James listened intently, his expression transformed from one of detached interest to one of growing concern. His eyes widened, and a faint gasp escaped his lips. "Good heavens," he muttered, his voice barely audible. The muscles in his jaw clenched, and he grasped the receiver tightly, his knuckles whitening as he leaned forward.

Fascinated by the drama unfolding before him, Mr. Silver watched as Sir James's face contorted in a mixture of shock and dismay. The journalist's usually composed demeanor had given way to an air of intense focus, his every fiber attuned to the conversation on the other end of the line.

As the moments ticked by, Sir James's gaze drifted toward the grandfather clock standing sentinel in the corner of the room, its gentle ticking a stark contrast to the turmoil brewing within him. With a sudden movement, he spoke to Mr. Silver over the top of the transmitter, his voice low and urgent, "Figgis, Williams – we need to find them immediately."

With a swift nod, Mr. Silver sprang into action, dashing from the room to execute his master's bidding. As the door closed behind him, Sir James's attention returned to the conversation at hand. His words were laced with a sense of incredulity, "You're certain of this, Mr. Bunner? How long has this been known?"
