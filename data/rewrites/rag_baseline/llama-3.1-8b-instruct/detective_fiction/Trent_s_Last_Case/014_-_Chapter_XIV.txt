He turned to his companion, seeking guidance. "Shall I resort to the trite and familiar, or attempt something more...substantial?"

His companion's suggestion, 'Say what you want to say,' proved unhelpful, and Trent's frustration grew. He had been wanting to announce his engagement to Mabel for what felt like an eternity, but he knew that a straightforward declaration would not suffice. "I must frame my words with care," he muttered to himself, "lest they appear too pedestrian, too lacking in intellectual heft."

As he scribbled a few tentative lines, his companion interrupted him, producing a manuscript from her reticule. "I thought you might find this of interest," she said, her eyes sparkling with curiosity. Trent's gaze fell upon the pages, and he felt a surge of trepidation. "Do you realize," he asked, "that the language employed here is deliberately stilted, designed to convey a sense of gravitas and authority?"

His companion's response was measured. "I fail to see the necessity for such verbosity. In my experience, individuals in positions of power do not speak thus. They employ clear, concise language, unencumbered by unnecessary ornamentation." Trent nodded thoughtfully, recognizing the validity of her observation.
