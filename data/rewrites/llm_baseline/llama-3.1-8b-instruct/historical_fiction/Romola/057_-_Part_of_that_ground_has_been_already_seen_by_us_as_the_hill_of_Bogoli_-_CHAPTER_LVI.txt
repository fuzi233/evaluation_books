As the morning sun cast its warm rays upon the cobblestone streets of Florence, Romola strolled along the ancient walls, her footsteps echoing off the buildings as she made her way from San Marco. The air was alive with the sweet scent of blooming flowers and the distant hum of vendors calling out their daily wares. She turned onto the narrow streets of Borgo La Croce, the silence a stark contrast to the vibrant energy of the city. Her eyes scanned the deserted alleys, taking in the intricate details of the stone facades and the terra cotta rooftops.

It was then that she spotted him – a small, tousled-haired boy, no more than three winters old, who had wandered away from his mother's watchful eye. His eyes, dark and wide with tears, locked onto Romola's as he stood frozen in the middle of the street, his chubby fists clenched around the hem of his white shirt. A tall, imposing figure loomed behind him, casting a long shadow that seemed to heighten the boy's distress. With a sob, he doubled over, his body wracked with tiny, convulsive movements.

Romola's maternal instincts kicked in, and she rushed towards the child, her hands extended in a soothing gesture. She knelt beside him, speaking softly, her voice a gentle melody that seemed to calm the storm within him. As she wrapped her arms around his small frame, he buried his face in her shoulder, his sobs gradually subsiding into sniffles. His grip on her arm loosened, and he gazed up at her with wonder, his eyes drinking in the sight of this kind stranger.

"You've lost your way, little one," Romola whispered, her lips brushing against his cheek. "Don't worry, we'll find your home. Perhaps your mother will be waiting for you." She guessed that the boy had slipped away when his mother's attention was diverted, and that he would soon be reclaimed.

With a gentle smile, Romola attempted to lift the boy, but he was too heavy. "Come, little one," she coaxed, "you must walk with me." His parted lips remained motionless, his one hand still clutching his shirt, but the other yielded to her gentle persuasion. Together, they set out, Romola's eyes scanning the familiar streets, searching for a glimpse of the boy's home.
