As I stood before the mirror in our Rue Fossette dormitory, Miss Fanshawe burst into a fit of laughter, momentarily suspending her attentions to her attire. I couldn't help but ask, "What now?" Her gaze still fixed on me, she replied with a mix of candor and impertinence, "It strikes me as peculiar, really, that you and I find ourselves now on equal footing, moving within the same social circles." Her words hung in the air like a challenge, and I found myself responding, "Indeed, yes, though I must admit I never had much esteem for the company you kept in the past—Mrs. Cholmondeley's set was hardly to my taste."

Her eyes sparkled with genuine curiosity as she pressed on, "And who, pray tell, are you, Miss Snowe?" The query caught me off guard, and I couldn't help but chuckle at the directness of her inquiry. "You recall, of course, when I first arrived here, I was employed as a nursery governess. I even took care of the children in this very house, carrying little Georgette in my arms just as one would a bonne—a role few governesses would have been willing to adopt. Now, Madame Beck treats me with a courtesy she reserves for no one else, including the native-born St. Pierre, and your cousin, that proud girl, has taken me under her wing."

Miss Fanshawe's expression betrayed her bewilderment, and I laughed at her perplexity. "It's almost as if you're a figure from another world," she mused. "I'm surprised you don't seem more pleased by all this attention." Her words struck a chord, and I felt a flush rise to my cheeks, but I chose to remain composed, focusing instead on the inconsequential nature of her earlier jibe. "Civility, after all, is merely a gesture," I pointed out. "Why does it unsettle you so?"

She continued to press her point, her words tumbling forth in a rush, "One can't help but wonder at the way things unfold sometimes. Your equanimity in the face of all this is quite remarkable, Miss Snowe." I shot back with a hint of irony, "Perhaps you're marveling at marvels of your own creation. Shall we proceed?"
