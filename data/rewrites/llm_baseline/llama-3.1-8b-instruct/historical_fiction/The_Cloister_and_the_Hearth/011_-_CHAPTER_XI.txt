As Gerard emerged from the darkness, a gentle grasp enveloped his hand, and Margaret stood by his side, her presence a beacon in the uncertain night. Together, they crept around the corner of the tower, wary of the trap that might await them. Gerard's mind seethed with resentment towards Ghysbrecht, convinced that the burgomaster had engineered this sinister snare to ensnare him.

But as they turned the bend, a sight took their breath away. Perched atop the tower, a glowing entity unlike any they had ever seen pulsed with an otherworldly energy. Its fiery head blazed like a lantern, casting an eerie glow across the dark stone. The creature's body remained shrouded in shadows, but its presence seemed almost palpable.

Below, a figure in white stood motionless, a ghostly apparition that sent shivers down their spines. Gerard's grip on Margaret's hand tightened as they watched in rapt attention. "The rope! The rope is ascending," he whispered, his voice trembling with a mix of awe and trepidation.

As they gazed, the glowworm vanished into Gerard's former prison, bathing the interior in a warm, golden light. The white figure remained stationary, an enigmatic presence that seemed to hold the key to the mystery. Such was the hold of the supernatural that even the sturdiest of wills could be reduced to a quivering mass of nerves. It was as if the very fabric of reality had been rent asunder, revealing a realm beyond mortal comprehension.

Margaret felt an inexplicable pull, as if drawn by an unseen force. Without warning, she broke free from Gerard's grasp and rushed towards the specter, her heart pounding in her chest. Gerard, lost in his own fear, failed to recognize the primal urge that drove his sister towards the unknown. He dropped to his knees, his voice rising in a desperate plea for protection.

"By the blessing of the blessed Virgin Mary, I command you to depart!" he exclaimed, his words echoing through the night air. But to his astonishment, the specter let out a faint cry of terror, its malevolent presence faltering for an instant. Emboldened by this weakness, Gerard redoubled his exorcisms, and soon the ghostly form knelt at Margaret's feet, its eyes beseeching for mercy.
