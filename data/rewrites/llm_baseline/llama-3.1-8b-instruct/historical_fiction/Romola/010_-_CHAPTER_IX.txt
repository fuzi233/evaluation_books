As Tito navigated the crowded streets, the sounds of the festival swirling around him, he couldn't shake the memory of the enigmatic contadina whose piercing blue eyes had caught his gaze earlier. Though he hadn't revealed the truth to Nello, Tito found himself hoping to catch another glimpse of her, to unravel the mystery of those captivating eyes. Yet, his mind soon wandered to the friar whose weathered face seemed to hold a long-forgotten significance for him. Where had he encountered that gaunt figure before? The recollection lingered, a faint whisper in the recesses of his mind, like the scent of wild herbs carried on the breeze.

Tito's thoughts turned to his impending visit to the Via de' Bardi, where his presence was eagerly anticipated. He pictured the scene unfolding: the warm glow emanating from the blind man's chair, Romola's face aglow with a radiant light, her skin flushed with a delicate pink hue. Her smile, a gentle blend of womanly sensuality and childlike innocence, left him enchanted. As they spent hours together, he found himself drawn to her simplicity, his heart responding to the unassuming nobility that shone within her. In her presence, he felt a deep reverence, akin to the ancient reverence paid to the earth goddess, whose power and essence transcended the boundaries of human understanding.

Though they had never been alone, Tito's imagination ran wild with the prospect of a future where Romola might confide in him, her words whispered softly in his ear. He recalled a chance encounter in Greece, where a shy peasant girl had boldly approached him, her dark eyes sparkling with a playful innocence. But Romola's love, if it came at all, would be a different tale altogether – a story woven from the threads of subtlety and restraint, rather than bold declarations. As he pondered this, Tito's thoughts were interrupted by the warmth of the sun on his skin, and the rhythmic beat of the festival drums in the distance. He quickened his pace, his footsteps echoing off the stone buildings as he made his way through the winding streets, lost in the intricate dance of his own desires and uncertainties.
