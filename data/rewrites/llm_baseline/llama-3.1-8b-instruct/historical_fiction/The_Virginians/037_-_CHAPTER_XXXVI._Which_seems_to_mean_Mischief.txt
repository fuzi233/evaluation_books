As Lady Maria entered the sitting room, she was met with an air of unexpected calmness from her aunt, the Baroness Bernstein. Despite their tumultuous encounter the previous day, the Baroness seemed unbothered by the events that had transpired. 

"Of course, my dear Maria," she began, "I couldn't help but feel that your marriage to Harry Warrington might not be the most prudent decision. His connection to me, as his near relative, makes it rather... complicated. However, I suppose if he chooses to pursue this union, it's ultimately his decision. And I must admit, my initial offer of five thousand pounds was perhaps made in haste." 

Lady Maria watched her aunt warily, sensing a shift in her demeanor. "So, you knew about the arrangement at Castlewood?"

The Baroness nodded, a hint of a smile on her lips. "Yes, I was aware of the discussions. If I had known the extent of it, I might have chosen to... temper my opposition."

A delicate silence fell between them before the Baroness continued, "It's not uncommon for misunderstandings to arise, especially when words are taken out of context. Your letters to Harry, for instance, seemed to imply certain... unpleasant truths about our family. I realize now that they were meant to defend yourself, not to malign others."

Lady Maria's expression softened slightly at this acknowledgment. "Yes, I wrote to clear my name and vindicate myself against the malicious rumors spread by our family members."

The Baroness leaned forward, her eyes taking on a knowing glint. "I see. In that case, your discretion was admirable. However, I must admit that I misinterpreted your intentions initially. It's only natural to feel protective of one's family's reputation."

As they spoke, the tension between them dissipated, replaced by a sense of mutual understanding. The Baroness's gaze drifted back to the past, her thoughts wandering to a memory. "When I saw you dance at the ball last night, I couldn't help but recall your earlier performances with the dancing-master at Kensington. You moved with such elegance, my dear."
