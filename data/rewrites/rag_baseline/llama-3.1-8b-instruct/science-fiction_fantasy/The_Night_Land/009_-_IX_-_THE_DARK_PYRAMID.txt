The darkness receded like a tide, revealing the skeletal remains of a land scorched by fire. I stood at the edge of a great gorge, the echoes of my footsteps swallowed by the stillness. The air was heavy with the scent of ash and smoke, a perpetual reminder of the cataclysm that had reshaped this world. Before me, the terrain unfolded like a twisted tapestry, a topography of fire and stone.

Volcanoes pierced the sky like jagged teeth, their summits hidden in the veil of night. I counted seven and twenty, their peaks a blur of black and crimson, as if the very earth itself was aflame. Two ranges of fire-hills, distant and foreboding, stretched towards the horizon, their presence a constant hum in the background. A hundred thousand lesser eruptions, scattered like embers, cast a reddish glow across the landscape.

A sea, seemingly no more than a mile from my vantage point, lapped gently against the shore, its surface broken by a small fire-hill that rose like a sentinel from the waves. Behind it, a vast expanse of water stretched into the distance, vanishing into the fiery haze. Three smaller seas, like shards of glass, reflected the blaze, while a mighty sea, lost in the red light of the volcanoes, seemed to stretch on forever, its end invisible.

Islands, born from the seas, supported their own volcanoes, while others rose directly from the water, their bases shrouded in steam. The air vibrated with a low, constant rumble, a muttering thunder that seemed to emanate from the fire-hills themselves, their voices a primal, unending chant. In this world, the din of human existence was replaced by the eternal pulse of the earth's fury.

As I stood there, frozen in awe, my gaze fell upon a massive, blackened mountain, its peak lost in the darkness. A towering volcano, five miles high, thrust upwards from its side, its presence a testament to the raw power that shaped this world. Above it, a second, equally imposing, rose like a specter, its height estimated to be nine or ten miles. And on the left crest of the mountain, two colossal fire-hills blazed, their flames casting an otherworldly glow, as if they were miniature suns in the night sky.
