As he settled into the worn armchair, a faint smile played on Mr. Morgan's lips, his eyes fixed intently on the patrons of the Wheel of Fortune public-house. He had a particular affinity for this establishment, where his master, Mr. Strong, often held court with the likes of Major Pendennis. The clubroom, tucked away on the ground floor, was a hub of whispered conversations and veiled alliances. The sign above the door read "Private," a nod to the exclusivity of its membership – the Confidentials, a coterie of influential individuals bound together by a shared desire for power and secrets.

Morgan's gaze drifted towards the parlour, where the Major and Sir Francis Clavering engaged in a heated discussion. He had been present in Major Pendennis' lodgings earlier, listening in on their conversation, and now, from the vantage point of the Confidential room, he found himself privy to their every word. The air was thick with tension as they debated the particulars of a sensitive matter – one that, if revealed, would send shockwaves throughout the upper echelons of society.

"...married to my Lady?" Clavering's words hung in the air, a challenge to Pendennis' assertion. "No more than I am!" he spat, his voice low and menacing. Morgan's ears pricked up, his mind racing with the implications. Altamont, the convict, stood at the centre of this maelstrom. Young Harthur, eager to secure a seat in Parliament, had leveraged his connection to secure a advantageous marriage – to Blanche, a woman of considerable wealth and influence. The game was afoot, and Morgan's keen mind began to weave a tapestry of possibilities.

As the conversation drew to a close, Morgan's thoughts turned to the potential windfall. Each of the parties involved stood to lose something precious if the truth were to emerge. Clavering's reputation would be tarnished, Altamont's neck would be on the line, and Harthur's social standing would be irreparably damaged. Lady C., meanwhile, might find herself in a precarious position, torn between her loyalty to her husband and her concern for her son. Blanche, the prize at the centre of this game, would undoubtedly be a valuable ally to the one who could secure her rightful inheritance.
