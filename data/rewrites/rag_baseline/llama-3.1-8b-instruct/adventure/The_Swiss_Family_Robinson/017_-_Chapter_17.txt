As we recounted our tale to the expectant faces of my wife and Franz, it became apparent that the absence of Fritz had sparked a palpable sense of unease within our small community. The memory of his disappearance weighed heavily on my mind, particularly given the harrowing encounter with the lioness and her mate, which had left both my wife and Jack visibly shaken. The recollection of Juno's tragic fate also stirred a deep emotional response from Franz, who, with tears in his eyes, requested that her remains be laid to rest beside the house where she had lived for so many years. The epitaph inscribed by Ernest served as a poignant tribute to her unwavering loyalty and devotion, a testament to the profound bond between a dog and her human companions.

In the days that followed, the tension surrounding Fritz's disappearance continued to build, and I found myself increasingly anxious to uncover the truth behind his vanishing act. The promise of adventure and the prospect of sailing to Whale Island, where the lion skins and boar's head awaited, served as a welcome distraction from our collective unease. When I proposed the idea of setting sail, my wife, despite initial reservations, eventually agreed to join us, and our little party set off with a sense of purpose and trepidation.

As we navigated the waters, the sea breeze filled our spirits, and the rhythmic motion of the pinnace lulled us into a state of relative calm. However, my attention was suddenly diverted by a dark shape looming beneath the surface of the water. Initially, I suspected a sunken rock, but the proximity to the shore and the peculiar circumstances surrounding our previous encounter with the lioness left me with an unsettling feeling of foreboding. The boat surged forward, and for a fleeting moment, I feared the worst.

The shock was brief, but the implications were far-reaching. As I regained my composure, a second shout from Jack alerted me to another submerged obstacle, this time to starboard. The crew's cries of alarm gave way to a mixture of awe and trepidation as a massive black form emerged from the depths, its enormous size and ferocity sending waves crashing against our vessel. The cacophony of sounds was deafening, and it wasn't until the creature retreated, its intentions unclear, that we realized the identity of our unexpected adversary – a cachalot whale, its temper notorious for its ferocity and destructive capabilities.
