These sounds heightened the sense of loneliness, felt deeply by both of us.

Always, the Maid thought in her heart that I had come through this place alone to search for her in the unknown worlds. Naturally, I felt a swelling pride that she remembered and cherished my efforts; her love seemed to grow with every recollection. A man finds natural satisfaction in knowing he has done his best for his beloved. Do not the echoes of such proud thoughts resonate strangely with familiar pain?

On the fifth day, around the seventh hour, I heard strange noises echoing from the rocks. I urged the Maid to stay close, gripping the Diskos, and we proceeded with great caution. We passed three locations where gas-fires danced, emitting low moans and occasional whistles, while other odd sounds arose from the rocks themselves—strange yet familiar.

I approached the Mighty Pyramid, looming closer with every step, while my home ascended vast into the everlasting night like a mountain of sweet life and safety. Its sheer magnitude should have filled me with awe anew, yet despair and exhaustion gripped my heart so tightly that I cared for nothing but bringing my own Maid safely within the refuge's walls. She remained far ahead, a distant beacon.

I pressed forward across the desolate land with a determined stride. As I passed a hollow where a fire-hole burned, something emerged from the darkness. It crawled upward, revealing itself as a great, hairy man. He looked at me, then advanced eagerly, extending his hands. In the flickering firelight, I saw them clearly: monstrous, armed with brutish, horrid claws capable of tearing flesh like a wild beast.

My own hands flew to the ground; I cared not for life anymore. This creature was delaying me, fueling my fierce despair that anything could halt my progress. Suddenly, I leaped forward with cold fury at the giant. I struck at the monster, but he sidestepped instantly, escaping the blow. From the half-darkness cast by the dancing fire, he flung his massive arm out, snatching my helmet from my head with such brute strength that he threw me nearly twelve feet onto my back.

I was unharmed internally, though shaken and bruised. Rising immediately, I charged the giant again, swinging the Diskos—a weapon that roared and blazed in my grip. I brought it down upon his midsection. The Diskos glutted itself, piercing the giant as if he were nothing, despite his immense size and strength. His upper body collapsed horrifically to the earth, leaving his legs and torso standing stark in the firelight, while blood erupted upward like a fountain against the night sky.

I made no pause. Leaping toward my Maid, I gathered her into my arms and surged onward past the fallen corpse, which crashed down with a terrible sound. The night seemed filled with astonishment and upliftment for the Millions; their spiritual cries echoed around me, telling me they had witnessed this feat and poured their love and delight upon me, stirring a vast excitement within them.

Scarcely had I traveled another mile when two vague shapes emerged from a dark rocky area. I struck them down with the Diskos and continued, unaware of their true nature. After that, it seemed I was fighting forever. Strange things crept from bushes and rocks repeatedly, making the entire land crawl with foul, monstrous life. I moved as in a dream, smiting relentlessly, driven by increasingly fierce despair. Surely, the end of our lives had arrived, and I lacked the power to save my Maid.

The land groaned with grim, monstrous roars and eerie, deadly low sounds. Once, I heard the noise of giants running. All night, evil reigned. How I avoided being slain by some dread force remains unknown, unless I had been burned free of all weaknesses that an Evil Power could exploit, thanks to the bitter training I endured for so long.

Then, the deep, dreadful baying of the Night-Hounds returned from the Southeast, growing nearer. I realized no strength of mine could protect my own. Suddenly, from the upper reaches of the night where the Last Light hung, a strange blue flash descended, striking the land toward the Southeast. Again, and perhaps a dozen times thereafter, flashes fell from the heights, accompanied by a peculiar crackling sound—less thunderous than the storms of this age, yet louder than any other sound imaginable. I knew then that the Humans were beginning to fight for me, striving to bring my Maid safely home.

It felt as though all previous wakefulness in the land was merely sleep compared to the awakening now occurring. The night rocked with the roarings of monsters and the stirring of great forces. Overhead, the yowling of that strange, dreadful laughter drifted from the hidden country in the lost East. Constantly, the hoarse, terrifying bays of the hounds signaled a mighty pack on the hunt. They appeared no more than a mile away to the Southeast. I stood alone, holding only the dying Maid in my arms. I looked vainly and desperately for the Hundred Thousand prepared to aid me, who had supposedly descended to my rescue. Truly, there was nothing to see but strange lights and shadows, and the movement of monstrous life everywhere. The hounds closed in with every passing moment; death was imminent.

Yet, I ceased not my stride. I began to run, for the Pyramid was not far in the night. The shine of the Circle surrounding it was plainly visible, save where obscured by strange shadows. A despairing hope fueled me: perhaps I could still reach the safety of the Circle with my Maid.

The baying grew ever nearer. It was doubly hideous and bitter to lose my beloved one so close to home, watching the great mountain of my sanctuary rise before me into the night, appearing so near that I felt almost there, yet perhaps still two great miles away. Behold, I cried out in vain despair, wondering why no aid came in this extremity. The hounds bayed now but half a mile to my left, surely having scented me by their dreadful cries.

Truly, the Millions felt an anguish of sympathy for me. The spiritual noise of their emotion was plain to my spirit. They had seen and interpreted my desperate looks and calls. In a moment, a great and sweet spiritual force surrounded me, born of their shared understanding and love. They perceived I was nearing the end of hope, and the hounds were almost upon me.

In this critical moment, the shaking beat of the Earth-Current reached my ears. I knew the Humans were taking desperate measures to save us. To my left, a vast pack of hounds rushed forward at a great pace, their heads low, towering as large as horses. Seen plainly, then vanishing into shadow, then reappearing—all in the same breath—as they closed in.

In truth, I knew we would both be dead within a minute if the Humans did not act swiftly. I stopped where I stood; there was no point in running further. Looking from the hounds to the Mighty Pyramid, and back again, my hope faded. The hounds were scarcely two hundred fathoms away, numbering in the hundreds. Just as I took that final glance at the Pyramid, a monstrous bursting flame erupted from the sealed lower section of the structure. The flame rushed downward, striking the land where the hounds ran. All the night vanished from my sight in the brightness and strangeness of that mighty flame. I saw no more of the Pyramid or anything else, only the shining, dreadful glory of the fire.

The flame created a blast in the night and a heat that seemed to wither me even at a distance. I perceived that the Humans had truly unleashed the Earth-Current upon the hounds to ensure my survival. A constant great thundering rolled over the land as the Earth-Force tore and split the air, ripping up the earth. The roaring of the monsters was drowned out by this mighty sound. I saw no trace of the hounds, only flames and broken landscapes where the Earth-Force struck. Great rocks were hurled everywhere with a vast noise. Truly, it was a mercy I was not slain a hundred times by the failings and burstings of these great rocks and boulders.

In a moment, the Humans cut off the Earth-Force, regaining control. Now, a great silence settled over the land, an utter darkness, save for the lingering flames and noise from where the Current had struck. Quickly recovering from the daze, I resumed my run. In truth, it seemed I might yet win safety with my Maid.

My eyes adjusted to their usual function. I scanned all directions, fearing some new threat might claim us even then. For a while, I saw nothing, nor heard the waking sounds of the land, except for the grim, horrid laughter drifting from the dead East.

Often, as I ran, I stared hungrily upward at the Mighty Pyramid. It always seemed less bright than before. Initially, I attributed this to my eyes being dazzled by the great Flame of the Earth-Force. Soon, however, I realized it was otherwise: there was truly a diminished brightness emanating from the Mighty Redoubt. I conceived this loss owed to the great expenditure of the Earth-Current's power used to save us. This thought chilled my heart deeply. If the Force of the Current dropped too low, there would be danger for all the Humans living, including the great Millions of the Mighty Refuge. This was surely known by the Masters; they would have no more power to aid me with the Current until it flowed strong again, lest they destroy all the peoples of the Earth. All this became clear to me in a moment as I ran, making me even more desperate to reach instant safety with the Maid.

Surely, I expected the Hundred Thousand to arrive, but they did not come. Around me, the land began to emit the noises of the monsters again, sending forth new and peculiar sounds, indicating more entities were awake than I had ever heard before. Presently, I saw living things creeping between me and the light of the Circle. I knew I still faced a bitter fight if I wished to bring the Maid safe. I swung the Diskos free and ran on.

Suddenly, my spirit warned me of a new peril. I looked upward into the night, hoping the Master Monstruwacan might signal the danger via the Set Speech. But truly, there came no quick flashings of the Set Speech; only an upward stillness and a dimming of the Pyramid's lights. When consciousness returned to me, I realized I was ascending in the Lift, lying on that same bed—a place I had convinced myself I would never need again, nor ascend from, within the Silent Country. Yet, I sensed vaguely and strangely that the deep, thunderous resonance of the Underground Organs was rising from the world's mighty depths. It sounded like a strange, utter music playing beyond death itself. There was a constant, rolling chant, as though countless voices sang from beyond far mountains; sometimes the sound was like a low wind blowing deep below, and other times it resolved into the clear, ancient melody of the Song of Honour.

I knew, as in a dream, that the millions living in that deep country were making an honor and rejoicing over this wonder of joy that had occurred. However, everything remained faint and half-hidden to me. My eyes seemed powerless to open, and I felt as though I were constantly being lifted upon strange waters of unreality. Yet, amidst this, there were sweet and lovely odors—odors of true reality emanating from the great Fields where flowers always grew along the passages of the Lifts, for the Lift was still moving upward through its vast miles.

Perhaps I stirred slightly, for then came the low, gentle voice of the Master Doctor bidding me rest, assuring me that all was well with the Maid. Soon after, I drifted into a haze, experiencing what seemed like days where I lived only half and slept the other half, wondering without distress whether I was truly dead or alive. Then came periods of profound quietude where I harbored no thoughts whatsoever. During these hours, the Master Doctor often leaned over me, gazing keenly into my face.

Finally, after strange intervals, another figure bent over me. It was the dear, lovely face of Mine Own looking down upon me, her eyes speaking love directly into my soul. She remained calm and hushed. In that moment, I began to live within my body again. Perhaps I made some fumbling movements with my hands; she took them gently, holding them close. Life flowed from her to me. She was ever wordless and tender, and contentment began to grow within me, leading eventually to a natural slumber.

One day, I was permitted to rise. Those who cared for me carried me to one of the Quiet Gardens of the Pyramid, setting me down and seemingly leaving me alone. Suddenly, someone emerged from behind a bush, looking at me for a moment with a hint of shyness. Only the radiant love shining in her eyes made that shyness seem insignificant. Truly, I recognized it was Mine Own Maid, though I had never before seen Naani dressed so prettily as a maiden. Looking at her, I knew she appeared more dainty than I could have imagined possible.

Suddenly, I tried to rise to approach her, but she ran quickly to stop this natural foolishness. Instead, she sat beside me, taking my head against her breast, refusing to deny me her lips. In that instant, she was both maid and mother to me. Afterward, she urged me to remain perfectly still. We sat there in utter, silent happiness until the attendants returned. The Master of the Doctors was among them, and I noticed a trace of satisfaction in his expression.

From that day forward, I saw Mine Own Maid every single day. I recovered my health with astonishing speed, for Love healed me. Soon, I was allowed to descend to the Fields, though only via private paths, lest the Multitudes follow me incessantly; I needed peace and quiet. The Maid accompanied me. The Master Monstruwacan and the Master of the Doctors had agreed on this arrangement, appointing an Officer of Marriage to wed us quietly and simply. I was still too weak for a public ceremony, which was reserved for later. When that time finally arrived, truly, the Millions erected a Guard of Honour eight miles high, stretching from the top to the bottom of the Mighty Pyramid. But that was a future ceremonial event, a tribute the People could not deny themselves for giving me such honor.

Surely, the Maid stayed with me always, becoming my wife and my enduring strength, allowing Mine Own to regain perfect health. Indeed, we were now in the "Love Days"—the most beautiful days imaginable if Love is True. We wandered freely through the mighty Fields, walking the Love Paths near the villages. We hid our names to avoid being besieged by others driven by natural curiosity and kindness, needing to remain utterly united and quiet. We chose sleeping spots where the floral beauty was most wondrous, carrying some food with us, though we also ate whenever we reached the villages scattered throughout the Fields—which were truly as vast as countries.

Mine Own kept her promise a hundredfold, preparing me great and hearty meals. As I ate, she teased me relentlessly, calling me a glutton, and kissed me to ensure I never had a chance to defend myself. Truly, she was everything my heart and spirit desired. She companionship me with Love and brought Joy into my spirit.

Once, we ventured downward into the Country of Silence, though we did not stay long, as my memories began to return. In later times, we wandered there often, filled with memory, the holiness of great thoughts, and a Love that holds all things. As we left that realm, I told Mine Own how, when she had been suspended by the Horrid Force of the House, I had suffered dreadful pain knowing I might never wake to discover her kissing me while I slept. Surely, Mine Own Dear One blushed most beautifully, having never known I was aware of her sweet naughtiness. She then considered entirely my agony during the time she was "dead" before the Earth-Force's vapour freed her spirit from the Silence. She came to me with dear understanding.

She explained that the Doctors said she had been stunned and frozen in spirit, her entire being and life suspended. The great life-force of the Earth-Current had awakened her spirit, restoring life to her body and proper flow to her blood. The Doctors had discussed this extensively, searching the old records of their work. They had found a similar occurrence in ancient times, but nothing quite like it had happened through a mighty age of years.

While wandering and resting in the Fields, I often recounted various matters to Mine Own. I knew she had learned some odd things before I regained health, though not much; for she, too, had been utterly helpless, forced from her bed while I lay so still. The Master Doctor had ordered this precaution, fearing I would truly die unless something was done to awaken my spirit. In truth, you must consider the depth of my Love as I realized she had held my hands so bravely and gently while she herself had scarce power to stand. I offered a little holy praise for Mine Own.

Thus, I reach my conclusion, with just one final tale to share. This happened a while later, after we had undergone the second marriage—the Public Marriage. One day, my Wife, Mine Own, took me with sweet cunning to the Hall of Honour. Upon arrival, I saw many of the People standing silently within that great hall, waiting expectantly for something. My Wife led me forward to the center of the Hall. Suddenly, I understood why she had brought me there with such affectionate secrecy. Standing in the Place of Honour was a statue of a man in broken armor, eternally carrying a maid.

I stood dumbfounded. How could anyone of this Age understand the magnitude of such honor? Such honors were bestowed only upon the Great Dead. Yet, I was merely a young man, far removed from greatness, except that I loved with all my heart and spirit, rendering death a small price compared to Love. You must understand how Love makes the heart sweet and brave, granting humility alongside wonder and natural pride that anyone would deem worthy of such honor. Mine Own wept with joy and honest pride beside me, proud of her man.

There was an utter silence of dear sympathy filling the great Hall of Honour. Those present allowed me to pass in quietness with Mine Own, a lovely testament to mutual understanding. Walking lovingly and thoughtfully with my Wife, she stayed very close to me. I had gained Honor, yet I learned that Honor is but the ash of Life unless accompanied by Love. To possess Love is to possess all, for True Love mothers both Honor and Faithfulness; these three build the House of Joy.
