Two weeks later, a pale, bowed form slipped through the gates of the Dominican convent in the suburbs of Gouda, seeking audience with Brother Ambrose. As the temporary governor following the recent death of the prior, Ambrose received the visitor with a warmth that belied his official duties. This stranger was Gerard, a man who had returned not to begin anew, but to close the circle of a life already weary. He had entered years ago as a mere novice, a boy on probation, yet deep down he had known himself to be failing. Now, driven by a desperate need for peace, he sought sanctuary within the gentle walls where his heart and home had once been so freely given.

Inside, Gerard found no solace. His cell, once a refuge, now felt like a prison of memory; every corner echoed with the presence of her, making his dwelling far heavier than his broken spirit could bear. Yet Ambrose, perceiving both the worth of his friend and the depth of his sorrow, harbored a tender hope. He believed that kindness alone might heal Gerard, restore him to health, and return him to his parish. To this end, Ambrose placed him in a comfortable chamber above the gateway, strictly forbidding fasting or any penitential hardships.

However, fate intervened swiftly. A few days after Gerard's arrival, the newly appointed Prior took up residence, bringing with him the iron discipline of a "very Tartar." At first, he was consumed by a zeal to purge abuses and tighten the monastic rules, but soon his attention turned to the matter at hand. Hearing that the Vicar of Gouda had taken the habit of a novice, the Prior's countenance darkened. "It is well," he declared sharply, "that we address this immediately. Let him resign his vicarage forthwith, or depart; I shall have no fat parsons in my house."

With swift authority, the Prior summoned Gerard. The friar obeyed and presented himself before the stern superior. But the moment their eyes met across the distance, time seemed to suspend. Startled by the recognition, they stood frozen.

"Clement!" cried the newcomer.

"Jerome!" replied the other, his voice breaking with emotion.
