It was she who first brought the Moonstone into our lives, and it was she who, through her love and devotion, made it possible for me to serve the family with loyalty and dedication. If we start with her, we can be sure of tracing the threads of fate back far enough.

In the world of fashion, few names shine brighter than those of the three lovely Miss Herncastles: Adelaide, Caroline, and Julia. And among them, I have always believed Julia to be the most radiant, with a beauty that sparkled like the finest gemstones. I had the privilege of serving as a young page in the household of their father, the esteemed Lord Herncastle, when I was just fifteen years old. What a stern but fair master he was! His temper may have been short, but his wit was sharper still. I am grateful that our paths did not cross often in the course of my duties.

Years passed, and I rose through the ranks, earning the trust and respect of the family. When Miss Julia married the kind-hearted Sir John Verinder, I accompanied them to their country estate, where I continued to serve as their loyal steward. It was during this time that I first met my future wife, Selina Goby, who managed the household with kindness and efficiency. She was a hard worker, with a strong sense of duty and a quick mind. I was drawn to her warmth and intelligence, and soon we were married.

Selina was a skilled cook, and our home was always filled with the delicious aromas of roasting meats and freshly baked bread. I would often join her in the kitchen, watching as she expertly prepared meals for our guests. Her food was a testament to her love and care, and it brought joy to all who partook of it. As I think back on those happy times, I am reminded of the simple pleasures in life: a warm hearth, a loving partner, and a well-cooked meal. These are the things that bring us happiness, and it is in these moments that we find true contentment.
