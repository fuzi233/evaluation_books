The four men sat around the dinner table, each lost in their own thoughts. Harry, unchanged in spirit, yet grown more distinguished with age, was the central figure, his dark hair flecked with a hint of grey, his features still as striking as they had been all those years ago. To Harry's left sat Durrance, a loyal friend and fellow soldier, his rugged features a reminder of the trials he had faced in the unforgiving terrain of India.
