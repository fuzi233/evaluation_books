As Margaret stepped into her kitchen, the aroma of freshly baked bread wafted through the air, mingling with the scent of simmering stew. Today was a bittersweet day, filled with the promise of a dear friend's visit, but also the news that Gerard was alone in a far-off land. Margaret's thoughts drifted back to the days when Denys had been a constant presence, bringing laughter and warmth to her life. His antics still made her chuckle, especially the way he struggled to balance his natural politeness with his more mischievous tendencies. She remembered the countless times she'd scolded him for using foul language, only to be met with a sheepish grin.

Just as she was lost in thought, a knock at the door broke the silence. It was Catherine, Gerard's mother, who had written to Margaret expressing her desire to reconnect after a falling out. Margaret's response had been guarded, and now Catherine sought to make amends. As Denys ushered Catherine into the kitchen, Margaret's heart remained closed, her emotions raw from the pain of the past. The memory of Gerard's departure still lingered, and the thought of Catherine's people, who had driven him away, only fueled her bitterness.

Denys, ever the mediator, attempted to bridge the gap between them. He regaled Catherine with stories of his adventures and the laughter they shared, hoping to soften Margaret's resolve. But Margaret's heart was hardened, and Denys's words only seemed to push her further away. As the tension mounted, Denys tried to reason with her, but Margaret's emotions boiled over, and she burst into tears. Denys, taken aback by her reaction, stumbled over his words, and in the chaos, he found himself making a solemn promise he wasn't quite sure he could keep.

In the midst of the commotion, the savory aroma of the stew wafted through the air, a reminder of the simple pleasures in life. As the day wore on, the sun dipped below the horizon, casting a warm glow over the kitchen. The sound of sizzling meat and the murmur of conversation filled the air, a contrast to the turmoil that had erupted just hours before. As the evening drew to a close, Denys remained hopeful that he could somehow mend the rift between Margaret and Catherine, but for now, the wound remained open, a reminder of the complexities of human relationships.
