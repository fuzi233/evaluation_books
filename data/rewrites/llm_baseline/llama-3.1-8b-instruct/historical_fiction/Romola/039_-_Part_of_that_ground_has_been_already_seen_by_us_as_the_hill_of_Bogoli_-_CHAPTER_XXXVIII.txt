The evening air was alive with the scent of blooming jasmine as Tito made his way to the Rucellai Gardens, his footsteps echoing off the stone pavement of the Via della Scala. He had been looking forward to this supper all day, and the promise of fine meats, choice wines, and stimulating conversation had lifted his spirits. The host, Bernardo Rucellai, was a man of great distinction, known for his elegant taste and refined sensibilities. His family name, Rucellai, was a testament to the legacy of his ancestors, who had brought back the secrets of the prized oricello dye from the Levant, imbuing the city of Florence with a splash of exotic color and flair.

As Tito entered the garden, he was greeted by the soft glow of lanterns suspended among the trees, casting a warm, golden light over the carefully manicured paths and lush greenery. The air was filled with the sweet fragrance of blooming flowers and the murmur of conversation from within the garden's walls. Tito's anticipation grew as he made his way towards the sound of laughter and music, his heart beating in time with the lively rhythm of the night.

Bernardo Rucellai was a man of many talents and accomplishments, and his reputation as a patron of the arts and sciences was well-deserved. He had been a member of the Platonic Academy, and his love of learning had led him to write a treatise on the topography of ancient Rome. His collection of antiquities was renowned, and his garden was a testament to his refined taste, a true oasis in the heart of the city. Tonight, however, Tito suspected that the evening's gathering was not solely for the sake of pleasure, but rather a calculated move to further certain...political interests.

As he entered the garden, Tito was struck by the array of distinguished guests, each one a luminary in their own right. There was Lorenzo Tornabuoni, Bernardo's emissary, who had extended the invitation to Tito; the noblewoman, Contessina de' Bardi; and several other notable figures, all gathered together in the warm, golden light of the lanterns. The air was alive with the hum of conversation, and Tito felt his excitement grow as he made his way towards the center of the gathering.
