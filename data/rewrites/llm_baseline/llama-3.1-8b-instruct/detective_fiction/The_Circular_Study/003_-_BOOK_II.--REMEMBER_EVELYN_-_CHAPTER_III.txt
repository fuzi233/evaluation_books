As the man who had reenacted the tragedy with such haunting accuracy stood up, a jarring dissonance echoed through the room. He stooped to pick up the glass he had dropped, but Mr. Gryce's firm voice halted him mid-motion.

"Wait, man," he commanded. "We need to understand how your master met his end."

The command was unexpected, but the man didn't flinch. Instead, he continued walking away, his back to the room. Styles, however, seized his shoulder, holding him fast.

"Stop!" Styles said, his voice laced with urgency. "Don't ignore us. What do you mean by disappearing like that?"

This time, the man's composure faltered. He stumbled, dropping the glass once more. The sound of shattering glass mingled with a cry that was almost a scream, as if he had finally grasped the gravity of his situation. When he turned to face them, his eyes revealed a deep-seated terror, and his posture cowered in submission.

Styles' grip tightened, restraining the man as he tried to break free. But as he gazed upon the lifeless body on the bearskin rug, something within him shifted. A fleeting glimmer of joy flickered across his face, a morbid spark that seemed to ignite his entire being. A low, unholy chuckle escaped his lips, echoing through the room like a macabre melody.

Mr. Gryce watched this spectacle with growing unease, his usual composure ruffled by the servant's unbridled emotions. When the laughter finally subsided, he stepped forward, his eyes locking onto the man's with an unnerving intensity.

"Speak!" he demanded. "Who killed your master? You were in the house with him; surely you know what happened."

The man's lips parted, releasing a string of unintelligible sounds. With his free hand, he touched his ears and lips, then shook his head violently. It was clear to all present that the man was deaf and mute.

The revelation sent Styles stumbling backward, releasing his hold on the servant. The man retreated to his original position, his eyes flashing with an otherworldly light as he reenacted the events that had transpired, his performance even more vivid than before. As he finished, he stood poised, his gaze gleaming with an unholy excitement that made his presence in the room feel like a sacrilegious affront.
