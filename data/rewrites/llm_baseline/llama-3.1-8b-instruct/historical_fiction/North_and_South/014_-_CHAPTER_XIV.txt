As the night wore on, Mrs. Hale found herself drifting into a wakeful habit, her mind replaying the same haunting dreams that had plagued her for months – Frederick's ship battling against the fury of the waves, the glass-green walls of water towering above the vessel like some monstrous serpentine creature.

Margaret, sensing her mother's unease, reached out to offer what comfort she could. Her mother, usually reserved, had grown increasingly tender towards her daughter, drawing her close as a trusted confidante. As they sat together in the dimly lit parlor, the fire crackling softly in the hearth, Margaret felt a sense of purpose stir within her. She had always longed to be a source of solace for her mother, to ease the weight of her worries and fears.
