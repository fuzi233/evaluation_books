The sun beat down upon the desert sands, relentless in its ferocity, as I trudged along the banks of the oasis stream. The air was heavy with the scent of acacia trees and the distant tang of salt. I walked ahead of my companions, Sir Henry and Good, my eyes scanning the terrain for any sign of interest. Suddenly, I stopped dead in my tracks, my heart racing with a mixture of excitement and trepidation. Before me, nestled in the shade of a majestic fig tree, stood a small, ramshackle hut, its grass-thatched roof a vibrant green against the bleached expanse of the desert.

"What in the name of all that's holy?" I muttered to myself, my mind reeling with the impossibility of it all. A hut, here, in this desolate place? It defied logic. I turned to the others, my voice barely above a whisper. "Guys, look. Is that...a man?"

Sir Henry and Good exchanged a skeptical glance, but as they drew closer, a figure emerged from the hut, limping towards us with an unmistakable air of urgency. His face was shrouded in a wild tangle of black beard, and his skin was a deep, burnished brown. I blinked, wondering if the sun had finally gotten the better of me. No hunter would ever venture this far into the desert, let alone settle here. Yet, there he was.

As the stranger drew closer, he let out a blood-curdling cry and collapsed at our feet, his body wracked with a spasm of pain. Sir Henry sprang into action, kneeling beside him with an expression of concern. "Great heavens!" he exclaimed, his voice trembling with emotion. "It's my brother George!"

A second figure, equally bedecked in animal hides, burst from the hut, a rifle slung over his shoulder. He took one look at me and let out a shout of recognition. "Macumazahn! Baas! Don't you know me? I'm Jim, the hunter! I lost the note you gave me, and we've been stuck here for nigh on two years."

As Jim flung himself at my feet, weeping with joy, I couldn't help but feel a pang of irritation. What kind of fool loses a note in the middle of nowhere? I shot him a stern glance. "You careless devil, you should be thrashed within an inch of your life!"
