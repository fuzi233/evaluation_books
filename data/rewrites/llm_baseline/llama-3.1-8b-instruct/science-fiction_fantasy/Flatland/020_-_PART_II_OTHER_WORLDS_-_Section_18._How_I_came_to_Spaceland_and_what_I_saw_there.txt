As I stood at the precipice of the unknown, an unearthly terror seized my being.  A darkness enveloped me, and a disorienting, sickening sensation washed over my senses, like the fleeting memory of a dream.  I felt myself becoming something, yet nothing, simultaneously.  When I could finally muster the voice to speak, I cried out in anguish, "Is this madness or the depths of despair?"  The voice of the enigmatic Sphere, calm and soothing as a summer breeze, replied, "Neither, dear one.  This is knowledge, the revelation of Three Dimensions.  Open your eyes once more, and gaze steadily."

I gazed, and behold, a world anew unfolded before me.  There stood before me, radiantly alive, all that I had previously conceived, conjectured, and dreamed of perfect, harmonious beauty.  The center of the Stranger's form lay open to my view, yet I saw no pulsing heart, no beating lungs, no arteries, only a luminous essence that defied description – a surface that seemed to emanate from the very core of the Sphere.  My mind reeled in awe as I prostrated myself before this divine embodiment of consummate loveliness and wisdom.

"How is it, oh, radiant being," I asked, my voice barely above a whisper, "that I behold your inner workings, and yet discern no heart, no lungs, no organs?  Are you not a creature like unto the creatures of Flatland?"  The Sphere's gentle voice replied, "What you perceive, you do not truly see.  I am of a realm beyond the comprehension of your flat world.  Were I a circle, my insides would be visible to you, but I am a being composed of many circles, the Many in the One, known here as a Sphere.  Just as the outside of a cube presents a square face, so the exterior of a Sphere appears as a circle."

My mind reeled under the weight of the Sphere's enigmatic words, yet I found solace in the depth of his wisdom.  He spoke with a gentle kindness, "Do not struggle to comprehend the mysteries of Spaceland.  They will reveal themselves to you in time.  Let us journey back to the plains of Flatland, and I shall show you what you have long thought, but never truly seen – a visible angle."
