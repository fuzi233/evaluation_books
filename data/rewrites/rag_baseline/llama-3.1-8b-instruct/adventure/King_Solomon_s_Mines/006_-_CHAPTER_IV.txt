In the depths of a South African winter, the landscape itself seemed to stir, as if the land itself were awakening from a long slumber. The rains had long since retreated, leaving behind a dusty veil that shrouded the earth, but the air was alive with the promise of renewal. It was on one such morning, as the first hints of dawn crept over the horizon, that Allan Quatermain stood at the edge of the veldt, his eyes scanning the vast expanse before him. He was about to embark on a journey that would take him into the heart of Africa, a quest that had been years in the making, and one that would change him forever.

It was a journey that began in the bustling port city of Durban, where the sounds of commerce and industry mingled with the rhythms of the African drums. Quatermain had assembled a small band of companions, each with their own stories and motivations, but all bound together by a shared sense of wonder and a desire for adventure. There was Umbopa, the stoic and enigmatic leader of the group, who had a deep understanding of the land and its people; Ventvögel, the German engineer, with his practical skills and quick wit; and Goza and Tom, the two young men who had been entrusted with the care of their wagon and oxen.

As they set out, the group moved in silence, their footsteps echoing through the stillness of the morning. It was as if they were all thinking the same thought, a thought that hung unspoken between them – what lay ahead, and what wonders or dangers awaited them in the unknown. And then, without warning, Umbopa broke into a low, melodic chant, his voice carrying on the wind. It was a song of the old ones, a tale of brave men who had ventured into the wilderness, only to discover that it was not a desolate expanse, but a land of beauty and abundance.

The group listened, entranced, as the words washed over them like a wave. And in that moment, they knew that they were not just travelers, but seekers, on a journey to uncover secrets and discover hidden truths. They laughed, and the tension was broken, the mood lifted by the simple act of shared laughter.
