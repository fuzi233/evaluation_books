Her mother, Mrs. Davilow, had arrived just a short while ago, and Gwendolen's resolve to leave this gem of the sea behind her began to take shape.

"I shall never set foot on this shore again," Gwendolen declared to her mother, who listened intently, her eyes filled with a mixture of understanding and unspoken concern. Mrs. Davilow knew that her daughter's words masked a deeper pain, one that lingered beneath the surface like the undertow of a great ocean.

As the days passed, Gwendolen found solace in her mother's presence, a comfort that had been lacking in her marriage.
