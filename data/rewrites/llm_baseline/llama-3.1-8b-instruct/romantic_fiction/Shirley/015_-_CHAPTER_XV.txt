As the morning sun cast its warm rays upon the estate, Shirley emerged from the breakfast room, a bright smile on her face. "I'm still basking in the joy of yesterday's gathering," she exclaimed to Caroline. "There's something delightful about hosting a small group of gentlemen and watching them savor each dish with such relish. It's almost as if they retain a childlike wonder about food, don't you think? I find myself wanting to please them, especially when they display the refined taste of our esteemed rector." 

Caroline smiled in response, her eyes sparkling with amusement. She had grown accustomed to discussing Robert's character with Shirley, a subject she would normally avoid, but with her friend's encouragement, she found herself drawn to it. "My uncle and Dr. Boultby are certainly not immune to his charms," she said, her voice barely above a whisper.

Shirley's eyes sparkled with interest. "Ah, but what is it about Robert that captivates you? I've noticed he seems to be constantly observing you, as if trying to unravel the threads of your thoughts and intentions." She leaned in, her voice taking on a conspiratorial tone. "I've encountered men like him before – men who seem to wear a mask of reserve, only to reveal a keen intellect lurking beneath. It's a fascinating quality, don't you agree?"

Caroline's cheeks flushed slightly as she replied, "I suppose it's a bit unnerving, to be honest. But I've grown accustomed to his company." Shirley's eyes narrowed, as if intrigued by this admission. "Tell me, Caroline, how do you feel when he watches you? Does it make you uncomfortable?"

Their conversation was interrupted by the sound of the doorbell ringing out across the hallway. "Ah, perfect timing!" Shirley exclaimed, rushing to the window to gaze out at the arrival. "And look, it seems we have two visitors today. Perhaps one of them will catch your eye, Caroline?" She laughed, her eyes sparkling with mischief. As she spoke, the black-muzzled dog, Tartar, let out a low growl, followed by a series of deep barks that echoed through the hall.

Shirley's laughter filled the air as she exclaimed, "Tartar's making quite the entrance, isn't he? Don't worry, the gentlemen will soon learn to recognize his playful nature." However, their arrival was met with a stern voice, commanding the dog to calm down.
