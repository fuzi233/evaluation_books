"It is a tale of two realities," Cadwalader began, his voice measured and deliberate. "One, a facade of tranquility; the other, a maelstrom of emotions that threatened to consume us all. To understand the events that transpired, I must start anew, untainted by the distortions of my previous account."

Mr. Gryce's expression turned thoughtful as he leaned forward, his eyes never leaving Cadwalader's face. With a swift motion, he scribbled a series of notes on a small piece of paper, which he then handed to the inspector. As Cadwalader spoke, the inspector's eyes darted back and forth, his brow furrowed in concentration.
