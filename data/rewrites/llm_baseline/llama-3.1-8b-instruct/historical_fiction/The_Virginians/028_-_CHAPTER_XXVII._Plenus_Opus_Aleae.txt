As they strolled through the quiet afternoon, Maria turned to Harry with a curious expression. "Tell me more about the children at the Lambert estate," she said, her voice gentle. "I couldn't help but notice them when we first arrived. They seemed so full of life."

Harry's eyes lit up at the mention of the children. "Ah, yes! They're truly exceptional young people. Mrs. Lambert was a dear friend of my mother's during her time in Europe, and Colonel Lambert is a seasoned gentleman with a wealth of experience. He's served under royalty and fought in various battles, including Scotland and Flanders." Harry's enthusiasm was palpable as he continued, "Their kindness to me has been unwavering, and I'm determined to repay them somehow. I've been thinking of gifting them something – perhaps a beautiful instrument or a rare book?"

Maria's eyes sparkled with amusement. "Why not visit the toy shop with me and select a lovely doll for Hester and Theo? I'm sure they'd appreciate the thought, and it would be a more fitting gesture than any grand gift."

Harry chuckled, his fingers absently drumming against his thigh. "Dolls for Hester and Theo? Oh, Maria, you always underestimate the intelligence and talent of women. Those girls are far beyond their years. They're accomplished musicians, fluent in multiple languages, and talented composers. Theo even writes her own songs, which she sings with remarkable skill."

Maria's face lit up with surprise. "Really? I had no idea. They seemed so... youthful."

"That's often the case with those who underestimate others based on appearance," Harry said with a hint of mischief. "At home, I recall how you and Mrs. Esmond would sometimes criticize women who received praise from me or George. But I assure you, Maria, these young ladies are truly remarkable."

Maria's expression softened, and she reached out to touch Harry's arm. "I wouldn't dream of criticizing anyone who shows kindness to you, Harry. You were so hurt and ill when you first arrived, and I... I wasn't able to stay with you as I would have liked."
