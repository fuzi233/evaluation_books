The morning sunlight streaming through the window cast a warm glow over the room, imbuing the air with a sense of tranquility. It was one of those perfect mornings, the kind that made you feel invigorated and ready to take on the world. I emerged from beneath the cool waters of the shower, feeling refreshed and rejuvenated, like a spring morning dew-kissed flower. My mind, too, was clear and focused, having spent the previous evening asserting my independence with Jeeves. For far too long, I'd allowed him to dominate our daily routine, dictating every aspect of my attire and demeanor. Yesterday, I'd finally found the courage to push back, insisting on wearing the Country Gentleman hat, despite his initial objections. The memory of that small victory still lingered, leaving me feeling manly and self-assured.

As I began to dress, the aroma of freshly brewed coffee wafted through the air, teasing my senses and building my anticipation for the day ahead. My stomach growled softly, reminding me to decide on breakfast. I pondered the options while massaging my back with a rough towel, humming a gentle tune to myself. Suddenly, a discreet knock at the door interrupted my reverie.

"Who's there?" I called out, pausing mid-hum.

"Jeeves, sir," he replied. "Lady Malvern wishes to see you."

I raised an eyebrow, perplexed. "Eh? I'm not expecting anyone. Who's Lady Malvern?"

"I couldn't gather that information from her ladyship, sir," Jeeves replied, his tone neutral.

I frowned, intrigued. "And is she alone?"

"No, sir. Her ladyship is accompanied by a Lord Pershore, whom I believe is her son."

I nodded, my curiosity piqued. "Put out something suitable for a guest, then. I'll be dressing."

"The heather-mixture lounge is ready, sir," Jeeves informed me.

As I continued to dress, my mind wandered back to the possible identity of Lady Malvern. It wasn't until I'd buttoned my shirt and reached for the studs that I recalled a connection. "Ah ha!" I exclaimed. "I remember now. She's a friend of my Aunt Agatha's."

"Indeed, sir?" Jeeves asked, his eyes meeting mine in the mirror.
