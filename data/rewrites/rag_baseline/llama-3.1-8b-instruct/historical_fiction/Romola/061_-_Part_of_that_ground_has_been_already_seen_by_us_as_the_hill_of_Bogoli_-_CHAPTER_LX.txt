The darkness of the Florentine night was alive with the thrum of anticipation, the air thick with the weight of expectation. Three days had passed since the council chamber within the Old Palace had deliberated late into the night, their voices raised in fervent debate over the fate of the five condemned men. The decision had been made, the votes tallied, and the outcome was clear: the nine black beans of the fatal affirmative had sealed the prisoners' doom. As the moon climbed above the rooftops, casting an eerie glow over the deserted piazza, the crowd gathered before the palace grew restless, their murmurs and shouts a cacophony of despair and outrage.

Within the council chamber, the Signoria had reached its verdict, their decision a culmination of hours of heated discussion. The arguments for and against the Appeal had been presented, the persuasive words of Francesco Valori ringing out in a fiery speech that had left no doubt as to his stance. His words had been laced with a sense of foreboding, a warning to those who opposed the will of the people. And now, as the Eight prepared to escort the condemned men to the Bargello, the very air seemed to vibrate with tension.

Romola stood against the massive pillar in the palace courtyard, her heart heavy with the weight of the impending event. She had gained permission to visit her godfather, Niccolò Ridolfi, in the evening, and now she waited with his confessor to follow the guard that would lead him to his fate. Her resolve was unwavering, driven by a deep-seated loyalty to the childless old man who had been a second father to her. Giovan Battista Ridolfi, a devout follower of Savonarola, stood by her side, his expression a mask of resignation and sorrow as he contemplated the loss of his elder brother.

As the minutes ticked by, the crowd outside grew more agitated, their cries and shouts mingling with the distant clang of hammering on metal, the sound of the executioner's tools being readied. The darkness seemed to press in around Romola, the shadows cast by the flickering torches twisting and writhing like living things. And yet, amidst the chaos and uncertainty, she stood firm, her heart fixed on the figure of her godfather, the man who had guided her through the tumultuous waters of life.
