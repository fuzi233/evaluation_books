The Peasants' Fair.

As Tito pushed through the crowded piazza, the cacophony of sounds and the sea of unfamiliar faces washed over him like a balm to his weary soul. The scent of roasting chestnuts and sizzling farinaceous delicacies wafted through the air, mingling with the sweet fragrance of fresh bread and the pungency of strong cheese. His stomach growled in anticipation, reminding him that, despite the promise of a better life, his current circumstances left much to be desired. He recalled Nello's words about the Fierucoloni, and a spark of curiosity ignited within him, drawing him deeper into the throng.

At one end of the piazza, he found himself surrounded by the rustic beauty of the contadine, their bronzed faces weathered from years of toil under the unforgiving Tuscan sun. Their sun-dried clothing, adorned with intricate embroidery, told stories of a heritage that seemed frozen in time, like a relic from a bygone era. The sight brought to mind the traditional dishes of his homeland, where simplicity and rich flavors merged in perfect harmony – a hearty ribollita, thick with vegetables and beans, or a fragrant pappardelle, tossed with wild boar ragù.

As he navigated the crowd, Tito caught glimpses of various vendors showcasing their wares: a wise old trader peddling his hot berlingozzi, a staple of the local diet; a tumbler performing acrobatic feats on a small platform; and a group of apprentices engaged in a fierce game of calcio storico, their sweat-drenched brows a testament to their competitive spirit. The air was alive with the rhythm of drums, the tinkling of small bells, and the shrill cries of children, all blending together in a joyful cacophony that seemed to lift the very spirits.

Suddenly, Tito found himself face to face with his acquaintance Bratti, who stood with an air of superiority, his hand firmly grasping a serge-covered basket. Tito's curiosity got the better of him, and he approached Bratti, intrigued by the unusual display of reserve. As he drew closer, he noticed two women approaching Bratti's basket with a sense of trepidation, their eyes fixed on the mysterious contents.
