I regained consciousness to find myself wracked with agony, drowning in a fog of pain and bewildered forgetfulness. Struggling to rise, I discovered I was pinned down by a strange, invisible force—a manifestation of my own profound weakness. Lying on my back, I heard a faint, labored breathing nearby. Turning my head with agonizing slowness, I saw the Maid lying beside me, still naked. She was gasping for air, pushing desperately against the raft with a long pole—the very one I had severed earlier when the Hump-Men attacked.

In that instant, memory returned. We were adrift on the raft; she was using the pole to propel us away. A whimper escaped my lips, but she did not hear me. Her gaze was fixed intently backward toward the shore, her expression grim and anxious. From the distance came the terrifying howls of the Hump-Men. It became clear she had awakened just in time, dragging me onto the raft while I lay unconscious, saving me from their grasp. How she managed this feat remained a mystery to both of us; she knew only that her love had granted her a desperate strength sufficient to save her man.

Later, she recounted the horror of waking beneath me. She had felt my heavy head resting on her chest, realizing I was already slipping away from life due to my stillness. When she carefully rolled me onto the ground, her heart nearly shattered at the sight of my blood, which had soaked everything around me. Yet, when she confirmed I was alive, a surge of hope ignited within her. As she attended to me, scanning the surroundings, she noted only the corpse of the fallen Hump-Man nearby and the bodies of others scattered across the flat-topped rock.

She sprinted to the raft, filling my helmet with river water and dousing me, but I remained unresponsive. Suddenly, sensing a renewed threat approaching, she realized we must save each other or perish together. With frantic effort, she dragged the exhausted me back to the raft. Just as she secured me, she noticed her torn clothes still clutched in the hand of the living Hump-Man who had tried to stop her. Snatching them from his grip, she fled to the raft, pushed off from the shore, and leaped aboard.

As she began to pole away, a sound erupted from the woods. Two surviving Hump-Men emerged, tracking her path. They descended silently toward the shore, intent on capturing her. Driven by sheer desperation, she worked the pole until the raft gained significant distance. Neither pursuer attempted to swim; perhaps they lacked the ability, or perhaps they feared the "Dread" of the water. Instead, they stood staring stupidly before letting out a cacophony of howls—sounds I heard clearly as I finally stirred on the raft. By this account, you can piece together the events as I have; I know no more than these few details, aside from the oddities learned later that only deepened my devotion to the Maid. These were merely fleeting moments of affection shared between us, barely registered in my fading memory.

As the howling faded into the distance, driven by her relentless poling, I felt a desperate urge to help her. Attempting to rise, I slipped back into unconsciousness. In that state, the Maid piloted me safely to the little island harbor, caring for me with selfless love, thinking only of my survival. There, hovering between life and death, utterly helpless, I relied entirely on the vigilance of my Beloved.

Though I fought a valiant battle, I recall little clearly for a long period thereafter. Only fragments remain: pain, weariness, half-waking states, and periods of total confusion. Yet, amidst the black mists of weakness and stealthy despair, a constant presence of Love surrounded me—a gentle watchfulness that offered solace and hope.

Eventually, I woke to find the burdens lifted. I lay softly on something cushioned, enveloped in a sweet quietude, feeling a healthy drowsiness settle into my bones. Slowly, I perceived the Maid kneeling beside me, gazing at me with such radiant love and gladness that I felt healed, drinking in a drowsy joy and peace. She leaned down, kissing my mouth with tender gentleness, her tears tracing sweet paths down my face. I kissed her back with pure contentment.

She cradled my head in her arm and offered me liquid to drink. Afterward, she kissed me lightly, like a breeze brushing my lips, and adjusted my position. Soon, I drifted back to sleep under her care.

Three times this cycle repeated. On the third awakening, I sensed returning strength, moving my hand slightly. She understood immediately, holding it firmly, allowing me to slip back into sleep while I gazed lovingly into her eyes.

On the fourth awakening, I whispered my love for her. Instantly, she burst into tears, clutching my hand tightly against her breast.

By the fifth awakening, I understood my surroundings fully. I lay naked within a cloak, my body wrapped in bandages—made from the Maid's own torn garments. Looking at her, I saw she had dressed again in the clothes I had given her previously: the outer layer and the inner garment ripped by the Hump-Man, which she had stolen to escape.

Remarkably, she had ingeniously repaired the clothing while sitting by my side for hours. Using threads from her ruined dress and crafting needles from the sharp thorns of the island's bushes, she had stitched herself into neat attire. The thorns often broke, requiring her to start anew, yet she persisted a hundred times over.

Seeing my interest, she blushed naturally, recalling how I had last viewed her. She kissed me to divert my gaze, and I wished fervently to regain strength so I could kneel in reverent gratitude. This is the nature of true love, as you who have loved will understand.

My recovery progressed steadily. The Maid tended to me constantly, administering broth and water according to my timepiece. She washed me frequently, cleaning and drying the bandages to reuse them, for supplies were scarce.

On the fifth day, I felt completely relieved, wondrously happy, and engaged in pleasant conversation, though kept quiet due to my lingering weakness. On the sixth day, I was permitted to speak freely, declaring my immense love for her—an emotion my eyes had always conveyed during my silences. She assured me she was healthy and recovered, though I observed she had grown thin and her eyes bore the marks of exhaustion, despite their overflowing love and joy for me.

I instructed her to bring my tablets (food) to me. After kissing them and eating, I asked her to prepare my broth. Once ready, I requested the Diskos (my great weapon) be placed near me. Bidding her lie by my side, I took her head upon my arm, telling her to rest without fear of tiring me, for having her beside me would restore my own strength.

Initially, she worried about being too heavy, but I demonstrated my recovering power by pressing her gently against me. She nestled contentedly, falling into a deep sleep she desperately needed. For twelve hours, she slept almost motionless, occasionally making a soft moan or turning her face closer to mine in her dreams. I felt no loneliness or fatigue, only utter contentment, looking down at the Maid sleeping peacefully in the hollow of my arm. Her face possessed such loveliness and grace that it seemed to imbue my heart with holiness, lifting my spirit in a quiet, constant glory of love.

At the third, sixth, and ninth hours, I drank portions of the broth, finishing it by the end. My right arm was free to handle the Diskos; I touched the great weapon repeatedly, treating it as a true companion. Indeed, the Diskos is a strange, wondrous entity, believed to share an oneness with its user. My elevated spirits likely contributed to this feeling, yet the bond was undeniable.

At the twelfth hour, the Maid awoke suddenly, rising quickly to confirm my well-being. Seeing my quiet laughter at her concern, she felt relief mixed with reproach for worrying. I adopted a mock-stern tone, forbidding her to dwell on the matter, urging her instead to rejoice in our mutual happiness.

True to her playful nature, the impudent Maid raised her small fist to threaten me. I laughed heartily, fearing my wounds might reopen, causing her to reproach herself again, though I suffered no harm.

Once able to speak properly, I asked if she had siblings, noting her childish playfulness. Realizing my question might seem harsh, I immediately took her hand to assure her of my kindness. She nodded quietly, kissed my hand, and slipped away briefly, presumably to hide her tears. Returning soon, she smiled cheerfully, though I saw she concealed fresh tears while preparing new broth. Before eating, I pulled her into my arms; she submitted gladly but kept her weight light to avoid hurting my wounds.

We ate happily, engaging in joyful conversation. Shortly after, I fell asleep, insisting she stay near, awake if necessary. We were perfectly content together.

The following day proved wonderful. Waking to find the Maid sleeping like a child against my side, she roused instantly, having watched over me throughout the night. After washing and caring for me, we ate and drank together. Now restored to full capacity, I received my tablets whole, followed by water, a luxury that delighted me. I ached to regain strength quickly—to protect the Maid and continue our journey toward the safety of the Mighty Pyramid. With my tablets intact, I felt nearing fitness, and they satisfied my hunger better than broth.

She fed me numerous tablets, counting them to ensure sufficiency.
