The sun had barely begun its ascent, casting a faint glow over the desolate landscape, when I stirred, my parched body screaming for respite from the relentless heat. Two hours of fitful sleep had barely alleviated the torment of my thirst, and now, as I slowly sat up, the dryness of my lips and eyelids seemed to mock me. My hands, roughened by the desert terrain, scraped against my face in a futile attempt to moisten the cracked skin. The air was heavy with an oppressive stillness, a thick murkiness that clung to my skin like a damp shroud.

As the first light of dawn crept over the horizon, I reached for the worn leather satchel slung over my shoulder, rummaging through its contents until I found a tattered copy of the "Ingoldsby Legends". The words on its yellowed pages offered a fleeting escape from the desolation around me, and I lost myself in the tale of "The Jackdaw of Rheims". When I read the lines:

"A nice little boy held a golden ewer,
Embossed, and filled with water as pure
As any that flows between Rheims and Namur,"

my mouth felt like the cracked earth, dry and barren. I could almost taste the sweetness of that water, and my mind wandered to the Cardinal's opulent chambers, where such luxuries were taken for granted. Even the thought of defiling his sacred water with my unwashed face and hands seemed a tantalizing prospect, and I chuckled at the absurdity of it all. My laughter echoed through the stillness, jolting the others awake.

As we sat amidst the silence, our dry mouths and gritty eyes a testament to our plight, we discussed our situation. The water bottles lay empty, their once-promising contents reduced to nothing more than hollow shells. Good, ever the optimist, eyed the flask of brandy wistfully, but Sir Henry's firm hand stayed his grasp. To drink the raw spirit would only hasten our demise.

"If we don't find water, we'll perish," he said, his voice laced with a quiet determination.

I nodded in agreement, my thoughts drifting to the map, the only lead we had to find salvation. Yet, even that seemed a tenuous thread, and our conversation was met with a collective skepticism.
