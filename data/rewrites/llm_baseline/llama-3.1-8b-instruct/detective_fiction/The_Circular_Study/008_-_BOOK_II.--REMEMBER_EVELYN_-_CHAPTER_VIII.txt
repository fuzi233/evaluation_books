In a small, unassuming house on a quiet street, the residents stirred, each one carrying with them secrets and stories that had been simmering beneath the surface for years. It was on this morning that Mr. Gryce received a discreet message from Miss Butterworth, a woman whose calm demeanor belied the turmoil brewing within her. Her note was as enigmatic as ever, running:

"You were quite correct, Mr. Gryce. I have made some adjustments to my attire."

Mr. Gryce's response was just as cryptic, though laced with a hint of intrigue:

"My compliments, Miss Butterworth. A small discovery has been made. A piece of paper was found clenched between the deceased's teeth. This paper holds a secret that may soon be revealed. Certain individuals may be privy to its contents before the inquest."

Miss Butterworth's reaction was a study in composure, but the household staff couldn't help but wonder what lay behind her serene facade. Meanwhile, at the local police headquarters, detectives were inundated with tales from would-be informants, each one eager to share their version of events surrounding the tragic death of Mr. Adams. A gaggle of elderly women claimed to have seen a lady of refinement greet an elderly gentleman in a long coat, while a young woman in a fashionable dress recounted a chance encounter with a couple – a pale young man and an even paler young woman, whose hat featured a striking gray feather.

As the hours passed, the stories piled up, but none added significantly to the puzzle. Night fell, casting a veil of darkness over the town, and the investigation remained stuck in a cycle of fruitless leads and unfulfilled promises. The next day, Mr. Adams's funeral procession wound its way through the streets, a somber reminder of the tragedy that had shaken the community. With no family or close friends in attendance, the funeral was a subdued affair, overseen by the landlord and the banker, who served as chief mourner.

As the coffin was carried away, a group of detectives lingered at the edge of the crowd, but their surveillance yielded nothing.
