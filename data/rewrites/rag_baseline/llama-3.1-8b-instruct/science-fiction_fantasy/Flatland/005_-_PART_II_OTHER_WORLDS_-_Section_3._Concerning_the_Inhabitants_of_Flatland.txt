It was a realm where the inhabitants were bound by a strict hierarchy, their forms determined by the number of sides they possessed. Lady Aria, a Square of discerning taste and intellect, sat amidst the hushed tones of the library, her thoughts consumed by the intricacies of the social ladder.

Their vertices, honed to a fine point, seemed to cut through the air with an air of superiority, a reminder that even in a world governed by strict laws, there existed those who sought to defy convention. The Isosceles, with their two equal sides and a base so short it was almost imperceptible, walked with an air of quiet confidence, their eyes fixed on the horizon of possibilities.
