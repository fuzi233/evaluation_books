As the harsh winter months settled over the Dunwich countryside, Wilbur's thoughts turned to the distant libraries that held the secrets he sought. For months, he had corresponded with the esteemed institutions of Harvard, Paris, Buenos Aires, and Miskatonic University, all in a bid to acquire a rare and ancient tome – the Latin translation of the forbidden _Necronomicon_ by Olaus Wormius. The text was said to contain knowledge that had lain hidden for centuries, and Wilbur was determined to unlock its secrets.

He packed a worn valise from Osborn's general store and set off on the long journey to Arkham, a city he had never seen. His appearance was rough, his beard unkempt, and his dialect thick with the local accent. Yet, his eyes burned with an intensity that hinted at a deeper purpose. As he walked through the city streets, he seemed oblivious to the curious glances of the townsfolk, his gaze fixed on the university grounds ahead.

At Miskatonic University, Wilbur presented himself to the librarian, Henry Armitage, a man of erudite reputation and vast knowledge. Armitage was intrigued by Wilbur's quest and engaged him in conversation, asking questions about his research. Wilbur revealed that he was searching for a specific passage in the _Necronomicon_ – one that contained the name _Yog-Sothoth_ – and he hoped to find a formula or incantation within its pages. As they spoke, Armitage's curiosity grew, and he watched with interest as Wilbur pored over the ancient text, comparing it to his own imperfect copy of Dr. Dee's English translation.

The passage that caught Wilbur's attention was written in the left-hand margin of the Latin text, its words seeming to leap off the page like a dark and foreboding melody. Armitage's eyes widened as he translated the words in his mind:

"Not in the spaces we know, but between them, they walk serene and primal, undimensioned and to us unseen... Yog-Sothoth knows the gate. Yog-Sothoth is the gate. Yog-Sothoth is the key and guardian of the gate... Past, present, future, all are one in Yog-Sothoth."
