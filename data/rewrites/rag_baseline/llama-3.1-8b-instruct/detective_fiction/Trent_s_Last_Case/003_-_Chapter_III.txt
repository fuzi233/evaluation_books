The excitement surrounding the discovery of a deceased individual had momentarily disrupted his appetite, leading to a less-than-satisfactory meal. However, this morning's prolonged wakefulness had reignited his hunger, prompting him to recalibrate his expectations.

As he savored the view, Mr. Cupples' trained eye traversed the rugged coastline, where a majestic rock formation pierced the glassy sea, and the meticulously cultivated landscape unfolded towards the distant moor. His upbringing in a household that prized order and discipline had instilled in him an innate kindness and a capacity for innocent humor, traits he had retained despite his austere lifestyle.
