How could one so exquisite be consumed by such malevolent fury? I recalled the girl's confession, and my heart ached anew. Had I handled the situation poorly, or was this merely the natural consequence of my actions?

As I stood there, frozen in uncertainty, Issus' eyes seemed to bore into my very soul, searching for some hidden weakness to exploit. Her words dripped with venom, each sentence a dagger aimed at my heart. Yet, amidst her tirade, I sensed a fleeting moment of distraction. Her gaze wandered to the dark waters outside the port-hole, and for an instant, her composure faltered. The realization dawned on me that she had forgotten the precarious circumstances of our confinement, and the suffocating weight of our descent into the depths.

Without intending to provoke her further, I nodded discreetly toward the port-hole, and Issus' attention snapped back to our surroundings. The sight of the dark, churning waters seemed to knock the wind from her lungs, and she crumpled onto a nearby bench, her sobs muffled by her arms. Down, down we sank, the pressure building with each passing moment. The glass of the port-holes grew warmer, a reminder of the unforgiving environment that surrounded us.

Time lost all meaning as we descended into the darkness. The only sound was the gentle hum of the propellers, pushing us deeper into the unknown. Then, just as suddenly, our motion ceased, and we began to rise. The light from outside grew stronger, and I caught glimpses of the rock-lined passage we had traversed. Xodar and his men emerged from the shadows, their faces illuminated by the faint glow of a distant searchlight. They led us out of the submarine, into a small, dimly lit vault.
