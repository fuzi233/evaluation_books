The scent of blooming lilacs wafted in through the open window, mingling with the fragrance of the tea leaves and the warm, sweet aroma of freshly baked cakes.

His eyes, drawn inexorably to Margaret, found her standing by the tea table, her delicate features set in a determined expression, her slender fingers moving with a quiet precision as she poured the tea.

He felt a pang of regret as he realized that the evening's pleasures would soon be interrupted by the obligation of eating and drinking.
