Silence enveloped the Pyramid's vast expanse like a shroud, punctuated only by the faint hum of machinery and the soft rustle of dusty pages turning. I stood at the edge of the Tower of Observation, my feet bare against the cold stone floor. Below me, the sprawling city of the Pyramid stretched out like a map of forgotten dreams. The wind carried the whispers of countless conversations, but I strained to listen for a single, specific voice – the one that lingered in my memory, a melody that had haunted me since childhood.

As a young man, I'd often found myself lost in thought, wandering the narrow corridors of the Tower, searching for answers to questions I didn't dare ask aloud. My Master Monstruwacan, a wise and enigmatic figure, had taken me under his wing, recognizing the unique gift I possessed – the ability to hear the 'invisible vibrations' of the aether. This rare talent allowed me to pick up messages from the eternal darkness, a skill I'd honed over the years to near perfection.

My mind wandered to the countless hours spent listening to the whispers of the aether, the endless stream of information flowing through the Pyramid's labyrinthine networks. But one voice remained elusive, a haunting refrain that echoed through my memory-dreams like a ghostly melody. Mirdath the Beautiful, a love from a bygone era, whose voice I'd never known but felt deeply connected to. Her presence lingered, a constant reminder of what I'd lost, and what I yearned for.

As the fifteenth hour approached, marking the beginning of the Sleep-Time, I stood at the precipice of the Tower, my heart pounding in anticipation. The city below slumbered, its inhabitants unaware of the subtle vibrations that coursed through the aether. I closed my eyes, focusing on the silence, and suddenly, I was transported to a realm beyond the Pyramid's walls. A whisper began to take shape, a gentle caress that seemed to come from the very fabric of the universe itself.
