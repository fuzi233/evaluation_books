As Oleron stood by the hearth, lost in contemplation, the warm glow of the fire danced across the room, casting a golden light upon the space where Miss Bengough's words still lingered. Her prognostication that difficulties awaited him in his work weighed heavily on his mind, and he couldn't help but feel that her words had sullied the promise of this new place. The room, with its elegant lines and subtle charm, had been designed to soothe the soul, yet he couldn't shake the feeling that it had somehow been diminished by her presence. The soft patter of the rain outside seemed to underscore the melancholy mood that had settled over him.

He rose from his chair and walked to the window, the curtains billowing softly behind him like a gentle lover's caress. The darkness outside seemed to press in, a physical manifestation of the doubts that had taken hold of him. The room, once a sanctuary, now felt cramped and confining. He thought of Miss Bengough's words, how they had struck at the very heart of his confidence. Speech itself had become a deterrent, and now he found himself questioning whether he truly belonged here.

The scent of wood smoke and damp earth filled his nostrils as he stood there, lost in thought. He recalled the way the light had highlighted the lines of her face, the flush of color that had risen to her cheeks as she spoke. Her words had been laced with a warmth and concern that had almost convinced him of their validity. Yet, as he looked around the room, he saw it in a different light – a space that was both beautiful and barren, a testament to the fragility of human connection.

A faint clinking sound drifted from the kitchen, the dripping of water from an imperfectly turned-off tap into the vessel beneath it. Oleron's gaze drifted towards the sound, his mind still reeling from the tumult of emotions that had been unleashed within him. He felt a pang of guilt, a recognition that his thoughts had been cruel, that he had judged her without giving her a chance to defend herself. The fire crackled and spat, a reminder that even in the midst of turmoil, there was still beauty to be found.
