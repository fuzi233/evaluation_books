She chose not to press the matter further, instead retreating to her chambers to collect her thoughts, the silence a balm to her troubled mind.

Her thoughts drifted to the mysterious Sphere, whose ethereal presence still lingered in her memory like the faint scent of incense on a summer breeze. She recalled the way he had spoken of the realms beyond Flatland, of Lineland's singular dimension and the boundless expanse of Spaceland. And then, as if beckoned by an unseen force, her mind's eye strayed to the abyssal depths of Pointland, a place where beings dwelled without form, their existence a mere whisper of nothingness.
