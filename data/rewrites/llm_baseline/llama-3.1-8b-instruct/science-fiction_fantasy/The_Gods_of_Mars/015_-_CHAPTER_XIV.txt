As I stood in the dimly lit cabin of our airship, the scent of roasting meats wafted through the air, teasing my senses and momentarily distracting me from the warm, golden light of the setting sun casting through the windows. My son, Carthoris, approached me, his bright grey eyes shining with a mix of excitement and trepidation. I couldn't help but notice the strong resemblance between us – the sharp jawline, the piercing gaze – and my heart swelled with pride.

"Tell me of your mother," I said, my voice low and inviting, as I gestured for him to take a seat beside me. The aroma of the roasting meats grew stronger, and my stomach growled in anticipation of the feast to come.

Carthoris' face lit up, and he launched into a vivid description of his mother, Dejah Thoris. He spoke of her unchanging beauty, her kindness, and her unwavering spirit. As he spoke, I was transported back to the moments we shared together, the memories of her laughter, her smile, and her gentle touch still etched vividly in my mind. The thought of her longing for me, even now, brought a pang to my chest.

"We must prepare dinner soon," I said, breaking the spell. "I've instructed Xodar to start preparing the evening meal. Tonight, we'll dine on a traditional Heliumian dish, one passed down through generations of my family – a recipe for a savory meat pie infused with the spices and herbs of the red planet."

Carthoris' eyes sparkled at the prospect, and he eagerly agreed to help with the preparations. As we worked together, side by side, the tension in the air dissipated, replaced by the warmth of our shared activity. The smell of roasting meats and the sizzle of vegetables on the cooking surface filled the cabin, and I felt a sense of contentment wash over me.

Just then, Xodar called out from the cockpit, his voice laced with concern. "She's losing altitude, John Carter! We're taking on water from her forward ray tanks." I quickly made my way to the cockpit, my heart sinking as I assessed the damage. The wound to her bow had compromised one of her forward ray tanks, threatening to leave us adrift and helpless.
