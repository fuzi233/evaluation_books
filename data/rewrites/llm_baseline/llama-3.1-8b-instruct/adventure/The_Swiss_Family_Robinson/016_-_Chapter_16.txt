As I sit amidst the pages of my journal, reflecting on ten years of life on this isolated island, I find myself pondering the words of King David: 'We spend our years as a tale that is told.' It is a poignant reminder that time slips away from us, leaving behind only memories and lessons learned.

Looking back, I am filled with gratitude for the blessings that have been bestowed upon my family. Our children, now grown and strengthened by the values we instilled in them, stand poised to make their mark on the world. Their futures are bright, and I am proud to think that they will carry the lessons of our experiences with them wherever their paths may lead.

As I write these words, I hope that they will serve as a testament to the transformative power of a harmonious family life. It is in the peaceful, industrious, and pious atmosphere of our home that strong, pure, and manly characters are forged. Those who emerge from such a crucible are well-equipped to navigate the complexities of the world, to build meaningful relationships, and to contribute positively to their communities.

As I consider the years we've spent in our little domain, now affectionately known as New Switzerland, I am reminded of the importance of community and the interconnectedness of all living things. Our relationship with the natural world has been one of mutual respect and reciprocity. We have worked tirelessly to cultivate the land, to understand its rhythms and patterns, and to live in harmony with its creatures.

At Rockburg and Falconhurst, our winter and summer headquarters, we have made significant strides in creating a thriving ecosystem. The gardens, trellised verandahs, and plantations have transformed the landscape, bringing beauty and vitality to our surroundings. The once-barren terrain is now teeming with life, as flowering creepers cascade over balconies and pillars, and native and European trees grow luxuriantly in our carefully tended groves.

Shark Island, with its sentinel palms, watches over the entrance to Safety Bay, where the battery and flagstaff proudly stand as symbols of our resilience and ingenuity. The swamp, once a hindrance, has been transformed into a serene lake, a haven for waterfowl and a testament to our dedication to sustainability.
