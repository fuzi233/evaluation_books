As Mr. Draper stormed out of his conversation with Harry, he thought he heard the young prisoner's voice behind him, and indeed, Harry had risen to call out to the lawyer. However, Harry's pride got the better of him, and he checked himself, unwilling to humble himself before the man. The lawyer continued on his way, leaving Harry to his dark thoughts. The weight of his predicament hung heavy on his shoulders – the debt, the engagement to Maria Esmond, and the sense of being manipulated by those he trusted. He felt a pang of shame and anger towards his cousins, who he believed had taken advantage of his naivety. The memory of their actions stung, and Harry let out a deep sigh, his eyes welling up with tears.

Just as he was succumbing to despair, Gumbo appeared at his door, announcing the arrival of a visitor. Behind the servant came the lady in the capuchin, who had previously caught Harry's eye. She rushed towards him, her face etched with concern, and enveloped him in a warm hug. Her tears flowed freely as she held him close, her voice trembling as she whispered, "Oh, Harry, I never thought I'd see you here."

Startled by her sudden appearance, Harry pulled back, but the lady quickly settled beside him, taking his hand in hers. Her touch was gentle, and her words were laced with genuine affection. As she looked at him, her expression softened, and she reached out to wipe away his tears. The somber surroundings and Harry's downtrodden appearance filled her with a deep sense of compassion and love.

"I thought no one would come," Harry muttered, his voice barely above a whisper.

The lady's response was a cascade of tears, kisses on his hand, and tender squeezes. For a moment, she was overcome with emotion, her love and pity for him palpable.

"Oh, my dear, my dear, I couldn't bear the thought of you suffering like this," she sobbed out.

As she spoke, Harry's hardened exterior began to crack, revealing a vulnerable young man beneath. His kinswoman's love and concern for him were a balm to his wounded soul. She apologized for her past mistakes, acknowledging that her extravagance had contributed to his financial woes. With a gesture of remorse, she produced a trove of trinkets, jewelry, and coins, which she poured onto his lap.

The sight of these tokens of her affection moved Harry deeply.
