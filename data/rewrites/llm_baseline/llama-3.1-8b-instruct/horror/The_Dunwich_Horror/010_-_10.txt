As the three men from Arkham, Dr. Armitage, Professor Rice, and Dr. Morgan, ascended the mountain, the air grew thick with an unsettling stillness. Their footsteps echoed off the trees, the only sound in the oppressive quiet. The group below, who had been watching them through the borrowed telescope, felt a sense of trepidation wash over them as the men disappeared from view. 

After a while, Curtis Whateley, a young man from the village, took hold of the telescope, his eyes scanning the landscape for any sign of the three climbers. As he focused the lens, he noticed that the Arkham party had deviated from their initial path, making a beeline for a smaller peak that overlooked the swath at a point ahead of where the trees were beginning to thin out. This realization sent a shiver down Curtis's spine, and he felt a sense of foreboding settle in the pit of his stomach.

Wesley Corey, who had taken the glass from Curtis, suddenly pointed out that Dr. Armitage was adjusting the sprayer that Professor Rice held. A murmur of unease rippled through the crowd as they recalled the significance of the sprayer – it was meant to render the unseen horror visible for a fleeting moment. Curtis, however, couldn't bear to look away, his eyes straining against the lens as he watched Dr. Rice prepare to release the powder. 

For an instant, the crowd caught a glimpse of a gray cloud, about the size of a large building, hovering near the summit. Curtis's reaction was immediate and visceral – he dropped the telescope into the mud, his eyes wide with terror. He stumbled, and his friends rushed to catch him, holding him upright as he struggled to speak. "Oh, great God... that... that..." he whispered, his voice barely audible.

A cacophony of questions erupted from the crowd, but Curtis's words were barely intelligible.
