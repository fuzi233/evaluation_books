As the sun dipped below the horizon, casting a warm orange glow over the sleepy town of Roville, Sally made her way back to the Casino Municipale, her footsteps echoing off the stone façade of the Palace Massena. With a sense of trepidation, she pushed open the heavy doors and slipped inside, her eyes scanning the crowded room for a glimpse of Ginger. Her heart skipped a beat as she spotted him, hunched over a roulette table, his brow furrowed in concentration.

The air was thick with the smell of smoke and the murmur of hushed conversations, the only sound breaking the stillness as the croupier spun the wheel. Sally navigated through the throng of onlookers, her gaze fixed on Ginger's profile. He was completely absorbed in the game, his eyes locked onto the spinning wheel as if willing it to land on his chosen number.

A wistful voice whispered in her ear, "Epatant!" A man nearby gesticulated wildly, his eyes shining with excitement. Sally smiled sympathetically, understanding the collective thrill of watching someone else's luck change. The crowd seemed to vibrate with anticipation, their faces aglow with a mix of awe and envy.

As the croupier's moustache twitched and the wheel whirred to a stop, a hush fell over the room. Sally shifted positions, creating space between herself and the surrounding onlookers. From her new vantage point, she could see Ginger's face, and her lips curled into a silent smile. His eyes sparkled with an unbridled intensity, his hair standing on end as if electrified by the thrill of the game.

But when Sally's soft laughter escaped, the room's tranquility shattered. The sound cut through the air like a knife, its clarity jarring the crowd. Gasps and disapproving glances shot her way, their faces etched with a mixture of shock and disappointment. Ginger's head jerked up, his eyes locking onto hers before darting back to the table. The ball clattered into a red compartment, a defeated murmur rippling through the crowd. As the croupier announced the losing colour, a collective sigh of dismay hung in the air, and Sally felt the weight of their disapproval settle upon her.
