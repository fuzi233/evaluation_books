As Deronda stepped into the banking-house in the Schuster Strasse at Mainz, the echoes of the past seemed to reverberate within him. He approached the counter, his heart beating with a mix of trepidation and anticipation, and inquired about Joseph Kalonymos. The attendant nodded discreetly and led him to an inner room, where the white-bearded man sat, surrounded by scattered papers and letters. The same felt hat, worn and faded, rested on the table beside him, and a packed portmanteau stood nearby, adorned with a worn wrap and overcoat.

Upon entering, Kalonymos rose, his eyes narrowing slightly as he took in Deronda's appearance. His gaze was like a piercing beam, illuminating the young man's features with an intensity that made him feel both seen and understood. "Ah, you've come," Kalonymos said, his voice low and gravelly, "I've been expecting you."

Deronda smiled, feeling a sense of relief wash over him. "I'm here to thank you, sir," he began, switching to German with ease. "My grandfather spoke highly of you, and I'm deeply grateful for your kindness in helping me uncover the truth about my heritage." Kalonymos's face creased into a warm smile, and he extended a gnarled hand, which Deronda shook firmly.

As they sat down, Kalonymos laid aside his hat, revealing a head thickly covered in white hair, and began to stroke his beard thoughtfully. His eyes, like two black jewels, sparkled with a deep understanding as he regarded Deronda. The young man felt a shiver run down his spine as he sensed the weight of their connection – a bond forged across generations, a tie that transcended time and space.

In that moment, Deronda felt the past and present converging, like the threads of a rich tapestry weaving together. He recalled the words of a wise poet, "The acts of old, though done in days of yore, still bear fruit in our lives today." As Kalonymos's gaze bore into his soul, he felt the electric current of his ancestry coursing through him, connecting him to the past, to his people, and to the land of his forefathers.
