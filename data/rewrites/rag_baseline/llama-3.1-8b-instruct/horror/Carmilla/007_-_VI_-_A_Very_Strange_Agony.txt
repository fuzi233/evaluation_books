Carmilla, reclining on the sofa, her dark hair a rich frame for her pale complexion, smiled warmly as he approached. The card party, involving Madame and Mademoiselle De Lafontaine, had concluded, and the atmosphere was tranquil, save for the distant rumble of thunder outside.

My father settled beside Carmilla, his eyes betraying a hint of concern as he inquired if she'd received any news from her mother since her arrival. Carmilla's response was enigmatic, leaving him to press for further information. He broached the subject of her departure, voicing his reluctance to part with her, and I shared his sentiment. As he spoke, I couldn't help but notice the way the fading light danced across Carmilla's face, casting an otherworldly aura around her.
