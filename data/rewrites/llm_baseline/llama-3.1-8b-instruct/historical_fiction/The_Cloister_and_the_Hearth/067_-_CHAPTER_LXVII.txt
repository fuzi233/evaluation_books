As the last wisps of sunlight dissolved into the horizon, Gerard stole away from the monastery, his footsteps quiet on the dusty path that led to the river. The air was alive with the scent of ripening fruit and the soft chirping of crickets, a stark contrast to the turmoil brewing within him. With a sense of determination etched on his face, he hastened to the riverbank, his pockets heavy with the silver and copper coins he had managed to gather.

His mind was set on ending his life, a decision born from the desperation that had been growing inside him like a slow-burning fire. He had taken pains to ensure he was alone, scanning the surroundings with a practiced eye, but a figure leaning against the corner of a nearby alley caught his attention. Gerard tried to brush past, feigning nonchalance, but his heart quickened as the stranger emerged from the shadows, their eyes locked onto his.

A shiver ran down Gerard's spine as he wondered if he was being watched. Impossible, he thought, yet the sensation lingered. He turned and walked away, only to return a short while later, his steps deliberate and measured. This time, however, the stranger had vanished. Gerard's gaze swept the area, his eyes adjusting to the fading light, and that was when he spotted the man – a few yards behind him, pretending to adjust his shoe.

A cold dread crept over Gerard as he realized he was being stalked. In the moonlight, a glint of steel caught his eye – a gauntlet worn by the mysterious figure. A chill ran down his spine as he grasped the truth: he was being pursued by an assassin. Yet, it never occurred to him that his own life was the target. He was oblivious to the danger that lurked in the shadows, and it wasn't until now that the reality began to dawn on him.

With a newfound sense of purpose, Gerard approached the stranger, a mixture of fear and defiance etched on his face. "Sell me your arm," he pleaded, pressing a handful of coins into the man's palm. "Just one blow, and I'll be free of this wretched existence." Even as he spoke, he loosened his doublet, baring his chest in a desperate bid for release.
