I felt the gentle touch of each plant beneath my bare feet, and with it, the secrets of the universe unfolded before me. The boundaries between self and world dissolved, and I became one with the land, my being entwined with the essence of existence.

I was the ocean, vast and peaceful, with waves of delight washing over me in endless succession. My companion walked beside me, and together we journeyed towards the heart of our world, our steps guided by the gentle light of dawn.

Yet, despite their appearance, they were not dead, for as long as humanity harbored cruelty and malice, these abominations would continue to thrive.
