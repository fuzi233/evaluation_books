Tars Tarkas' soft chuckle cut through the quiet, a gentle, almost whimsical sound, a stark contrast to the horror that had preceded it. His people often found amusement in the darkest of situations, and I'd witnessed it before – the way they'd erupt into uncontrollable laughter at the cruel games of the Great Fete, the torture and suffering of innocent lives. I couldn't help but smile, a mix of fascination and unease. In this desolate place, a smile was a rare commodity, and I knew I needed it to keep my wits about me.

"What do you make of it all?" I asked, trying to sound nonchalant despite the turmoil brewing inside me. "Where in the world are we?"

Tars Tarkas turned to me with a look of genuine surprise. "Where are we?" he repeated, his deep voice tinged with amusement. "Tell me, John Carter, that you don't know where you are?"

I shook my head, feeling a pang of frustration. "All I know is that we're on Barsoom, but the sights I've seen today are as foreign to me as the world I left behind ten years ago. Without you and those massive white apes, I wouldn't even guess our location."
