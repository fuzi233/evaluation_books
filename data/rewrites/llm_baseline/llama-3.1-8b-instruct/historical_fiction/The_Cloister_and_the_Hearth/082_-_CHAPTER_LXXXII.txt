Margaret's hands trembled as she held the steaming bowl of her grandmother's famous soup, the aroma wafting up to her nostrils, transporting her back to a time when life was simpler. Her loyal servant, Luke, stood beside her, his eyes fixed on the flames dancing in the hearth. 

"Mistress, they all say he is gone," he whispered, his voice barely audible over the crackling of the fire.

Margaret's eyes brimmed with tears as she replied, "Not so. They feed me still with hopes." Her words were laced with a mix of desperation and determination. "Aye, to my face, but behind my back they all say he is lost forever."

Luke's face fell, and he instinctively reached out to comfort her. His gentle touch brought a wave of calm, but Margaret's sorrow remained palpable. She thought to herself, If only I could cook a meal that would bring him back to me, just as Grandmother used to make. The memories of her father's words echoed in her mind – "I see him! Just so. Not like a failing man at all, but all ablaze, on a great river, coming this way."

As she recalled those words, a spark of hope ignited within her. "Luke, he is not gone," she declared, her voice firm. "Dying men are known to possess a strange sight. Think, if you were a woman, with a heart as tender as mine, you would understand the depth of my longing. Take a thought! The father of my child!"

Luke's brow furrowed in concentration as he pondered the riddle of the river. He recalled the stories of his childhood, the tales of the Maas and the Rhine, great rivers that flowed through the land. "On a great river...the Maas is a great river," he mused aloud. "But if it was the Maas, he would have been here by now. The Rhine is a greater river, longer and more winding. Perhaps it will be the Rhine."

Margaret nodded in agreement, her eyes shining with a glimmer of hope. "And so do I, Luke; for Denys bade him follow the Rhine. But even if it is, he may have turned off before reaching his birthplace. He does not pine for me as I for him; that is clear. Luke, do you not think he has abandoned me?"
