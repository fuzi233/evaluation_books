Above all, indeed, I insist upon this, you must write me letters at least once a week providing a comprehensive account of your activities and the events unfolding in the city, allowing me to enjoy these things at second-hand, as my wretched health precludes my direct enjoyment. Remember, I anticipate full details, and no detail is too trivial to interest.—Your affectionate Aunt, ISABEL ROCKMETTELLER."

"What about it?" asked Rocky.

"What about it?" I echoed.

"Yes. What on earth am I supposed to do?"

Only then did I truly grasp the peculiar attitude of this gentleman, considering the sudden descent of a generous windfall from a clear blue sky. To my mind, this was an occasion for a beaming smile and a joyous shout; yet here the man sat, appearing and speaking as if Fate had struck his solar plexus. It astounded me.

"Aren't you excited?" I asked.

"Bucked?"

"If I were in your shoes, I would be thoroughly braced. I consider this arrangement quite lenient for you."

He emitted a yelp, stared at me for a moment, and then began discussing New York in a manner reminiscent of Jimmy Mundy, the reformer. Jimmy had recently arrived in New York on a crusade, and I had attended a brief engagement at the Garden a few days prior. While Jimmy had certainly delivered some blunt truths about the city, seemingly disliking it, by Jove, dear old Rocky made him look like a publicity agent for the metropolis!

"Precisely lenient!" he cried. "To have to relocate to New York! To abandon my cozy cottage and occupy a stifling, malodorous, overheated apartment in this Heaven-forsaken, festering Gehenna! To mingle night after night with a mob that believes life is a form of St. Vitus's dance, imagining they are having a good time merely because they generate sufficient noise for six and consume excessive alcohol for ten! I loathe New York, Bertie. I would not approach the place if I needed to consult editors. There is a blight upon it. It suffers from moral delirium tremens. It is the limit. Merely contemplating staying there for more than a day induces nausea. And you call this arrangement lenient for me!"
