Elinor tried to reason with her, but Marianne's feelings ran deep, and she clung to the idea of returning to the place where she had once envisioned her future. Elinor chose not to press the point, instead focusing on the practicalities of their journey. Mrs. Jennings, who had grown fond of the Dashwood sisters, was dismayed by their decision to leave, but Elinor reassured her that their departure was inevitable.
