Four days passed like a slow melody, each day unfolding into the next without urgency. We glided across the calm waters, the raft's gentle rocking motion synchronizing with the ebb and flow of my breath. The Maid stood at the bow, her paddle dipping into the sea with a soothing rhythm. I mirrored her strokes, our movements becoming a symphony of repetition. Time lost all meaning as we paddled, our conversation reduced to gentle murmurs and the occasional burst of laughter.

As the sun climbed higher in the sky, we paused to rest and replenish our stores. The Maid spread a makeshift cloak on the raft's deck, and we shared a simple meal, the flavors mingling with the sea air. Our hunger sated, we settled into a deep slumber, lulled by the waves' constant lapping against the hull.

I awoke to the warmth of the sun on my skin, the Maid's eyes already open, gazing at me with a quiet affection. We smiled at each other, our faces creasing into lines of contentment. After a refreshing dip in the ocean, we resumed our paddling, our strokes growing stronger as the day wore on.

The landscape remained unchanged – an endless expanse of desolate beauty, punctuated by the occasional glimpse of a distant creature. Once, we spotted a massive beast emerging from the sea, its lumbering form a stark contrast to our fluid motion. We watched it graze on the coastal vegetation, its presence a reminder of the world's vast, untamed power. Yet, it did not fill us with fear; instead, it seemed a natural part of this strange, forgotten land.

Our journey continued, a peaceful progression through a world that seemed frozen in time. I pondered the notion that this place might be a remnant of a bygone era, a world where creatures lived in harmony with their surroundings. The idea tantalized me, but I pushed it aside, focusing on the present moment. The Maid's hand brushed against mine, and I felt a spark of connection, a reminder that even in this desolate landscape, there was beauty to be found in the simplest of things.
