As the morning sun streamed through the windows of the grand estate, Caroline returned upstairs to find Mrs. Pryor sitting in her favorite armchair, a look of quiet dejection etched on her face. The gentlewoman's eyes seemed to hold a deep sadness, a pain that only intensified as she reflected on the events of the previous night. Caroline, attuned to her governess's moods, approached her with a gentle smile and sat beside her on the couch.

"I've been wanting to talk to you about last night," Mrs. Pryor began, her voice barely above a whisper. "I fear I may have disappointed you, dear Caroline. My hesitation in making a decision has weighed heavily on my mind." She paused, collecting her thoughts before continuing, "Though I strive to do what is right, I often find myself uncertain in times of crisis. I worry that my lack of decisiveness may have caused trouble for you, dear one."

Just then, a soft knock at the door interrupted their conversation. Caroline rose to answer it, finding Miss Keeldar standing in the hallway, her expression contrite and apologetic. "How is Mrs. Pryor?" she asked, her voice tinged with remorse.

"Rather downcast," Caroline replied, ushering Miss Keeldar into the room.

Miss Keeldar approached Mrs. Pryor with a warm smile, her eyes shining with sincerity. "I'm so sorry, dear Mrs. Pryor. I was utterly wrong to snap at you last night. You were simply trying to do what was best, and I should have trusted your judgment." She knelt beside Mrs. Pryor, taking her hand in hers. "Please forgive me; I couldn't bear the thought of a misunderstanding between us."

Mrs. Pryor's expression softened, her eyes filling with tears. "There's nothing to forgive, my dear," she said, her voice trembling. "Let us put the matter behind us. The important thing is that we learn from our mistakes and grow stronger because of them." As they spoke, Caroline busied herself preparing a soothing tea, the aroma of which wafted through the room, mingling with the scent of fresh-baked bread and the warm sunlight streaming through the windows.
