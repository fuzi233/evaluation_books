The darkness of the Night Land seeped into my bones like a chill draft. Every step forward was a deliberate calculation, each movement weighed against the exhaustion that threatened to consume me. I had been walking for what felt like an eternity, the rhythmic crunch of gravel beneath my feet the only sound breaking the oppressive silence. My eyes scanned the horizon, searching for any sign of the Great Watcher, its presence a constant, nagging fear that haunted my every step.

I had established a routine to sustain myself on this desolate journey: eat every sixth hour, sleep for six hours, and walk for the remaining eighteen. It was a fragile balance, one that I struggled to maintain. The monotony of the landscape, the crushing weight of the unknown ahead, and the ever-present threat of danger all conspired against me. Yet, I pressed on, driven by a stubborn determination to reach the Pyramid.

As the hours ticked by, my resolve began to waver. The cold, the hunger, and the fatigue conspired against me, making every step feel like a Herculean task. I had been walking for twenty-one hours, and the weight of my pack, the ache in my legs, and the dryness in my throat seemed to grow with each passing moment. The thought of rest, of finding a place to hide and recover, became an all-consuming obsession.

In the distance, I spotted a faint glow – a fire-hole, a beacon of warmth in the frozen vastness of the Night Land. My heart quickened, and I hastened my pace, my footsteps lightening as hope crept in. The fire-hole grew larger, its warm light illuminating the dark, rocky terrain. I approached cautiously, my senses on high alert, and found myself at the edge of a small, bubbling pit. A faint smell of sulfur wafted up, mingling with the acrid scent of smoke.

I settled down, my back against a nearby rock, the Diskos cradled in my lap. For a while, I simply sat, letting the warmth of the fire seep into my bones. The weariness, the hunger, and the thirst still lingered, but for a moment, they receded, replaced by a sense of calm. I gazed back at the Pyramid, its imposing presence a reminder of the journey ahead. A mix of emotions swirled within me – a sense of trepidation, of longing, and of duty. I knew that countless eyes watched me from afar
