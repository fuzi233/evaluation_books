As Caroline stirred, her eyelids fluttered open, and she gazed up at her mother with a radiant smile. "Mamma, I've slept so well," she whispered, her voice barely audible over the hum of the cicadas outside. "I only dreamed and woke twice." Mrs. Pryor's heart swelled with joy at the sound of her daughter's voice, and she hastily composed herself, not wanting Caroline to notice the tears that pricked at the corners of her eyes.

One moment, the flame of life would flicker brightly, only to dwindle and threaten to extinguish altogether. Mrs. Pryor's anxiety was a constant companion, always lurking just beneath the surface, ready to pounce at the slightest sign of setback.

Mr. Hall and Louis Moore were trekking through the Lake District, their footsteps echoing off the mountainsides as they explored the rugged beauty of the north.
