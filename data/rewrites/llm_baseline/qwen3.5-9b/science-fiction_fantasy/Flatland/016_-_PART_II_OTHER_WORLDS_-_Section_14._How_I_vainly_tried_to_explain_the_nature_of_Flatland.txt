Deciding it was high time to drag the Monarch down from his ecstatic reverie to the plane of common sense, I resolved to offer him glimpses of the truth—that is, the true nature of our Flatland universe. Thus, I began:

"How does Your Royal Highness distinguish the shapes and positions of His subjects? By my own observation, prior to entering Your Kingdom, I noted via the sense of sight that some of Your people are Lines while others are Points, and that certain Lines vary greatly in magnitude—"

"You speak of an impossibility!" the King interrupted sharply. "You must have witnessed a hallucination. To differentiate between a Line and a Point using the sense of sight is, by the laws of nature, utterly impossible. However, it can be precisely detected through the sense of hearing, and by this same method, my exact shape may be ascertained. Behold—I am a Line, the longest in Lineland, spanning over six inches of Space."

"Of *Length*," I ventured to clarify.

"Fool!" he snapped. "Space *is* Length. Interrupt me again, and I shall cease speaking."

Apologizing hastily, I pressed on. Since he was impervious to logic, I decided to demonstrate his auditory superiority directly. "Listen well, Sire. Through my dual voices, I reveal my shape to my Wives, who are currently located six thousand miles, seventy yards, two feet, and eight inches away—one to the North, the other to the South. Hear me now."

He emitted a sharp chirp, then continued with smug satisfaction: "My Wives, receiving the sound of my first voice closely followed by the second, perceive that the latter arrives after an interval during which sound traverses 6.457 inches. From this delay, they infer that one of my mouths lies 6.457 inches farther from them than the other, thereby deducing my total length. Naturally, they do not perform this calculation anew with every greeting; they established it once, before our marriage. Yet, they possess the capacity to do so at any moment. Similarly, I can determine the shape of any male subject solely through sound."

"But what if," I asked, "a Man feigns a Woman's voice with one of his tones, or disguises his southern vocalization so thoroughly that it cannot be identified as an echo of his northern tone? Might such deceptions not cause grave inconvenience? Furthermore, have you no means of detecting fraud by commanding neighboring subjects to physically touch one another?"

This was, of course, a foolish query, for tactile sensation offered no solution here; yet I posed it deliberately to irritate the Monarch, succeeding beyond my hopes.

"What!" he shrieked in horror. "Explain your meaning!"

"Feel, touch, come into contact," I replied.

"If by 'FEELING' you mean approaching so closely that no space remains between two individuals, know, Stranger, that this offense is punishable by death within my dominions. The reasoning is obvious: the fragile form of a Woman is liable to shatter under such extreme approximation and must be protected by the State. Since Women cannot be distinguished from Men by sight alone, the Law universally decrees that neither Man nor Woman shall approach another so closely as to obliterate the interval between them."

"And indeed," the King scoffed, "what conceivable purpose could be served by this illegal and unnatural excess of approximation, which you term 'touching,' when all the ends of such a brutal and crude process are achieved instantly and with greater precision through the sense of hearing? Regarding your alleged danger of deception, it is nonexistent. The Voice is the very essence of one's Being; it cannot be altered at will. Come, suppose I possessed the power to pass through solid objects, penetrating my subjects one by one—even numbering a billion—to verify their size and distance via the sense of 'feeling.' How much time and energy would be squandered on this clumsy, inaccurate method! Wherefore, in a single moment of audition, I conduct the census and gather statistics—local, corporeal, mental, and spiritual—of every living soul in Lineland. Hark! Only hark!"

So saying, he paused, listening in a state of ecstasy to a sound that appeared to me no better than the tiny chirping of countless Lilliputian grasshoppers.

"Truly," I admitted, "Your sense of hearing serves you well, compensating for many deficiencies. Yet, permit me to observe that life in Lineland must be deplorably dull. To see nothing but a Point! Not even to contemplate a Straight Line! Nay, not even to comprehend what a Straight Line *is*! It would be preferable to possess no sense of sight at all than to see so little! I grant you I lack your discriminative faculty of hearing; for the grand concert of Lineland, which brings you such intense joy, sounds to me merely like a multitudinous twittering or chirping. But at least I can discern, by sight, a Line from a Point. Let me prove it. Just before entering Your Kingdom, I observed You dancing from left to right, and then from right to left, accompanied immediately on Your left by seven Men and a Woman, and on Your right by eight Men and two women. Is this accurate?"

"It is correct," the King conceded, "insofar as the numbers and sexes are concerned, though I confess I do not grasp what You mean by 'right' and 'left.' But I deny that You saw these things. How could You possibly see the Line—that is, the interior—of any Man? You must have heard these details and subsequently dreamt that You saw them. Moreover, pray tell, what do You mean by those words 'left' and 'right'? I presume it is Your dialectic equivalent for 'Northward' and 'Southward.'"

"Not so," I replied. "Besides Your motion of Northward and Southward, there exists another motion which I call movement from right to left."

"KING: Exhibit to me, if You please, this motion from left to right."

"I: Nay, I cannot do so unless You could step entirely out of Your Line."

"KING: Out of my Line? Do You mean out of the world? Out of Space itself?"

"I: Well, yes. Out of *Your* World. Out of *Your* Space. For Your Space is not the true Space. True Space is a Plane; but Your Space is merely a Line."

"If You cannot indicate this motion from left to right by moving within it Yourself, I beseech You to describe it to me in words."

"I: If You cannot distinguish Your right side from Your left, I fear no words of mine can render my meaning clear to You. Surely, however, You cannot be ignorant of such a simple distinction."

"KING: I do not in the least understand You."

"I: Alas! How shall I make it clear? When You move straight ahead, does it not occasionally occur to You that You *could* move in some other manner, turning Your eye around to gaze in the direction toward which Your side is currently facing? In other words, instead of perpetually moving toward one of Your extremities, do You never feel a desire to move in the direction, so to speak, of Your side?"

"KING: Never. And what do You mean? How can a man's inside 'face' in any direction? Or how can a man move in the direction of his inside?"

"Well then, since words fail to explain the matter, I shall resort to deed. I will gradually move my body out of Lineland in the direction I wish to indicate."

At my command, I began to withdraw my form from Lineland. As long as any portion of me remained within His dominion and visible to Him, the King exclaimed, "I see You! I see You still! You are not moving!" But once I had finally withdrawn completely from His Line, he cried out in his shrillest voice, "She has vanished! She is dead!"

"I am not dead," I replied calmly. "I am simply out of Lineland—that is, out of the Straight Line which You call Space—and situated in the true Space, wherein I can perceive things as they truly are. At this very moment, I can see Your Line, or rather, Your side—or whatever internal aspect You choose to call it. I can also see the Men and Women positioned to the North and South of You, whom I shall now enumerate, detailing their order, their dimensions, and the intervals separating each."
