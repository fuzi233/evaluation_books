As the last wisps of smoke dissipated from the hearth, Hetty wandered aimlessly through the empty rooms of the Lambert residence, her footsteps echoing off the walls. The absence of Mr. Harry weighed heavily upon her, and she couldn't help but imagine the dangers he faced, the cannonballs whizzing through the air, the cries of the wounded. She felt a pang in her chest, a knot of anxiety that refused to untangle itself. Her fingers instinctively reached for the worn wooden rosary hidden beneath her apron, a token from her father, a reminder of the prayers she whispered in secret.

Gumbo, the family's loyal servant, sensed her distress and drew near, his dark eyes filled with a deep understanding. Hetty's heart swelled with gratitude for the gentle giant, who had become a surrogate brother to her. She smiled weakly, and Gumbo, sensing her need for comfort, wrapped a massive arm around her shoulders, holding her close as they stood together in the quiet, flickering light of the evening. His presence was a balm to her frazzled nerves, a reminder that even in the midst of uncertainty, there was still a world of simple pleasures to be savored.

In the kitchen, the scent of roasting meat wafted through the air, mingling with the sweet aroma of freshly baked bread. Hetty's stomach growled in response, a reminder that even in times of worry, the body demanded sustenance. She smiled wryly, knowing that Gumbo would not be deterred from his favorite pastime, even in the face of her distress. As she watched, he carefully arranged a plate of cold meat and bread, his movements economical and precise, a testament to his years of service.

The room fell silent once more, the only sound the soft ticking of the clock on the mantle. Hetty's thoughts turned to her father, who had been away for so long, fighting in the war. She imagined him, brave and strong, facing danger with a steadfast heart. Her eyes drifted to the window, where the stars twinkled like diamonds in the night sky. She felt a shiver run down her spine as she wondered if he was thinking of her, if he knew she was worried sick.
