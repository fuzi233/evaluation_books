As I watched, a cloud obscured the moon, casting the scene in an eerie twilight. The woman didn't notice me, her attention fixed on some unseen threat ahead. I realized she was being chased, and my instincts told me to intervene. I stepped aside to intercept the pursuer, but before I could act, a swift and silent creature darted past me, striking me on the forehead with a glancing blow. I stumbled, but regained my footing in an instant. The attacker vanished into the darkness, leaving me with a throbbing wound.

A few strides later, I spotted a creature stumbling towards me, its white coat smeared with blood. The creature's gait was labored, its paws sinking into the damp earth with each faltering step. I watched, transfixed, as it passed by, its wounded paw leaving a trail of crimson behind.

A pang of compassion washed over me, and I felt a reluctance to harm the creature further. If it continued to bleed like that, it wouldn't survive for much longer. I decided to continue on my way, hoping to be of use to the woman and her child, and perhaps catch a glimpse of their protector.
