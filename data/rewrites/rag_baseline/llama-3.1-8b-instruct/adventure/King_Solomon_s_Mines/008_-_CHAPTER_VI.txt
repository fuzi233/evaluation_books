The unforgiving sun had surrendered to the vast expanse of the African sky, casting a deep indigo hue over the desolate landscape. In this desolate wilderness, where the air clung to skin like a damp shroud, Quatermain's parched throat constricted with each labored breath. He lay still, his gritty eyes straining towards the horizon, the weight of his thirst like a physical burden. His dreams, a fleeting respite from the unforgiving environment, had been of a tranquil oasis – a crystal-clear stream bountifully supplied with lush greenery and verdant trees. Waking, however, found him face to face with the unforgiving vastness of the savannah, the merciless sun beating down upon his weathered skin like a mallet. 

He rose from his makeshift bed of dusty grasses, his cracked lips barely parting as he attempted to speak. A faint tremble ran through his limbs as he rubbed the grit from his eyes with his calloused palms. The dry, cracked earth seemed to swallow the faint light of dawn, leaving behind an oppressive atmosphere that stifled even the most hopeful of souls. The others remained fast asleep, oblivious to the desperation that gripped Quatermain's heart. His thoughts careened wildly, fixated on the promise of liquid solace, as he recalled the words of Umbopa: "If we do not find water today, we shall perish miserably." No human being could survive for long in this furnace-like environment without it. He rose to his feet, surveying the desolate expanse with a mixture of hope and despair.

Quatermain rummaged through his worn leather pouch and produced a dog-eared pocket edition of the Ingoldsby Legends. The yellowed pages, worn smooth by countless readings, offered a brief respite from the torment that ravaged his body. He began to read, the words transporting him to a distant land where a cardinal poured water from a golden ewer into a waiting basin. The mere thought sent a shiver of longing through his parched frame. His mind conjured vivid images of the cardinal, the boy, and the majestic jackdaw, their faces a picture of serene tranquility as they savored the cool liquid. His dry lips yearned to feel the gentle trickle of water against them once more. He laughed aloud at the absurdity of the scene, the sound jarring the stillness of the air, and rousing the others from their slumber.
