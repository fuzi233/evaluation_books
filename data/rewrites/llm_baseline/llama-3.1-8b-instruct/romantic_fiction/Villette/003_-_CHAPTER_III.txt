The fire crackled softly, casting a warm glow across the room as Mr. Home settled into his chair beside Mrs. Bretton. For two days, he had been a reluctant guest, preferring to stay indoors, lost in his own thoughts. Mrs. Bretton's conversation was a gentle accompaniment to his reverie, neither too sympathetic nor too unsympathetic, with just a hint of warmth that only came from being slightly older and wiser.

Paulina, meanwhile, was a whirlwind of activity, content in her own little world. Her father would lift her onto his lap, and she would sit there, absorbed in the intricate patterns of his waistcoat, until she sensed his restlessness. Then, with a gentle smile, she would slide to the floor, and the game of sewing and snipping would begin anew. The scarlet-speckled handkerchief was a special project, one that Mr. Home had commissioned for himself, and Paulina was determined to finish it before he left.

As the evening drew in, Graham returned from school, bringing with him a burst of energy and a mischievous glint in his eye. His interactions with Paulina were always a highlight of the evening, and tonight was no exception. Their earlier encounter had ended in a frosty silence, with Paulina retreating behind a mask of haughtiness. But now, as they settled in together, Graham began to try and thaw her reserve.

He pulled open his desk drawer, revealing a treasure trove of curious objects: seals, wax sticks, penknives, and a medley of colorful engravings. Paulina's eyes flickered towards the display, drawn in by the vibrant colors and intriguing shapes. An etching of a child playing with a Blenheim spaniel caught her eye, and she let out a delighted gasp.

"Pretty little dog!" she exclaimed.

Graham remained impassive, waiting for her to make the next move. And move she did, slipping away from her seat to examine the etching more closely. The dog's big eyes and floppy ears, the child's hat and feathers – everything captivated her. "Nice picture," she said, her voice barely above a whisper.

Graham's response was immediate. "You may have it," he said, his voice low and smooth.
