Gerard's fingers instinctively closed around the steel bow slung over his back as he struggled to free himself from the current's deadly grip. The same bow that had nearly been his undoing now lay within his grasp, its wooden limbs creaking beneath his fingers. With a swift motion, he yanked the bow free, its cord springing taut as he grasped it firmly. Denys, buoyed aloft by the force of the crossbow, hung suspended in the water, his legs thrashing wildly.

"Wait, don't pull!" Gerard cried, horror etched on his face.

Denys's response was garbled, but Gerard's gaze never wavered from the task at hand. He seized the bow, took a deep breath, and plunged forward, using the momentum to aid their ascent. "Put your hand on my shoulder, slap the water with the other!" he bellowed, his voice carrying above the roar of the river. As Denys's long hair slipped through his fingers, Gerard swiftly twisted it around his teeth, his neck muscles straining to hold fast. With a mighty stroke, he propelled himself and Denys toward the western shore.

For a moment, Gerard hesitated, unsure which direction to aim for. Then, spotting the bank mere yards away, he veered toward it, choosing the path of least resistance. The current buffeted them about, sending them careening off course, but Gerard held firm, his grip unyielding. As they covered nearly a hundred yards, Denys's labored breathing grew more ragged, and he muttered anxiously, "How much longer?"

"Courage, Denys," Gerard reassured him, his voice low and soothing. "We'll make it. You're safer than you think."

The next instant, they found themselves in shallow water, and with a collective sigh, they stumbled onto dry land. There, they stood panting, their eyes locked in a wordless understanding. In that fleeting moment, life seemed sweeter than any feast, more precious than the finest delicacy. For Gerard, the thrill of saving his friend had awakened a deep affection, a feeling he couldn't quite explain. Together, they wrapped their arms around each other's necks, their hearts pounding in perfect harmony.
