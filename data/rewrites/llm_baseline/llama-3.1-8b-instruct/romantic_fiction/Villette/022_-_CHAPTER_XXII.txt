The lamp in the study cast a warm, golden glow, beckoning me to surrender to its tranquil ambiance. With the external distractions silenced, I ventured to the kitchen, where Goton, our kindly housekeeper, offered me two candles for a half-hour, her voice soft and soothing as she said, "Bien sûr, chère, take two if you wish." I ascended to the dormitory, my footsteps muffled by the thick carpet, only to find myself confronted with an unexpected presence – Mistress Ginevra Fanshawe, reclining under the muslin folds of her nightcap, her eyes closed in feigned slumber. Yet, I sensed a subtle flicker in her eyelids, betraying her watchful vigilance, a ruse employed to conceal her keen interest in the comings and goings of Timon. Her unwavering attention unnerved me, as I had hoped to savor my letter in peace.

Undeterred, I continued my search for seclusion, descending to the classrooms. However, my quest was thwarted by the weekly ritual of sweeping and purification, which had transformed the rooms into a dusty, disarrayed space. Benches lay stacked upon desks, the air heavy with the acrid scent of coffee grounds, used by the Labassecourien housemaids in place of tea leaves. Defeated but unyielding, I retreated, resolved to discover a haven of solitude elsewhere.

This sanctuary, hidden from prying eyes, offered me a refuge from the world.
