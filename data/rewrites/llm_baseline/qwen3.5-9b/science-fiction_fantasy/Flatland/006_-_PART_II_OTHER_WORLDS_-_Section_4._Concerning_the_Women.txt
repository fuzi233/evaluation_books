If our sharply pointed Triangles of the Soldier class are formidable, it requires little inference to deduce that our Women are far more dangerous. While a Soldier is merely a wedge, a Woman is a needle—essentially pure point at both extremities. Combine this lethal geometry with the ability to render oneself practically invisible at will, and one realizes that a Female in Flatland is a force never to be trifled with.

Perhaps my younger readers in Spaceland might wonder *how* a two-dimensional being achieves invisibility. The answer lies in perspective, requiring no complex machinery. Consider a needle placed on a table. Viewed from the side, its full length is visible; viewed from the tip, it appears as nothing but a tiny dot, effectively vanishing. So too with our Women. When their broad side faces us, we perceive a straight line—a distinct threat. When the end bearing their eye or mouth (which, in our dimension, serve as a single organ) turns toward us, we see only a brilliant, lustrous point, rendering them nearly invisible. Conversely, when their back is presented, appearing dull and dimmer than an inanimate object, their rear extremity functions as a natural "Invisible Cap."

The peril posed by such entities should be obvious to anyone of average intellect in Spaceland. If the obtuse angle of a respectable middle-class Triangle can cause injury; if colliding with a Working Man results in a deep gash; if striking an Officer inflicts a grievous wound; and if a mere brush with the vertex of a Private Soldier risks death—then running into a Woman guarantees absolute and immediate destruction. Furthermore, given their tendency to vanish into visibility, avoiding collision becomes a near-impossible feat for even the most cautious citizen.

Consequently, various states across Flatland have enacted strict laws to mitigate this existential hazard. These statutes are particularly rigorous in southern regions where gravity is stronger, causing inhabitants to move more erratically. The core of our Female Code generally includes three pillars:

1.  **Restricted Entry:** Every dwelling must feature a dedicated eastern entrance used exclusively by Females. They must approach "in a becoming and respectful manner," strictly forbidden from using the western door reserved for Males.
2.  **The Peace-Cry:** No Female may traverse any public space without continuously emitting a specific auditory signal—the "Peace-cry"—under penalty of death.
3.  **Elimination of Volatility:** Any Female certified to suffer from conditions causing involuntary motion—such as St. Vitus's Dance, epileptic fits, or severe allergic reactions—is to be instantly destroyed. Some states go further, mandating that women constantly sway their backs right-to-left to ensure visibility, or require them to be escorted by a son, servant, or husband. Others confine women entirely within their homes except during religious festivals.

However, the wisest legislators have recognized that excessive restriction breeds disaster. Over-confinement exasperates the female temperament, leading to domestic violence that often decimates entire villages in moments of collective outrage. Thus, the three laws above remain sufficient for stable societies. Ultimately, our greatest safeguard is not legislation, but self-preservation. A Woman can kill instantly with a backward thrust, but unless she can withdraw her stinging tip before the struggle ends, her own fragile form shatters.

Moreover, the power of fashion aids us. In civilized states, rhythmic back-swaying is considered a mark of breeding, a natural instinct rather than a legal requirement. Ladies of high rank perform a modulated undulation of the spine, envied and emulated by wives of lesser geometric shapes—from the simple pendulum-like swing of the Equilateral to the aspirational movements of the Isosceles. Consequently, in households of status, this "back motion" is ubiquitous, granting husbands and sons immunity from invisible attacks.

Do not mistake this for a lack of affection. Unfortunately, in the Frail Sex, the passion of the moment overrides reason. Deprived of angles, they possess no brain-power, lacking reflection, judgment, foresight, or memory. In a fit of fury, they recognize no distinctions or past grievances. I have witnessed a woman exterminate her entire household, only to ask thirty minutes later, after her rage had subsided, where her husband and children had gone. Therefore, a Woman must never be provoked while able to turn around. Once confined to apartments designed to deny her rotational freedom, she becomes harmless; unable to strike, she forgets threats and broken promises within minutes.

Domestic life proceeds relatively smoothly, save for the lower military classes. Here, husbands' reliance on their sharp angles rather than diplomacy leads to catastrophe. Lacking the tact to retreat or the skill to offer lavish, soothing promises, they provoke their wives with bluntness outside the home and fail to retract their words immediately. The result is massacre—an outcome some Circles view providentially as a method of culling redundant populations and stifling revolution.

Even in our most harmonious, circular families, life lacks the depth found in Spaceland. Safety comes at the cost of comfort. Harmony of taste is rare. An ancient instinct dictates that mothers and daughters must constantly face their husbands and male guests; for a lady of distinction to turn her back is deemed a portent of lost status. While working-class wives enjoy brief periods of silence while facing inward toward their chores, the homes of the elite are perpetually noisy. With eyes and mouths fixed eternally on the master, the stream of feminine discourse is relentless. The skills required to dodge a physical sting are useless against a verbal barrage. Since a Woman has nothing meaningful to say and no moral constraint to stop her, many cynical men prefer the silent danger of the invisible sting to the deafening noise of her open end.

To the citizens of Spaceland, the plight of our Women seems tragic, and rightly so. While a lowly Isosceles male might hope to improve his angle and elevate his caste, no Woman harbors such aspirations. "Once a Woman, always a Woman" is a decree of nature, seemingly suspending the laws of evolution against her. Yet, we find solace in this prearranged design: having no hopes for the future, they possess no memory to recall past humiliations and no foresight to anticipate new ones. Their misery is total, but so is their oblivion.
