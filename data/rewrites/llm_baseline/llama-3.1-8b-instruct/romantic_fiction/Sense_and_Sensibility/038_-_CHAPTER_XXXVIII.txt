As the warmth of the sun on a crisp late winter's day drew many to Kensington Gardens, Mrs. Jennings and Elinor strolled through the gates, taking in the vibrant colors of blooming flowers and the gentle rustle of leaves. It was a tranquil scene, one that allowed Elinor to indulge in quiet reflection, free from the distractions of their companion's lively chatter. An acquaintance of Mrs. Jennings soon joined them, and Elinor welcomed the reprieve, grateful for the opportunity to lose herself in thought.

Meanwhile, Marianne had chosen to remain at home, preferring the solitude of their quiet household to the bustling crowds of the gardens. She couldn't shake the feeling of unease that settled in the pit of her stomach at the prospect of encountering the Willoughbys, whose presence seemed to darken every pleasant occasion. As she went about her daily routine, her mind wandered to the events that had transpired in London, and the choices that had led her to this point.

Mrs. Jennings, ever the socialite, had been busy spreading the news of Edward's conduct, but it was clear that only Elinor and Marianne truly understood the gravity of his actions. Elinor took pride in his integrity, and Marianne, though struggling to come to terms with her own role in the situation, couldn't help but feel a sense of compassion for the difficulties he faced. Though their bond remained strong, the subject was one that neither sister cared to dwell on, especially when alone.

As Elinor walked alongside Mrs. Jennings, lost in thought, she noticed a figure approaching – Miss Steele, looking a bit hesitant, yet eager to reconnect. With a warm smile, Miss Steele grasped Elinor's arm, and began to speak in hushed tones. "I'm so glad to see you both," she said, her eyes darting nervously towards Mrs. Jennings. "I've been wanting to speak with you about something, but I wasn't sure if I should."

Mrs. Jennings, sensing an opportunity, leaned in close, her voice barely above a whisper. "Get it all out of her, my dear. She'll tell you anything if you ask." Elinor smiled knowingly, understanding the unspoken request. As Miss Steele continued to speak, it became clear that she had a tale to share, one that would likely prove enlightening, if not a bit scandalous.
