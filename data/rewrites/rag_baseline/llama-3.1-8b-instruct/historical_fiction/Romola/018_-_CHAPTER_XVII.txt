Under the Loggia.

The cypress-shaded loggia at the top of Bardo's house rose majestically above the terracotta-tiled rooftops of Florence, its quadrangular walls forming a serene gallery that seemed to capture the essence of the Tuscan countryside. As the sun began to set, casting a warm orange glow over the scene, Romola strolled along the narrow walkway, her footsteps echoing off the stone floor. The air was alive with the scent of freshly baked bread wafting from the nearby bakery, and the distant chirping of cicadas provided a soothing background melody.

Near one of the arched openings, Tito waited for her, his eyes fixed intently on the spot where she would appear. He had spent countless hours preparing for this moment, rehearsing the words he would say, the gestures he would make, and the look he would give her. Yet, as he watched her approach, he felt a knot form in his stomach. He had never been sure if his secret would ultimately drive Romola away, or if her love for him would be strong enough to overcome her doubts.

As she drew closer, Tito's anxiety gave way to a sense of wonder. Her long, dark hair cascaded down her back like a waterfall of night, and her hazel eyes sparkled with a warmth that seemed to light up the entire space. He felt his heart swell with emotion as he took in the gentle curve of her neck, the delicate shape of her ears, and the soft, full lips that smiled at him.

Romola, too, was lost in the moment. She had spent the morning wrestling with the weight of her family's expectations, and the secrets that had been revealed to her. But as she gazed into Tito's eyes, she felt a deep sense of peace wash over her. It was as if the world had slowed down, and all that mattered was the two of them, suspended in this perfect, golden light.

"My Romola," Tito whispered, his voice barely audible over the pounding of his heart. "My goddess." He reached out and gently brushed a strand of hair behind her ear, his fingers grazing the delicate skin of her neck. The touch sent shivers down her spine, and she leaned into him, her face tilted upwards in invitation.

As they stood there, wrapped in the silence of the loggia, the world outside seemed to fade away. The sounds of the city, the rustling of leaves, the distant hum of conversation –
