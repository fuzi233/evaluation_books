The air was heavy with the scent of blooming wildflowers as I labored, the rhythmic hum of bees weaving in and out of the tiny trees that stood sentinel between the larger trunks. I'd only been working for a brief span of time when I became aware of the presence of the Little Ones, their soft voices carried on the breeze. Before long, a throng of them emerged from the verdant undergrowth, their laughter like a chorus of tinkling wind chimes.

They approached me cautiously, their large eyes scanning the space around me as if searching for any sign of the giants. I gestured to them, trying to convey that my masters had merely departed, but they merely giggled and assured me the wind was the only witness to our gathering.

One of them asked if I liked the rope bound around my ankles, and I replied that I wanted the giants to believe it was still secure. He suggested I walk with short, careful steps to maintain the illusion, and his infectious laughter sent a wave of merriment through the group. As they played and capered around me, one of the larger girls knelt to untie the knot, her slender fingers deftly working the rope free.

Once liberated, they offered me an assortment of delectable fruits, which they carefully selected from the nearby bushes. After our snack, the smallest among them began to frolic with me, their antics sending me into peals of laughter. As the first child grew weary, others took their place, and the games continued until the sun began its slow descent towards the horizon. It was then that the sound of approaching footsteps echoed through the clearing, and the Little Ones swiftly dispersed, vanishing into the underbrush.

Their parting words, spoken by the girl who had freed me, sent a shiver down my spine: "We must be cautious; a careless step from one of the giants could prove disastrous for one of us." I hastened to rebind the rope around my ankles as the girl herself melted into the shadows, leaving behind only the faintest hint of her presence.

My master appeared soon after, his massive form looming over me as he released my ankles and dragged me towards the entrance of his hut. He discarded me on the ground, retied my feet, and delivered a harsh kick before departing.
