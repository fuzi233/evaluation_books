As Deronda stepped into the warm, golden light of the Friday evening, the closed shop came alive. The Christian servant, whose name was Emma, greeted him with a gentle smile and ushered him into the cozy room behind the shop. The space was a haven of tranquility, illuminated by a majestic brass lamp with seven oil-lights that cast a soft, ethereal glow on the snowy-white cloth spread across the central table. The air was thick with the scent of old wood and the faint hint of cooking spices wafting from the adjacent kitchen.

Mrs. Cohen, resplendent in a rich red dress with black trimmings, sat regally at the table, her dark hair styled in intricate braids that cascaded down her back. Her son Jacob, a miniature version of his father, perched on her lap, his bright black eyes shining with excitement as he listened to the conversation. The grandmother, a statuesque woman with a commanding presence, wore a warm, honey-brown dress adorned with a delicate gold chain around her neck. Her face, etched with deep lines and creases, seemed to radiate a quiet strength and wisdom.

The room was a testament to the family's heritage, with its old, dark wood furniture and ornate silverware. On the side-table, a beautiful blue and yellow ceramic dish sat proudly alongside two ancient silver vessels, their intricate designs glinting in the soft light. A large, leather-bound book lay open on the table, its pages turned to a passage marked by a ribbon. As Deronda's eyes roved over the scene, he felt a pang of wonder at the warmth and love that filled this humble home.

The grandmother's eyes sparkled as she watched Deronda engage with Jacob, who was eager to finalize the purchase of a new knife. Deronda produced a hook-handled knife, which Jacob examined carefully before handing over a cork-screw in exchange. As they haggled, the grandmother listened intently, her face aglow with pleasure.

Deronda asked Jacob why he preferred a hook to a cork-screw, and the boy explained that it was because a hook could grasp various objects, while a cork-screw was limited to only one use. The grandmother nodded approvingly, her dark eyes twinkling with amusement. As Deronda continued to chat with the family, he felt a sense of ease and belonging that he hadn't experienced before.
