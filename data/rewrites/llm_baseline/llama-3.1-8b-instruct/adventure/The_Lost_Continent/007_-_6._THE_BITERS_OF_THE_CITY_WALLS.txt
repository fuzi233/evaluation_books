As I stepped into the grand hall of the palace, the weight of my return to Atlantis settled upon me like a shroud. The city's new queen, Phorenice, had already made her presence known, and the air was thick with the scent of her ambition. Her self-made ascent to power had created a rift between the traditionalists and those who followed her rule, leaving me, a simple and honest man, torn between my loyalty to the crown and my duty to the Clan of the Priests.

Before, serving the king had been synonymous with serving the priests, whose influence had been paramount. But Phorenice was a force unto herself, and the Clan's disdain for her was palpable. I knew I had to tread carefully, weighing the merits of each faction before making a choice. The whispers of rebels and their claims against the current regime only added to the complexity of the situation. It was time to learn more about this alternative path, to hear the voices of those who dared challenge Phorenice's rule.

No one stopped me as I departed the banquet hall, the gruesome spectacle of Tarca's suffering still etched in my mind. The room had fallen silent after his display, and even the most voracious diners had lost their appetite. Phorenice's gaze had lingered on me, a mixture of anger and curiosity burning in her eyes. "You may be an honest man, Deucalion," she said, her voice laced with a hint of warmth, "but you are a cold one indeed. I wonder when you will thaw." She smiled, a fleeting glimmer of amusement, before turning to leave, accompanied by Ylga.

As the queen departed, I approached Tarca, placing my hand on his afflicted flesh. "You are a brave soldier, despite your misfortune," I said, my voice low and sincere. His gratitude was barely audible, and some nearby courtiers recoiled in distaste. I shot them a stern look, my expression unyielding. "Your charity seems as shallow as your courage, my lords. You condemned Zaemon's words and mocked his faith, but when faced with his plight, you reveal a discretion that is nothing short of cowardice. I would rather be Tarca, wretched as he is, than any of you, with all your pretensions."
