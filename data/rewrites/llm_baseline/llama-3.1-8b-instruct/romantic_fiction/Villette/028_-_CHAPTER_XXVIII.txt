The corridors of the pensionnat hummed with the gentle murmur of lessons being conducted in various classrooms, each with its own distinct cadence. In the carré, I sat engrossed in embroidering a piece of intricate work, my fingers moving deftly as I listened to the rising crescendo of a nearby voice. M. Paul Emanuel's tone grew increasingly agitated, and I couldn't help but feel a thrill of amusement at the prospect of witnessing another dramatic encounter with the intrepid portress, Rosine.

As she navigated the treacherous waters of the school's inner sanctum, she had already braved the wrath of M. Paul four times that morning, and it seemed she was about to face her greatest challenge yet. Her whispered cry of "Mon Dieu!" echoed down the corridor as she grasped the door handle, her hand trembling with anticipation.

"Mademoiselle La Malle au piano!" she called out, her voice barely audible over the din of the classroom. But before she could retreat, M. Paul's voice boomed out, his words dripping with menace: "Dès ce moment!—la classe est défendue. La première qui ouvrira cette porte, ou passera par cette division, sera pendue—fut-ce Madame Beck elle-même!"

Ten minutes passed, and then Rosine's soft pantoufles padded back into the corridor, accompanied by a whispered plea for assistance. "Mademoiselle, I wouldn't dare venture into that classroom again today – Monsieur's gaze is simply terrifying. And to make matters worse, a commissionaire has arrived with a message from the Athénée. I've told Madame Beck I'm too afraid, and she's asked me to entrust it to you instead."

I hesitated, feeling a shiver run down my spine as I contemplated the prospect of facing M. Paul's ire. "Impossible, Rosine," I said finally, trying to sound braver than I felt. "You're only a coward. What's the message?"

Rosine's eyes darted nervously towards the classroom door before responding in a hushed tone, "It's an official summons for Monsieur to attend the Athénée immediately, as there's an inspector or some such person waiting for him.
