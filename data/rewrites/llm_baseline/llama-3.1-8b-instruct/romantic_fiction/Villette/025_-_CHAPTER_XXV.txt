The evening air outside was alive with the mournful howl of winter, a haunting melody that pierced the windows of La Terrasse like a mournful sigh. It was a sound that often stirred the imaginations of those warm and snug by the fire, transporting them to the desolate landscapes beyond their sheltered walls. Women and girls, wrapped in their cozy firesides, might find themselves torn away from the comforting warmth, compelled to brave the tempests, to wait at lonely gates and stiles, listening for the sound of footsteps, the beat of hooves on the frozen earth.

As if summoned by the symphony of the storm, Father and son arrived at the château, accompanied by the Count de Bassompierre. Dr. Bretton, I recall, was the first to notice the sound of their approach; the ferocity of the wind warranted our hastening to the entrance hall, where we would greet them as they dismounted. However, they cautioned us to maintain a safe distance, their faces as white as the snow itself. Mrs. Bretton, observing their condition, ordered them straightaway to the kitchen, instructing them not to set foot on her carpeted stairs until they had shed their wintry attire. Though we were unable to resist following them into the kitchen, we soon found ourselves in a spacious, old Dutch kitchen, its rustic charm and warmth a welcome respite from the biting cold outside.

There, amidst the kitchen's warmth, the little Countess danced around her father, her laughter like the tinkling of a silver bell as she exclaimed, "Papa, papa, you resemble an enormous polar bear!" The bear, or rather the man beneath the bear-like disguise, shook himself, sending a cascade of snowflakes swirling about him, prompting the little sprite to leap away, her eyes sparkling with mirth. Undeterred, she returned, her hands extended, eager to assist in stripping away the frozen cloak. The Count, in a playful threat, brandished the snow-shroud above her head, only to have her bound away from it, her movements fluid as a chamois leaping across the mountainous terrain.

Her laughter was a melodic blend of silver and crystal, a sound that seemed to emanate from within, as if her very soul was singing. As she took her father's chilled hands and rubbed them gently, standing on tiptoe to reach his lips for a kiss, a radiant aura of affection surrounded her, illuminating the space around her.
