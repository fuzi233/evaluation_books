The Day of the Betrothal.

Florence was alive with the vibrant rhythms of Carnival, the last week's festivities reaching a crescendo of color and sound. In the midst of this whirlwind, Tito Melema navigated the crowded streets with a sense of purpose, his mind focused on the evening's events. Beside him walked young Bernardo Dovizi, whose piercing gaze would later capture the attention of artists and historians alike. As they strolled along the Por' Santa Maria, their conversation flowed effortlessly, touching on topics both serious and trivial. Yet, amidst the lively exchange, Tito's thoughts lingered on the forthcoming ceremony, his heart filled with a mix of emotions.

The betrothal, previously set for after Easter, had been brought forward due to an unexpected opportunity: Tito's chance to accompany Cardinal Giovanni to Rome and contribute his expertise in arranging a library. This move promised advancement, but it also meant a hasty union with Romola. Tito had grown insistent on securing their bond before leaving, knowing that a public commitment would strengthen his claim on her hand, shielding it from potential obstacles like Messer Bernardo del Nero's influence.

As they turned onto the Lung' Arno, Tito's gaze darted around the crowd, his instincts warning him of someone nearby he would rather not encounter at this moment. His attention was divided between the vibrant spectacle unfolding around him and the quiet anticipation building within. The air was alive with the smell of roasting chestnuts and the distant tang of woodsmoke, carrying the promise of a festive evening ahead.

Their path led them toward the Ponte Rubaconte, where the Arno River flowed gently beneath, its tranquil surface a soothing counterpoint to the tumultuous atmosphere. The sun climbed higher, casting a warm glow over the scene, and the laughter of the crowd grew louder still. It was a day like any other in Florence, yet for Tito, every moment counted – a day that would culminate in a momentous occasion, one that would alter the course of his life forever.
