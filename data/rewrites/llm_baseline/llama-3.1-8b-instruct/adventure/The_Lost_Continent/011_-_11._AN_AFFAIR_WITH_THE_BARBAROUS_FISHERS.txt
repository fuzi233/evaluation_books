As I stood before the Empress, her words cut through the air like a sharp instrument, their meaning clear and unmistakable. My mind, honed by years of navigating treacherous situations, swiftly assessed the situation and devised a plan. I had volunteered to sacrifice myself for Atlantis, as per the High Council of the Priests' decree, and if necessary, Nais would be sacrificed alongside me. Yet, in the blink of an eye, a scheme to save her formed in my mind.

With a deep bow, I assured the Empress, "In all matters where human agency is possible, I will fulfill your desires, Phorenice." Her eyes, like two shimmering pools, sparkled with gratitude, and a wave of emotion rose from their depths. The gods had indeed gifted her with a captivating appearance, a voice that could charm the gods themselves, and a presence that commanded attention. It was no wonder she had won half her battles and half her schemes with such gifts.

As the merchants and harbor dwellers gathered around, offering changes of clothing and shelter, Phorenice radiated kindness. Though I was content with my damp coat, I gladly followed her example, exchanging it for a dry garment. She instructed the port captain to keep a close eye on Nais, her prisoner, before departing. I remained silent, averting my gaze from Nais, and made no attempt to bid her farewell. However, Phorenice was not satisfied with such a meager gesture and ordered the guard to remain vigilant.

Within the merchant's house, I was given a warm bath, and donned a simple garment, pleasing my host with the modest price. As I stepped outside onto the pavement, the distant rumble of cannons grew louder, and I couldn't help but feel a wry amusement at the merchant's crestfallen expression. His store of precious jewels and trinkets, purchased in partnership with other merchants, had caught Phorenice's eye, and she had taken them all, adorning herself with their splendor. "Don't they shine beautifully?" she asked, displaying them to me. "I'm grateful for the mammoth's wild ride and our unexpected tumble, for it has brought me this lovely gift. You may inform the merchant that when he's earned more, I'll return to visit him. Ah, they've brought litters. Send one away, and let us share this one. Today, we'll play the role of lovers, even if true love may come to us both
