As the moon cast its silvery glow over the estate, I stepped out into the gardens, the scent of blooming roses and fresh-cut grass filling my senses. The night had been uneventful, with no sign of the Sergeant's attempts at communication with Miss Rachel bearing fruit. I had expected him to depart for Frizinghall at dawn, but instead, he lingered, his eyes fixed intently on some unknown point.

I continued my stroll, joining Mr. Franklin on his favorite walk along the shrubbery path. The dew-kissed air carried the sweet fragrance of the nearby flowers as we walked in comfortable silence. Suddenly, the Sergeant appeared, his footsteps quiet on the gravel path. Mr. Franklin greeted him curtly, his eyes narrowing slightly as he replied, "Have you anything to say to me?"

The Sergeant approached Mr. Franklin, his expression unreadable. "I have something to share with you, sir," he began, his voice low and measured. "Regarding the investigation I'm conducting here, I believe you're aware of its direction. As a member of this household, you're naturally concerned about the scandal surrounding your family. I'm not here to accuse you, sir."

Mr. Franklin's response was brusque, his tone sharp as he asked, "What do you want?" The Sergeant's eyes never wavered, his gaze fixed on Mr. Franklin's face. "I merely wish to remind you, sir, that I've yet to be proven wrong in my inquiries. Considering my position as an officer of the law, acting under the sanction of the mistress of the house, I believe it's your duty to cooperate with me, should you possess any relevant information."

"I possess no such information," Mr. Franklin replied, his voice firm.

The Sergeant's expression remained impassive, as if he'd expected this response. "You may spare my time, sir, if you choose to be forthcoming with me." Mr. Franklin shook his head, his eyes flashing with annoyance. "I don't understand what you're getting at, and I have nothing to say."

Just then, a figure emerged from the darkness at the end of the walkway – Rosanna Spearman, her features illuminated by the soft moonlight. She was accompanied by Penelope, who tried to guide her back toward the house. Mr. Franklin's gaze followed theirs, his expression unreadable. The Sergeant, however, seemed oblivious to their presence, his attention focused solely on Mr. Franklin.
