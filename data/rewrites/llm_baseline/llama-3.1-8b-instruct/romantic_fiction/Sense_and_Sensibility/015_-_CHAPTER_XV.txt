The scent of freshly baked bread wafted through the air as Mrs. Dashwood prepared for her visit to Lady Middleton, accompanied by two of her daughters. However, Marianne excused herself from joining the party, citing a pressing engagement in the garden. Her mother, assuming that Willoughby had promised to call on them the previous evening, was satisfied with her daughter's decision to stay behind.

As they returned from their afternoon stroll in the park, they spotted Willoughby's curricle parked outside the cottage, accompanied by his servant. Mrs. Dashwood's intuition proved correct – Willoughby was indeed there to pay his respects. Upon entering the house, they were met with a scene that neither of them could have anticipated. Marianne emerged from the parlour, her handkerchief pressed to her eyes, and hastily retreated upstairs without acknowledging their presence. Alarmed and perplexed, they entered the room she had just vacated, where they found Willoughby standing near the fireplace, his back turned towards them.

As they approached, he turned to face them, his expression mirroring the turmoil that seemed to beset Marianne. "Is everything all right?" Mrs. Dashwood asked, her voice laced with concern. "Is she unwell?"

Willoughby attempted to reassure her, forcing a smile onto his lips. "I hope not," he replied, "but I'm the one who may soon find myself indisposed." He paused, collecting his thoughts before continuing, "I've received an unexpected summons from Mrs. Smith, and I must travel to London on her behalf. I've just received my instructions and bid farewell to Allenham; I've come to say goodbye to you both, as well."

"To London? And are you leaving today?" Mrs. Dashwood asked, her tone tinged with disappointment.

"Yes, I must depart shortly," Willoughby replied, his words laced with a hint of reluctance.

"This is unfortunate, indeed. Mrs. Smith must be obeyed, though – and her business shouldn't keep you away from us for long, I hope," Mrs. Dashwood ventured.

Willoughby's complexion deepened as he replied, "You're too kind, but I won't be returning to Devonshire anytime soon. My visits to Mrs. Smith are infrequent, and I wouldn't wish to intrude on your hospitality."
