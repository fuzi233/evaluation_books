It was a feeling he knew well, a tension that ebbed and flowed like the gentle lapping of the river against the shore.

As they walked side by side through the quiet streets of St. Augustine, the sound of their footsteps echoed off the empty houses. The air was heavy with the scent of blooming magnolias, and May's laughter carried on the breeze like a melody. Newland's thoughts swirled with the memories of their past encounters, the stolen glances and whispered conversations that had become a language all their own.

They reached the old orange-garden, a tranquil oasis beyond the town. The trees, their branches tangled with vines, cast dappled shadows on the ground. May's eyes sparkled as she walked ahead, her long strides eating up the distance. Newland watched her, his heart pounding in his chest like a slow drumbeat.

As they sat down on a weathered bench, Newland wrapped his arm around May, pulling her close. His lips brushed against hers, and for a moment, time froze.
