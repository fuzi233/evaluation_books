In the warm light of dawn, Mr. Henleigh Mallinger Grandcourt sat at his breakfast table, surrounded by the tranquil beauty of his estate. The summer air wafted through the open windows, carrying the sweet scent of blooming wildflowers and the soft chirping of birds. His companion, Mr. Lush, sat across from him, savoring the rich flavors of his morning meal. As they broke their fast, the gentle rustle of leaves and the soft padding of paws on the floor filled the room, punctuated by the occasional bark of the dogs that roamed freely within.

A dozen canines of various breeds wandered about, their tails wagging lazily as they vied for attention. Fetch, a beautiful liver-colored water-spaniel, stood steadfastly by Grandcourt's side, her expressive brown eyes fixed adoringly on her master. In his lap, he cradled a tiny Maltese dog, its delicate features and silver collar a testament to his affection for the smaller creatures. As he absently stroked the tiny dog's fur, his gaze remained fixed on Fetch, whose eyes seemed to plead silently for recognition.

The air was thick with the scent of cigar smoke as Grandcourt puffed on his favorite pipe. Lush, seated opposite him, watched the scene unfold with a mixture of amusement and curiosity. The dogs, sensing the tension between the two men, moved restlessly about the room, their tails wagging in anticipation of treats or attention. Fetch, however, remained steadfast, her eyes never leaving her master's face.

As the morning wore on, the atmosphere grew more strained. Grandcourt's patience with Fetch wore thin, and he finally lost his temper, pushing her away with a curt gesture. The tiny Maltese dog, sensing its master's distraction, tumbled to the table, its black nose peeking out from beneath a salt-cellar. Lush, sensing an opportunity to intervene, rose to take Fetch outside, depositing her with a gentle smile and a pat on the head.
