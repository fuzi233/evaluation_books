The scent of stale wine and damp earth hung heavy over the dining hall. The Esmond family's evening meal unfolded with an air of forced harmony, each member navigating the delicate balance of polite conversation. Madame de Bernstein's bright smile illuminated the room, her words a gentle brook that flowed effortlessly between the topics of food, family, and the passing of years. As she spoke of her mother's beauty and kindness, her eyes sparkled with a mix of nostalgia and longing.

Harry, seated across from her, listened intently, his gaze drawn to the stories of his grandmother's life. The tales of her happiness with her second husband, a union marked by a significant age disparity, seemed to captivate the young man. Maria, too, was entranced, her eyes locked onto Madame de Bernstein as she spoke of her own past transgressions. The Baroness's words wove a spell of self-deprecation, each apology and confession a thread that stitched together the intricate tapestry of family relationships.

As the evening wore on, Lord Castlewood's expression remained impassive, a mask that concealed his thoughts. His aunt's sudden display of amiability puzzled him, but he chose not to probe the matter further. The Baroness's good humor, however, was contagious, spreading throughout the table like a warm breeze on a summer's day. Even the usually reserved Mr. Will found himself smiling, his eyes shining with genuine warmth as he responded to Madame de Bernstein's playful jabs.

The evening's conversation ebbed and flowed like a lazy river, each participant contributing their own unique melody to the symphony of words. As the night wore on, the boundaries between past and present began to blur, and the room seemed to vibrate with the echoes of memories long forgotten. In this atmosphere of easy camaraderie, even the most guarded of hearts began to open, and the weight of unspoken words started to lift.
