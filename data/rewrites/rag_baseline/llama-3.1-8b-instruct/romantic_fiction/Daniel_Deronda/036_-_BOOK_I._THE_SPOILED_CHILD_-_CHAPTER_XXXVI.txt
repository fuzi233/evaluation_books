Among them, Daniel Deronda stood poised, observing the murmurs of conversation that ebbed and flowed like a slow-moving river. His gaze drifted to the imposing figure of Mr. Vandernoodt, a man whose words carried the weight of a whispered secret.

"What a curious creature Grandcourt is," Mr. Vandernoodt mused, his eyes glinting with amusement. "A man of considerable means, yet he's chosen to marry this young woman, Gwendolen, instead of the woman who has held him enthralled for nigh on ten years." He paused, his voice taking on a conspiratorial tone. "Some say she's a beauty, this Mrs. Glasher, with a fire in her eyes that could melt the coldest of hearts."

Deronda's expression remained inscrutable, but a spark of curiosity danced within him. He had heard whispers of Grandcourt's past, of the scandalous affair that had left a trail of broken hearts in its wake. Yet, he couldn't help but wonder if there was truth to the rumors. "What right did he have to marry Gwendolen?" he asked, his voice low and measured.

Mr. Vandernoodt chuckled, his cigar smoke curling lazily towards the ceiling. "Ah, but that's the question, isn't it? Some say Grandcourt was swayed by his own stubbornness, that he chose to marry Gwendolen out of sheer obstinacy. Others claim he was driven by a desire to prove himself, to show the world that he could rise above his humble beginnings." He leaned in, his voice dropping to a whisper. "But I've heard tell of a darker truth, one that speaks of a woman who wields power and influence over Grandcourt, a woman who has been his mistress for nigh on a decade."

Deronda's eyes narrowed, his mind racing with the implications. Could it be true? Did Grandcourt truly have a secret life, one that he kept hidden from the prying eyes of society?
