As we drove along the winding road, the crumbling stones of the abandoned village came into view, their thatched roofs worn smooth by the elements. My mind, however, was elsewhere, consumed by the unsettling words of the old General. His voice carried on the breeze, a gentle reminder of the eerie coincidences that seemed to be unfolding around me. The languor that had beset Millarca, the enigmatic disappearances from her room, and the unexplained nocturnal wanderings all seemed to mirror the strange occurrences that had begun to affect me. I felt a shiver run down my spine as I recalled the vivid dreams that had invaded my own slumber, the sensation of icy fingers tracing patterns on my skin, and the oppressive feeling of being slowly strangled.

We passed beneath the twisted branches of ancient trees, their gnarled limbs reaching out like skeletal fingers. The old General's words hung in the air, a somber prophecy that seemed to foreshadow the darkness that lay ahead. "This was once the palatial residence of the Karnsteins," he murmured, his eyes fixed on the ruined castle that loomed before us. "A family cursed by their own depravity, their malevolent legacy etched into the very fabric of this forsaken place." He gestured toward the chapel, its Gothic spires piercing the sky like shards of bone. "Their bloodstained annals remain, a testament to the horrors that unfolded within these walls."

As we stepped down from the carriage, the crunch of gravel beneath our feet seemed to echo through the stillness. The air was heavy with the scent of damp earth and decay. We ascended the overgrown path, our footsteps leading us deeper into the heart of the castle. The old General's words trailed behind us, a haunting refrain that seemed to grow louder with each step. "The rustics preserve the local traditions of great families," he said, his voice barely above a whisper. "Perhaps the woodman who tends to the trees surrounding the chapel will possess the knowledge we seek. He may be able to guide us to the resting place of Mircalla, Countess of Karnstein, and unlock the secrets that lie within these ancient stones."
