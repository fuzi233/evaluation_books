As Major Pendennis navigated the intricate web of social obligations and professional relationships, it became increasingly evident that his reputation as a seasoned military officer was inextricably linked to his ability to manage the delicate balance between personal relationships and professional conduct. The morning following the tumultuous events of the previous evening, Morgan, his loyal but stoic valet, attended to the Major's morning routine with the same gravity and respect he had displayed countless times before. As he laid out the silver dressing-case and strapped the razor, he presented a picture of unyielding dedication, his demeanor a testament to the strict codes of conduct that governed their interactions.

Noticing the Major's subdued mood, Morgan tactfully broached the subject of securing a temporary replacement, having discreetly approached Frosch, a seasoned courier and valet, at the Wheel of Fortune Club the previous night. Morgan's recommendation was motivated by a desire to ensure the Major's comfort during his stay, as much as by a genuine concern for his own future prospects. The introduction of Frosch, who had served under the esteemed Lord De la Pole and later accompanied young Lord Cubley on a foreign tour, marked a turning point in the Major's plans, one that would require a measured approach to diplomacy and tact.

As Morgan departed, the Major's biting sarcasm and condescending remarks left the valet feeling both frustrated and hurt. Yet, he remained steadfast in his duty, attending to the Major's needs with the same attention to detail and decorum that had characterized their relationship for so long. Meanwhile, the Major retreated to his quarters, where he began to prepare for the day ahead, surrounded by the sparse but meaningful possessions that had accumulated over the years: a well-worn Bible, a prized novel by Pen, the Duke of Wellington's dispatches, and a selection of prints, maps, and portraits that served as a poignant reminder of his storied past. As he readied himself for the challenges that lay ahead, the Major's thoughts turned to the complexities of his own character, and the delicate art of navigating the intricate social hierarchies that governed his world.
