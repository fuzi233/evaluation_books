As the last rays of sunlight faded from the landscape, I found myself reluctant to follow my guide into the silence of the evening. The thought of surrendering to sleep, surrounded by the stillness of the dead, held little appeal. In a world where reason should remain constant, why succumb to the rhythms of the dead, only to risk losing track of time? The sleepers in this house seemed to disregard the passage of hours, their days blending into an endless night. I hesitated, unsure of what else to do, and trailed behind my guide in silence.

The librarian led the way, his footsteps quiet on the heather-covered ground. As night descended, the chill of the chamber of death began to seep into the air, weighing heavily on my heart. I lost sight of my guide, but the sound of the raven's wings beating in the darkness guided me forward. Fireflies and butterflies flitted about, their ethereal forms illuminated by the faint moonlight.

As the moon slowly rose above the horizon, casting an eerie glow across the landscape, the raven landed on a nearby stone. His piercing gaze settled upon me, and he spoke in a low, raspy voice, "You must make acquaintance with the horse that will carry you in the morning." With a flick of his beak, he summoned the majestic creature from the shadows.

A faint drumming echoed through the air, growing louder with each passing moment. The moonlight danced across the landscape, and suddenly, a powerful stallion emerged from the darkness. His mane flowed behind him like a wild sea crest, and his tail whipped about, casting shadows on the ground. The raven's call had brought forth a creature worthy of the gods – a horse of imposing stature, with muscles honed to perfection and a coat as black as the night sky. His eyes gleamed with an otherworldly intensity, like two glowing coals shining behind a veil of blue film.

I felt an overwhelming urge to possess this magnificent beast, to own him and cherish him as I had never owned anything before. The sight of him awakened a deep longing within me, a desire that bordered on covetousness. I reached out to touch his sleek hide, feeling the smooth, cool surface beneath my fingers. His ears pricked up, and he nuzzled my hand, drawing me closer. I whispered softly into his ear, breathing life into him, and he responded with a gentle snort, his breath mingling with mine.
