Hester's revelation hung heavy in the air, like a whispered secret shared between the trees. Martin Lambert's initial reaction was a deep-seated anger, directed towards the young man who had captured his daughter's heart. "Fie upon such rascals, both English and Indian!" he exclaimed, his voice laced with indignation. "I wish he'd stumbled upon a doorpost and broken his nose instead of winning over my child's affections."

His wife, Molly, intervened gently, her voice a soothing balm to the Colonel's ire. "Perhaps, dear Martin, this young man's arrival is a providential blessing. We may have the chance to guide him towards a better path, and in doing so, bring joy to our family." Martin's scowl deepened, his brow furrowed in concern. "It's a cruel irony, Molly. We nurture and care for them, tending to their every need, and then, like a thief in the night, they steal our hearts away. I'm a king in my own home, only to have my reign usurped by a pair of sparkling eyes and a mop of golden hair."

Molly laid a gentle hand on her husband's arm, her eyes shining with a knowing glint. "We women are bound to follow our husbands, dear Martin. Our courtship was a brief one, but I believe I did my duty to you." The Colonel's expression softened, his love for his wife evident in the gentle curve of his lips. "Ah, Molly, you were always a treasure, and I'm a lucky man to have you by my side. But, oh, how I fear for the future when my daughters marry and bring their husbands into our midst!"

Molly's laughter was like music, a sweet melody that soothed Martin's frazzled nerves. "Of course, my dear girls will marry, and I'll welcome their husbands with open arms. And when that day comes, I'll... Well, let's just say I'll have to be on my best behavior." Martin's eyes twinkled with mirth, and he leaned in to kiss his wife's cheek. "I'll make sure to be on my toes, my dear, for the sake of our family's peace."
