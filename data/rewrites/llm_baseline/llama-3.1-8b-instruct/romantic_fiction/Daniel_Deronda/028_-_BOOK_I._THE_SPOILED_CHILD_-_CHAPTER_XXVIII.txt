As the evening sun cast its golden light upon the rectory, Mr. and Mrs. Gascoigne, accompanied by their daughter Anna, made their way to Offendene, eager to offer their congratulations to Gwendolen on her recent engagement to Sir Hugo Grandcourt. The news had spread like wildfire, and the air was alive with whispers and speculation about the union.

"My dear, let me offer my warmest felicitations," said the rector, his eyes twinkling with warmth. "A lifelong partnership is a solemn commitment, indeed. But from what I've observed of Mr. Grandcourt's behavior, I believe this unexpected turn of events may prove a blessing in disguise. His generosity towards Mrs. Davilow has shown a side of him that bodes well for your future together."

Mr. Gascoigne nodded in agreement, recalling how Grandcourt had spoken of providing for his bride-to-be, a gesture that Gwendolen had recounted with striking accuracy. "Indeed, my dear, it's clear that Mr. Grandcourt has a kind heart, and I daresay he would have made the same offer regardless of your time abroad."

Mrs. Gascoigne added, "Though, of course, it's natural to feel a sense of gratitude towards a suitor who perseveres in his affection. A woman owes a great debt of thanks to a man who makes such a commitment." She glanced at Gwendolen, expecting her to nod in agreement, but instead, the young woman's expression remained uncertain.

"I'm not entirely convinced that I do, aunt," Gwendolen replied, her tone tinged with a hint of mischief. "I'm not certain what's expected of me in this situation." Her words sparked a gentle smile from the rector, who patted her shoulder reassuringly.

Anna, who had been watching the exchange, felt a pang of sadness. She had once fantasized about Gwendolen and Rex marrying, but now she found herself struggling to muster genuine joy for her friend's engagement. As she leaned in to kiss Gwendolen, she whispered, "I do hope you'll find happiness, dear one." Yet, beneath her words, a small, unspoken prayer whispered in her heart: May I find contentment in serving you, Gwendolen, even if it means Rex remains out of reach.
