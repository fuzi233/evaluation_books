My gaze fell upon Naani, her delicate features etched with fatigue, her eyes sunken from the weight of our arduous trek. A pang of guilt struck me, for I had pushed her too hard, neglecting her fragile constitution in my relentless pursuit of our destination. I vowed to be more mindful of her needs, to temper my strength with compassion.

Naani, however, refused to let me berate myself, her expression softening as she gazed up at me with eyes that sparkled like stars on a clear night.
