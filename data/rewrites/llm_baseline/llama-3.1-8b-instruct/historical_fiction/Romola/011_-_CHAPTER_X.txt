As the sun beat down on the cobblestone streets of Florence, Tito walked towards the Via de' Bardi, his florins safely in hand. It had been three weeks since he'd handed them over to Cennini, and his demeanor betrayed no hint of unease. His nature was inherently optimistic, his very presence a reflection of the vibrant atmosphere that surrounded him. With a carefree toss of his dark curls, he shed his berretta, allowing the warm breeze to caress his skin. His brow, a finely chiseled expanse of smooth, unlined skin, wore no mask of duplicity or guile – merely a testament to his unassuming charm.

As he navigated the narrow alleys, Tito's gaze wandered upwards, taking in the upper windows of the houses, their shutters adorned with festive ribbons in honor of San Giovanni. His eyes, bright and open, drank in the scene before him, their cerulean hue a perfect match for the sky above. His eyelids, unwearied and untroubled, gazed out upon the world with an unwavering innocence. There was something enigmatic about Tito's face, a quality that defied easy interpretation. Some might find themselves drawn to him, while others might be repelled; yet, to those who beheld him, he presented a singularly authentic presence – a reflection of his inner peace.

The festivities of the Festa were in full swing, and the narrow side-streets were beginning to empty as the crowd spilled out in search of new attractions. Amidst the throngs, a knot of people had gathered near the hostelry of the Bertucce, their attention fixed on some spectacle. Tito was about to turn aside into a nearby alley when a plaintive cry pierced the air: "Holy Virgin, help me!" A young contadina, her white hood askew, struggled to free herself from the grasp of a conjuror, who sought to placate her with jests and smiles. The onlookers, their faces aglow with amusement, urged the contadina to surrender, using a variety of terms to describe her stubbornness. Tito's ears picked up the commotion, and he made his way through the crowd, his heart moved by the distress in the contadina's voice.
