As I wandered through the winding corridors of the thousandth floor, the whispers of the past echoed through my mind. It was a tale that had been told and retold in every city of the Great Redoubt, a legend of a second Place of Refuge, hidden away in the desolate expanse of the Night Lands. A sanctuary where millions of humans had gathered to resist the end of the world, their spirits unbroken despite the crushing weight of the void.

I had heard this story countless times during my travels, which began when I turned seventeen and lasted for three years and two hundred and twenty-five days. Each city, like a small, self-contained world, had its own version of this myth, but the essence remained the same. I had met people along the way, some of whom left an indelible mark on my heart, but our paths diverged, and I was left with only memories. Life, it seemed, was a finite canvas, and each person had to contribute their brushstrokes to the grand tapestry of the Redoubt.

In the libraries of the cities, I had uncovered numerous texts that spoke of this other Refuge, some written with conviction, others laced with doubt. Scholars pored over these ancient scrolls, their minds grappling with the possibility of a haven that might have existed. Yet, as the years went by, the Records became relics of a bygone era, cherished by a select few who struggled to reconcile the past with the present.

For me, however, the notion of this Refuge never wavered. From the moment our Master Monstruwacan spoke of it, I was convinced of its existence. His words had been a spark that ignited a fire within me, and I felt an affinity for him that transcended our differences in age. As I grew older, our bond deepened, and when I turned twenty-one, he offered me a place in the Tower of Observation, the pinnacle of the Pyramid. This was a gift I treasured, for in the Redoubt, the Tower was the epicenter of knowledge, where the human quest for understanding was nurtured and sustained.

My unique gift – the ability to hear the Night-Hearing – played a significant role in my appointment. It was a rare talent, one that allowed me to perceive the invisible vibrations of the aether, tuning into the eternal darkness like a skilled musician listening for a haunting melody.
