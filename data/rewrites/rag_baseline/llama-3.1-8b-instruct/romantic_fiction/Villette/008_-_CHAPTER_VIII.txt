As I navigated the labyrinthine corridors of Madame Beck's establishment, I found myself drawn into a world of contrasts. The kitchen, a space that defied expectations, seemed to operate on a different paradigm altogether. A behemoth of a furnace dominated one corner, its presence a testament to the ingenuity of those who had designed this peculiar culinary setup. I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease as I realized that this was, in fact, a viable alternative to traditional cooking methods.

The maîtresse, a woman of unassuming demeanor, escorted me to a small inner room, dubbed a "cabinet." There, a cook presented me with a meal consisting of unfamiliar meats served in a piquant sauce, accompanied by chopped potatoes infused with an intriguing mixture of vinegar and sugar, a tartine, and a baked pear. My hunger got the better of me, and I partook in the repast with gratitude.

Following the evening prayers, Madame Beck made her way to my quarters, her purpose clear. She requested that I accompany her upstairs, leading me through a series of diminutive dormitories, each one a testament to the complex history of the building. We passed through the oratory, a long, somber room illuminated by two flickering candles, their soft light casting an eerie glow on the pale crucifix adorning the wall.

In the adjacent room, three children slumbered peacefully in their tiny beds, surrounded by the oppressive heat emanating from a nearby stove. The air was heavy with the scent of whisky, a potent aroma that clashed jarringly with the tranquil atmosphere. Beside the table, a coarse woman, resplendent in a garish striped dress and apron, reclined in a chair, fast asleep, with a bottle and an empty glass at her elbow.

Madame Beck surveyed this tableau with an air of detached serenity, neither smiling nor frowning, her countenance untroubled by the scene before her. With a serene gesture, she indicated a fourth bed, beckoning me to take my place beside the others. As she extinguished the candle and replaced it with a night-lamp, I felt a sense of relief wash over me, tempered by the realization that I was now in the care of this enigmatic woman.
